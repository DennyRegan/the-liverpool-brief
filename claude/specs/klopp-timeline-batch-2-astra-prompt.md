# Prompt for Astra: Klopp timeline, batch 2 (extended entries)

Paste everything below the line into Astra. It is self-contained: Astra needs no file access.

---

You are fact-checking the second batch of entries (the extended entries, which readers see when they expand a season) for an interactive timeline of Jürgen Klopp's Liverpool (October 2015 to May 2024) for The Liverpool Brief. Each entry is a short card with a date, a result line (matches only) and a one-sentence summary. Check every date, score, scorer, minute and statement of fact. Do not rewrite for style.

## Rules
1. Find your own sources on the web and open each page you rely on. Do not use memory, search snippets or anything I give you here as evidence. The entries below are the claims to test, not sources.
2. Prefer primary or near-primary records: the club, UEFA, FIFA, the Premier League, the FA, match reports on the day, and LFChistory's season archives for dates and results. Use at least two independent sources for any date or score you cannot confirm on a primary page.
3. Keep fact and judgement apart. Dates, results, scorers and stated events are fact and are what you check. Which matches count as "main" is editorial and is not yours to rate.
4. Where sources conflict, do not pick one silently. Report both, say which you think is right and why, and mark the claim CONFLICT.
5. Announcement dates and arrival dates for signings are different things. Report the announced date and the effective (arrival or registration) date separately wherever both exist.
6. Never invent a day. If an entry is labelled by month or window ("July 2016", "Summer 2017") and you can find the exact day, give it and the source, but do not assume it.
7. Transfer fees are deliberately left out of the timeline. Do not check or add fees.
8. Attribute quotes and motives. If an entry states something Klopp, a player or a club said, check the wording or the paraphrase against the original and give the date and outlet.
9. Say plainly if you could not confirm something. "Unverified" is a valid verdict.

## Verdicts
- CONFIRMED: two sources or one primary page agree.
- CORRECT: the entry is wrong. Give the fix and the page.
- CONFLICT: reliable sources disagree. Give both.
- UNVERIFIED: nothing reliable found.

## What to return
1. A table, one row per claim: `entry id | claim | verdict | correct value if different | source URL (page you opened) | note`.
2. A short list of CORRECT, CONFLICT and UNVERIFIED items at the top, so they can be actioned first.
3. For the dates marked PRIORITY below, the exact date you found, or a clear statement that none could be found.

Do not edit anything. Report only.

## Priority checks
- Every date and score in the result line.
- Anything stated in a summary: scorers, who did what, records (for example the 116 km figure, "record ninth assist", "44 league games without defeat").
- Dates of the signings: Oxlade-Chamberlain 31 August 2017 (deadline day); Keïta confirmed 29 August 2017 and joined 1 July 2018; Shaqiri "mid-July" 2018 (find the day).
- european-cup-parade-2019: confirm the route (Allerton Maze to the waterfront) and the day.
- Where a summary is vaguer than the sources ("late on", "deep into stoppage time"), say whether it is accurate. Do not ask for precision the card does not claim.

## The entries

### Season 2015-16
**tottenham-first-match** (match). 17 October 2015. Premier League. Tottenham Hotspur 0–0 Liverpool.
Liverpool covered 116 kilometres, their highest figure of the season, in a goalless draw that was Klopp's first match in charge.

**bournemouth-league-cup** (match). 28 October 2015. League Cup fourth round. Liverpool 1–0 Bournemouth.
Klopp's first victory as Liverpool manager came in the League Cup, three days before his first league win at Chelsea.

**chelsea-3-1-stamford-bridge** (match). 31 October 2015. Premier League. Chelsea 1–3 Liverpool.
Coutinho scored twice and Benteke once as Liverpool won at the reigning champions, with Firmino playing as a false nine.

**city-4-1-etihad** (match). 21 November 2015. Premier League. Manchester City 1–4 Liverpool.
Coutinho, Firmino and Škrtel scored, along with an own goal by Mangala, as Liverpool won 4–1 at Manchester City.

**norwich-5-4** (match). 23 January 2016. Premier League. Norwich City 4–5 Liverpool.
Adam Lallana scored the winner in a 5–4 win at Carrow Road, and Klopp's glasses were broken in the celebrations.

**league-cup-final-2016** (match). 28 February 2016. League Cup final. Liverpool 1–1 Manchester City (City won 3–1 on penalties).
Coutinho equalised late on, but City won 3–1 on penalties after extra time in Klopp's first final as Liverpool manager.

**united-europa-league-2016** (match). 17 March 2016. Europa League round of 16, second leg. Manchester United 1–1 Liverpool.
Coutinho's away goal settled the tie at 3–1 on aggregate after a 2–0 first leg at Anfield, in the clubs' first European meeting.

### Season 2016-17
**arsenal-4-3-mane-debut** (match). 14 August 2016. Premier League. Arsenal 3–4 Liverpool.
Sadio Mané scored on his debut in a 4–3 win at the Emirates, in a match Liverpool had led 4–1.

**leicester-4-1-main-stand** (match). 10 September 2016. Premier League. Liverpool 4–1 Leicester City.
The champions were beaten on the first day the rebuilt Main Stand was in use at Anfield, with Firmino scoring twice.

**everton-derby-2016** (match). 19 December 2016. Premier League. Everton 0–1 Liverpool.
Mané reacted first after Sturridge hit the post and scored into an empty net in stoppage time at Goodison Park.

**watford-can-goal** (match). 1 May 2017. Premier League. Watford 0–1 Liverpool.
Emre Can's goal won the match at Vicarage Road and was later voted the club's goal of the season.

### Season 2017-18
**oxlade-chamberlain-signs** (signing). 31 August 2017.
Oxlade-Chamberlain joined on transfer deadline day, a midfielder who was in the last year of his contract at Arsenal.

**tottenham-wembley-4-1** (match). 22 October 2017. Premier League. Tottenham Hotspur 4–1 Liverpool.
Klopp took off Dejan Lovren after 31 minutes, and the defeat left Liverpool ninth in the table after nine league matches.

**roma-5-2-first-leg** (match). 24 April 2018. Champions League semi-final, first leg. Liverpool 5–2 Roma.
Salah scored twice against his former club and Milner made a record ninth assist of the campaign, before Liverpool went through 7–6 on aggregate.

### Season 2018-19
**keita-joins-leipzig** (signing). 1 July 2018.
Liverpool confirmed Keïta's signing in August 2017, and he joined a year later, on the same date as Fabinho.

**shaqiri-joins-stoke** (signing). July 2018.
Shaqiri joined from relegated Stoke in mid-July, with Liverpool meeting the release clause in his contract.

**everton-origi-derby-2018** (match). 2 December 2018. Premier League. Liverpool 1–0 Everton.
Divock Origi headed in from close range deep into stoppage time after Van Dijk's volley came back off the crossbar.

**napoli-1-0-group-stage** (match). 11 December 2018. Champions League group stage. Liverpool 1–0 Napoli.
Salah scored and Alisson blocked Milik's shot in stoppage time, which kept Liverpool in the competition on goals scored.

**city-2-1-etihad-2019** (match). 3 January 2019. Premier League. Manchester City 2–1 Liverpool.
Leroy Sané scored the winner 18 minutes from time, and goal-line technology showed an earlier Mané shot had stopped about a centimetre short.

**bayern-3-1-munich** (match). 13 March 2019. Champions League round of 16, second leg. Bayern Munich 1–3 Liverpool.
Mané scored twice and Van Dijk headed in a corner as Liverpool won in Munich after a goalless first leg at Anfield.

**newcastle-origi-2019** (match). 4 May 2019. Premier League. Newcastle United 2–3 Liverpool.
Divock Origi headed in Shaqiri's free-kick four minutes from time, three days after the 3–0 first-leg defeat in Barcelona.

**european-cup-parade-2019** (moment). 2 June 2019.
An open-top bus carried the squad from Allerton Maze to the waterfront the day after the final in Madrid.

### Season 2019-20
**super-cup-2019** (match). 14 August 2019. UEFA Super Cup. Liverpool 2–2 Chelsea (Liverpool won 5–4 on penalties).
Liverpool won the Super Cup in Istanbul on penalties after extra time, with the new goalkeeper Adrián saving the decisive kick.

**villa-2-1-late-comeback** (match). 2 November 2019. Premier League. Aston Villa 1–2 Liverpool.
Robertson equalised late on and Mané headed the winner in stoppage time, after Liverpool had trailed to a Trezeguet volley.

**city-3-1-anfield-2019** (match). 10 November 2019. Premier League. Liverpool 3–1 Manchester City.
Fabinho, Salah and Mané scored, leaving Liverpool eight points clear at the top of the table.

**leicester-4-0-boxing-day** (match). 26 December 2019. Premier League. Leicester City 0–4 Liverpool.
Firmino scored twice, Milner scored a penalty with his first touch and Alexander-Arnold added the fourth, against the side then second in the table.

**united-2-0-anfield-2020** (match). 19 January 2020. Premier League. Liverpool 2–0 Manchester United.
Van Dijk headed Liverpool in front early, and Salah finished after Alisson's long kick in stoppage time.

**watford-3-0-unbeaten-ends** (match). 29 February 2020. Premier League. Watford 3–0 Liverpool.
Ismaïla Sarr scored twice and Troy Deeney once, ending a run of 44 league games without defeat.

**atletico-2-3-aet** (match). 11 March 2020. Champions League round of 16, second leg. Liverpool 2–3 Atlético Madrid (after extra time).
Atlético went through 4–2 on aggregate, ending the holders' defence of the trophy in Liverpool's last match in front of a crowd that season.

**palace-4-0-restart** (match). 24 June 2020. Premier League. Liverpool 4–0 Crystal Palace.
Alexander-Arnold, Salah, Fabinho and Mané scored, leaving Liverpool needing only Manchester City to drop points the next evening.

## Out of scope
