## Chronology

- 22 Aug 1959 — Liverpool begin their league campaign with a 2-3 defeat away to Cardiff City (Second Division).
- 26 Aug 1959 — First home league win: 4-2 v Bristol City at Anfield.
- 29 Aug 1959 — 5-3 v Hull City at Anfield (league).
- 9 Sep 1959 — Roger Hunt makes his Liverpool first-team debut and scores in a 2-0 league win v Scunthorpe United at Anfield.
- 7 Nov 1959 — 2-1 v Aston Villa at Anfield (league).
- 6 Nov 1959 — Dave Hickson signs from Everton (reported fee circa £12,000; some sources list £10,000).
- 14 Nov 1959 — 2-4 defeat at Lincoln City (league), in Phil Taylor’s final match in charge.
- 17 Nov 1959 — Phil Taylor resigns as Liverpool manager.
- 20 Nov 1959 — Tommy Leishman signs from St Mirren (commonly reported fee £10,000; some sources list £9,000).
- 21 Nov 1959 — 4-3 v Leyton Orient at Anfield under a caretaker manager (league).
- 28 Nov 1959 — 0-1 at Huddersfield Town under a caretaker manager (league).
- 1 Dec 1959 — Liverpool appoint Bill Shankly as manager (announcement/appointment timing varies across sources between 30 Nov and 1 Dec).
- 5 Dec 1959 — 3-1 v Ipswich Town at Anfield (league).
- 12 Dec 1959 — 2-0 at Bristol Rovers (league).
- 19 Dec 1959 — Bill Shankly’s first match in charge: 0-4 v Cardiff City at Anfield (league).
- 26/28 Dec 1959 — Back-to-back league games v Charlton Athletic (0-3 away; 2-0 home).
- 2 Jan 1960 — 1-0 at Hull City (league).
- 9 Jan 1960 — FA Cup third round: 2-1 v Leyton Orient at Anfield.
- 30 Jan 1960 — FA Cup fourth round: 1-3 v Manchester United at Anfield; Liverpool eliminated.
- 5 Mar 1960 — 5-1 v Stoke City at Anfield (league).
- 12 Mar 1960 — 1-2 at Portsmouth (league).
- 19 Mar 1960 — 2-2 v Huddersfield Town at Anfield (league).
- 30 Mar 1960 — 4-4 at Aston Villa (league).
- 2 Apr 1960 — 1-3 v Lincoln City at Anfield (league).
- 16 Apr 1960 — Ian Callaghan makes his Liverpool debut in a 4-0 win v Bristol Rovers at Anfield (league).
- 18–19 Apr 1960 — 3-0 v Rotherham United (H); 2-2 at Rotherham United (league).
- 23 Apr 1960 — 1-0 at Ipswich Town (league).
- 27 Apr 1960 — Liverpool Senior Cup: 2-3 v Everton at Goodison Park.
- 30 Apr 1960 — Final league match: 3-0 v Sunderland at Anfield; Liverpool finish 3rd in the Second Division.

## Unresolved

- Identity of the November 1959 caretaker: LFChistory’s match pages label “Caretaker Manager” for 21 and 28 Nov 1959, but do not name him. Wikipedia states Bob Paisley filled the role for those two games; an official club source naming the caretaker for those specific dates was not found in this research pass. Further contemporary newspaper confirmation would strengthen this. ([lfchistory.net](https://www.lfchistory.net/games/18?utm_source=openai))

- Shankly appointment/announcement date: LFC official material gives 1 December 1959 as the appointment date; LFChistory notes the club “announced” the appointment on 30 November 1959; a later secondary feature (This Is Anfield) refers to 14 December 1959 in relation to securing his signature/arrival. These likely reflect different stages (announcement vs. board ratification vs. start on site), but the exact administrative sequence is variably presented. ([members.liverpoolfc.com](https://members.liverpoolfc.com/history/past-managers/bill-shankly?utm_source=openai))

- Transfer fees: Dave Hickson’s fee is variously reported as £12,000 (Liverpool Echo via LFChistory) and £10,000 (EFC Statto). Tommy Leishman’s fee appears as £10,000 (LFChistory/LFC) and £9,000 (St Mirren database). Absent access to definitive contract documentation, keep fees as “reported” and note the ranges. ([lfchistory.net](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/1068?utm_source=openai))
