## Chronology

- 25 May 2005 — UEFA Champions League Final, Atatürk Olympic Stadium, Istanbul, Turkey; kick-off 21:45 local (20:45 CET).
- 1' — Paolo Maldini scores for Milan (volley from an Andrea Pirlo free-kick).
- 39' — Hernán Crespo scores for Milan (tapped in from Shevchenko square pass after counter).
- 44' — Hernán Crespo scores for Milan (through ball from Kaká; dinked finish).
- Half-time — Milan lead 3-0.
- 54' — Steven Gerrard scores for Liverpool (header from John Arne Riise cross).
- 56' — Vladimír Šmicer scores for Liverpool (low right-footed drive).
- 60' — Xabi Alonso scores for Liverpool (rebounds after his penalty is saved; penalty awarded for Gennaro Gattuso’s foul on Gerrard).
- Late ET — Jerzy Dudek makes a double save from Andriy Shevchenko to keep the match level.
- Penalties — Milan shoot first; Liverpool win the shoot-out 3-2 to take the European title.

## Unresolved

- Exact second of Maldini’s goal: UEFA states 52 seconds; 11v11 lists 51 seconds. The record (fastest Champions League final goal) is consistent, but the precise second varies by source.  
- Exact minute of Dudek’s double save: Commonly reported as 117’, while UEFA’s match report describes “with two minutes left.” Use “late in extra time” if an exact minute is required to avoid conflicting attributions.  
- Attendance figure: UEFA Technical Report lists 69,600; some secondary compilations list 69,000 (rounded). The UEFA figure is preferred but note the rounding discrepancy.  
- Baroš booking (81’): Present on 11v11; not found in the Guardian’s minute-by-minute. Treated as single-source unless a second contemporary record is located.
