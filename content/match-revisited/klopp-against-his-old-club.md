Jürgen Klopp managed Borussia Dortmund for seven years. He won the Bundesliga twice with them and took them to a Champions League final. He left in the summer of 2015 and became Liverpool manager that October.

In March 2016 the Europa League quarter-final draw put the two clubs together. The first leg in Dortmund on 7 April ended 1–1. Divock Origi scored for Liverpool and Mats Hummels equalised early in the second half. Origi's away goal gave Liverpool a slight advantage for the second leg at Anfield a week later.

Dortmund were second in the Bundesliga, behind Bayern Munich. Their team included Hummels, Marco Reus, Henrikh Mkhitaryan and Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang.

Klopp picked Simon Mignolet; Nathaniel Clyne, Dejan Lovren, Mamadou Sakho, Alberto Moreno; Emre Can, James Milner, Adam Lallana; Philippe Coutinho, Roberto Firmino, Divock Origi.

* * *

Dortmund scored twice in the first nine minutes. Mignolet blocked a close-range shot from Aubameyang, and Mkhitaryan scored from the rebound. Four minutes later Reus set up Aubameyang to score again. Liverpool now needed three goals to go through. It was 2–0 at half-time.

Three minutes after the restart Can played a pass through to Origi, who scored. Then, just before the hour, Hummels found Reus, who curled the ball in. Dortmund led 3–1 on the night and 4–2 on aggregate, with three away goals. Liverpool needed three more.

Klopp sent on Joe Allen and Daniel Sturridge for Lallana and Firmino after 62 minutes. Four minutes later Coutinho exchanged passes with Milner and bent a shot from the edge of the area into the bottom corner.

With 13 minutes left Sakho headed in a Coutinho corner. It was 3–3 on the night, but Dortmund were still through on away goals.

* * *

The fourth came in the first minute of stoppage time. Liverpool took a free-kick short, Sturridge backheeled the ball to Milner, and Milner crossed. Lovren headed it in at the far post, in front of the Kop. Liverpool had won 4–3 on the night and 5–4 on aggregate.

Steven Beacom of the Belfast Telegraph described Lovren as an unlikely hero. Lovren said the feeling was amazing, and that the players had never stopped believing in themselves. Klopp praised the character of his team and the supporters. According to the Belfast Telegraph, he also said he believed Liverpool could now go on and win the competition.

Liverpool beat Villarreal in the semi-final, overturning a 1–0 defeat in Spain. They lost the final to Sevilla in Basel, 3–1.
