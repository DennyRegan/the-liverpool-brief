Liverpool spent much of Sunday afternoon looking for a way through at Bournemouth. Alexander Isak needed one clear opening. His 57th-minute finish gave Andoni Iraola a 1–0 win on his return to the Vitality Stadium and Liverpool their second Premier League victory of the season.

The first half offered little room for either side. Dominik Szoboszlai sent a free-kick over the bar, while Alisson made the sharper intervention at the other end, reacting to keep out Evanilson’s flick from Marcus Tavernier’s low cross. Bradley Barcola came closest for Liverpool before the interval, finding space to shoot through a crowd of players only for Đorđe Petrović to save with his legs.

Bournemouth began the second half with a better chance than any Liverpool had made. Rayan crossed into the six-yard area, but Ryan Christie steered his effort wide. Christie missed the target again during a spell in which the home side looked more likely to score.

Then Cody Gakpo changed the direction of the match. He beat two defenders down the right and sent a cross into the area. Florian Wirtz met it under pressure; the ball looped towards Isak, unmarked inside the box, and the striker finished. It was a goal made from Gakpo’s persistence as much as Isak’s composure.

Liverpool still had work to do. Evanilson broke free and drew a diving save from Alisson, while Bournemouth continued to send balls into the box. Jeremy Jacquet and Virgil van Dijk helped keep those attacks from becoming a sustained run of clear chances. At the other end, substitute Lewis Koumas had a late effort saved by Petrović.

There was no rush of Liverpool chances, and Bournemouth will regret Christie’s opportunity before the goal. But after three draws in their first four league games, Liverpool found a way to turn a close contest into three points. Isak supplied the finish; the clean sheet made it count.
