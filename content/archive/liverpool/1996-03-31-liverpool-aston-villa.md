Robbie Fowler scored twice at Old Trafford on 31 March 1996 to send Liverpool into the FA Cup final. Jason McAteer added a third in stoppage time as Roy Evans's side beat Aston Villa 3-0 and secured a Wembley meeting with Manchester United.

Villa had won the League Cup a week earlier. Liverpool had already beaten them twice in the league, but this semi-final remained close until Fowler scored his second with four minutes of normal time left.

Jamie Redknapp returned to Liverpool's starting side after a lengthy absence and supplied the opening goal. His free-kick reached Fowler, who headed past Mark Bosnich in the 16th minute. Gareth Southgate, troubled by a knee injury, was replaced by Steve Staunton soon afterwards.

Villa threatened an equaliser. David James saved from Dwight Yorke at close range before half-time and blocked Ugo Ehiogu's effort after the break. Liverpool still held only a one-goal lead as the match entered its closing stages.

The second goal came from another Redknapp free-kick. Staunton's clearing header reached Fowler near the edge of the penalty area, and the striker volleyed in to make it 2-0. It was his 33rd goal of the season.

McAteer then met Steve McManaman's low cross to complete the scoring. Liverpool had reached their eleventh FA Cup final, with Fowler's early header and late volley deciding a match in which James had also made important saves.

Evans's next concern was the league fixture against Newcastle at Anfield three days later. Speaking after the semi-final, he stressed the need to keep collecting league points rather than depend on the FA Cup for European qualification. The Wembley place was secured; the league campaign still had to be played out.
