*Liverpool 0–3 Manchester City · Premier League · Anfield · Sunday 11 October 2026, 4.30pm BST*

For an hour, Liverpool matched the unbeaten league leaders and, by their head coach's reckoning, bettered them. Then Ryan Gravenberch was sent off for a second booking, and within 16 minutes Manchester City had scored three times. Erling Haaland, Jérémy Doku and Rayan Cherki ended Liverpool's unbeaten start to the season and took Enzo Maresca's side to six league wins from six.

## The first hour

Andoni Iraola went into the game without Alexander Isak, Cody Gakpo and Dominik Szoboszlai, who Iraola said had returned from international duty with "tightness, an overload". Lewis Koumas made his first Premier League start as the central striker, with Bradley Barcola and Florian Wirtz around him and Gravenberch alongside Alexis Mac Allister in midfield.

Anfield was loud from the start in City's first game since an independent commission found them guilty of breaching the league's financial rules. The first half was tight rather than open. Jeremy Jacquet and Matheus Nunes each made important interventions to stop Cherki and Barcola. Munoz's cross-shot was deflected behind by Nico O'Reilly, and he later drove a low shot from 20 yards straight at Gianluigi Donnarumma. Alisson Becker gathered Enzo Fernández's snap-shot at the other end. Ronald Araújo was booked for protesting after a Marc Guéhi handball, a decision Iraola returned to afterwards. It was goalless at the break.

Liverpool pressed early in the second half. Barcola set up Koumas, whose shot deflected off Guéhi and was palmed away by Donnarumma. The City goalkeeper also saved from Gravenberch, and Mac Allister curled narrowly wide. Milos Kerkez blocked a shot from Ndiaye at the other end.

## The turning point

Gravenberch had already been booked early in the half for a foul on Elliot Anderson. On 62 minutes he tried to stop a Doku counter-attack, caught the substitute, and referee Chris Kavanagh showed him a second yellow card. Doku had been on the pitch for six minutes, having replaced Ndiaye.

City scored almost immediately. Doku dribbled into the area, the ball broke into the middle and Haaland drove it into the left side of the net. Three minutes later Doku came in from the left and curled a shot over Alisson into the top corner. On 78 minutes the substitute Joško Gvardiol flicked the ball into Cherki, who beat his man with a step-over and a dragback and finished from close range. Haaland had another chance after Antoine Semenyo's pass put him through, but he did not hit the target.

Iraola changed his side after the red card. Trey Nyoni replaced Wirtz, Jeremie Frimpong and Rio Ngumoha came on for Araújo and Koumas, and Joe Gomez and Federico Chiesa replaced Jacquet and Barcola late on. None of it changed the course of the game.

## What it means

Liverpool's first defeat of the season leaves them on nine points from six league games, nine behind City. They are also still without a home league win under Iraola. Sports Mole had them ninth after the game, a point behind fourth-placed Brentford, where they play on Saturday.

City are three points clear of Arsenal at the top and remain the only unbeaten side in the division. According to the club, it is the first time they have won consecutive league games at Anfield since January 1953, following February's 2–1 win.

## The managers

Iraola's verdict was that "we played two different games – one until the red card and one from the red card until the end", and that Liverpool were "the better team until the red card in the first 62 minutes". He was firm about Gravenberch's first booking: "I think the first foul of Ryan is never a yellow card – never." He told Sky Sports that "the referee gets in trouble with the first one", because a player on a yellow "cannot be as aggressive, you have to wait and defend lower". He linked it to Araújo's booking, saying that by that standard "every time someone complains has to be a yellow card".

He told BBC Sport he was "very proud of my players" and that "it's a loss and we didn't get anything from the performance but I liked it a lot". He also praised a crowd that had been "fantastic before, during, after". Virgil van Dijk told Sky Sports the red card "changed the game, especially when you concede quickly afterwards".

Maresca called it "a great result for sure" and admitted City had "struggled a bit" in the first half. With his club's appeal against the commission's findings still pending, he said that "all the organisation needs this kind of win". On the atmosphere he said City "need to learn how to deal with the noise because the noise will be with us at all of our away games". He added that by the end, "the noise was from us".

There is little time to dwell on it. Iraola noted that Liverpool "play four games in 10 days" and fly to Austria to face LASK in the Champions League on Wednesday. Iraola warned that "it's going to be difficult to recover any of the players" who missed this game. City host Paris Saint-Germain the same night.
