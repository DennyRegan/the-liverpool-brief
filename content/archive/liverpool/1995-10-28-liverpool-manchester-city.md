Manchester City returned to Anfield on 28 October 1995, three days after losing there 4–0 in the League Cup. Liverpool won the league meeting 6–0, with Ian Rush and Robbie Fowler scoring twice each and Jamie Redknapp and Neil Ruddock adding the other goals.

City were two down inside five minutes. Rush scored the first after goalkeeper Eike Immel blocked Jason McAteer's shot. Redknapp then beat Immel with a deflected free-kick. Liverpool had established the lead they would carry into half-time almost as soon as the match began.

Ruddock replaced John Barnes before the interval. He would become one of four Liverpool scorers during a second-half spell in which the home side turned their two-goal advantage into six.

Fowler made it 3–0 shortly after the restart. Mark Wright advanced down the right and found him with a cross; Fowler worked past defenders and finished low. Ruddock then headed in Redknapp's corner, and Fowler scored again on the hour after receiving McAteer's delivery.

Rush completed the scoring in the 64th minute. Immel saved Redknapp's effort, but Rush followed up and his shot looped into the net. Both starting forwards now had two goals, with more than 25 minutes still to play.

Liverpool finished the afternoon third, with seven wins and 23 points from 11 league matches. Alan Ball's City remained bottom with two points and no league victory. Across their two visits to Anfield that week, they had conceded ten goals without scoring; the league defeat was the heavier of the two.
