Liverpool lost the FA Cup final 1-0 to Manchester United at Wembley on 11 May 1996. Eric Cantona scored the only goal late in the second half, completing United's league and FA Cup Double and ending Liverpool's season with defeat.

A crowd of 79,007 saw the match remain goalless until its closing minutes. David James saved from David Beckham early on, while at the other end Stan Collymore forced Peter Schmeichel to turn a shot round his near post.

Liverpool had another opening shortly before half-time. Steve McManaman found Mark Wright, who laid the ball back for Jamie Redknapp. The midfielder shot over the bar, leaving the sides level at the interval.

James was required again early in the second half, saving Cantona's volley and preventing Andy Cole from converting the rebound. Redknapp later tested Schmeichel from distance, but neither side had made a breakthrough when Roy Evans changed his attack.

Ian Rush replaced Collymore in the 74th minute. It was Rush's final appearance for Liverpool, but the striker who had scored so often for the club could not provide a winning farewell.

Instead, Cantona decided the final. James came to punch away a Beckham corner, and the ball reached the United captain outside the penalty area. Cantona struck through the players in front of him and into the net.

Liverpool were unable to equalise. United became the first English club to win the league and FA Cup Double twice, having also done so in 1994.

For Liverpool, the cup run ended one match short of the trophy. Their 3-0 semi-final victory over Aston Villa had earned the Wembley place; the final itself was settled by one late finish after more than 80 minutes without a goal.
