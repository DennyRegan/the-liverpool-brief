Stan Collymore scored in stoppage time to give Liverpool a 4-3 victory over Newcastle United at Anfield on 3 April 1996. Both he and Robbie Fowler scored twice as Liverpool recovered from two separate deficits to win in front of 40,702 spectators.

Fowler opened the scoring in the second minute, heading in Collymore's cross. Newcastle answered through Les Ferdinand after ten minutes and David Ginola four minutes later. Ferdinand met Faustino Asprilla's pull-back for the equaliser, then supplied the pass that released Ginola for the second.

Kevin Keegan's side took their 2-1 lead into half-time. Roy Evans replaced Mark Wright with Steve Harkness at the interval, and Liverpool drew level in the 55th minute when Steve McManaman found Fowler for his second goal.

Newcastle were ahead again almost immediately. Asprilla got through and finished past the advancing David James to make it 3-2. Liverpool's next equaliser came when Collymore met Jason McAteer's cross.

The match was still level when Evans sent on Ian Rush for Rob Jones late in the second half. In stoppage time, John Barnes found Collymore on the left and the striker drove his shot past Pavel Srnicek. Newcastle had scored three away goals and led twice, but Liverpool took all three points.

The result left Manchester United top with 67 points from 32 matches. Newcastle had 64 from 31, while Liverpool were third on 62 from 32. Liverpool had reduced their own deficit to five points; Newcastle remained three points behind United with a game in hand.

The victory therefore kept Liverpool involved in the title race without settling it. For Evans's side, it also followed their 3-0 FA Cup semi-final victory over Aston Villa three days earlier. After securing a place at Wembley, they had beaten the team immediately above them in the league.
