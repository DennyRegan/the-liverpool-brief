Robbie Fowler scored four times against Bolton Wanderers at Anfield on 23 September 1995. Liverpool led 4–0 through his goals before two late replies briefly reduced the margin. Steve Harkness then completed a 5–2 win.

Fowler started alongside Stan Collymore, with Ian Rush on the bench and John Barnes captaining Liverpool. The first goal came after 11 minutes: Jamie Redknapp sent Fowler through and the striker finished low past Keith Branagan.

His second followed a much longer delivery. David James's clearance carried beyond Bolton's defence, and Fowler ran on to score while the visitors appealed for offside. Half an hour had been played, and Liverpool took that two-goal advantage into the interval.

The hat-trick arrived almost immediately after the restart. Redknapp crossed from the right and the ball broke to Fowler, who finished from close range. Branagan subsequently saved an angled shot and a scissor-kick from him, but could not prevent his fourth. In the 67th minute, Fowler controlled Steve McManaman's delivery and curled his shot inside the post.

Bolton still found two goals. Substitute Andy Todd headed in after 77 minutes, and Mark Patterson converted a penalty four minutes later. A four-goal lead had become two, but Harkness scored Liverpool's fifth in the 83rd minute to complete the scoring.

The result left Liverpool with five wins from their opening seven league matches. Fowler had supplied four of their five goals in front of 40,104 spectators, scoring twice before half-time and twice after it. Bolton's late response changed the scoreline without overturning the advantage he had established.
