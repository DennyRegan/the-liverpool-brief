Thiago Alcântara played 98 games for Liverpool and scored three goals. His four seasons there produced 30, 39, 28 and one appearance, according to LFChistory. The best of them was 2021–22. He made 39 appearances, scored a penalty in the FA Cup final shoot-out and was named in the PFA Premier League Team of the Year. He also won the 2022 Community Shield. Injuries took most of his last two seasons.

## Barcelona and Bayern

Thiago was born in San Pietro Vernotico, Italy, on 11 April 1991. He came through the Barcelona academy and played for the first team from 2010–11. He won 46 caps for Spain and scored two goals.

He joined Bayern Munich in 2013 and won the Bundesliga in each of his seven seasons there. His last season in Germany, 2019–20, ended with the treble: the Bundesliga, the DFB-Pokal and the Champions League.

## A record debut

Liverpool announced his signing on 18 September 2020. He was 29, and Liverpool had just won the 2019–20 league title.

Two days later, on 20 September, he made his debut at Stamford Bridge. He came on at half-time for the injured Jordan Henderson. Chelsea had been reduced to ten men just before the break, when Andreas Christensen was sent off, and Liverpool won 2–0. Thiago completed 75 passes. The club's analysis at the time said that was the most by a Premier League player who played no more than 45 minutes since Opta's passing data began in 2003–04.

## Injury in the derby

His first season was soon interrupted. On 17 October 2020, in the Merseyside derby at Everton, a challenge from Richarlison injured his knee. Richarlison was sent off. Virgil van Dijk was also injured in the same game, and other centre-backs followed him onto the injury list.

Thiago did not play again until 30 December, at Newcastle. He came back to a team that kept changing around him as the defensive injuries piled up.

Liverpool finished third after going unbeaten in their final ten league matches. Thiago's first goal for the club came during that run. Against Southampton at Anfield on 8 May 2021, Sadio Mané scored in the 31st minute. In the 90th, Thiago side-footed in from the edge of the area to make it 2–0.

## The Porto goal

His best-known goal came the next season. Porto visited Anfield in the Champions League group stage on 24 November 2021. In the 52nd minute a Liverpool set piece was only half cleared, and Thiago met the ball with a first-time half-volley from about 20 yards. It went in low, into the bottom corner. Mohamed Salah added a second, and Liverpool won 2–0.

Afterwards Thiago told the club that it was always special to score in that stadium. "The games are special against big teams," he said, "and the small details make the difference."

On 3 June 2022 UEFA announced that supporters had voted it the best goal of the 2021–22 Champions League. UEFA's official Goal of the Season, chosen by its Technical Observer panel, was Karim Benzema's header at Chelsea. The panel ranked Thiago's goal tenth.

## 2021–22

That season he played in midfield alongside Fabinho. On 19 April 2022 Liverpool beat Manchester United 4–0 at Anfield. Luis Díaz scored after five minutes, Salah scored twice and Mané once. Supporters voted Thiago player of the match in the club's poll, though he had neither scored nor set up a goal.

He was named in the PFA Premier League Team of the Year, one of six Liverpool players in it.

There were setbacks too. He was named to start the League Cup final against Chelsea on 27 February 2022, but was injured in the warm-up and replaced by Naby Keïta. Liverpool won on penalties without him.

When the teams met again in the FA Cup final on 14 May, Thiago started. The game finished 0–0, and he scored Liverpool's second penalty in a 6–5 shoot-out win.

Liverpool finished the league with 92 points, one behind Manchester City's 93. Thiago also started the Champions League final against Real Madrid on 28 May, which Liverpool lost 1–0. He made 39 appearances in all competitions that season and scored two goals.

## The last two seasons

He played 28 times in 2022–23 and did not score. Hamstring and hip problems kept interrupting that season, and in May 2023 he had a hip operation that ruled him out of the remaining games.

He did not play again until 4 February 2024, when he came on late in a 3–1 defeat at Arsenal. It was his first game in ten months. He was injured again in that match and did not play for Liverpool again, so it was his only appearance of 2023–24.

His contract ran out that summer. His Liverpool record closed at 98 games and three goals.

## Retirement

Thiago announced his retirement on 8 July 2024, aged 33. On 14 September he went back to Anfield to watch Liverpool play Nottingham Forest from the stands. He told the club that the decision to retire had been forced by physical issues, and he described his time at Liverpool as one of the best times of his life.

By then he had already tried coaching. In July 2024 he joined Hansi Flick's staff at Barcelona, but he left on 16 August, after about a month. ESPN later reported that bureaucratic issues had forced him back to Liverpool, where he held his residency.

He rejoined Flick's staff in September 2025. In May 2026 Flick confirmed that Thiago would leave at the end of the season. He said the staff would miss him but that he understood Thiago had his own plans. Fox Sports reported that Thiago wanted more time for his family and his business interests, which include co-owning CE L'Hospitalet with Jordi Alba. As of November 2026 he has no confirmed club role.
