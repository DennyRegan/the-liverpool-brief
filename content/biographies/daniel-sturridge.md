Daniel Sturridge scored 67 goals in 160 appearances for Liverpool over six and a half years. Fifty of them came in the Premier League, in 116 league games, according to LFChistory. He was the seventh Liverpool player to reach 50 Premier League goals. In 2013–14 he scored 21 league goals alongside Luis Suárez as Liverpool finished second. Injuries kept interrupting him after that, but he still scored in the last 16, the semi-final and the final of the 2016 Europa League, and he left in 2019 with a Champions League winner's medal.

## Signing and a fast start

Liverpool signed Sturridge from Chelsea on 2 January 2013. LFChistory gives the fee as £12m. Brendan Rodgers was the manager.

He made his debut on 6 January in the FA Cup third round at Mansfield Town and scored after seven minutes. Liverpool won 2–1.

## The title challenge

In 2013–14 Sturridge and Suárez led Liverpool's attack. Suárez scored 31 league goals and Sturridge 21, the second-highest total in the squad. Liverpool finished runners-up.

After that season injuries kept interrupting his career. Jürgen Klopp replaced Rodgers as manager in October 2015.

## The run to Basel

On 2 December 2015 Liverpool played Southampton in the League Cup quarter-final. It was Sturridge's first start since 4 October. Sadio Mané put Southampton ahead after about 40 seconds. Sturridge cut inside and equalised in the 26th minute from Joe Allen's pass, then scored again within four minutes from Emre Can's pass. Liverpool won 6–1.

The Europa League gave him his best run under Klopp. In the last-16 first leg against Manchester United at Anfield on 10 March 2016, he scored a 20th-minute penalty. Roberto Firmino added the second, and Liverpool won 2–0.

In the semi-final Villarreal won the first leg 1–0. In the second leg at Anfield on 5 May, Bruno's own goal levelled the tie after seven minutes. Sturridge scored in the 63rd minute to put Liverpool ahead in the tie for the first time, and Adam Lallana made it 3–0.

The final against Sevilla was played at St Jakob-Park in Basel on 18 May 2016. In the 35th minute Philippe Coutinho passed to Sturridge, who bent the ball into the corner with the outside of his left foot. Liverpool led at half-time. Kevin Gameiro equalised 17 seconds after the restart, and Coke scored twice. Sevilla won 3–1.

Sturridge later told the club that the goal came from practice. "In training, I try to use the outside of my foot quite a lot," he said. He named Didi Hamann, Deco and Paul Merson as players he had learned from. He also said: "I speak to the goalkeepers a lot to try to find out things they don't like."

## A smaller role

As Klopp rebuilt the attack, Sturridge started less often. He still had his moments.

At West Ham on 14 May 2017 he ran onto Coutinho's through-ball after about 35 minutes, rounded the goalkeeper Adrián and scored. Liverpool won 4–0 and moved up to third as they chased a top-four finish.

On 1 November 2017 he came off the bench against Maribor and scored the third goal in a 3–0 win. It was his first Champions League goal for Liverpool. On 29 January 2018 he joined West Bromwich Albion on loan for the rest of the season, so he missed the knockout run to the Champions League final in Kyiv.

## His last goals for the club

He came back for 2018–19. On 18 September 2018, against Paris Saint-Germain at Anfield, he headed in Andy Robertson's cross to open the scoring. James Milner scored a penalty and Firmino won it 3–2 in stoppage time. "I thought he was different class for 70 minutes, he was brilliant," Robertson told the club afterwards.

Eleven days later Liverpool were losing 1–0 at Chelsea. Sturridge replaced Milner with about four minutes left. In the 89th minute he curled a left-footed shot from well outside the penalty area past Kepa Arrizabalaga into the far top corner. The game finished 1–1. It was his 50th Premier League goal for Liverpool and his last goal for the club.

Klopp spoke about his fitness after the game. "He is a fantastic footballer and had a full pre-season," he said. "He is the best shape since I have known him."

## Madrid and leaving

Liverpool reached the 2019 Champions League final and beat Tottenham Hotspur 2–0 in Madrid. Sturridge was an unused substitute; the substitutes used were Divock Origi, Milner and Joe Gomez. He still received a winner's medal for the campaign, in which he had scored against PSG.

On 4 June 2019 Liverpool announced that he would leave when his contract ended. He left on 30 June 2019, with 67 goals in 160 games in all competitions.

## After Liverpool

He joined Trabzonspor in Turkey in August 2019. A month earlier, an independent regulatory commission had found two charges against him proven under the FA's betting rules. It banned him for six weeks, with four suspended, and fined him £75,000. The FA appealed. On 2 March 2020 an appeal board found two further charges proven. It increased his ban to four months, until the end of 17 June 2020, and doubled the fine to £150,000. FIFA gave the ban worldwide effect. Trabzonspor ended his contract by mutual agreement the same day.

He later joined Perth Glory in Australia, where he made six A-League appearances without scoring. His contract there ended in June 2022, and he has not played for a club since. He joined Sky Sports as a pundit for the 2023–24 season.
