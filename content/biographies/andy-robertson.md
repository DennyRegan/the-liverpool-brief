Andy Robertson made 378 appearances for Liverpool in nine seasons and scored 14 goals. He won eight major trophies: the Premier League in 2019–20 and 2024–25, the Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup in 2019, the FA Cup in 2022 and the League Cup in 2022 and 2024. In three Premier League seasons he reached double figures for assists. He arrived from a relegated club for an initial £8m and became Jürgen Klopp's first-choice left-back in his first season.

## The longer route

Robertson was born in Glasgow on 11 March 1994. Celtic released him at 15. He joined the Queen's Park youth system in 2009 and made his senior debut on 28 July 2012, in a Challenge Cup game at Berwick, according to the Scottish FA. Queen's Park were an amateur club, and he worked at Hampden Park and in a supermarket while he played.

He moved to Dundee United on 3 June 2013. Within a year he had played in the 2014 Scottish Cup final, which United lost 2–0 to St Johnstone, won the PFA Scotland Young Player of the Year award and made his Scotland debut against Poland in Warsaw on 5 March 2014.

Hull City signed him in July 2014. In three seasons there he was relegated in 2014–15, promoted in 2015–16 and relegated again in 2016–17.

## Signing and debut

Liverpool signed him on 21 July 2017. ESPN reported the fee as £8m, rising to £10m with add-ons. He was 23.

His debut was a 1–0 home win over Crystal Palace on 19 August 2017. A regular place did not follow straight away. Alberto Moreno started the season as first choice, but he injured his ankle in December, and Robertson took over, as LFChistory records.

## The chase against City

On 14 January 2018 Liverpool beat Manchester City 4–3 at Anfield. During the game Robertson pressed City's players from the halfway line on the left flank to the right side of their penalty area. The run ended with a foul on Nicolás Otamendi, and the Kop sang his name. Liverpool's own retrospective describes that moment as the catalyst for supporters' affection for him.

Liverpool's goalkeeper, Loris Karius, said afterwards: "That moment just showed how hard he is working and how well he fits into our side."

By May Robertson was starting at left-back in the Champions League final against Real Madrid in Kyiv. Liverpool lost 3–1, but the position was now his.

## Crossing from the left

Robertson's running came with a good delivery. With Sadio Mané ahead of him, Liverpool had two ways of attacking down the left. In a 2020 interview with the club, Mané praised Robertson's hunger, his wish to keep learning and improving, and his ability to keep going forward while still defending properly.

Trent Alexander-Arnold did the same job on the right. In the 2018–19 Premier League, Robertson made 11 assists and Alexander-Arnold 12, according to the Premier League. The following season the figures were 12 and 13. In 2021–22 they were 10 and 12.

## Champions of Europe

Robertson played the first half of the Champions League semi-final second leg against Barcelona at Anfield on 7 May 2019. Liverpool were 3–0 down from the first leg. He was injured, and Georginio Wijnaldum replaced him at half-time. UEFA's report notes that Wijnaldum then scored twice in three minutes. Liverpool won 4–0.

Robertson was fit for the final against Tottenham at the Metropolitano in Madrid on 1 June 2019. He played the whole match, and Liverpool won 2–0. A year after losing in Kyiv, he was a European champion.

## The title

Liverpool won the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup in 2019, before the league title arrived the following summer.

At Aston Villa on 2 November 2019 they were 1–0 down after a goal from Trézéguet. In the 87th minute Robertson met Mané's cross with a header at the back post. Mané scored the winner in the fourth minute of stoppage time, and Liverpool stayed six points clear of City.

Robertson played in 36 of the 38 league games in 2019–20 as Liverpool won the league for the first time in 30 years.

## Cups in 2022

In 2021–22 he made 47 appearances. Liverpool won the FA Cup and the League Cup. On 24 April 2022 he headed the first goal against Everton at Anfield, from a Mohamed Salah cross, in the 62nd minute. Divock Origi added a second, and Liverpool won 2–0. They reached another Champions League final but lost 1–0 to Real Madrid in Paris on 28 May.

## Injury and the League Cup

In October 2023 he hurt his shoulder playing for Scotland against Spain. Klopp confirmed later that month that Robertson had had surgery. He was out until January 2024. He then played in the League Cup final on 25 February 2024, when Liverpool beat Chelsea 1–0 after extra time.

## Scotland

Alex McLeish made him Scotland captain on 3 September 2018. He captained his country at the European Championships of 2020 and 2024, and at the 2026 World Cup.

## After Klopp

Robertson stayed when Klopp left in 2024. Under Arne Slot he won a second Premier League title in 2024–25.

On 9 April 2026 Liverpool announced that he would leave at the end of the season. He had made 373 appearances at that point. His last game for the club was on 24 May 2026, and his contract ended on 30 June. His final total was 378 appearances and 14 goals.

Tottenham announced his signing on a free transfer on 5 June 2026, and he joined them on 1 July, after Scotland had gone out of the World Cup. He is now a Tottenham player.
