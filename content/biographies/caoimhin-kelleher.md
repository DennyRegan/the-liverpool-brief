Caoimhín Kelleher made 67 senior appearances for Liverpool and kept 24 clean sheets. For most of his time at the club he was the second-choice goalkeeper behind Alisson Becker. He still played in two League Cup finals against Chelsea, in 2022 and 2024, and Liverpool won both. He won four penalty shoot-outs, more than any Liverpool goalkeeper before him, according to the club, and his six shoot-out saves are also a club record. He left for Brentford in June 2025 for a fee reported at £12.5m, rising to £18m.

## From Cork to the Academy

Kelleher grew up in Cork and played as a striker until he was about 14. He told The Athletic, in an interview carried by the club, that he had already begun goalkeeper training when his team's keeper left, which moved him into goal sooner than he expected. He joined Liverpool's Academy from Ringmahon Rangers in the summer of 2015.

## A debut at MK Dons

His senior debut came on 25 September 2019, in the League Cup at MK Dons. Liverpool won 2–0, with goals from James Milner and Ki-Jana Hoever. Kelleher kept a clean sheet and made two saves from Joe Bowery in the second half.

Afterwards Jürgen Klopp called him "a very good footballer, very good with his feet." He added: "He's just a very calm person, which helps as well as a goalie."

## Champions League debut

The next big chance came against Ajax at Anfield on 1 December 2020. Alisson was out with a hamstring injury and the game was played behind closed doors. Kelleher made his Champions League debut. He made a flying save from Noussair Mazraoui's shot from 25 yards and, late in the game, a point-blank save from Klaas-Jan Huntelaar. Curtis Jones scored the only goal. The 1–0 win put Liverpool into the last 16 as winners of Group D.

Klopp was asked afterwards whether Kelleher had done enough to be considered the No.2. He agreed that he had, but said that in life you still have to prove things. He also pointed out the problem every reserve goalkeeper faces: "there's only one goalie on the pitch and if this goalie plays for 10 years in a row, it's pretty difficult."

## Wembley, 2022

Klopp picked Kelleher for the final against Chelsea at Wembley on 27 February 2022, ahead of Alisson. The match finished 0–0 after extra time. Kelleher made saves from Christian Pulisic, Mason Mount and Romelu Lukaku, among others, as both sides went close.

Chelsea brought on Kepa Arrizabalaga for Édouard Mendy in the 120th minute, for the shoot-out. Kelleher did not save a penalty. Every kick was scored until the 11th round. After ten Liverpool players had scored, Kelleher stepped up and put his penalty into the top corner. Kepa then sent his kick over the bar, and Liverpool won 11–10.

Afterwards Klopp called Kelleher "the best No.2 in the world". He said: "The game he played tonight was absolutely incredible." He also said that, as a reserve, "you just have to be ready when you are called."

## Three saves against Derby

That November Kelleher won another shoot-out, this time by stopping penalties. Liverpool played Derby County at Anfield on 9 November 2022 in the League Cup third round. The game finished 0–0. Kelleher saved from Conor Hourihane, Craig Forsyth and Lewis Dobbin, and Liverpool won the shoot-out 3–2.

Those three saves took his total in shoot-outs for the club to six, passing Pepe Reina's five. It was also his fourth shoot-out win for Liverpool, after Arsenal and Leicester in the League Cup and Chelsea in the 2022 final.

## Wembley, 2024

Klopp's last season as manager gave Kelleher more games than any other. Alisson was injured for part of 2023–24, and Kelleher made 26 appearances: ten in the Premier League, two in the FA Cup, six in the League Cup and eight in the Europa League.

Chelsea were the opponents in the League Cup final again, on 25 February 2024. Liverpool were without Mohamed Salah and Darwin Núñez, and Ryan Gravenberch went off injured in the first half. By the end, teenagers Jayden Danns, Bobby Clark and James McConnell were on the pitch.

Kelleher made an early point-blank save from Cole Palmer and came off his line to stop Conor Gallagher. There was no shoot-out this time. In the 118th minute Virgil van Dijk headed in from a corner, and Liverpool won 1–0.

The official player of the match award went to Van Dijk. Liverpool supporters voted for Kelleher as the club's player of the match.

## The title season and Brentford

Kelleher stayed for Arne Slot's first season in 2024–25. He made 20 appearances, ten of them in the Premier League, as Liverpool won the league title.

On 3 June 2025 he joined Brentford on a five-year contract, with a club option for a further year. The fee was officially undisclosed. It was reported as £12.5m, rising to £18m with add-ons. He had spent ten years at Liverpool, from the Academy to the first team.

He never took Alisson's place for good. He played most of his games in the cups and in spells when Alisson was injured, but he was in goal for both League Cup final wins.

## After Liverpool

In his first season at Brentford, 2025–26, he made 38 appearances: 37 in the Premier League and one in the FA Cup. He is now in his second season there, in Keith Andrews' squad.
