Darwin Núñez scored 40 goals in 143 games for Liverpool after joining from Benfica in 2022. In three seasons he won the Premier League, the League Cup and the Community Shield. The fee could rise to a club record. A number of his goals came in the last minutes of matches, often after he had come on as a substitute, and they won Liverpool points and a cup tie.

## Before Liverpool

Núñez was born in Artigas, Uruguay, on 24 June 1999. He made his senior debut for Peñarol in November 2017, spent the 2019–20 season with Almería in Spain and joined Benfica in 2020.

His second season in Portugal made his name. In 2021–22 he scored 34 goals in 41 games, 26 of them in the league, and finished as the Portuguese league's top scorer, winning the Bola de Prata. Liverpool saw him at close range that spring, in the Champions League. He scored in both legs of the quarter-final against them: in the 49th minute of Benfica's 3–1 home defeat, and in the 82nd minute of the 3–3 draw at Anfield.

Liverpool announced his signing on 14 June 2022. He was 22, ten days short of his 23rd birthday. The initial fee was £64m, rising to about £85m with add-ons, according to LFChistory. With the add-ons, it was a club record.

## A mixed start

His competitive debut was the Community Shield against Manchester City at the King Power Stadium on 30 July 2022. He came off the bench around the hour mark. His header struck Rúben Dias's arm, and Mohamed Salah scored the penalty in the 83rd minute. In stoppage time Núñez scored with a header of his own. Liverpool won 3–1.

His home debut, against Crystal Palace on 15 August, went badly. Wilfried Zaha put Palace ahead in the 32nd minute, and Núñez was later sent off for headbutting Joachim Andersen. Luis Díaz equalised after the red card and the game finished 1–1. Núñez was banned for three matches.

He scored 15 goals in 42 games in all competitions that season.

## Newcastle

His best-known afternoon for the club came away to Newcastle United on 27 August 2023. Anthony Gordon put Newcastle ahead in the 25th minute. Three minutes later Virgil van Dijk was sent off for denying Alexander Isak a goalscoring chance. Jürgen Klopp brought Núñez on in the 77th minute, with Liverpool a goal and a man down.

Four minutes later Diogo Jota played a pass that Sven Botman failed to block. Núñez drove it low past Nick Pope to equalise. In the third minute of stoppage time Salah sent him through, and he scored again. Liverpool won 2–1.

## Missed chances and late winners

He also missed chances, sometimes several in one game. Against Chelsea on 31 January 2024 he hit the woodwork four times, including a penalty in first-half stoppage time that struck the post. According to Opta, no player had hit the woodwork four times in one Premier League game since its records began in 2003–04. He still played a part in the result: his cross set up Díaz's volley in a 4–1 win.

His late goals continued. In the League Cup fourth round at Bournemouth on 1 November 2023, he came on for Curtis Jones around the hour. About ten minutes later he struck a dipping shot from the left edge of the box into the top corner, and Liverpool won 2–1. They went on to win the competition that season, though Núñez missed the final through injury.

His first game back from that injury was at Nottingham Forest on 2 March 2024. He came off the bench around the hour again, and in the 99th minute he headed in Alexis Mac Allister's cross. It was the only goal of the game.

He scored 18 goals in 54 games in 2023–24, Klopp's last season.

## Champions under Slot

Under Arne Slot in 2024–25 he scored seven goals in 47 games. One afternoon stood out. At Brentford on 18 January 2025 he came on for Díaz in the 65th minute. The game was goalless until the first minute of stoppage time, when Núñez scored. Two minutes later he scored again. Liverpool won 2–0, and at the end of the season they were champions.

## After Liverpool

He joined Al-Hilal in Saudi Arabia on 9 August 2025, after 143 appearances across three seasons. The fee was £46.3m, rising to £56.6m with add-ons, according to LFChistory.

He did not finish that season in their league side. Al-Hilal signed Karim Benzema in February 2026. Because of the Saudi Pro League's limit on foreign players, they left Núñez out of their league squad for the rest of the season.

In August 2026 he joined Al-Diriyah, newly promoted to the Saudi Pro League, on a season-long loan to June 2027.

At Liverpool he scored 15, 18 and seven goals in his three seasons, 40 in all, and won three trophies. Several of the goals came in the final minutes of games Liverpool had been drawing or losing.
