Luis Díaz made 148 appearances for Liverpool and scored 41 goals in three and a half seasons. He won four major trophies: the League Cup in 2022 and 2024, the FA Cup in 2022 and the Premier League in 2024–25. He arrived in January 2022, and within three months Jürgen Klopp was turning to him when a Champions League semi-final was slipping away.

## Villarreal

On 3 May 2022 Liverpool went to Villarreal 2–0 up from the first leg at Anfield. By half-time the lead had gone. Boulaye Dia scored in the third minute and Francis Coquelin in the 41st, and the tie was level at 2–2.

Klopp took off Diogo Jota at half-time and sent on Díaz. In the 62nd minute Fabinho's shot went through Gerónimo Rulli's legs to restore Liverpool's aggregate lead. Five minutes later Díaz headed Trent Alexander-Arnold's cross down and, again, through Rulli's legs. Sadio Mané scored the third in the 74th minute. Liverpool won 3–2 on the night and 5–2 on aggregate.

UEFA named Díaz player of the match. Its match observer said he "made a major impact that changed the game, scoring and proving to be a constant threat".

## Colombia and Porto

Díaz was born in Barrancas, Colombia, on 13 January 1997. He first came to wider attention at the 2015 Copa América of Indigenous Peoples in Chile, where Colombia finished runners-up and he scored twice, according to Liverpool's own account of his early career.

He made his senior debut for Barranquilla in April 2016 and joined Junior in 2017. Porto signed him in the summer of 2019.

By January 2022 he had scored 14 goals in 18 league appearances for Porto that season. He had also played against Liverpool twice in the Champions League group stage. Liverpool announced his signing on 30 January 2022, a little over a fortnight after his 25th birthday. The initial fee was £37.5m, with up to £12.5m more in add-ons.

## The first weeks

His debut came against Cardiff City in the FA Cup on 6 February. He came on in the 57th minute, and the club's match report credited his persistence near the byline with setting up Takumi Minamino for the second goal in a 3–1 win.

Thirteen days later, on 19 February, he scored his first Liverpool goal. Norwich City had gone ahead at Anfield through Milot Rashica in the 48th minute. Mané and Mohamed Salah scored, and then Jordan Henderson found Díaz running behind the defence. He clipped the ball over Angus Gunn to make it 3–1.

## Three finals

Díaz started all three of Liverpool's cup finals that season. The first was the League Cup final against Chelsea on 27 February. It finished 0–0, and Liverpool won 11–10 on penalties. Díaz had been substituted for Divock Origi in the 97th minute.

Liverpool met Chelsea again in the FA Cup final on 14 May. It was 0–0 again, and Liverpool won 6–5 on penalties. Díaz hit the outside of a post, and the FA named him man of the match.

The season ended with second place in the league and defeat by Real Madrid in the Champions League final.

## Six months out

In October 2022 he injured his knee. He suffered a setback in December and needed surgery. He returned on 17 April 2023, six months later, coming on in the 81st minute of a 6–1 win over Leeds United.

## October and November 2023

On 28 October 2023 his parents were seized in Barrancas. Police rescued his mother, Cilenis Marulanda, within hours. His father remained in captivity.

On 5 November Díaz came off the bench at Luton Town. Liverpool were 1–0 down in the 95th minute when he headed in Harvey Elliott's cross. Under his shirt he wore a message, "Libertad para papá" (freedom for Dad).

His father was released four days later, on 9 November.

## Klopp's last season

In 2023–24, Klopp's final season, Díaz scored 13 goals in all competitions, eight of them in the league. He played every minute of the League Cup final against Chelsea on 25 February 2024, which Liverpool won 1–0 after extra time.

## Champions under Slot

Arne Slot replaced Klopp in the summer of 2024. In 2024–25 Díaz scored 13 goals and set up five more in 36 league games as Liverpool won the Premier League. His 17 goals in all competitions were his best total for the club in a season.

He joined Bayern Munich on 30 July 2025, on a contract to June 2029. He left Liverpool with 41 goals from 148 games.

## After Liverpool

In his first season in Germany Díaz scored 26 goals in 51 games. Bayern won the Bundesliga and the Franz Beckenbauer Supercup, in which he scored, and beat Stuttgart 3–0 in the DFB-Pokal final. They went out of the Champions League in the semi-finals.

At the 2026 World Cup he scored and set up a goal in his first game, a 3–1 win over Uzbekistan on 17 June. Colombia went out in the round of 16 on 8 July in Vancouver, losing 4–3 on penalties to Switzerland after a 0–0 draw. Díaz scored his penalty.

That summer Al-Hilal were reported to be preparing a €70m bid. Bayern's chief executive, Jan-Christian Dreesen, told Kicker: "We would be stupid if we sold Diaz, wouldn't we?" Díaz stayed, and he is still at Bayern.
