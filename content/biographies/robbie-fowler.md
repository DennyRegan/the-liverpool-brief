Robbie Fowler scored 183 goals in 369 competitive games for Liverpool, 128 of them in the league, according to LFChistory. Between 1994 and 1997 he scored 31, 36 and 31 in all competitions, 98 goals in three seasons. He won the League Cup twice, the FA Cup and the UEFA Cup, and came back to the club for a second spell in 2006. Supporters called him "God".

## Toxteth to Fulham

Fowler was born in Toxteth on 9 April 1975 and grew up supporting Everton. He came through Liverpool's youth system and signed professional terms in April 1992, aged 17.

Graeme Souness gave him his senior debut at Fulham on 22 September 1993, in the League Cup. Fowler scored in a 3–1 win. When the teams met in the second leg at Anfield on 5 October, he scored all five goals in a 5–0 win. He was 18 and playing only his fourth senior match, the club's records show.

## Four minutes 33 seconds

On 28 August 1994 Fowler scored a hat-trick against Arsenal at Anfield in four minutes 33 seconds. It was a Premier League record until Sadio Mané scored three for Southampton against Aston Villa in May 2015. Fowler scored another hat-trick against Arsenal on 23 December 1995, in a 3–1 win.

He was named PFA Young Player of the Year in 1995 and again in 1996. His first senior trophy came in the 1995 League Cup final, when Steve McManaman scored both goals in a 2–1 win over Bolton Wanderers.

## Roy Evans's side

Stan Collymore arrived from Nottingham Forest in July 1995. In 1995–96 the pair scored 42 league goals between them, Fowler 28 and Collymore 14.

Two 4–3 wins over Newcastle United came in that period. On 3 April 1996 Fowler scored twice and Collymore got the winner in stoppage time. On 10 March 1997 Liverpool led 3–0, Newcastle came back level, and Fowler headed the winner from Stig Inge Bjørnebye's cross in the 90th minute. It was his second goal of the night.

Liverpool finished third in 1995–96 and lost the FA Cup final 1–0 to Manchester United. They finished fourth the following season. Fowler won his first England cap against Bulgaria on 27 March 1996.

## The dockers and the bans

In March 1997 Liverpool beat Brann 3–0 at Anfield in the Cup Winners' Cup quarter-final. After scoring, Fowler showed a T-shirt supporting the 500 sacked Liverpool dockers, and UEFA fined him.

Later that month, at Highbury on 24 March, he told the referee that David Seaman had not fouled him. The penalty stood, Seaman saved Fowler's kick and Jason McAteer scored from the rebound in a 2–1 win. UEFA later recognised Fowler for fair play.

In April 1999 the FA banned him for six matches and fined him a record £32,000: two matches for taunting Chelsea's Graeme Le Saux about his sexuality, and four for a goal celebration against Everton that mimicked taking cocaine.

## Injuries

On 23 February 1998, in a 1–1 draw with Everton, Fowler collided with the Everton goalkeeper Thomas Myhre and damaged the posterior cruciate ligament in his knee. After he came back he had a serious ankle problem that needed two operations, according to LFChistory. He made only 14 appearances in 1999–2000 and scored three goals.

## The treble season

By 2000–01 Gérard Houllier was manager, and Michael Owen and Emile Heskey were competing with Fowler for places in attack. Fowler still scored 17 goals in 48 games that season.

In the League Cup final against Birmingham City in Cardiff on 25 February 2001, he scored from 25 yards after Heskey's flick-on. The game finished 1–1 and Liverpool won 5–4 on penalties. Fowler wore the armband and lifted the trophy.

In the FA Cup he scored a penalty in the 4–2 quarter-final win at Tranmere Rovers and a free kick in the 2–1 semi-final win over Wycombe Wanderers. He came on as a substitute in the final, a 2–1 win over Arsenal on 12 May.

Four days later Liverpool played Alavés in the UEFA Cup final in Dortmund. Fowler came on for Heskey and made it 4–3 late in normal time. Alavés equalised, and Liverpool won 5–4 with a golden goal, an own goal by Geli. Fowler later told the club: "I was devastated not to start in the FA Cup final and was disappointed again not to start in the UEFA Cup final."

The season ended at Charlton Athletic on 19 May. Fowler scored twice in a 4–0 win that secured third place and a place in Champions League qualifying.

## Leaving for Leeds

On 27 November 2001 Phil Thompson, in charge while Houllier was absent, said Liverpool wanted Fowler to stay. "We want him to stay at the club and further talks are imminent," he said. That evening Liverpool confirmed they had agreed a fee with Leeds United. Fowler joined Leeds for a reported £11m, with 171 Liverpool goals behind him.

Weeks after the move, Fowler told The Times he had become Liverpool's third-choice striker and did not want to miss a second World Cup. He also said he had been waiting for a new contract offer that never came.

He moved on to Manchester City in January 2003.

## The return

On 27 January 2006 Rafael Benítez brought Fowler back to Liverpool on a free transfer. He was 30. He scored five goals before the end of 2005–06 and seven more in 2006–07. His last competitive game for Liverpool was against Charlton at Anfield on 13 May 2007. His second spell brought 39 appearances and 12 goals.

He later played for Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers, North Queensland Fury, Perth Glory and Muangthong United. He won 26 England caps and scored seven goals, 22 caps and five goals while at Liverpool.
