Dirk Kuyt made 285 appearances for Liverpool and scored 71 goals between 2006 and 2012. He arrived as a centre-forward and became a right-sided attacker, playing for Rafael Benítez, Roy Hodgson and Kenny Dalglish. He scored in a Champions League final, took the winning kick in a Champions League semi-final shoot-out, scored twice from the spot in a derby at Goodison and got a hat-trick against Manchester United. He won one major honour with the club, the 2012 League Cup, and scored in the final.

## Katwijk to Rotterdam

Kuyt was born in Katwijk, in the Netherlands, on 22 July 1980. He began at the local club, Quick Boys, before playing senior football for Utrecht. At Utrecht he won the Dutch Cup in 2003, beating Feyenoord in the final. He joined Feyenoord that year and became a prolific scorer and their captain.

## Signing and first season

Liverpool signed him from Feyenoord on 18 August 2006 for £9 million, according to LFChistory. Benítez already had Peter Crouch, Craig Bellamy and Robbie Fowler, so Kuyt had to compete for a place.

He made his debut eight days later, coming on for Crouch at half-time in a 2–1 home win over West Ham United. He later said the applause he received that day gave him a big boost. His first goal came on 20 September against Newcastle United, from Steve Finnan's delivery, in a 2–0 win. He finished the season with 14 goals.

## Chelsea and Athens

Liverpool met Chelsea in the Champions League semi-finals. At Anfield on 1 May 2007 Daniel Agger cancelled out the first-leg deficit and the tie went to extra time. Kuyt headed against the crossbar and had a follow-up ruled out for offside.

In the shoot-out Pepe Reina saved from Arjen Robben and Geremi. Kuyt took Liverpool's fourth penalty and beat Petr Čech to win it 4–1. "Actually I had no nerves at all," he told UEFA. "I just had to take my responsibility."

In the final against AC Milan in Athens on 23 May, Benítez played him as the lone forward, with Steven Gerrard behind him and Jermaine Pennant and Boudewijn Zenden wide. Filippo Inzaghi scored just before half-time, when Andrea Pirlo's free-kick struck him, and again late in the second half. Kuyt headed in from Agger's flick-on from a corner in the 89th minute, but Liverpool lost 2–1.

## Moving to the right

Fernando Torres arrived in 2007, and Gerrard moved behind him. Benítez began to play Kuyt on the right. In his club column years later Kuyt said he had always wanted to play centrally, but that making a team work was "the best feeling you can get from football". He had played on the right before, at Utrecht.

His father died in June 2007, and Kuyt later said he struggled for form in the months that followed.

On 20 October 2007 Liverpool went behind at Goodison Park to a Sami Hyypiä own goal. Kuyt equalised from the spot after Tony Hibbert fouled Gerrard, and in stoppage time he scored a second penalty after Phil Neville handled on the line. Liverpool won 2–1, and both Hibbert and Neville were sent off. Kuyt later wrote that Andriy Voronin was meant to take the first penalty but told him, "I'm not feeling comfortable. You take it." He also admitted he was lucky to stay on the pitch after a lunge at Neville.

## European goals

He scored only three league goals in 2007–08, but in Europe he kept scoring. Against Internazionale at Anfield on 19 February 2008 his 85th-minute goal opened a 2–0 first-leg win. Gerrard added the second, and Torres scored the only goal in Milan.

In the quarter-final first leg at Arsenal on 2 April, Emmanuel Adebayor headed the home side in front. About three minutes later Gerrard got past Emmanuel Eboué and Kolo Touré and crossed low for Kuyt to equalise. It finished 1–1.

In the semi-final first leg against Chelsea on 22 April he scored at the Kop end just before half-time. Liverpool led until John Arne Riise's own goal deep in added time made it 1–1. Chelsea won the return.

## Standard Liège and 2008–09

The next European campaign nearly ended in the qualifying round. Standard Liège held Liverpool 0–0 in Belgium, and the second leg at Anfield on 27 August 2008 was still goalless after 118 minutes. Kuyt then scored from Ryan Babel's cross. It was his 100th appearance for the club. "His effort is amazing every game," Benítez said afterwards.

In 2008–09 Kuyt played in all 38 league games and scored 12 league goals, 15 in all competitions. At Manchester City on 5 October Liverpool were 2–0 down at half-time. Torres scored twice, and Kuyt got the winner in stoppage time.

Liverpool scored 77 league goals that season and finished second, four points behind Manchester United. It was the closest Kuyt came to a league title in England.

## Derby goals

Liverpool fell away in 2009–10, though Kuyt still made 53 appearances. He scored in both derbies. At Goodison on 29 November 2009 he made it 2–0 in the 80th minute after Tim Howard failed to hold Albert Riera's shot. At Anfield on 6 February 2010 he headed in Gerrard's corner for a 1–0 win after Sotirios Kyrgiakos had been sent off. Liverpool also reached the semi-finals of the Europa League that season, where Atlético Madrid knocked them out.

## The United hat-trick

Benítez left in 2010. Hodgson's spell was short, and Dalglish returned in January 2011. Torres left that month, and Luis Suárez and Andy Carroll arrived.

On 6 March 2011 Kuyt scored all three goals in a 3–1 win over Manchester United at Anfield. He followed Suárez's run to tap in the first, headed in after Nani's misdirected header for the second, and scored the third when Edwin van der Sar failed to hold a free-kick. He finished 2010–11 with 13 league goals, his best league return for Liverpool, and 15 in all.

## Wembley

He was no longer a certain starter in 2011–12. On 28 January 2012 he came off the bench against Manchester United in the FA Cup and scored a late winner in a 2–1 win.

In the League Cup final against Cardiff City on 26 February, Joe Mason put Cardiff ahead and Martin Škrtel equalised. Kuyt replaced Carroll in the 103rd minute. Five minutes later his first shot was blocked and he scored from the rebound. Ben Turner equalised in the 118th minute. In the shoot-out Steven Gerrard and Charlie Adam missed. Kuyt scored Liverpool's third kick, Stewart Downing and Glen Johnson scored, and Anthony Gerrard missed for Cardiff. Liverpool won 3–2 on penalties.

## Leaving

His last appearance was as a substitute at Swansea City on 13 May 2012, a 1–0 defeat. On 3 June the club announced that he had agreed a move to Fenerbahçe. After six years, he said, he wanted a new experience in a new country, and he described the messages from supporters as emotional. He left with 285 appearances and 71 goals.

## The Netherlands and Feyenoord

Kuyt won 104 caps for the Netherlands between 2004 and 2014 and scored 24 goals. He started the 2010 World Cup final.

He went back to Feyenoord in 2015. On 14 May 2017 he scored a hat-trick against Heracles that won Feyenoord their first league title in 18 years, and three days later he announced his retirement.

He moved into coaching with Feyenoord's youth side, then managed ADO Den Haag, Beerschot and FC Dordrecht. In June 2026 he joined the coaching staff at Fenerbahçe. Named interim coach in late October, he took the side against Liverpool in the Champions League in Istanbul on 4 November 2026, and Fenerbahçe won 3–2.
