Phil Thompson played 477 competitive games for Liverpool, 340 of them in the league, and scored 13 goals. The club credits him with seven league titles, an FA Cup, two UEFA Cups and League Cup wins in 1981 and 1982. He started the European Cup final wins of 1978 and 1981, and on 27 May 1981 he captained the side that beat Real Madrid 1–0 in Paris. He also won 42 England caps.

## Kirkby to Old Trafford

Thompson was born in Kensington, Liverpool, on 21 January 1954, and grew up in Kirkby. He told LFChistory that he went to Brookfield school there. He joined Liverpool in April 1970 and turned professional on 22 January 1971, the day after his 17th birthday.

His debut came at Old Trafford on 3 April 1972. He came on as a substitute for John Toshack, and Liverpool beat Manchester United 3–0. Bill Shankly first used him in midfield. In 1972–73 he made 14 First Division appearances as Liverpool won the league and the UEFA Cup.

## Hughes, Wembley and a missed final

In February 1974, with Larry Lloyd injured, Shankly moved him into central defence alongside the captain, Emlyn Hughes. On 4 May 1974 he started the FA Cup final against Newcastle United at Wembley. Kevin Keegan scored twice and Steve Heighway once, and Liverpool won 3–0. LFChistory credits Thompson, then 20, with containing Newcastle's forwards Malcolm Macdonald and John Tudor.

He became Hughes's regular partner. In 1975–76 he played 41 of the 42 league games and 57 matches in all competitions as Liverpool won the league and the UEFA Cup. He scored in the 1–1 draw with Barcelona at Anfield on 14 April 1976, the second leg of the semi-final, which took Liverpool through 2–1 on aggregate.

His 1976–77 season ended early. His last game was a 1–0 home win over Newcastle on 5 March 1977. He then needed a cartilage operation and missed the European Cup final in Rome.

He was back the following season. On 10 May 1978 he started the European Cup final against Club Brugge at Wembley. Kenny Dalglish scored in the 64th minute and Liverpool won 1–0. Alan Hansen had come in for the injured Tommy Smith. LFChistory records that late in the game Thompson cleared the ball off the line after a Hansen error to protect the lead.

## Hansen and the armband

Hansen became his regular partner. In 1978–79 Liverpool won the league and conceded 16 goals in 42 league games. Thompson was given the captaincy in April 1979, and in 1979–80, his first full season as captain, Liverpool won the title again.

In 1981 Liverpool won the League Cup for the first time and reached a third European Cup final under Bob Paisley. On 27 May they met Real Madrid at the Parc des Princes, with Thompson leading the side as captain. Alan Kennedy scored in the 81st minute and Liverpool won 1–0. Thompson was the second Liverpool captain to lift the European Cup, after Hughes. The club records that he took the trophy to The Falcon, his local pub in Kirkby.

## Losing the captaincy

On Boxing Day 1981 Liverpool lost 3–1 at home to Manchester City and were 12th in the league. Paisley then took the captaincy from Thompson and gave it to Graeme Souness. Thompson told LFChistory in 2008 that he was "absolutely devastated".

Souness's first game as captain was the FA Cup third-round tie at Swansea on 2 January 1982, a 4–0 win. Thompson played. Liverpool recovered to win the league in 1981–82 and again in 1982–83, his sixth and seventh title medals. He also won the 1982 League Cup, but missed the 1983 final through injury.

By then Mark Lawrenson had become Hansen's partner. Thompson's last Liverpool game was the Charity Shield against Manchester United at Wembley on 20 August 1983, a 2–0 defeat. He told LFChistory that he was the 17th man for the 1984 European Cup final in Rome and was not involved.

## England and after

Thompson made his England debut against Wales on 24 March 1976, a 2–1 win. He won 42 caps, scored one goal and, by LFChistory's count, captained England six times. At the 1982 World Cup in Spain he played in all five of England's matches. His last cap came against Greece in Thessaloniki on 17 November 1982, a 3–0 win.

He went on loan to Sheffield United in late 1984 and joined them permanently in March 1985. He returned to Liverpool as reserve-team coach in 1986, and from 1998 he was Gérard Houllier's assistant manager.
