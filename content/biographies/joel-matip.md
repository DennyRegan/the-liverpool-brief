Joël Matip made 201 appearances for Liverpool and scored 11 goals. He joined on a free transfer in 2016 and stayed for eight seasons. The club counts seven winner's medals for him, including the Champions League and the Premier League. In the 2019 Champions League final he started beside Virgil van Dijk and set up Divock Origi's goal, the second in a 2–0 win. Injuries took large parts of several seasons from him, but when he was fit he was a regular in Jürgen Klopp's defence.

## Leeds at Anfield

On 23 February 2022 Liverpool beat Leeds United 6–0 at Anfield. Matip scored the second goal, after about half an hour. He carried the ball forward from defence, found Mohamed Salah on the right and kept running. Salah returned it straight away, and Matip finished past Illan Meslier.

He explained it simply afterwards. "I didn't have to stop at all, I just could continue my run and it was the best pass," he told the club.

Those runs from centre-back became a feature of his game. A defender bringing the ball into midfield forces a choice on the opposition: let him keep going, or step out to him and leave someone else free.

## Schalke

Matip was born in Bochum, Germany, on 8 August 1991. He played international football for Cameroon, winning 27 caps and scoring once between 2010 and 2015, according to LFChistory.

He joined Schalke's youth system at the age of nine and came through to the first team. On his Bundesliga debut, on 7 November 2009, he headed the equaliser in a 1–1 draw with Bayern Munich. He also gained experience in midfield, as Liverpool's profile noted when he signed.

## Klopp's signing

Klopp knew him from the other side of the Ruhr rivalry. "Even when he played for the team you cannot love as Dortmund manager, you see his quality," he said. During his break between Dortmund and Liverpool, Klopp said, he had thought: "I should think about Joel, if there was a need."

Liverpool announced a pre-contract agreement on 15 February 2016, and Matip joined on 1 July, when his Schalke contract ended. There was no fee. Klopp described him as young but experienced, physically strong and quick. The club's profile added that he was comfortable and composed with the ball at his feet. Klopp also made clear that one signing would not fix the defence on its own. "We have to defend better as a team, first of all," he said.

Matip made 32 appearances in his first season, 29 of them in the league. Liverpool finished fourth and returned to the Champions League.

## Madrid

A thigh injury against Crystal Palace on 31 March 2018 needed surgery and ended his 2017–18 season. He missed the run-in and the Champions League final in Kyiv.

He was starting again the following season when he fractured his collarbone against Napoli on 11 December 2018. He was out for up to six weeks and came back in early 2019. From then on he played alongside Van Dijk as Liverpool challenged on two fronts. They finished the league with 97 points, one behind Manchester City.

In the Champions League he started five of the six knockout games before the final. He was on the bench for the home leg against Porto. He started the 4–0 win over Barcelona in the semi-final second leg.

On 1 June 2019 he started beside Van Dijk against Tottenham Hotspur in Madrid. Salah scored a penalty in the second minute. In the 87th minute Matip played the ball to Origi, who scored the second. Liverpool kept a clean sheet and won the final.

## Champions, with interruptions

Liverpool went on to win the UEFA Super Cup and, in 2019–20, the league title. He scored in the 2019 Community Shield, which Liverpool lost to Manchester City on penalties. Injuries then restricted him to nine league appearances in the title season, 13 in all competitions.

The next season ended early too. He damaged ankle ligaments against Tottenham on 28 January 2021, went off at half-time and did not play again that campaign.

## His fullest season

In 2021–22 he stayed fit for longer than at any other point at Liverpool. He made 43 appearances, his highest total for the club.

In February 2022 he was named Premier League Player of the Month, the first Cameroonian to win it. Liverpool won all four league games that month, against Leicester City, Burnley, Norwich City and Leeds. Matip played every minute, and they conceded once. Against Leicester he played a pass through a defender's legs for Diogo Jota to score in a 2–0 win. Against Leeds he scored that goal.

Liverpool won both domestic cups that season. Matip started the League Cup final on 27 February and was replaced in the 91st minute. In the FA Cup final on 14 May he came on for Van Dijk in the 91st minute. He was an unused substitute when Liverpool lost the Champions League final to Real Madrid in Paris.

## The last game

His final appearance for Liverpool came on 3 December 2023, a 4–3 home win over Fulham. He went off in the 69th minute. Scans confirmed that he had ruptured an anterior cruciate ligament, and he had surgery.

He did not play for Liverpool again. His contract ended on 30 June 2024. LFChistory also credits him with the 2024 League Cup, which Liverpool won that February while he was injured.

On 12 October 2024, aged 33, he announced his retirement. He left Liverpool with 201 appearances, 150 of them in the league, and 11 goals.
