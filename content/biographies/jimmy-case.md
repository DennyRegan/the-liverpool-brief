Jimmy Case made 269 appearances for Liverpool and scored 46 goals. He joined from South Liverpool for £500 in 1973 and left in 1981 with four league titles, three European Cups, the UEFA Cup, the League Cup and the European Super Cup. He played on the right of midfield through Bob Paisley's first years as manager. In the 1976 UEFA Cup final he came off the bench at half-time with Liverpool 2–0 down to Club Brugge and scored the equaliser.

## South Liverpool and the apprenticeship

Case was born in Allerton on 18 May 1954. He played for South Liverpool before joining Liverpool in May 1973, for a reported fee of £500.

He was training as an electrician at the time. As he later told LFChistory, he turned down a full-time contract at first because he had already done two years of his apprenticeship and wanted to finish it. Liverpool took over his semi-professional contract from South Liverpool instead.

His first-team debut came on 26 April 1975, the last day of the 1974–75 season, in a 3–1 home win over Queens Park Rangers. He was 20. Kevin Keegan, John Toshack and Ian Callaghan all started that day.

## Brugge, 1976

Case became a regular the following season. He made 39 appearances in all competitions and scored 12 goals, five of them in the UEFA Cup. Three came in one game, a 3–0 win over Śląsk Wrocław on 10 December 1975.

On 28 April 1976 Club Brugge led 2–0 at half-time in the first leg of the final at Anfield. Paisley took off Toshack and sent on Case. Ray Kennedy pulled a goal back, Case equalised, and Keegan scored a penalty. Liverpool had scored three times in about five minutes around the hour, and won 3–2.

Liverpool won the league and the UEFA Cup that season.

## How he played

Case described his job in simple terms. When he got the ball, he looked to play in the gifted players, such as Kenny Dalglish or Keegan. If defenders paid a lot of attention to the forwards, he said, there should be more scope for everyone else.

## Wembley and Rome, 1977

Liverpool had already won the league when they met Manchester United in the FA Cup final on 21 May 1977. Stuart Pearson scored for United in the 51st minute. A minute later Case turned and drove the equaliser past Alex Stepney. Jimmy Greenhoff scored United's winner three minutes after that, and they won 2–1.

Four days later Case started the European Cup final against Borussia Mönchengladbach in Rome. Terry McDermott put Liverpool ahead. Allan Simonsen equalised early in the second half. "I know I gave the ball away to Allan Simonsen for their goal," he told LFChistory. Tommy Smith headed Liverpool back in front and Phil Neal scored a penalty. Liverpool won 3–1 and were European champions for the first time.

## A second European Cup

Liverpool met Mönchengladbach again in the semi-final the following season. They lost the first leg 2–1 in Düsseldorf. In the second leg at Anfield on 12 April 1978, Case scored in a 3–0 win, with Kennedy and Dalglish getting the others.

He started the final against Club Brugge at Wembley on 10 May 1978. He was replaced by Steve Heighway on the hour, and Dalglish scored the only goal four minutes later. Case had now started two European Cup finals and won both.

## Two more titles

Liverpool won the league in 1978–79 and again in 1979–80. Case made 37 league appearances in each of those seasons. He scored nine goals in all competitions in the first and five in the second, according to LFChistory.

## Sammy Lee and Paris

In 1980–81 Sammy Lee established himself in midfield and Case's place was less certain. He still started the League Cup final replay against West Ham United at Villa Park on 1 April 1981, which Liverpool won 2–1.

He did not start the European Cup final against Real Madrid in Paris on 27 May 1981. Alan Kennedy scored in the 81st minute, and Case replaced Dalglish in the 86th. Liverpool won 1–0. It was Case's third European Cup and his last competitive game for the club.

## Leaving Liverpool

Case joined Brighton & Hove Albion in August 1981, aged 27. It was the same summer that Mark Lawrenson moved from Brighton to Liverpool, and Case later said he had been the makeweight in that deal.

He did not want to leave. "I didn't want to go anyway, to be honest," he told LFChistory, and he said he would have given a good go for the position, even though Lee was a really good friend of his. He also accepted that the players' life away from the pitch had counted against him. "We let our hair down a bit and that's exactly what we did," he said. "The club in them days didn't like that type of things."

## Brighton, Southampton and back

On 20 February 1983 Case returned to Anfield with Brighton for an FA Cup fifth-round tie. He scored the winner in the 71st minute, and Brighton won 2–1. He scored again in the semi-final against Sheffield Wednesday, from long range. Brighton reached the final and lost to Manchester United after a replay.

He joined Southampton in March 1985, became captain and stayed until 1991. He later went back to Brighton as a player and then managed the club.

In all, 186 of his 269 Liverpool appearances, and 23 of his 46 goals, came in the league.
