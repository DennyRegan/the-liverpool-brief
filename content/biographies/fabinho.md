Fabinho played 219 competitive games for Liverpool and scored 11 goals, according to LFChistory. In five seasons he won the Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup, the Premier League, the League Cup and the FA Cup. He played at the base of Jürgen Klopp's midfield, in front of the centre-backs. His job was to protect the defence so the players ahead of him could attack.

## Before Liverpool

Fábio Henrique Tavares was born in Campinas, Brazil, on 23 October 1993. He played for Paulínia and Fluminense in Brazil before joining Rio Ave in Portugal in 2012. He never played for Rio Ave's first team. Instead he went on loan to Real Madrid in 2012–13, where he played 30 games for the B team and made one substitute appearance in La Liga.

Monaco gave him a regular place. In 2016–17 he helped them win Ligue 1 and reach the Champions League semi-finals, where they lost to Juventus.

Liverpool agreed his transfer on 28 May 2018, and the deal went through on 1 July. He was 24.

## A slow start

His debut came on 18 September 2018, as an injury-time substitute against Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League. A month later he had made three appearances in all competitions and had not started a league game.

Klopp was asked about it at his press conference before the game at Huddersfield on 20 October. There was "only one reason", he said: "because another player fits better in the moment." It was up to a new player to close that gap, he added, and "it always took time and will always take time."

Two months later he showed what he could add. Against Manchester United at Anfield on 16 December, he lifted a pass over the defence. Sadio Mané controlled it on his chest and scored in the 24th minute. Jesse Lingard equalised, and Xherdan Shaqiri came off the bench to score twice in a 3–1 win.

## Champions of Europe and England

His main task was to make Liverpool hard to play through. He stood in front of the centre-backs and guarded the space there. That mattered in a team whose full-backs, Trent Alexander-Arnold and Andy Robertson, pushed forward so often.

He played the whole of the 2019 Champions League final, a 2–0 win over Tottenham in Madrid. His first season at Liverpool ended with the European Cup. The UEFA Super Cup followed in August 2019, and the league title in 2019–20. He made 28 league appearances in that title season.

His best-known goal came on 10 November 2019, when Manchester City, the champions, came to Anfield. In the sixth minute he hit a shot from about 25 yards low into the bottom-left corner. Mohamed Salah added a second in the 13th minute and Mané a third in the 51st. Bernardo Silva scored for City late on, and Liverpool won 3–1. The result left them eight points clear at the top of the table and nine ahead of City, who were fourth.

## Moved to centre-back

Injuries to Liverpool's defenders in 2020–21 meant he spent much of that season at centre-back. That left a gap in midfield.

On 10 March 2021 he went back to midfield for the Champions League last-16 second leg against RB Leipzig, played in Budapest. Salah and Mané scored in a 2–0 win, Liverpool went through 4–0 on aggregate, and UEFA named Fabinho player of the match. "Yes, I missed it a little bit playing the midfield position," he told the club afterwards.

Before the next game, at Wolves, Klopp explained what his return had done for Georginio Wijnaldum, who had been covering the defensive midfield role. Playing Wijnaldum deeper, he said, meant Liverpool missed him further forward. With Fabinho back in his natural position, he helped everyone around him, in defence and in attack.

## Villarreal

In 2021–22 Liverpool won the League Cup and the FA Cup. Fabinho scored eight goals in all competitions, five of them in the league. It was his best scoring season for the club.

One of those goals came in the Champions League semi-final second leg at Villarreal on 3 May 2022. Liverpool had won the first leg 2–0 at Anfield. Boulaye Dia scored in the third minute and Francis Coquelin in the 41st, and at half-time the tie was level.

In the 62nd minute Fabinho drove a shot through the legs of the goalkeeper, Gerónimo Rulli. Luis Díaz scored five minutes later and Mané in the 74th minute. Liverpool won 3–2 on the night and 5–2 on aggregate, and reached another final.

## The last season

His final season was harder. Before the game against Wolves in February 2023, Klopp spoke openly about his form. "At the moment, it's not clicking and that's true as well, you have to go through that," he said. He added that it was "really helpful" to have Stefan Bajčetić, "because he's doing really well."

Fabinho joined Al-Ittihad in Saudi Arabia on 31 July 2023, for a reported £40m. He had played 151 league games for Liverpool and scored eight league goals.

## Since Liverpool

At Al-Ittihad he won the Saudi Pro League and King's Cup double in 2024–25. His contract ran out in the summer of 2026, and he left. On 24 August 2026 Trabzonspor announced that he had joined them on a free transfer, on a two-year contract. Salah had joined the Turkish club earlier that month.

He scored against City and at Villarreal, but most of his work for Liverpool was done in front of the defence: winning the ball back and passing it on.
