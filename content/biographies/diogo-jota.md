Diogo Jota scored 65 goals in 182 appearances for Liverpool in all competitions. In five seasons he won four major honours: the League Cup in 2022 and 2024, the FA Cup in 2022 and the Premier League in 2024–25. Many of his goals settled tight games, from cup semi-finals to a late winner against Tottenham and the only goal of a Merseyside derby.

He needed little time to make his case. At Atalanta on 3 November 2020, 45 days after joining Liverpool, he scored three times in a 5–0 Champions League win. The finishes were all different. He lifted the first over the goalkeeper from Trent Alexander-Arnold's pass. For the second he took Joe Gomez's pass past a defender and drove it in at the near post. For the third he went round the goalkeeper from Sadio Mané's pass and rolled it into an empty net.

## Porto to Wolves

Jota was born in Porto on 4 December 1996 and grew up in Gondomar. He made his senior debut for Paços de Ferreira in October 2014, aged 17. Atlético Madrid signed him in 2016, but he never played for them. He spent the next season on loan at Porto, where his coach was Nuno Espírito Santo.

When Nuno moved to Wolverhampton Wanderers, Jota followed him on a season-long loan in the summer of 2017. He scored 17 Championship goals in 2017–18 as Wolves won promotion. Two seasons in the Premier League followed. Liverpool completed his signing on 19 September 2020, when he was 23.

## What Klopp saw

In December 2021, before a game at Wolves, Jürgen Klopp explained why he had wanted him. Jota had caught his eye because of how "intense he was in all the situations", Klopp said. Wolves gave their wide forwards a heavy defensive workload, and Klopp said it was clear Jota would take his next steps at Liverpool.

Klopp also said Jota's goals were not instinct alone. In his view they were roughly half instinct and half being in the right place, which was something the team rehearsed in training.

## Breaking into the attack

The front three ahead of him were well established. Mohamed Salah, Mané and Roberto Firmino had helped Liverpool win the Champions League in 2019 and the league title in 2019–20. Jota made room for himself quickly.

His first appearance was in the League Cup against Lincoln City on 24 September 2020. Four days later he came off the bench on his Premier League debut against Arsenal at Anfield and scored the third goal in a 3–1 win. The Atalanta hat-trick came five weeks after that.

He could start a match or change one from the bench. On 9 December 2020 he injured his knee against Midtjylland and missed about three months. He still finished his first season with 13 goals in 30 appearances.

## The cup season

In 2021–22 he made 55 appearances and scored 21 goals, according to LFChistory. Liverpool were competing on every front, and his goals kept the cup runs going.

The League Cup semi-final first leg against Arsenal finished 0–0. In the second leg at the Emirates on 20 January 2022, Jota scored both goals in a 2–0 win to send Liverpool to Wembley. Two months later, on 20 March, he scored the only goal at Nottingham Forest in the FA Cup quarter-final.

Liverpool went on to win both domestic cups that season. Jota did not score in either final, but the club's tribute singles out his double at Arsenal and his winner at Forest.

## Injuries and comebacks

Injuries kept breaking his rhythm. On 16 October 2022 he hurt his calf against Manchester City. He was out for about four months and missed the World Cup in Qatar. When he came back, the goals did not come straight away. He scored seven times that season.

One of them is among his best remembered. Against Tottenham at Anfield on 30 April 2023, Liverpool led 3–0 inside 15 minutes. Spurs came back, and Richarlison made it 3–3. In the 94th minute Jota scored the winner.

In January 2024 he was named Premier League Player of the Month for the first time. He scored three goals and made two assists in three league games, all won: 4–2 against Newcastle, 4–0 against Bournemouth and 4–1 against Chelsea. Liverpool won the League Cup again that season, though he was injured at the time of the final.

## Champions under Slot

Klopp left in the summer of 2024, and Jota stayed an important player under Arne Slot. Liverpool won the Premier League in 2024–25.

On 2 April 2025 he scored the only goal of the Merseyside derby at Anfield, in the 57th minute. The 1–0 win restored Liverpool's 12-point lead at the top. It was his last goal for the club. His last Liverpool appearance came on 25 May 2025.

His last match of all was for Portugal. On 8 June 2025 they beat Spain on penalties in the Nations League final.

## His death

Diogo Jota died on 3 July 2025 with his brother, André Silva, in a road accident in Spain. He was 28. André Silva was also a professional footballer, with Penafiel. In a statement, the club said: "Liverpool Football Club are devastated by the tragic passing of Diogo Jota."

## The number 20

On 11 July 2025, after consulting his wife, Rute, and his family, Liverpool retired the number 20 at every level of the club, including LFC Women and the Academy. The club said it was the first time it had given this honour. Michael Edwards, FSG's chief executive of football, said: "As far as Liverpool Football Club is concerned, he will be forever our number 20."

According to the club, supporters sang his song in the 20th minute of every Liverpool match through the 2025–26 season.

On 2 July 2026, the day before the first anniversary of his death, the club unveiled a permanent memorial to both brothers on 97 Avenue outside Anfield. Called "Forever 20", it was made by the sculptor Emma Rodgers. It takes the shape of a heart, after his goal celebration, and shows the numbers 20 and 30, the brothers' shirt numbers, from different angles. Scarves and shirts that supporters left at the temporary memorial are built into it, and the plinth includes stone from Gondomar.

Jota played 182 games for Liverpool and scored 65 goals. He won the League Cup twice, the FA Cup once and the Premier League once.
