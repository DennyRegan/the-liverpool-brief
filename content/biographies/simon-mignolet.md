Simon Mignolet made 204 appearances for Liverpool between 2013 and 2019 and kept 65 clean sheets, according to LFChistory. He played 155 league games, 12 in the FA Cup, 12 in the League Cup and 25 in Europe. He was first choice under Brendan Rodgers and in Jürgen Klopp's first season, when Liverpool reached two finals. He later lost his place, first to Loris Karius and then to Alisson, and was in the squad that won the Champions League in 2019.

## Stoke, April 2017

One of his best afternoons came at Stoke on 8 April 2017. Jonathan Walters had put Stoke ahead just before half-time. Early in the second half, with Liverpool still behind, Charlie Adam had a close-range chance and Mignolet touched it wide.

Philippe Coutinho equalised, and Roberto Firmino made it 2–1 a couple of minutes later. Seconds after Firmino's goal, Saido Berahino met Marko Arnautovic's cross at the back post. Mignolet saved at point-blank range, and Liverpool held on to win 2–1.

Klopp said afterwards that the save from Berahino was "one of the best I've ever seen". He added that it was well deserved for Mignolet, "because he has got a lot of criticism in his Liverpool career".

## A debut save

Mignolet joined Liverpool from Sunderland on 25 June 2013 for £9m, according to LFChistory. Rodgers was the manager.

His debut was against Stoke at Anfield on 17 August 2013. Daniel Sturridge scored the only goal. Late in the game Stoke were given a penalty. Mignolet saved Walters' kick and then the rebound from Kenwyne Jones. Liverpool won 1–0.

Afterwards he gave the credit to the staff. "You study those things as a goalkeeper," he told the club. "All credit to the guys at the training ground who helped me analyse those things."

Liverpool finished second in 2013–14, with 84 points. Mignolet played in all 38 league games that season, and 40 in all competitions.

## Klopp's first season

Klopp was appointed on 8 October 2015 and kept Mignolet as his goalkeeper. He made 55 appearances that season, 15 of them in Europe, and Liverpool reached two finals.

The first came through a League Cup semi-final against Stoke. The second leg at Anfield on 26 January 2016 left the tie 1–1 on aggregate. In the shoot-out Mignolet saved from Peter Crouch and, in sudden death, from Marc Muniesa. Joe Allen scored the winning kick, and Liverpool went through 6–5 on penalties.

Mignolet again pointed to the preparation. "We studied the penalties of the opponents, as always. It paid off," he said. "All credit to the goalkeeping coaches and analysts who put a lot of work into that."

The final against Manchester City at Wembley on 28 February was a mixed day for him. Early in the second half Fernandinho scored from a tight angle, the ball going underneath Mignolet's dive. He also made several saves, including what the club's report called a "magnificent reflex save" from Sergio Agüero. The game finished 1–1 after extra time. In the shoot-out City's Willy Caballero saved from Lucas Leiva, Coutinho and Adam Lallana, and City won 3–1 on penalties.

Mignolet was also in goal for the Europa League final against Sevilla in Basel on 18 May 2016. Liverpool lost 3–1.

## Dropped and recalled

Karius joined in 2016 and took over in goal that autumn. Mignolet was recalled for the game at Middlesbrough on 14 December 2016, which Liverpool won 3–0.

Klopp said Karius had not been able to show his quality in the previous few games. Of Mignolet, he said: "He played really well at the beginning of the season." He added: "Now it is Simon's chance." There was, he said, no timeframe for when Karius would be back.

Eight days after the win at Stoke, Liverpool went to West Bromwich Albion on 16 April 2017. Firmino scored in first-half stoppage time. About 10 minutes from the end, Matt Phillips broke clear, and Mignolet stopped him with his legs. Liverpool won 1–0.

They finished fourth, sealing a Champions League place with a 3–0 win over Middlesbrough on the last day, 21 May 2017.

## Losing the place

The following season Klopp switched again. On 14 January 2018, after a 4–3 win over Manchester City, he told the German broadcaster DAZN that Karius was his number one. "When it comes to goalkeepers you have to make a decision and stick with it," Klopp said. "He will stay in goal."

Alisson arrived in the summer of 2018, and Mignolet became his back-up. In 2018–19 he made two appearances, one in the League Cup and one in the FA Cup. The last was on 7 January 2019. He did not play in the league or in Europe that season.

He was still part of the squad in Madrid on 1 June 2019. He was named as a substitute for the Champions League final against Tottenham and was not used. Liverpool won 2–0.

## Leaving for Brugge

Mignolet joined Club Brugge on 5 August 2019. In his tribute Klopp called Mignolet one of "the most standout professionals I've worked with in the game" and said: "The respect he commands in our dressing room is unrivalled." Klopp said that what Mignolet had done for the club meant "he earns the right to do something that helps him in this moment".

## After Liverpool

By the spring of 2026 Mignolet had won four Belgian league titles and a Belgian Cup with Brugge, according to Liverpool's website. He won 35 caps for Belgium and retired from international football in 2023.

On 2 April 2026, aged 38, he announced he would retire at the end of the 2025–26 season. Brugge won the league title that season, and his farewell came at the end of the Belgian season in late May. He was due to start a management role at the Royal Belgian Football Association on 1 September 2026.
