Divock Origi scored 41 goals in 175 appearances for Liverpool, according to LFChistory. He was often outside the starting eleven, but several of those goals came at decisive moments. He scored twice in the 4–0 win over Barcelona that took Liverpool to the 2019 Champions League final, then scored the second goal in the final itself. He also scored six goals against Everton, one of them a winner in stoppage time. He left with medals from the Premier League, the Champions League, the FA Cup, the League Cup, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup.

## Before Liverpool

Origi was born in Ostend, Belgium, on 18 April 1995. He came through at Genk before moving to Lille. Liverpool signed the Belgium international on 29 July 2014 and loaned him straight back to Lille for the 2014–15 season.

He made his Liverpool debut on 12 September 2015, under Brendan Rodgers. Rodgers was sacked on 4 October, and Jürgen Klopp was appointed four days later.

## Early promise

His first Liverpool goals were a hat-trick in the League Cup quarter-final at St Mary's on 2 December 2015. Liverpool beat Southampton 6–1.

The following spring he scored in both legs of the Europa League quarter-final against Borussia Dortmund. He scored in the 1–1 draw in Germany and again in the 4–3 win at Anfield that took Liverpool through.

On 20 April 2016 he headed the first goal in a 4–0 win over Everton, then injured his ankle in a challenge by Ramiro Funes Mori. He was fit enough to come off the bench in the second half of the Europa League final against Sevilla in Basel on 18 May, which Liverpool lost 3–1.

He scored 10 goals in 33 games in 2015–16 and 11 in 43 the season after. He made one appearance at the start of 2017–18 before joining Wolfsburg on a season-long loan, agreed on 31 August 2017.

## Back in the squad

By the time he returned, Mohamed Salah, Sadio Mané and Roberto Firmino were Liverpool's established front three. Origi could play through the middle or on the left, and he had to find his chances around them.

The first big one came on 2 December 2018, at Anfield against Everton. The derby was goalless when he replaced Firmino in the 84th minute. Soon after coming on he hit the woodwork. Then, in the sixth minute of stoppage time, Virgil van Dijk skied a shot. Jordan Pickford palmed it onto the bar, and Origi headed the rebound in on the line. Liverpool won 1–0.

He scored seven goals in 21 appearances that season, most of them in the spring.

## Barcelona and Madrid

Liverpool lost the first leg of the Champions League semi-final 3–0 in Barcelona. In the second leg at Anfield on 7 May 2019, Origi scored after seven minutes. Georginio Wijnaldum came on at half-time and scored twice to level the tie.

In the 79th minute Liverpool won a corner. Trent Alexander-Arnold took it quickly while Barcelona were still organising themselves, and Origi finished. Liverpool won 4–0 on the night and 4–3 on aggregate.

The final against Tottenham was at the Estadio Metropolitano in Madrid on 1 June 2019. Salah scored a penalty in the second minute, and Origi came on as a substitute. In the 87th minute Liverpool took their ninth corner. Spurs failed to clear it, Joël Matip helped the ball on, and Origi drove a low shot from the left across Hugo Lloris and into the far corner. Liverpool won 2–0. It was their sixth European Cup.

## Champions

Origi was part of the squad that won the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup in 2019. In between, on 4 December 2019, he scored twice in a 5–2 win over Everton at Anfield. The first came in the sixth minute from Mané's pass. For the second, in the 31st minute, he took Dejan Lovren's long ball with one touch and lifted it over Pickford.

Afterwards he talked about the service and the space behind Everton's defence. "We used the spacing well, that's one of our weapons in going in behind and then we just tried to do it," he told the club. "It was wonderful service and that was good today."

Liverpool won the Premier League that season. Origi made 42 appearances in all competitions and scored six goals.

## Wolves, 2021

On 4 December 2021 Liverpool were drawing 0–0 at Wolves going into stoppage time. In the 94th minute Origi received Salah's pass, turned inside the penalty area and scored the only goal.

"And then Divock Origi, the legend, came and finished it off for us," Klopp said afterwards. He called him an incredible finisher who did not need much time to get into a game. "Because he is an incredible finisher – if we don't know it at Liverpool, who should know it then?"

Klopp also spoke about Origi's place in the squad. "This team is outstanding and if you are our No.12, 13 or 14 you have to be outstanding – and Div is," he said. He admitted he had expected Origi to leave: "If I would be in another club I would go for him and I thought that would happen…" He added: "I am very happy that he is still here, actually."

## The last season

Liverpool won the League Cup and the FA Cup in 2021–22. Origi's last goal for the club came against Everton on 24 April 2022, a header in a 2–0 win at Anfield. His contract ended on 30 June 2022 and he left on a free transfer.

His final record, according to LFChistory, was 175 appearances and 41 goals in all competitions.

## After Liverpool

Origi joined AC Milan on a free transfer in 2022. He made 36 appearances for them, according to beIN Sports, and spent the 2023–24 season on loan at Nottingham Forest. He did not play competitively after the Forest loan. Milan ended his contract by mutual consent in December 2025.

In early June 2026, aged 31, he announced his retirement on social media. "My purpose in the game as a player is fulfilled," he wrote. "The mission is complete. Now I step into my next calling." He did not say what that would be.
