Terry McDermott made 329 appearances for Liverpool and scored 81 goals, a big return for a midfielder. He scored the opening goal in Rome in 1977, when Liverpool won their first European Cup, and started the 1978 and 1981 finals as well. The club credits him with five league titles. In 1980 he became the first player to win the Football Writers' Association and PFA player of the year awards in the same season. He also won 25 England caps between 1977 and 1982 and scored three goals.

## From Kirkby to Anfield

McDermott was born in Kirkby on 8 December 1951 and grew up a Liverpool supporter. He began his career at Bury and moved to Newcastle United for £25,000 in 1973.

On 4 May 1974 he played for Newcastle against Liverpool in the FA Cup final. Liverpool won 3–0. It was Bill Shankly's last competitive match as manager.

Six months later Bob Paisley signed him for about £170,000. He made his debut in the Merseyside derby at Goodison Park on 16 November 1974, a 0–0 draw. Phil Neal made his Liverpool debut in the same game.

## The wait

McDermott did not walk into the team. He made 15 appearances in the rest of 1974–75 and ten in all competitions the following season. Liverpool won the league and the UEFA Cup in 1975–76, and he made only nine league appearances in the title-winning campaign.

The breakthrough came in 1976–77. He made 38 appearances as Liverpool retained the league title.

## Two goals on the way to Rome

On 23 April 1977 Liverpool met Everton in the FA Cup semi-final at Maine Road. McDermott opened the scoring after ten minutes with a chip over David Lawson. The game finished 2–2, and Liverpool won the replay 3–0.

Manchester United beat them 2–1 in the final on 21 May. For McDermott it was a second Wembley defeat in four years, and it ended Liverpool's hopes of a treble. The European Cup final was four days later.

At the Stadio Olimpico on 25 May, Liverpool played Borussia Mönchengladbach. McDermott moved into space to receive Steve Heighway's pass and finished midway through the first half. Allan Simonsen equalised in the 51st minute. Tommy Smith headed Liverpool back in front from a Heighway corner, and Neal scored a penalty in the 82nd minute. Liverpool won 3–1.

## Hamburg

Kevin Keegan had left Liverpool for Hamburg that summer. On 6 December 1977 he came back to Anfield for the second leg of the European Super Cup. Liverpool won 6–0, and 7–1 on aggregate. McDermott scored a hat-trick, with one goal just before half-time and two soon after it.

He would later describe that hat-trick as the making of him at Liverpool, according to LFChistory.

Liverpool kept the European Cup in May 1978. McDermott started the final against Club Brugge at Wembley, and Kenny Dalglish scored the only goal in the 64th minute.

## Spurs, Forest and the double award

On 2 September 1978 Liverpool beat Tottenham Hotspur 7–0 at Anfield. McDermott scored the seventh in the 76th minute. The move began with him on the post, defending a Spurs corner. The ball went upfield, David Johnson released Heighway down the left, and McDermott arrived at the far end to head in Heighway's cross.

On 9 December 1978 he scored both goals in a 2–0 win over Nottingham Forest at Anfield. The first was a penalty in the 29th minute, after Archie Gemmill fouled Dalglish. The second came in the 48th minute. The result ended Forest's run of 42 league games unbeaten.

Liverpool won the league in 1978–79 and again in 1979–80. In 1980 McDermott won the FWA Footballer of the Year and the PFA Players' Player of the Year, the first player to take both in the same season.

## The volley and the chip

On 8 March 1980 Liverpool played Tottenham at White Hart Lane in the FA Cup quarter-final. In the 18th minute Osvaldo Ardiles misplaced a clearance. McDermott flicked the ball up and volleyed it into the far corner. It was the only goal of the game and was voted the BBC's goal of the season.

McDermott was never sure how much of it he had planned. "I still say to this day I don't know whether I meant to flick it up or whether it was just bad control, probably bad control," he told the club.

At Pittodrie on 22 October 1980 he scored the only goal of the European Cup second-round first leg against Aberdeen. It came in the fifth minute, from a Johnson pass, and he chipped it in from a tight angle.

## 1980–81

That season was his most prolific at Liverpool: 22 goals in 60 appearances in all competitions. Liverpool lost the league title but won the League Cup for the first time. In the final replay against West Ham United at Villa Park on 1 April 1981, McDermott set up Dalglish's equaliser, and Alan Hansen scored the winner in a 2–1 win.

On 27 May he started the European Cup final against Real Madrid in Paris. Alan Kennedy scored in the 81st minute, and Liverpool won 1–0. It was McDermott's third European Cup winner's medal.

## Younger players

McDermott scored 20 goals in 1981–82, 14 of them in the league. That was his best league total for Liverpool. But he played 29 league games, down from 40 the season before. Younger players were now challenging for his place.

In the League Cup final against Tottenham on 13 March 1982 he started but was replaced by Johnson in the 65th minute. Ronnie Whelan equalised late in normal time and scored again in extra time, and Ian Rush added a third in a 3–1 win. Liverpool also won back the league title that season.

His last three full seasons brought him 58 goals. His final appearance came against Dundalk at Anfield in the European Cup on 28 September 1982. He set up Whelan for the only goal in the 81st minute, and Liverpool went through 5–1 on aggregate. The next day he rejoined Newcastle for £100,000.

## After playing

McDermott later went back to Newcastle as a coach alongside Keegan.

In an interview with Tony Barrett, reproduced by LFChistory, he said: "I like Anfield when it is empty." In the same piece he told the story of the seventh goal against Spurs.

In August 2021 he announced through the club that hospital tests had found he was in the early stages of Lewy body dementia.

He left Liverpool with 329 appearances and 81 goals, 54 of them in the league.
