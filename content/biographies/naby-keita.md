Naby Keïta played 129 games for Liverpool and scored 11 goals, according to LFChistory. In five seasons he won the Champions League, the Club World Cup, the Premier League, the League Cup, the FA Cup and the Community Shield. He was a midfielder who could carry the ball forward, beat a man in a tight space and arrive in the box as an extra attacker. Injuries meant Liverpool saw that less often than they had hoped. His most regular season, 2021–22, brought 40 appearances. His last brought 13.

## Before Liverpool

Keïta was born in Conakry, Guinea, on 10 February 1995. He came through at Horoya and played for their senior side in 2012–13. He then moved to Istres in France in 2013, Red Bull Salzburg in 2014 and RB Leipzig in 2016. In 2016–17 he scored eight goals in 31 Bundesliga games as Leipzig finished second.

Liverpool announced on 29 August 2017 that they had agreed to sign him, but he stayed at Leipzig for another season and joined on 1 July 2018. Liverpool triggered his £48m release clause and paid an undisclosed premium on top, according to Sky Sports. That was more than the £39.5m they had paid Roma for Mohamed Salah that summer, and a club record when the deal was agreed. Virgil van Dijk's £75m move from Southampton, agreed that December, passed it before Keïta arrived. LFChistory gives Keïta's fee as £52.75m.

## The No.8

Keïta was the first Liverpool player to wear the No.8 since Steven Gerrard left in 2015. Gerrard gave him the shirt himself, at Melwood in August 2017, on Keïta's first visit after the deal was agreed. That was almost a year before he arrived. "It was an incredible day for me. He gave me the No.8 jersey," Keïta told the club at his unveiling in June 2018. "When it happened, it was a surprise and I didn't expect it."

## First goals, then an injury

His first Liverpool goal came on 5 April 2019, in his first season. Liverpool were 1–0 down at Southampton, and in the 36th minute he headed in a cross from Trent Alexander-Arnold. Salah and Jordan Henderson scored late on, and Liverpool won 3–1 to go back to the top of the table.

In the next game, four days later, he scored after five minutes against Porto at Anfield. Roberto Firmino added a second, and Liverpool won the Champions League quarter-final first leg 2–0.

On 26 April he scored after 15 seconds in a 5–0 win over Huddersfield. At the time it was the fastest goal Liverpool had scored in the Premier League. It was his third goal in three weeks.

Then the injury came. In the Champions League semi-final first leg at Barcelona on 1 May 2019, he went off in the first half with an adductor injury. Liverpool lost 3–0. Two days later Klopp said "the tendon is ruptured" and that Keïta would be out for at least two months. His season was over. He missed the 4–0 second-leg win at Anfield and the final against Tottenham in Madrid, which Liverpool won.

## Qatar and the title

He was back for the Club World Cup in Qatar that December. In the semi-final against Monterrey on 18 December he ran on to Salah's pass and scored after 12 minutes. Monterrey equalised two minutes later, and Firmino won it 2–1 in stoppage time. Keïta started the final against Flamengo three days later. He played 100 minutes before James Milner replaced him, and Liverpool won 1–0 after extra time.

On 22 July 2020, the night Liverpool were presented with the Premier League trophy, he scored the first goal in a 5–3 win over Chelsea at Anfield. It came after 23 minutes, a shot from just outside the area that went in off the crossbar.

## The 2021–22 season

His best run of games came in 2021–22. He made 40 appearances and scored four goals, according to LFChistory, his most in a season for the club.

On 24 October 2021 he scored after five minutes at Old Trafford, from Salah's pass, as Liverpool won 5–0. Liverpool reached all three cup finals that season, and he played in each of them. He started the League Cup final against Chelsea on 27 February, but only because Thiago Alcântara was injured in the warm-up. He went off after 80 minutes, and Liverpool won on penalties. He started the FA Cup final against Chelsea on 14 May, went off after 74 minutes, and Liverpool again won on penalties. In the Champions League final against Real Madrid in Paris on 28 May he came on for Henderson. Liverpool lost 1–0.

The goal people remember from that season came at Newcastle on 30 April 2022. In the 19th minute he played a one-two with Diogo Jota, cut inside, went round the goalkeeper Martin Dúbravka and shot past the defenders running back to cover. Newcastle's own report described them as a "line of backtracking United defenders". It was the only goal. It put Liverpool two points clear at the top for a few hours, until Manchester City beat Leeds later that day.

## Last season

Injuries limited him to 13 appearances in 2022–23. His last game for Liverpool was on 25 February 2023. On 17 May the club confirmed he would leave when his contract ran out that summer, and he left on 30 June. His Liverpool record was 129 appearances and 11 goals: 84 games in the Premier League, 24 in the Champions League, eight each in the FA Cup and League Cup, three in the Community Shield and two in the Club World Cup.

## After Liverpool

Werder Bremen announced his signing on a free transfer on 9 June 2023. He made only five Bundesliga appearances for them. In April 2024 Bremen suspended him after he refused to travel to a match if he was not starting. Their sporting director, Clemens Fritz, said Keïta would not play for the club again.

In January 2025 he joined Ferencváros in Hungary on a year's loan. On 14 January 2026 Bremen announced that the move had become permanent, for an undisclosed fee. He had made 28 appearances for Ferencváros by then. He is still a Ferencváros player.
