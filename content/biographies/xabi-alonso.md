Xabi Alonso made 210 appearances for Liverpool between 2004 and 2009 and scored 19 goals, according to LFChistory. He won the Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup and the FA Cup, and played in a second Champions League final. In Istanbul in 2005 Dida saved his penalty, and Alonso followed the ball in to score the rebound. It made the score 3–3 against Milan. Liverpool won the final on penalties.

## From Tolosa to Anfield

Alonso was born in Tolosa on 25 November 1981. His father, Periko Alonso, played for Spain, including at the 1982 World Cup.

He came through at Real Sociedad. After a loan at Eibar in the first half of 2000–01, John Toshack recalled him and made him captain at 19. Sociedad finished second in Spain in 2002–03, two points behind Real Madrid. Alonso started for Spain against Portugal at Euro 2004.

Liverpool signed him on 20 August 2004, aged 22, as Rafael Benítez began rebuilding the team. They paid a reported £10.7 million. Luis García joined from Barcelona in the same window. Alonso wore the No.14 shirt.

In a 2017 interview with Sky Sports, Alonso said Liverpool was his first experience of living on his own and away from his home town. Until then he had lived with his parents. He described it as the time he became an adult.

## First season

His debut came in a 1–0 defeat at Bolton on 29 August 2004. On 16 October Liverpool were 2–0 down at Fulham at half-time, and Benítez sent Alonso on for Salif Diao. Liverpool won 4–2. After the deficit had been wiped out, Alonso scored his first goal for the club with a deflected free-kick in the 79th minute.

On New Year's Day 2005 he broke his ankle in a challenge with Frank Lampard. Chelsea won the game at Anfield 1–0. The first estimate was five to six weeks out. His first start after the injury did not come until 13 April, away to Juventus in the Champions League quarter-final.

Liverpool led 2–1 from the first leg, and Steven Gerrard was out with a groin injury. Alonso played in central midfield with Igor Bišćan, and the game finished 0–0. His pass sent Milan Baroš through for a chance, which Baroš put wide.

He was booked late in the goalless first leg of the semi-final against Chelsea, his third booking of the competition, and missed the second leg. Liverpool went through without him.

## Istanbul

Alonso started the final against Milan. Paolo Maldini scored in the first minute and Hernán Crespo added two before half-time. Liverpool were 3–0 down at the interval. Dietmar Hamann came on for Steve Finnan at the start of the second half.

Gerrard headed in after 54 minutes, and Vladimír Šmicer scored from distance two minutes later. Then Gennaro Gattuso fouled Gerrard in the area. Dida saved Alonso's penalty, but Alonso beat Alessandro Nesta to the rebound and scored. Liverpool won the shoot-out 3–2 after extra time.

He made 32 appearances and scored three goals in that first season.

## Luton, West Ham and Newcastle

Liverpool won the UEFA Super Cup in August 2005. On 7 January 2006 they went to Luton Town in the FA Cup third round and fell 3–1 behind. Florent Sinama-Pongolle made it 3–2. Alonso levelled with a shot from 40 yards. Sinama-Pongolle put Liverpool ahead, and near the end, with Luton's goalkeeper Marlon Beresford up in attack, Alonso scored with his left foot from inside his own half. Liverpool won 5–3.

In the final against West Ham on 13 May 2006, Alonso crossed, Peter Crouch headed the ball down and Gerrard scored Liverpool's second goal. The match ended 3–3, and Liverpool won the shoot-out 3–1.

On 20 September 2006 he scored another long-range goal, against Newcastle at Anfield. He won the ball from Charles N'Zogbia, saw Steve Harper off his line and shot with his right foot. Harper slipped trying to get back, and the ball dropped in. Liverpool won 2–0.

The following May, Liverpool met Milan again in the Champions League final, in Athens. Alonso fouled Kaká just before half-time, and Andrea Pirlo's free-kick deflected in off Filippo Inzaghi. Inzaghi scored again late on. Dirk Kuyt replied in the 89th minute, and Milan won 2–1.

## Mascherano, Torres and Barry

Javier Mascherano's arrival gave Benítez another central midfielder, and Fernando Torres joined in 2007. With Mascherano covering and Alonso passing, Gerrard could play further forward behind Torres.

Injuries limited Alonso to 27 appearances in 2007–08. Liverpool reached the Champions League semi-finals again and lost to Chelsea.

That summer Benítez tried to sign Gareth Barry, and Alonso was told he could be sold. He spoke about it in a 2017 interview with the Liverpool Echo, and earlier in *Ring of Fire* and to Fox Sports in 2011. He had not expected the manager to say it, and he felt his place in the team changed a little that summer. He accepted that the decision was the manager's. Barry did not arrive, and Alonso stayed.

## The last season

On 26 October 2008 Alonso scored the only goal at Stamford Bridge. His shot in the 10th minute deflected off José Bosingwa and wrong-footed Petr Čech. He also hit the post with a free-kick. The win ended Chelsea's run of 86 home league games unbeaten and put Liverpool three points clear at the top.

He made 47 appearances that season and scored five goals, 33 of the appearances and four of the goals in the league. Liverpool finished second with 86 points, four behind Manchester United.

Real Madrid completed his transfer on 5 August 2009. "I thought it was the right moment to find a fresh challenge," he told UEFA.

## After Liverpool

At Madrid he won La Liga in 2011–12 and the Champions League in 2014. He joined Bayern Munich in August 2014 and won the Bundesliga in 2015, 2016 and 2017. With Spain he won Euro 2008 while he was a Liverpool player, then the 2010 World Cup and Euro 2012. He won 114 caps.

His last game was Bayern's 4–1 win over Freiburg on 20 May 2017. In his retirement tribute, Gerrard called him "the best passer of a ball I've ever played with."

In 2022 he returned to Anfield for LFC Legends games against Manchester United and Bayern Munich.

He went into management with Real Sociedad's reserve side and led them to promotion to Spain's second division in 2021. He then managed Bayer Leverkusen from 2022 to 2025 and won the Bundesliga in 2023–24, the club's first league title. He moved to Real Madrid in 2025 and left in January 2026. He was appointed Chelsea head coach in 2026.
