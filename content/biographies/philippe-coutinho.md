Philippe Coutinho made 201 appearances for Liverpool and scored 54 goals. He joined in January 2013 and left for Barcelona five years later. He scored the winner against Manchester City in the 2013–14 title race, twice in Jürgen Klopp's first Premier League win and a hat-trick as captain in the Champions League. He played in two finals for the club and won neither.

## Arrival

Liverpool signed him from Internazionale in January 2013, when Brendan Rodgers was manager. He made his debut as a substitute against West Bromwich Albion. Six days later he made his first start, against Swansea City, and scored.

In 2013–14 Liverpool challenged for the title, with Luis Suárez and Daniel Sturridge up front and Coutinho among the players supplying them. On 13 April 2014 Manchester City came to Anfield. Raheem Sterling and Martin Skrtel put Liverpool 2–0 up. David Silva pulled one back, and then a Silva shot went in off Glen Johnson. In the 78th minute Vincent Kompany miscued a clearance and Coutinho scored the winner. Liverpool won 3–2, but the title went to City.

## Klopp's first win

Klopp arrived in October 2015. On 31 October Liverpool went to Chelsea and fell behind to a goal from Ramires. Coutinho equalised in first-half stoppage time and scored again in the 74th minute with a deflected shot. Christian Benteke added a third. It was Klopp's first Premier League win as Liverpool manager.

## Two finals

The 2015–16 season took Liverpool to two finals. In the League Cup final against Manchester City on 28 February 2016, Fernandinho scored in the 49th minute. Coutinho equalised with seven minutes left, and the game finished 1–1 after extra time. In the shoot-out City's goalkeeper, Willy Caballero, saved from Lucas Leiva, Coutinho and Adam Lallana, and City won the cup.

In the Europa League, Coutinho scored against Manchester United and Borussia Dortmund on the way to the final.

He started the final against Sevilla in Basel. Sturridge gave Liverpool the lead in the 35th minute, but Kevin Gameiro and Coke, with two, turned it round. Liverpool lost 3–1.

## Fourth place

Coutinho scored 14 goals in all competitions in 2016–17. In May 2017 Klopp played him deeper and more centrally at West Ham. He set up Sturridge for the opening goal and scored twice in a 4–0 win.

Dejan Lovren said afterwards: "Philippe was amazing today, he was the difference I would say." He explained why: "He was taking two players on straight away and then you had space for the long ball, for Sturridge, for Divock."

Liverpool then beat Middlesbrough 3–0. Georginio Wijnaldum opened the scoring, Coutinho scored from a free-kick and Lallana got the third. The win secured fourth place and a place in the qualifying round of the next season's Champions League.

## Summer 2017

Barcelona tried to sign him in the summer of 2017. By Liverpool's own account, the club blocked the move, and he was still a Liverpool player when the window closed.

Klopp later gave his account of that summer. "It is no secret that Philippe has wanted this move to happen since July," he said, adding that Coutinho "was insistent with me, the owners and even his teammates". Klopp said the club had managed to keep him beyond the summer window. He also praised Coutinho for "training hard each day at Melwood after the summer window had closed".

## The last months

The 2017–18 season gave Klopp a new attack. Mohamed Salah had joined, and at times Coutinho played behind Salah, Firmino and Sadio Mané.

On 6 December 2017 Coutinho captained Liverpool against Spartak Moscow in the Champions League. He scored a penalty in the fourth minute and completed his first Liverpool hat-trick in a 7–0 win. Liverpool finished top of Group E.

Four days later, in the matchday programme for the Merseyside derby, he talked about his deeper role. "Playing there is a position where you get to play the ball a great deal," he said. He credited Klopp with improving how he played "when we don't have the ball when the team is under pressure". "He's helped me a lot," he said.

## The move to Barcelona

On 6 January 2018 Liverpool announced that they had agreed his transfer to Barcelona. The move was completed on 8 January, and he signed a five-and-a-half-year contract.

He left with 54 goals in 201 games. He had reached two finals with Liverpool and won no major trophy there. Liverpool won the Champions League in 2019 and the league title in 2020, after he had gone.

## After Liverpool

In 2024 he returned to Vasco da Gama in Brazil. He left the club by mutual agreement on 18 February 2026.

Santos announced his signing on a free transfer on 14 September 2026, on a contract to the end of the year. He is still a Santos player.
