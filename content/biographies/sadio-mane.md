Sadio Mané scored 120 goals in 269 games for Liverpool, 90 of them in 196 league matches. In six seasons he won the Champions League, the Premier League, the FA Cup, the League Cup, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup. The goals were only part of his value. Peter Krawietz, Jürgen Klopp's assistant, rated what he did without the ball just as highly.

## Before Liverpool

Mané was born in Sédhiou, Senegal, on 10 April 1992. He came to European football with Metz, moved to Red Bull Salzburg in 2012 and won the Austrian league and cup double there in 2013–14. Southampton signed him on 1 September 2014.

On 16 May 2015 he scored a hat-trick against Aston Villa in two minutes and 56 seconds, the fastest in Premier League history. On 20 March 2016 he hurt Liverpool directly. Southampton were 2–0 down at St Mary's, and Mané scored twice as they came back to win 3–2.

## The first season

Liverpool signed him on 28 June 2016, before Klopp's first full season. He scored on his competitive debut, a 4–3 win at Arsenal on 14 August. On 19 December he settled the Merseyside derby at Goodison Park with a goal in the 94th minute. Injury ended his season early, but he had already scored 13 league goals. He was voted the club's Player of the Season, and Liverpool finished fourth.

## The front three

He began on the right. When Mohamed Salah arrived in 2017, Mané moved to the left, with Roberto Firmino in the middle. He said during his second season that his job was the same wherever he played: always to be an option, and to be aggressive and direct.

His second season began badly. He was sent off at Manchester City on 9 September 2017 and served a three-match ban. But in the Champions League he scored ten goals, including a hat-trick in the 5–0 win at Porto in February and Liverpool's goal in the 3–1 defeat by Real Madrid in the final in Kyiv.

In 2018–19 he scored 22 league goals, none of them penalties, according to LFChistory. It was his best league season for Liverpool, and he shared the Golden Boot with Salah and Arsenal's Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang.

## Munich and Madrid

In Munich on 13 March 2019 he scored twice. Virgil van Dijk hit a long ball over the top. Mané brought it down, turned past Manuel Neuer, who had come out of his goal, and finished with his left foot. Later he headed in a cross from Salah. Liverpool won 3–1 to reach the quarter-finals, and UEFA named him man of the match.

He did not score in the 4–0 win over Barcelona in the semi-final second leg, but Krawietz later picked him out anyway. "He defended everything and he was dribbling with the ball," Krawietz told the club in 2021. In his view Mané was "the value of two players in one" that night.

In the final against Tottenham in Madrid, his cross into the box hit Moussa Sissoko's arm in the first minute. Salah scored the penalty, and Liverpool won 2–0.

## Champions

In August 2019 he scored both of Liverpool's goals in the 2–2 draw with Chelsea in the Super Cup in Istanbul, which Liverpool won on penalties. On 2 November 2019 his header in stoppage time won the game 2–1 at Aston Villa. Liverpool had been behind until Andy Robertson equalised in the 87th minute. The Club World Cup followed in December, and the league title in June 2020, Liverpool's first in 30 years. Mané scored 18 league goals in that campaign.

Krawietz listed his positioning, readiness and attitude, his pace, his finishing and how quickly he learned. He was not the tallest, Krawietz said, but he was one of the strongest in the air.

## Harder years, and a new position

Injuries across the squad disrupted the 2020–21 title defence, and Mané's league total fell to 11. He still scored both goals in the 2–0 win over Crystal Palace on the final day, which secured third place and a return to the Champions League.

In 2021–22 Klopp moved him into the centre. The FA Cup semi-final against Manchester City at Wembley on 16 April showed both sides of his game: the pressing and the finishing. He closed down Zack Steffen as the goalkeeper dwelt on the ball and stretched to turn it into the net. Just before half-time he volleyed in his second. Liverpool led 3–0 at the break and held on to win 3–2.

He scored 23 goals in all competitions that season and won both domestic cups. Liverpool finished one point behind City in the league and lost the Champions League final 1–0 to Real Madrid in Paris on 28 May 2022. That was his last game for the club. His transfer to Bayern Munich was completed on 22 June.

## Senegal

That season also brought his biggest moment for his country. In the Africa Cup of Nations final against Egypt on 6 February 2022, his early penalty was saved. The game finished 0–0, and in the shoot-out he scored the winning kick. Senegal were African champions for the first time.

He captained Senegal in the next final, against the hosts, Morocco, in Rabat on 18 January 2026, with Kalidou Koulibaly suspended. When his team-mates walked off in protest at a late penalty, he persuaded them to come back, and Senegal won 1–0 after extra time. He was named player of the tournament. In March CAF's appeal board ruled that Senegal had forfeited the match and awarded the title to Morocco. Senegal have appealed to the Court of Arbitration for Sport, which expects to rule at the end of November 2026.

## After Liverpool

At Bayern he scored 12 goals in 38 games and won the Bundesliga and the German Super Cup. A leg injury kept him out of the 2022 World Cup. He joined Al Nassr in August 2023 and won the Saudi Pro League with them in 2025–26. He was on the 30-man shortlist for the 2026 Ballon d'Or and is still at Al Nassr.

At Liverpool he played on the right, on the left and through the middle. Wherever he played, he scored goals and won the ball back.
