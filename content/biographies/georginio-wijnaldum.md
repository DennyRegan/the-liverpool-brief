Georginio Wijnaldum made 237 competitive appearances for Liverpool and scored 22 goals, according to LFChistory. In five seasons he won the Champions League, the Premier League, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup. He played in 37 of the 38 league games in the 2019–20 title season and in all 38 the season after. His best-remembered night came against Barcelona, when he scored twice in just over two minutes.

## Barcelona, 7 May 2019

Liverpool had lost the first leg of the Champions League semi-final 3–0. Divock Origi scored in the 7th minute at Anfield, and Liverpool led 1–0 at half-time. They still needed two goals just to draw level on aggregate. Andy Robertson was injured, and Wijnaldum came on in his place.

In the 54th minute he met Trent Alexander-Arnold's cross with a low drive from 12 yards. In the 56th he headed in Xherdan Shaqiri's cross. The tie was level at 3–3. In the 79th minute Origi scored from Alexander-Arnold's quick corner, and Liverpool went through 4–3 on aggregate. Both of Wijnaldum's goals came in the first 11 minutes after he came on.

## Before Liverpool

Wijnaldum was born in Rotterdam on 11 November 1990. He joined the Sparta Rotterdam academy at the age of six, then moved to Feyenoord, where he made his first-team debut in April 2007. He later joined PSV. He was made captain there in summer 2013 and led the side to the Eredivisie title in 2014–15.

He then moved to Newcastle United. He played in all 38 Premier League games in 2015–16 and scored 11 goals. Four of them came in one match, a 6–2 win over Norwich City. Liverpool signed him from Newcastle on 22 July 2016 for £25m.

## The first season

Wijnaldum made his Liverpool debut on 14 August 2016. At Newcastle he had scored goals from further forward. Under Jürgen Klopp his main job was different: to keep the ball, link the team and support the attack at the right moments.

On the final day of his first season, 21 May 2017, Liverpool played Middlesbrough at Anfield. A win would secure fourth place and a place in the Champions League play-offs. In first-half stoppage time Wijnaldum took Roberto Firmino's pass and scored past Brad Guzan. Philippe Coutinho scored from a free kick in the 51st minute. Five minutes later Wijnaldum nodded the ball down for Adam Lallana to make it 3–0.

In 2020 he looked back on the game. "When we scored the goal it looked like things just fell off our shoulders," he said, as reported by the Liverpool Echo. He did not claim that his goal alone had changed the club's course.

## Different roles in midfield

His job changed from season to season, and sometimes from game to game. In February 2019 the Premier League analyst Adrian Clarke set out the roles he had played in 2018–19. Early in the season he was the main holding midfielder. He then played on the right of a midfield three, or as a box-to-box player in a pair. In each role, Clarke wrote, he was "looking after possession incredibly well".

Klopp described the same range in October 2019. "It is both directions, small spaces, big spaces," he said. The manager also pointed to his heading. "He is not the tallest, but he is good in the air," Klopp said, calling him "a good jumper with good timing". The header against Barcelona had shown that.

## Champions

Liverpool won the European Cup in 2019. The Super Cup and the Club World Cup followed later that year. In 2019–20 Liverpool won the league, and Wijnaldum missed only one of the 38 league games.

One of his goals that season won a tight game. At Sheffield United on 28 September 2019, Liverpool missed three good chances before half-time. At the other end, Adrián saved from Oli McBurnie and Callum Robinson shot past the post.

In the 70th minute Origi's cross was only half-cleared. Wijnaldum volleyed from the edge of the box, and the ball went through Dean Henderson's grasp and over the line. It was the only goal of the game.

## The last season

The 2020–21 season was disrupted by injuries across the squad, and much of it was played without supporters. Wijnaldum played in all 38 league games, 34 of them as a starter, and Liverpool recovered to finish third.

His contract ran out at the end of June 2021. On 10 June Paris Saint-Germain announced that he would join them on a free transfer. His last game for Liverpool was on 23 May 2021. He had played 179 league games for the club and scored 16 league goals. The other six came in the FA Cup and in Europe.

## After Liverpool

He spent one season at Paris Saint-Germain, who then loaned him to Roma for 2022–23. In September 2023 he joined Al-Ettifaq in Saudi Arabia on a three-year contract. It was not renewed when it ran out in summer 2026, a decision Voetbal International put down to the club's finances. In September 2026 he signed a one-year contract with Al-Ittihad, also in the Saudi Pro League, on a free transfer. He is still there, and turns 36 on 11 November.

At Liverpool he played as a holding midfielder, on the right of a three and in a pair. He scored 22 goals in 237 games, and two of them came in just over two minutes against Barcelona.
