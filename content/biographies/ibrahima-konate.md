Ibrahima Konaté made 183 appearances for Liverpool and scored seven goals. In five seasons he won the Premier League, the FA Cup, the League Cup twice and the Community Shield. According to the club, he played 154 matches alongside Virgil van Dijk, more than with any other team-mate, and no France-born player has made more appearances for Liverpool.

## Before Liverpool

Konaté was born in Paris on 25 May 1999. He came through at Paris FC and Sochaux, played senior football for Sochaux in 2016–17 and joined RB Leipzig in 2017. He spent four years in Germany.

Liverpool agreed to sign him on 28 May 2021, three days after his 22nd birthday. The reported fee was £36m, and the move took effect on 1 July. Liverpool had struggled with injuries at centre-back the season before, and Jürgen Klopp mentioned those problems when he explained the signing. He described Konaté as quick, very strong and "dominant in the air". He also said he had "room to improve because of his massive potential".

## Built for the space behind

In a pre-season piece for the Premier League website on 1 August 2021, the analyst Adrian Clarke noted that Klopp's Liverpool had always played a high defensive line. Losing pace at the back, he wrote, had left them open to rapid counter-attacks. Clarke said Konaté could use his acceleration to provide cover, and picked out his ability to bring the ball out of defence as one of his standout qualities.

His competitive debut came at Anfield on 18 September 2021, alongside Van Dijk, in a 3–0 win over Crystal Palace. Sadio Mané, Mohamed Salah and Naby Keïta scored. It was not a comfortable clean sheet. Alisson had to claw a shot from Wilfried Zaha off the post early on, and later smothered the ball at Odsonne Édouard's feet, according to the club's match report.

## The first season

On 27 February 2022 Liverpool played Chelsea in the League Cup final at Wembley. Konaté came off the bench, and in the shoot-out he was Liverpool's tenth taker. He scored, and Liverpool won it 11–10.

In April he scored in three consecutive appearances. The first was his first goal for the club, in the Champions League quarter-final first leg at Benfica on 5 April. He headed in Andy Robertson's corner after 17 minutes, and Liverpool won 3–1. The same match showed how much he still had to learn. When Rafa Silva crossed from the right, Konaté tried to clear and missed the ball entirely, and Darwin Núñez scored behind him. UEFA's technical analysis recorded both moments.

He scored again in the second leg at Anfield on 13 April, after 21 minutes. Three days later he played in the FA Cup semi-final against Manchester City at Wembley. In the ninth minute he rose above Nathan Aké to meet another Robertson corner and opened the scoring. Mané added two more before half-time, and Liverpool held on to win 3–2.

On 14 May he started the FA Cup final against Chelsea, again alongside Van Dijk. Van Dijk went off at the start of extra time, but Konaté played the full 120 minutes. The game finished 0–0, and Liverpool won the shoot-out 6–5.

Klopp picked him for the Champions League final against Real Madrid at the Stade de France on 28 May. Vinícius Júnior scored the only goal in the 59th minute. It was the first time Konaté had lost with Liverpool. According to the club, he had gone unbeaten in his first 28 appearances: 20 wins, three shoot-out wins and five draws. LFChistory lists 29 appearances for him that season, so the final was his 29th game.

He had been signed to add depth at centre-back. By the end of his first season he was starting a Champions League final.

## Injuries and a new role

The next season started badly. He was injured in a pre-season friendly against Strasbourg on 31 July 2022 and missed the start of the campaign, including the opening game at Fulham. He made 24 appearances in 2022–23.

In spring 2023 Klopp changed the team's shape. Trent Alexander-Arnold moved into midfield from right-back when Liverpool had the ball, which left Konaté with more of the right side to cover. He told Sky Sports in May 2023: "I have to cover more ground but we can see the tactical benefits from an attacking point of view." He added: "It's true that I have a lot more work to do which means I'm more tired after a game now!"

On 25 February 2024 he started the League Cup final against Chelsea. He was replaced by Jarell Quansah after 106 minutes, and Van Dijk headed the winner in the 118th. Liverpool won 1–0 after extra time. It was the last trophy Klopp won before he left that summer.

## Champions under Slot

Konaté's best years for availability came after Klopp. Under Arne Slot he made 42 appearances in 2024–25, 31 of them in the league, as Liverpool won the Premier League. He made 51 appearances in 2025–26. Those two seasons account for 93 of his 183 games for Liverpool.

## Leaving for Madrid

His contract ran to the end of June 2026, and talks over a new one broke down. Liverpool announced on 31 May that he would leave, and he left on 30 June. He joined Real Madrid on a free transfer, on a contract to 2030.

At Liverpool he won five trophies and scored seven goals, three of them in one fortnight in April 2022. He was signed for his pace behind a high line, and most of his games were played next to Van Dijk.
