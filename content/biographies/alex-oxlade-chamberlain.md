Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain made 146 appearances for Liverpool and scored 18 goals, according to LFChistory. In six seasons he won the Premier League, the Champions League, the UEFA Super Cup, the Club World Cup, the FA Cup and the League Cup. He gave Jürgen Klopp's midfield pace and a goal threat from distance. He scored in two wins over Manchester City in his first season, then a serious knee injury kept him out for a year. He played most in 2019–20, when Liverpool won the league.

## Before Liverpool

Oxlade-Chamberlain was born in Portsmouth on 15 August 1993. He joined Southampton's academy at the age of seven and moved to Arsenal on 8 August 2011. He spent six seasons there.

Liverpool completed his signing on 31 August 2017. LFChistory gives the fee as £35m. Klopp said he had followed him since September 2014, when he saw him come on as a substitute for Arsenal against Klopp's Borussia Dortmund. Explaining the signing, Klopp spoke about his pace, his skills and his attitude, and said he was willing to take risks to try to make positive things happen.

## Settling in

His first Liverpool appearance came as a substitute in the 5–0 defeat at Manchester City on 9 September 2017. It took him time to settle.

By the return league game at Anfield on 14 January 2018, he was central to the win. In the ninth minute he got past a challenge from Fernandinho, ran on and scored with a low right-foot shot from outside the box. He then set up Roberto Firmino's goal just before the hour. Liverpool won 4–3. It was City's first league defeat of the season.

His role was to add another runner from midfield. Mohamed Salah, Sadio Mané and Firmino occupied defenders, and Oxlade-Chamberlain could carry the ball through the space behind them, pass it forward or keep running beyond the forwards. He could also shoot from outside the box.

On 4 April 2018 he did that against City again. In the Champions League quarter-final first leg at Anfield, he scored Liverpool's second goal after about 20 minutes with a long-range right-foot drive. Salah had opened the scoring and Mané added a third. Liverpool won 3–0.

Afterwards Klopp rejected the idea that Liverpool had simply put right what had gone wrong at Arsenal. He put the improvement down to the player's own "potential" and "quality", and said Oxlade-Chamberlain had always taken responsibility himself. He said Oxlade-Chamberlain loved Arsène Wenger and had never blamed him, and put the change down to different circumstances. The difference now, Klopp said, was that he used his abilities "in the right moment, the right spaces".

## A year out

On 24 April 2018 Liverpool played Roma in the first leg of the Champions League semi-final. After 18 minutes Oxlade-Chamberlain went down while trying to make a challenge and had to go off. Liverpool won the game 5–2, but he had damaged his knee ligaments. The club said he would miss the rest of the season and England's World Cup. He was not available for the final against Real Madrid in Kyiv, which Liverpool lost.

He played his first under-23 game back on 8 March 2019. His next senior appearance came on 26 April 2019, when he replaced Daniel Sturridge after 72 minutes of a 5–0 win over Huddersfield Town. It was a year and two days after the injury. Salah set him up soon after he came on, but the Huddersfield goalkeeper, Jonas Lössl, saved his shot with his feet.

He made two appearances in 2018–19. On 1 June 2019 he was an unused substitute in Madrid, where Liverpool beat Tottenham 2–0 to win the Champions League.

## The title season

He played much more in 2019–20. At Genk on 23 October 2019 he scored twice in a 4–1 Champions League win. The first came in the second minute, a deflected shot from about 25 yards. The second came just before the hour. He met Firmino's pass with the outside of his right boot from about 20 yards, and the ball went in off the crossbar.

On 29 January 2020 he scored the second goal in a 2–0 win at West Ham, finishing a counter-attack from Salah's pass.

He made 43 appearances that season, the most he managed in any season for Liverpool. Thirty of them were in the league, as Liverpool won their first championship for 30 years. He scored eight goals in all competitions: four in the league, one in the League Cup and three in Europe.

## The last three seasons

A knee injury in pre-season kept him out until December 2020. He made 29 appearances in 2021–22, 17 of them in the league, as Liverpool won the FA Cup and the League Cup. A hamstring injury in pre-season then kept him out of the start of 2022–23. LFChistory says he missed the first two months; the club said three.

His last appearance for Liverpool came on 1 April 2023. His contract ran out on 30 June 2023 and he left the club.

## After Liverpool

He joined Beşiktaş in Turkey in August 2023 on a three-year contract. Beşiktaş ended the deal on 27 August 2025. He was then a free agent and trained at Arsenal.

Celtic signed him on 7 February 2026 on a contract to the end of the season. On his debut, at home to Livingston on 11 February, he came off the bench and scored a stoppage-time winner in a 2–1 win. On 4 July 2026 Celtic extended his contract by a year, to summer 2027. He is still a Celtic player.
