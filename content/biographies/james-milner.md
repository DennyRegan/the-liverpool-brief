James Milner made 332 appearances for Liverpool and scored 26 goals. He also set up 45, according to LFChistory. In eight seasons he won the Champions League, the Premier League, the FA Cup, the League Cup, the UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup. He played in midfield and at full-back, as a starter and as a substitute.

## Before Liverpool

Milner made his Premier League debut for Leeds United as a substitute against West Ham on 10 November 2002. He was 16 years and 309 days old. A loan at Swindon Town followed in 2003. He then moved to Newcastle United, spent 2005–06 on loan at Aston Villa and signed for Villa permanently in 2008. At Manchester City he won the league twice, in 2011–12 and 2013–14.

## Joining before Klopp

Milner joined Liverpool on a free transfer from City on 1 July 2015, aged 29. Brendan Rodgers brought him in and made him vice-captain to Jordan Henderson. Three months later Rodgers was gone and Jürgen Klopp was manager.

Milner started Klopp's first match, against Tottenham in October 2015. In a Sky Sports interview in July 2020 he recalled "the tempo and the intensity in that first game at Spurs", and said the consistency came gradually after that.

## Dortmund

In April 2016 Liverpool played the Europa League quarter-final second leg against Borussia Dortmund. The score was 3–3 going into stoppage time, and Liverpool needed a goal to go through.

Liverpool had a free-kick near the halfway line. Milner played it short to Daniel Sturridge, got the ball back and crossed for Dejan Lovren to head in, in the 91st minute. Liverpool won 4–3 on the night and 5–4 on aggregate.

Afterwards Milner told the club that "everyone wants to get it forward and get it into the box", and that his job was to "make the right decisions and keep calm".

## Left-back

In 2016–17 Klopp used him mostly at left-back. He made 36 league appearances, 40 in all competitions, and Liverpool finished fourth to return to the Champions League.

It was not his usual position. Sky Sports later described it as "not an enjoyable role at the time". Milner's own explanation was simpler: "It was what the manager needed at the time." He set out to learn the role.

## The assists record

Back in midfield in 2017–18, he set up goals regularly in Europe. In the 5–2 semi-final first-leg win over Roma on 24 April 2018, Roberto Firmino scored from his corner. It was Milner's ninth assist of that Champions League season. UEFA reported it as a record since the format introduced in 2003–04, passing the eight set by Wayne Rooney in 2013–14 and Neymar in 2016–17.

Liverpool lost the final to Real Madrid in Kyiv.

## Barcelona and Madrid

A year later Liverpool were back in the semi-finals, 3–0 down to Barcelona after the first leg. At Anfield Divock Origi scored early, but at half-time Liverpool still needed two more goals to draw level on aggregate. Andy Robertson was injured, and Georginio Wijnaldum replaced him. Milner, according to LFChistory, went to left-back for the second half.

Wijnaldum scored in the 54th and 56th minutes. Origi scored the fourth in the 79th. Liverpool won 4–0 and reached the final.

In Madrid on 1 June 2019 Milner came on in the 62nd minute of the 2–0 win over Tottenham. It was Liverpool's sixth European Cup. The UEFA Super Cup and the Club World Cup followed later that year.

## The title season

During the 2019–20 title campaign, Leicester came to Anfield on 5 October 2019. Milner's pass set up Sadio Mané's goal in the 40th minute. James Maddison equalised in the 80th.

In stoppage time Marc Albrighton fouled Mané, and the penalty was given after a VAR check. Milner took it and scored in the 95th minute to win the game 2–1. He told the club afterwards that "there was a bit of a wait", and that he tried to "concentrate on what was going to happen".

Liverpool won the league in 2020, their first title in 30 years.

In 2020–21 he became the oldest player to reach 250 appearances for Liverpool, according to the club.

## The Wembley shootouts

In 2022 Liverpool played Chelsea in two cup finals at Wembley, and both went to penalties. Milner came off the bench in each and took Liverpool's first kick both times.

In the League Cup final on 27 February he came on in the 80th minute and scored in the shootout, which Liverpool won 11–10. In the FA Cup final on 14 May he came on in the 74th minute. Near the end of normal time his cross found Robertson, who hit the post from close range. In the shootout Milner scored Liverpool's first penalty, and Liverpool won 6–5.

He also came on as a substitute in the 3–1 win over Manchester City in the Community Shield on 30 July 2022.

## Leaving

Milner played his last game for Liverpool on 28 May 2023 and left when his contract ended on 30 June. He joined Brighton & Hove Albion. Looking back on his six major trophies, the club's profile of Milner quotes Klopp: "Without Millie, it wouldn't have been possible."

At Brighton he passed Gareth Barry's Premier League appearance record with his 654th game in February 2026. He finished with 658. On 1 June 2026, aged 40, he announced his retirement after 24 Premier League seasons. No coaching or media role has been confirmed.
