Luis Suárez scored 82 goals in 133 competitive appearances for Liverpool between January 2011 and the summer of 2014, according to LFChistory. He won the League Cup in 2012. In his last season he scored 31 Premier League goals, none of them penalties, and Liverpool finished two points behind the champions, Manchester City. He was also banned for eight matches after an FA commission found he had racially abused Patrice Evra, and for ten matches for biting Branislav Ivanović.

## Uruguay and the Netherlands

Suárez was born in Salto, Uruguay, on 24 January 1987 and began his senior career with Nacional in Montevideo. Groningen signed him in 2006, and he scored 10 goals in 29 league games in his first Dutch season. In August 2007 Ajax agreed a €7.5m fee for him, aged 20. The fee followed a Dutch FA ruling that his Groningen contract was binding.

At Ajax he was the Eredivisie's top scorer with 35 goals in 2009–10. In November 2010 he was banned for seven matches for biting PSV's Otman Bakkal.

At the 2010 World Cup he scored three goals as Uruguay reached the semi-finals. In the quarter-final against Ghana he was sent off for handling on the goal line. Asamoah Gyan missed the penalty and Uruguay won the shoot-out.

## Deadline day

Kenny Dalglish returned as Liverpool manager on 8 January 2011. On 31 January, the day Fernando Torres left for Chelsea and Andy Carroll arrived from Newcastle, Suárez joined from Ajax in a deal worth up to £22.8m. He took the No. 7 shirt.

His debut came against Stoke City at Anfield on 2 February. He came on for Fabio Aurelio after 63 minutes with Liverpool 1–0 up, and 16 minutes later rounded the goalkeeper and scored at the Kop end. Liverpool won 2–0.

On 6 March Liverpool beat Manchester United 3–1 at Anfield. Dirk Kuyt scored all three, and Suárez was involved in each of them. He finished the half-season with four goals in 13 league appearances.

That summer he won the Copa América with Uruguay, scoring in the final, and was named the tournament's best player.

## The League Cup

On 26 October 2011 Liverpool were behind at Stoke in the League Cup. Suárez equalised with a curling shot and won the tie 2–1 with a late header. Liverpool reached the final, against Cardiff City on 26 February 2012. It finished 2–2, with goals from Martin Škrtel and Kuyt, and Liverpool won on penalties, with Suárez in the side. It was the club's first major trophy since the 2006 FA Cup and his only major honour at Liverpool.

On 13 March he set up two of Steven Gerrard's three goals in a 3–0 derby win at Anfield. In the FA Cup semi-final against Everton on 14 April he equalised, and Carroll headed the winner in the 87th minute. Liverpool lost the final to Chelsea. Suárez finished the season with 17 goals in 39 games.

## The Evra case

The incident with Evra came during a 1–1 draw with Manchester United at Anfield on 15 October 2011. On 20 December an independent FA regulatory commission found that Suárez had used insulting words towards Evra that "included a reference to Mr Evra's colour". He was banned for eight matches and fined £40,000.

In its written reasons the commission said Evra was a credible witness and that Suárez's evidence was unreliable on matters of critical importance. Expert witnesses said the language would be considered racially offensive. The FA did not allege, and the commission did not find, that Suárez was a racist. The charge concerned the words he used.

On 3 January 2012 Liverpool said they would not appeal, while disputing the findings. Suárez kept up his denial. On 4 January he apologised for any offence caused, while still insisting that he had never used the word in a derogatory way.

On 11 February, at Old Trafford, he did not shake Evra's hand before the game. He apologised the next day. Ian Ayre, the club's managing director, said it had been made clear to Suárez that his behaviour was not acceptable.

## Rodgers

Brendan Rodgers became manager in 2012, and Suárez signed a new contract in August. On 30 August his 88th-minute goal from a tight angle against Hearts took Liverpool through a Europa League qualifier 2–1 on aggregate.

Daniel Sturridge and Philippe Coutinho joined in January 2013. Suárez scored 30 goals in 44 games that season, 23 of them in the league. He finished second to Gareth Bale in the PFA Players' Player of the Year vote and was named in the PFA Team of the Year. Liverpool finished seventh.

## Ivanović and the summer of 2013

On 21 April 2013, in a 2–2 draw with Chelsea at Anfield, Suárez bit Ivanović on the arm. The officials did not see it. Suárez stayed on and headed the equaliser in the seventh minute of stoppage time. He apologised that evening, and Rodgers and Ayre both criticised his behaviour.

On 24 April an FA commission banned him for ten matches. He had admitted violent conduct but argued that the standard three-match ban was enough. The commission called three matches clearly insufficient. He did not appeal. The ban covered the last four games of 2012–13 and the first six of 2013–14, five in the Premier League and one in the League Cup.

That summer Liverpool rejected a bid of £40,000,001 from Arsenal, and Rodgers made Suárez train apart from the first team for a time. He returned to competitive football in the League Cup at Manchester United on 25 September, and on 20 December he signed a new long-term contract.

## 2013–14

Suárez scored 31 Premier League goals in 33 appearances in 2013–14. That equalled the record for a 38-game season, then shared by Alan Shearer and Cristiano Ronaldo, even though he missed the first five league games.

On 4 December Liverpool beat Norwich City 5–1 at Anfield. Suárez scored four: a long-range first-time strike, a close-range volley, a flick over Leroy Fer followed by a half-volley, and a free-kick. He also set up Raheem Sterling's goal. He became the first player to score three Premier League hat-tricks against one club.

On 15 December, with Gerrard out, he captained Liverpool in a 5–0 win at Tottenham and scored twice. At Cardiff on 22 March Liverpool came from behind twice to win 6–3. Suárez scored three and Sturridge one, and each set up a goal for the other.

An 11-match winning run in the league began with a 5–1 win over Arsenal and included a 3–0 win at Old Trafford and a 3–2 home win over Manchester City. Then Chelsea won 2–0 at Anfield, and Liverpool drew 3–3 at Crystal Palace after leading 3–0. City finished on 86 points, Liverpool on 84.

Suárez won the Golden Boot, the PFA Players' Player of the Year, the Football Writers' Footballer of the Year and the Premier League Player of the Season. He shared the European Golden Shoe with Ronaldo, on 31 goals each.

## Barcelona

At the 2014 World Cup, on 24 June, Suárez bit Italy's Giorgio Chiellini. FIFA banned him for nine international matches and for four months from all football activity. In August the Court of Arbitration for Sport upheld the bans but allowed him to train.

On 11 July Liverpool announced an agreement to sell him to Barcelona for a fee widely reported at £75m. Rodgers said the club had agreed to his wish with great reluctance.

At Barcelona he won the Champions League in 2015 and scored in the 3–1 final win over Juventus in Berlin. He won four Spanish titles with Barcelona and another with Atlético Madrid in 2020–21. He won 143 caps for Uruguay and scored a national record 69 goals, then retired from international football in 2024.
