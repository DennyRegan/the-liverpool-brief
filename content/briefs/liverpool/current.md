  - headline: "FSG agree sale of 30% Liverpool stake to Bhatia-led consortium"
    summary: "Fenway Sports Group have entered a definitive agreement to sell around 30% of Liverpool to 1892 Holdings, a consortium led by Amit Bhatia and funded by Bhatia and the Mittal Family Trusts, K5 Sports with Jeff Bezos as lead investor, and EE Capital, the family office of Eduardo and Elaine Saverin. The stake is reported at around £1.65bn, with the club's valuation placed by different outlets between roughly $6bn and $7.5bn. FSG remain the majority owner and retain operational control, and Bhatia becomes vice-chairman. Reports add that the consortium holds an option to move to a majority stake within 12 months at a valuation of around $8bn, though FSG say the deal does not commit either side to that. The transaction is subject to regulatory approval."

    summary: "Liverpool remain in talks with PSG over Bradley Barcola, who is understood to be keen on the move, but the fee remains unresolved. PSG have meanwhile agreed a €50m deal with Barcelona for Ferran Torres, who has been cleared to travel for a medical, and reached a verbal agreement with Ajax worth around €55m for Mika Godts, with documents yet to be exchanged.
