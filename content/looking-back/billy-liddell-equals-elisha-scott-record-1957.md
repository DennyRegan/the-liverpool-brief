Before a ball was kicked at Anfield on Saturday 9 November 1957, every Notts County player walked to the centre of the pitch to shake hands with Liverpool's captain. Billy Liddell was playing his 430th league game for the club, drawing level with Elisha Scott, Liverpool's goalkeeper from 1912 to 1934.

At the time Scott's total was thought to be 429, so the afternoon was treated as the day Liddell broke the record. Later counting showed he had only equalled it. Liddell was said to be self-conscious about the fuss. Notts County's manager that day was Tommy Lawton, a friend of his.

The game itself went Liverpool's way. They were top of the Second Division, and 39,735 watched them win 4–0. Jimmy Melia scored four minutes before half-time, and Alan A'Court added a second on 49 minutes. Two minutes later Liddell made it 3–0 himself, from Melia's pass. Tony Rowley completed the scoring seven minutes from time.
