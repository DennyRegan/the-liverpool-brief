Liverpool met Manchester City at Anfield on Sunday 10 November 2019 and came out of it eight points clear at the top of the Premier League.

The game was settled early. Fabinho scored in the sixth minute, and seven minutes later Mohamed Salah headed a second. Sadio Mané made it 3–0 six minutes after half-time. Bernardo Silva pulled one back for City on 78 minutes, too late to change anything. The crowd was 53,324 and the referee was Michael Oliver.

The result left Liverpool with 34 points from 12 games: eleven wins and a draw. According to 11v11, only Manchester United in 1993–94 had held a bigger lead at that stage of a Premier League season.

Sky Sports' verdict the next day was that the win showed this "could be their year". It was. Liverpool went on to win the 2019–20 Premier League, the club's first league title in 30 years.
