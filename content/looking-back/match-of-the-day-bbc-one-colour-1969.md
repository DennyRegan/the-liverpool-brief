Saturday 15 November 1969 was the day BBC One began broadcasting in colour. ITV started its colour service on the same day. Match of the Day was one of the first programmes in the new schedule, and its featured game was Liverpool against West Ham United at Anfield.

It was not quite the programme's first time in colour. One edition had gone out in colour on 2 November 1968, during a week when Match of the Day moved to BBC2. But this was the first on BBC One.

Not everyone could see it. According to British Online Archives, outdated transmitters meant only half of households could receive the new service at first.

The 39,668 inside Anfield saw Liverpool win 2–0 in the First Division. Chris Lawler opened the scoring in the first half, and Bobby Graham made it 2–0 on the hour. It was also a debut for Steve Peplow.
