## Summary

The draft is reliable overall. Dates, totals, the 2019 final, the 2021–22 figures and the injury timeline all hold up. I found one clear factual error and two smaller problems:

1. **The Leeds goal (opening paragraph):** "Matip lifted his finish over Illan Meslier" is wrong. Sky Sports says he "smashed" it past Meslier and Liverpool's own report says he "swept it in". Nothing describes a lifted finish or a chip.
2. **2018–19 timeline:** the draft says his chance "developed after he returned to fitness around the turn of the year". That is misleading. He was already starting before he broke his collarbone against Napoli in December 2018, and he was out for up to six weeks after it.
3. **Klopp's reasons:** "technical quality" is not in Klopp's recorded words. Klopp said "experienced", "physically strong" and "quick". The ball-playing point comes from the club's profile, not from Klopp.

There are no direct quotations in the draft, and no Heysel or Hillsborough material.

## Corrections

1. **Para 1:** "Salah returned it, and Matip lifted his finish over Illan Meslier." He did not lift it. Sky Sports says he "smashed the finish past Illan Meslier" and Liverpool's report says he "swept it in". Neutral wording such as "finished past Illan Meslier" is safe.
2. **Para "A place beside Van Dijk":** "His opportunity in the following campaign developed after he returned to fitness around the turn of the year." This is misleading. He was already in the team before fracturing his collarbone against Napoli on 11 December 2018, and was then out for up to six weeks. The return date is not stated in my sources; "early 2019" is the safe wording.
3. **Para "A familiar opponent":** "the attraction was a combination of experience, physical ability and technical quality". Klopp's recorded words cover experience ("very young but experienced") and physical qualities ("physically strong", "quick"). "Technical quality" is not in the interview. Either drop it or credit the composure-on-the-ball point to the club's signing profile ("comfortable and composed with the ball at his feet").
