# Fact-check: Jürgen Klopp (Liverpool manager 2015–2024)

Draft checked: `/home/claude/bios/jurgen-klopp.md`
Checked: 6 November 2026. The earlier AI research record and audit were used only as pointers to sources. Every verdict below rests on pages opened in this check.

## Summary

The draft is reliable. I found **no factual errors** in the body. Every date, score, scorer, venue and total I checked is correct, and the trophy count (seven major honours plus the 2022 Community Shield) is consistent. The draft has no direct quotes. The problems are elsewhere:

1. **It is out of date on Klopp's current status.** It stops in May 2024 and never mentions his Red Bull role (from January 2025) or his appointment as **Germany head coach** (announced 24 July 2026, in post since 15 August 2026). A biography published now without either will read as stale.
2. A few claims rest on a single source: the 1,035 goals, Liverpool being tenth when he arrived, and the UEFA apology wording. All are probably right, but each is single-sourced in this check.
3. Outside the scope of fact-checking, but it will block publishing: the frontmatter does not match the site's current `BiographySchema` (see the note at the end).

## Unverifiable claims

None that need cutting. Two notes on precision:
- "Klopp said afterwards that they all had to learn to react better, and that it started with him." His words were "We all have to learn to react better" and "It's all about us, it's all about me" (LFC reaction page). "It started with him" goes slightly beyond what he said. Suggested wording: "...and that this included him."
- The excerpt says "left nine years later" and the body says "nine years at Anfield". October 2015 to May 2024 is eight years and seven months. Use "almost nine years" in both places.
