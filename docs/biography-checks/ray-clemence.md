## Facts

Every claim in the edited biography is either in the fact-check figure list or I checked it again today. The editor introduced no errors.

The items I was asked to check:
- **Kop ovation quote:** "It's probably the most emotional I have ever been at a football ground." This matches LFChistory (players/282) word for word, and the fact-check had a second source (LEP).
- **Occasion of the quote:** LFChistory says he played the first half at the Anfield Road end and ran to the Kop goal at the start of the second half, where he got a standing ovation. LFC.com says the ovation came on his first return to Anfield "when he came out for the second 45 minutes", that Liverpool won 3–1 and that they won the title "with a game to spare". LFChistory game 1279 gives the date, 15 May 1982, and the half-time score, 0–1.
- **"A win would secure the league title...":** this is supported. LFChistory says Liverpool were "needing a win to secure" the championship. The fact-check's concern about "needed victory" no longer applies.
- **"Long kick-out":** this is the club's own wording (liverpoolfc.com, Shankly farewell article).
- **Liverpool career:** LFChistory confirms the signing from Scunthorpe (12 June 1967, £18,000), the debut (25 September 1968 v Swansea in the League Cup), the league debut at Forest (last day of January 1970), the 336 consecutive games (9 September 1972 to 4 March 1978), the 323 clean sheets and the 665 appearances. The biography credits LFChistory for the 336 and the 323, as it should, because LFChistory is the only source for them.
- **Matches and honours:** the fact-check confirmed these with two or more sources each: 16 goals conceded in 1978–79, the five titles, three European Cups, the 1973 and 1976 UEFA Cups, the 1974 FA Cup and the 1981 League Cup, the Heynckes penalty save in 1973 and the Stielike save in 1977 at 1–1.
- **Tottenham:** LFChistory confirms he left on 15 August 1981. The £300,000 fee comes from the fact-check, but the fee isn't in the biography. The 330 appearances come from Tottenham's own record and Sky. The 1982 FA Cup and his time as an injured, unused substitute in the 1984 UEFA Cup final are confirmed.
- **England and later career:** 61 caps between 1972 and 1983, England coaching staff from 1996 and retirement from the FA in 2013 are confirmed by the FA and Sky.
- **Death:** 15 November 2020, aged 72, is confirmed by the FA, Sky and LFChistory. The illness is not named.
- **historicalPeriod:** "1967–1981", his Liverpool years, is correct.
