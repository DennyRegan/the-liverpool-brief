## Deliberate exclusions and review guidance

- No quotes, invented dialogue, private motive, locker-room scene or attribution of thoughts.
- Consecutive-appearance totals are omitted: sources disagree between 336 and 337. The league availability measure is explicit and independently reviewable.
- Injury cause at retirement is omitted because club and PFA summaries disagree; retirement year is secure.
- The Guardian was consulted only for the short shutout statistic. Independent review also verified 28 league clean sheets in LFChistory, so the final figure need not depend on the restricted source. Do not expand Guardian-derived material.
- J contains inaccurate ages in one recollection; those ages are not used. Its quoted goalkeeper and full-back recollections support only the specified playing characteristics and defensive personnel.
- Both disaster topics excluded. No medical advice or cause-of-death assertion.
- The argument that continuity helped Paisley is editorial interpretation from documented selection and changing final teams. It should not be converted into an invented quotation or a claim that he never made errors.
