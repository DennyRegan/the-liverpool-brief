## 1. Summary

The draft is mostly reliable. All the main statistics agree with LFChistory, and most are confirmed by a second source: 297 appearances and 158 goals, 216 league games and 118 league goals, and every season total. The Golden Boots, the youngest-hat-trick record, the 100-goal record, the 2001 cup finals, the Super Cup, the Ballon d'Or, the Leverkusen tie, the 2003 League Cup final and the Real Madrid deal (about £8m plus Núñez) all check out. The real problems are the minutes of his Wimbledon debut, one unqualified "youngest goalscorer" line, and two remembered-quote passages that could not be found in Owen's own words. Separately, the file does not fit the site's format (see the note at the end).

Errors and issues:
1. **Wimbledon debut minutes (line 24).** The draft says he came on in the 58th minute and scored 16 minutes later. No source gives the minute he came on. Sources disagree on when he scored: LFChistory says 58', while the Irish Times match report from the time and Sports Mole say the 74th minute ("16 minutes from time"). The draft looks like a blend of the two. Recommended: remove the minutes.
2. **"The club had a new youngest goalscorer" (line 24)** is true for 1997 but needs "at the time". liverpoolfc.com says "the youngest goalscorer in Reds history at the time". The record has since been broken by Ben Woodburn (2016).
3. **"Owen later identified that injury as a pivotal event in his career, recalling how it had affected his ability to play as freely as before" (line 36).** No source found where Owen says this about the Leeds injury. The Premier League "Inside Matters" piece says he did not grasp how serious it was at the time. His remarks about no longer playing instinctively are general, not tied to Leeds. Soften or cut.
4. **"Owen remembered the equalising finish as instinctive" (line 48)** could not be verified because the cited liverpoolfc.com "Treble memories" page would not load. The other half of that sentence is confirmed: he would relive that day above any other.
5. Minor: "a run into space followed by a controlled finish" (line 18) and "carry the ball at pace" (line 48) are fine. Sources describe Berger's long ball over the top, Owen holding off Dixon and Adams, and a left-foot shot across Seaman.

No other factual errors found.

- **Retired player.** He announced on 19 March 2013 that he would retire at the end of 2012–13 at Stoke City (FourFourTwo).
- **Now:** a football pundit and co-commentator, still in the media in 2026 (Daily Maverick, January 2026; LADbible, June 2026). One weak source (extratimetalk.com, January 2026) names Premier League Productions as his main TV employer. He also owns and breeds racehorses through Manor House Stables in Cheshire, co-owned with Andrew Black. The draft makes no claim about his current role. If one is added, use the generic wording "now a broadcaster and racehorse owner" unless a stronger source names the employer.
- Whether he is still LFC international ambassador (appointed April 2016) was not checked, and the draft does not say he is.
