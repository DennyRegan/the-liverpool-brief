## 1. Summary

The draft is very reliable on facts. Birth date, the free transfer at 35, debut, the Arsenal red card, first goal, the League Cup shootout, every detail of the Goodison free-kick, the Barcelona penalty, the five-match scoring run, both 2001 finals, Charlton, the Charity Shield, the Super Cup, the season totals (49/7 and 38/2, 87/9 overall), the last appearance and the 2015 coaching spell all check out against LFChistory and at least one other source. The draft has **no quotations in quotation marks**: every view is paraphrased, and the paraphrases mostly match what he and Gerrard said. The errors are one wrong figure (Coventry league goals), loose wording in places, and a big gap at the end: the draft stops at 2015 and leaves out his three years as Gerrard's assistant at Rangers and his year at Aston Villa (2018–2022). The front matter would also fail the current `BiographySchema` (see the format note at the end).

Errors and weak points:
1. "he scored 13 league goals" for Coventry in 1999–2000: **wrong**. 11 league goals (38 apps); 13 is his total in all competitions. Player of the season is correct.
2. The draft ends with the 2015 ambassador role and leaves out Rangers (assistant to Gerrard, June 2018–November 2021, including the 2020–21 Scottish title) and Aston Villa (assistant to Gerrard, November 2021–October 2022). This is a material omission, and as written the ending implies the ambassador role was his last.
3. "Subsequent coaching work included Leeds": misleading. He was Leeds **manager** (January–December 2008), not a coach. At Middlesbrough he was first-team coach (from May 2010).
4. "Houllier selected a younger midfield" for the FA Cup final: unsourced. The sources say only that Houllier did not start him and that it was a very hot day against a physical Arsenal side. Reword (C3).
5. "In explaining the move at the time, he described the exchange as reciprocal": the 2000 interview (Daily Post, 26 May 2000) is one-way (he would help the youngsters). The two-way point comes from his later LFChistory interview. Reword (C2).
6. "McAllister had recently taken a similar free-kick, which Sami Hyypiä had headed narrowly wide": correct, but it was **earlier in the same match**, not "recently". Small clarification (C4).
7. "Patrick Berger": the player's name is spelled **Patrik** Berger.
8. Scotland caps (57 caps, 5 goals) and his MBE are not in the draft. Optional; not errors.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

- Alive, aged 61 (born 25 December 1964, Motherwell).
- Last confirmed post: assistant manager to Steven Gerrard at Aston Villa, from November 2021 until Gerrard was sacked in October 2022 (LFChistory profile; Aston Villa backroom-staff announcement, 13 November 2021). Before that, Gerrard's assistant at Rangers from June 2018, three seasons, including the 2020–21 Scottish Premiership title.
- **No current club or coaching role found.** In July 2026 Sports Mole described him only as a "former Liverpool midfielder" in an interview piece. He works as an occasional media voice. Safe wording: "He later worked as Steven Gerrard's assistant at Rangers, where they won the 2020–21 Scottish title, and at Aston Villa." Do not give him a current job.
