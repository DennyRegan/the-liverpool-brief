## Conflicts and decisions

- **Fee:** LFChistory, liverpoolfc.com (legacy and Boot Room) give £100,000; Scottish Football Museum and National Football Museum give £110,000. Both stated in the piece, attributed.
- **Captaincy year:** Club pages say 1985 (replacing Neal); LFChistory gives no year, only "new club captain" lifting the 1986 FA Cup. Not a true conflict; used 1985 per the club.
- **League Cups:** SFM says three; LFChistory, the club and NFM say four (1981–84). Used four.
- **Scotland cap span:** SFM says caps "between 1982 and 1987"; LFChistory gives a debut of 19 May 1979 v Wales and London Hearts lists all 26 caps from 1979. Used 1979–1987.
- **1984 opening goal minute:** Boot Room says 15th; LFChistory (cited in our Phil Neal piece) 14th. Minute left out.
- **1978 final position:** NFM says he partnered Thompson; the LFChistory profile summary suggested a different position. Position left out.
- **Retirement date:** No source opened gives an exact date. Piece says only "shortly after Dalglish stood down as manager in March 1991" (LFChistory). Hansen's own years at the club are given as 1977–1990 by liverpoolfc.com; historicalPeriod uses 1977–1991 as in the brief, as he remained a registered player until 1991 (LFChistory lists contract expiry 1 March 1991 and retirement after March 1991).
- **Junior sports:** Boot Room adds basketball; only LFChistory's three used.
- Hillsborough is mentioned on LFC-P in connection with 1988–89; deliberately left out per brief.
- Left out (not confirmed in an opened source): brother John's 1971 League Cup final date, first goal details, exact date of the Goodison 5–0.

The captaincy date (1985) is attributed to the club because LFChistory frames it around 1986.
