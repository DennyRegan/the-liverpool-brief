## Conflicts and decisions

- **When he lost the captaincy.** P says "later in 1981"; W says start of 1981-82 (tagged citation needed); I1/I3 say early the next year / around the turn of the year. G82 and TIA pin it to the days after 26 December 1981, with Souness's first game as outright captain on 2 January 1982. Used G82/TIA.
- **League position/gap at the time.** TIA: 12th, 12 points behind. I1: 12th, 18 points behind. Used "12th" only.
- **When he became captain.** P and LFC: April 1979. W: late 1979 after Hughes left for Wolves. Used April 1979.
- **Birthplace.** P: Kensington, Liverpool, raised in Kirkby. W: Kirkby. Used P, attributed.
- **Honours count.** P lists four European Cups (incl. 1977, which he missed through injury, and 1984, when he did not play) and three League Cups (incl. 1983, final missed). LFC lists three European Cups and two League Cups. Piece names only the League Cups of 1981 and 1982 and says he missed the 1977 and 1984 finals and the 1983 final; no European Cup total is given.
- **Playing years.** Brief suggested 1971–1985. First-team appearances run 3 April 1972 to 20 August 1983 (P, S72, S84; LFC also says 1972-1983), so historicalPeriod is "1972–1983". He was registered with Liverpool until March 1985.
- **Coaching return.** P: reserve coach 1986. W: recruited by Dalglish in 1985. Used P's 1986.
- **Sheffield United appearances.** Only W (37 league, 45 total); not used.
- **Injury type.** P says cartilage operation; no source opened names the knee, so "knee" omitted.
- **Tommy Smith took his place in 1977.** W only; omitted.
- **1977 Super Cup goal v Hamburg; 1978 League Cup final penalty.** W only; omitted.
- **Position in 1974 final.** Not stated by an opened source; omitted.
- Sporting Heroes page also says "35 caps" in one passage against 42 elsewhere; 42 is supported by P, 11 and W.

## Fresh review and publication — 13 November 2026

Written on Denny's instruction of 13 November 2026 to add the missing 1970s biographies, drafted by a writer agent from the sources above, then checked by two fresh reviewers who had not seen the draft or this register.

- Fact-check: 62 claims checked, 57 confirmed. Corrections applied: the honours list (seven titles, FA Cup, two UEFA Cups, League Cups 1981 and 1982) is now attributed to the club site, because LFChistory lists three League Cups and four European Cups including finals he did not play in; the move to centre-back was moved before the 1974 FA Cup final and dated February 1974 (Larry Lloyd's last game was 2 February 1974); the Sheffield United loan start is disputed between sources, so it now says "late 1984"; the Brookfield school detail is attributed to Thompson's own LFChistory interview. Confirmed: April 1979 captaincy (liverpoolfc.com), "absolutely devastated" (LFChistory interview, 12 November 2008), all match dates and scores, England figures (RSSSF). The 12th-place position after Boxing Day 1981 rests on secondary sources only.
- Editorial: FAIL, edits applied: chronology fixed; opening now includes the 1978 and 1981 European Cup finals he started; unclear "they" sentence fixed; "sole captain" changed to "captain"; title, excerpt and closing section rewritten; the duplicate totals at the end removed. The reviewer asked for a description of his playing style; no checked source supplied one beyond containing Macdonald and Tudor in 1974, so none was added.
