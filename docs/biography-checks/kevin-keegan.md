## SENSITIVE FLAGS (read first)

- **Death:** The draft says Keegan "died on 20 July 2026, aged 75." This is **correct**. LFChistory gives his date of death as 20 July 2026. His family announced it that day; obituaries from the Irish Times, ESPN, Sky Sports and others went out on 20–21 July. Handle with care: this is a recent death.
- **Illness:** He had cancer. His family made the diagnosis public in January 2026 and said in June 2026 that it was stage four. The family statement says he "was surrounded by his wife and daughters in his final moments." The draft does not mention the illness. That is acceptable. If it is added, use the family's own framing ("after a battle with cancer") and nothing more.
- **Heysel / Hillsborough:** Neither is mentioned. No flag.

## 1. Summary

The draft is highly reliable. Every hard figure I checked is correct: birth date, signing date, debut goal and minute, 323 appearances and 100 goals, 230 league games and 68 league goals, the 61 matches in 1973–74, season goal totals, final scores and scorers, the Ballon d'Or years and the date of death. Issues (none is a hard error):
1. "Shankly initially considered him for a midfield role" is misleading. LFChistory says Shankly "supposedly envisaged replacing Ian Callaghan on the right flank". Keegan had been a midfielder at Scunthorpe.
2. "Tommy Smith's low delivery": the official club account says Smith "fed the ball across the box". It does not say "low". This is minor, and the safest fix is to drop "low".
3. "Liverpool finished second in the league" (1974–75): I only found this in Wikipedia-type sources, so it does not meet the two-source standard. It is almost certainly true (Liverpool were runners-up to Derby), but it needs a second source or softer wording.
4. "By his final season, Liverpool knew he intended to move abroad": I did not find a source for this in this check. It is low risk, but it needs a source or a softer version.
5. Keegan's move to Hamburg "that summer": LFChistory gives 3 June 1977 as his leaving date and says the £500,000 fee was a British record. The draft does not state the fee. If the brief's "£500,000" is added to the text, cite LFChistory (single source). I could not get the HSV page itself (access was blocked).
6. Background on the signing fee (the draft gives no fee): sources disagree. LFChistory and the Irish Times say £33,000. Liverpoolfc.com's "on this day" article says £35,000. If a fee is added, use "about £33,000" or "£33,000 (some accounts say £35,000)".
