**Errors and weak points**
1. **"A broken leg in pre-season training" (1987–88):** sources conflict. Mølby's 2014 Express & Star interview and These Football Times both describe a **foot** injury or break. Only Wikipedia says leg. Use neutral wording.
2. **"Footage recorded by United's match-analysis team was released by Mølby to LFCTV in 2009":** this comes from one club article (Goal of the Day). Another club article (40 years on, Nov 2025) says the footage "did eventually surface three decades later". A 2009 report says Ron Atkinson gave Mølby a tape and that it was first shown at a charity night at Anfield. Soften.
3. **West Brom paraphrase ("despite playing below their best"):** the club article confirms only that he called it the most nervous game of the run-in. The "below their best" part is not in the source.
4. **Fitness paraphrase ("Mølby acknowledged that interruptions made maintaining his condition harder, whereas regular football helped him"):** could not be found in any source.
5. **Prison paraphrase ("described the period as deeply difficult"):** could not be found. LFChistory has him saying "I know I did wrong". The acknowledgement of wrongdoing is supported, but "deeply difficult" is not.
6. Minor: the sweeper paragraph credits only Dalglish. Mølby says Souness also played him there, and had used him there first. This is not wrong as written, because it only describes Dalglish's reason.
7. Note (outside fact-checking, for the editor): the draft's frontmatter uses retired History fields (`category`, `articleType`, `historyEras`, `playerIds`, `editorialMode`). It also has an H1 and a `## Sources` section. `BiographySchema` has no sources field and expects `title`, `slug`, `date`, `historicalPeriod`, `decade`, `excerpt` and an optional `role`. This needs fixing at the edit stage.

   Replace with: "Footage filmed by Manchester United for their own analysis eventually surfaced years later, after Mølby was given a copy, and supporters can now see it." (Or cut the sentence. Liverpool's own two articles disagree on when and how it emerged.)

3. Para 12 (West Brom):
   > "Looking back, Mølby singled out the 2–1 away win at West Bromwich Albion in April as a nervous game Liverpool had negotiated despite playing below their best."

   Replace with: "Looking back, Mølby singled out the 2–1 away win at West Bromwich Albion in April as the most nervous game of the run-in."

4. Para 15 (prison):
   > "In later recollections he acknowledged wrongdoing and described the period as deeply difficult."

   Replace with: "He later acknowledged that he had done wrong."

5. Para 22 (fitness):
   > "Mølby acknowledged that interruptions made maintaining his condition harder, whereas regular football helped him. That account explains part of the difficulty without turning physique into a joke or treating every absence as a failure of effort."

   Replace with: "Fitness had always been a complication, and repeated interruptions made it harder to sustain." (No attributed claim.) Or cut both sentences.
