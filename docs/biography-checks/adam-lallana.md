## Summary

The draft is highly reliable. Every date, score, scorer, season total, career total and honour I checked is correct. I found no factual errors. These are the points to fix or note:

1. **Current status is out of date (most important).** The draft ends with his retirement on 25 June 2025. Since then he has worked as Southampton first-team coach. He took interim charge of the Under-21s in November 2025, and on 30 January 2026 Southampton made him the **permanent Under-21s manager**. He was still in that job in August 2026. The draft doesn't have to mention this, but as it stands it implies he left the game when he retired.

4. **One linked source (the Southampton retirement announcement) could not be opened** in this session. The 25 June 2025 retirement date is confirmed elsewhere (see the figure list).

There are no direct quotations in the body and no mention of Heysel or Hillsborough.
