The couple also said they had not been in touch with Cameron for two years (Reuters via Cyprus Mail, 27 March 2026). The facts are disputed and involve a family estrangement. **Recommendation: do not mention a diagnosis.** If a health line is wanted at all, only say he has spoken about memory problems since a serious Covid illness. Sources also disagree on the date of the Covid illness (Reuters says 2020; Wikipedia says February 2022).

## 1. Summary

The draft is reliable on the big numbers and the main match facts. Its weaknesses are colour details and recollections that are not in the sources. Dates, scores, scorers, the £110,000 record fee, the 247 appearances and 96 goals, the 23-goal 1975–76 season, both UEFA Cup finals, the Wolves title game, the Camp Nou winner, the Rome absence, the last game and the Swansea promotions all check out against LFChistory plus at least one other source. There are no outright errors in the headline figures. The problems are small embellishments ("towards the Kop", "struck low", "beneath his Swansea tracksuit"), a misdescribed injury ("muscle injury" where his own account says thigh), and several paraphrased recollections from Coaches' Voice and FourFourTwo that I could not open, so they are not verified. The draft contains no direct quotations. It does not mention his Wales caps or his managerial career beyond Swansea, though the brief asked about both. Those are omissions, not errors.

Errors and weaknesses:
1. **Leicester medical, "a muscle injury":** his own account (LFChistory interview) calls it "a problem with my left thigh", a chronic thigh injury. LFChistory links it to calcification of a muscle. "Thigh injury" is safer.
2. **"Headed the equaliser towards the Kop":** the header is confirmed. The Kop end is not found in any source. Cut "towards the Kop".
3. **"Struck low past goalkeeper Pere Mora":** the goal (13 min, Keegan assist, Mora in goal) is confirmed. "Low" is not. Cut "low" or say "beat Pere Mora".
4. **"Revealed a Liverpool shirt beneath his Swansea tracksuit":** sources say he **wore a Liverpool shirt during the minute's silence** for Shankly. Neither the tracksuit nor a "reveal" appears in any source I could reach. Simplify.
5. **"When Shankly paired them in training"** and **Keegan "admiring his honesty as a teammate":** not verified (the FourFourTwo source was blocked). In the LFChistory interview Toshack says they "never really worked a great deal at our game". The chemistry itself is supported (FourFourTwo headline; Keegan's quotes in LFC's "Liverpool's Greatest"). Soften.
6. **Recovery after Leicester ("specialised training, swimming and shorter, sharper sessions") and "Paisley was not entirely convinced about him":** not verified (the Coaches' Voice source was blocked). The nearest sourced line is Toshack saying he "had a falling out with Bob" before the Leicester move (LFChistory interview). Paraphrase cautiously or cut.
7. **"Told his playing career might have little time left":** supported by Wikipedia (specialists gave him around twelve months). That is a single, weak source, since Coaches' Voice could not be opened. Acceptable as "was warned his career might be short", but mark it for a second check.
8. **First leg 1973 "abandonment":** confirmed by UEFA ("halted... resumed the next day"). liverpoolfc.com calls it a 24-hour postponement. The draft's wording is fine. Just don't give a minute of abandonment unless you source one.
9. **Keegan's arrival "in the summer of 1971":** he signed in May 1971 (end of season). This is acceptable. "In May 1971" is more precise.
10. **Fee:** £110,000 (LFChistory, the Liverpool annual). Wikipedia gives £111,000 including the levy and the player's share. Keep £110,000.
11. **Omissions (not errors):** Wales caps (40 caps, 13 goals, 1969–80) and his managerial career (Sporting, Real Sociedad with the 1987 Copa del Rey, Real Madrid with the 1989–90 La Liga title, Wales twice, and others) are not mentioned. The draft only says "well beyond Wales". Figures are in the table below if wanted.
