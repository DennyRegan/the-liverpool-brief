## 1. Summary

The draft is very reliable. Every major figure checks out against LFChistory and at least one other source: birth date, debut, 549 appearances and 61 goals, 406 league appearances and 41 league goals, the 316-game run, three league games missed between 1965 and 1973, all 66 games in 1972–73, the 11 goals in 1969–70, four England caps and one goal, the cup finals and line-ups, the Portsmouth move and his later clubs. The problems are in a few details: the minute of the derby winner, a remark credited to Stephen Done that no source supports, and a misleading account of the Red Star goal.

**Errors found:**
1. **Derby winner timing ("Six minutes from the end").** Sources disagree. LFChistory's match page gives 76 minutes (14 from the end), the contemporary *Liverpool Echo* report says "five minutes from time", and liverpoolfc.com says six minutes from time. Use softer wording.
2. **"Liverpool historian Stephen Done later singled out Lawler's defensive contribution in that difficult away leg."** No source supports this. The cited LFC article praises Lawler's "Jamie Carragher-style" defending in the final, but it does not attribute the remark to Done or say which leg it means. Cut it or reword it.
3. **Red Star, 6 November 1973: "After Toshack's contribution, he equalised late in the second leg."** This is misleading. Toshack did not score; he set up Lawler's goal. Red Star had taken the lead through Lazarević on 60 minutes. The minute of Lawler's goal is unclear on LFChistory (72 or 84), so "late" is not safe.
4. **Minor point:** the draft says nothing false about the 1963–64 title. It says he played six league games, which is correct. Note, though, that LFChistory and liverpoolfc.com both list him as a 1963–64 champion, so his medal count is 3 league titles on those sites. The draft does not need changing.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

- Born 20 October 1943, so he is **83**.
- He has no current role at the club. He was Liverpool reserve-team coach from 1982 until Dalglish's arrival as manager (1985 according to LFChistory's Silent Knight article; Wikipedia says 1986, "released"). Wikipedia says he later scouted for Liverpool. The draft makes no claim about his current status, which is safe.

## 4. Corrections

1. **Quoted:** "Six minutes from the end of the Merseyside derby at Anfield on 21 November 1970, Chris Lawler scored the goal that completed Liverpool's recovery from 2–0 down."
   **Replace with:** "In the closing stages of the Merseyside derby at Anfield on 21 November 1970, Chris Lawler scored the goal that completed Liverpool's recovery from 2–0 down."
   **Reason:** sources give 76 minutes, five minutes from time and six minutes from time.

2. **Quoted:** "They held on to win the tie 3–2. Liverpool historian Stephen Done later singled out Lawler's defensive contribution in that difficult away leg."
   **Replace with:** "They held on to win the tie 3–2, and the club's own account of the final singles out Lawler's defending."
   **Alternatively:** cut the second sentence. No source attributes this to Stephen Done, and the LFC article does not say which leg it means.

3. **Quoted:** "After Toshack's contribution, he equalised late in the second leg, but the visitors scored again and won 2–1."
   **Replace with:** "Red Star led through Vojin Lazarević before Lawler, set up by Toshack, equalised in the second half of the second leg, but the visitors scored again and won 2–1."
   **Reason:** Toshack provided the assist and did not score. The minute of Lawler's goal is unclear (72' or 84'), so do not say "late".
