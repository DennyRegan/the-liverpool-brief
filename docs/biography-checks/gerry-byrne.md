# Fact-check: Gerry Byrne (draft: /home/claude/bios/gpt/gerry-byrne.md)

Checked 10 November 2026 by an independent fact-checker.

## Sensitive events flag

- **Death:** the draft mentions his death (28 November 2015, aged 77). This is confirmed. The draft does not mention the cause. Sources say he died in a care or nursing home in Wrexham after a long illness with Alzheimer's disease. If that is ever added, handle it with care. As written, nothing here is a problem.
- **Heysel / Hillsborough disaster:** not mentioned.
- No other sensitive personal matters. The injuries are footballing injuries.

## 1. Summary

The draft is reliable. Almost every date, score, line-up, appearance total and honour checks out against LFChistory, the official club site and at least one other outlet. There is one factual error: the minute given for his first goal. There is also one paraphrase that goes further than its source. The draft puts no words in quotation marks, so there are no quotes to clear.

**Errors**
1. **Brighton, 3 February 1962:** the draft says Byrne equalised "in the 52nd minute". In fact Roger Hunt scored in the 52nd minute to make it 2–1. Brighton led 1–0 at half-time, so Byrne's equaliser came between 46 and 51 minutes. No source gives the exact minute.
2. **Rotherham report, 1960 (minor):** the draft says the newspaper "praised the thoughtfulness of Byrne's defending". The report's actual words call him "a scientific defender of the Cresswell type". It is close in meaning, but it is safer to use the paper's own word.

Minor points that need no change:
- **When the collarbone broke:** sources disagree on the exact minute. LFC's 1965 feature says about the 5th minute, Sky and the Irish Times say the 7th, and Wikipedia says the 3rd. LFChistory says "the opening minutes". The draft's "early collision" is the safe wording, so keep it and don't add a minute.
- **How long he played injured:** Byrne himself said he "played for 117 minutes with a broken collar-bone". The draft's "120 minutes of football in all" refers to the whole match, which he did complete. That is accurate.
- **Testimonial:** the date (8 April 1970) is confirmed by LFChistory. LFC's tribute article loosely says "later in 1969", which is wrong. Crowd figures vary: about 40,000 (LFChistory player page, Daily Post), 41,000 (LFChistory match page) and 42,000 (Byrne's own memory). "Around 40,000" is safe.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

Deceased. Gerry Byrne died on 28 November 2015, aged 77, in Wrexham (sources: LFChistory, Sky Sports, Wikipedia).
