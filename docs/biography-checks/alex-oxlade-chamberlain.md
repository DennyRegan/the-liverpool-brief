1. The draft paraphrases Klopp's signing comments as "pace, technique and a willingness to take risks for the team". The transcript says "his pace and skills and his attitude" and "willing to take risks to try and make positive things happen". "Technique" and "for the team" are not his words. "Pace, skill and a willingness to take risks" would be accurate.

It leaves out that Beşiktaş ended his contract (27 August 2025), that he joined Celtic as a free agent, and that he signed a new one-year Celtic deal on 4 July 2026 and is still there. Whether to add this is an editorial choice.
