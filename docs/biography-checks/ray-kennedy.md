## Source limits and conflicts

Each fetched source carries a 200-word derivation ceiling except the BBC/Guardian/Independent items, which carry 25-word limits and are **not used**. No quotations are reproduced. Approximate prose allocation, with compact register entries leaving margin: member profile 95 words; LFChistory player profile 145; UEFA obituary 105; Wilson 45; current LFC profile 135; This Is Anfield 90; UEFA final 105; LFC Spurs analysis 85; 1981 campaign 125; Swansea 35; LFC obituary 35. These are allocation estimates including overlapping corroboration, not a licence to reuse every source up to its limit. Duplicate URLs/versions are treated as the same source.

- LFC profile header says 1974–1981, but its own text and transfer records say January 1982. Historical period uses 1974–1982; final appearance was in 1981.
- Fifth Liverpool title medal was for participation before leaving during 1981–82, not a complete fifth championship season at the club.
- LFChistory's later-career prose contains chronological problems and calls Swansea's 1982 finish fifth; Swansea itself records sixth. Later clubs only are taken from Swansea, no later-career dates or placing needed.
- Health diagnosis dates conflict (Swansea 1984; LFChistory 1986). No diagnosis, causal claim about form, or symptom reconstruction appears in the biography.
- This Is Anfield's old tribute has an incorrect departure date and appearance total. Used solely for its description of passing, strength and delayed attacking runs, corroborated by the match evidence; no dates or totals taken from it.
- Wilson misremembers the goal timing and Kennedy's age in 1971. Neither appears in this biography.
- No historic assist total, imagined scene, private thought, quote, Heysel or Hillsborough content.
- Stronger-foot comparison removed at writer self-check; the evidenced right-foot finish is sufficient.
