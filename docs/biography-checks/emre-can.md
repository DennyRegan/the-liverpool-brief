## Summary

The draft is reliable. Every date, score, total and match detail I checked holds up. I found no substantive errors, only two small wording points:

1. **Hoffenheim was the play-off round, not the "qualifying round".** The draft says Can "helped them through the qualifying round itself". The 2017 tie against Hoffenheim was the Champions League play-off round, the last stage before the group stage. "Qualifying round" is loose rather than wrong.

His current status has changed since the draft was written (see below). The draft makes no claim about the present, so nothing in it is wrong because of this. The draft contains no direct quotations and no Heysel or Hillsborough material.

## Corrections

1. **Hoffenheim round name.**
   - Draft: "He then helped them through the qualifying round itself."
   - Correct: the tie was the Champions League **play-off round**, August 2017, the last stage before the groups. "Through the play-off round" would be exact.
   - Severity: minor. The draft is loose rather than wrong.
2. **Watford run.**
   - Draft: "Emre Can had run beyond Watford's midfield."
   - Correct: reports and Can's own description have him running **in behind the Watford defence** into the penalty area: "I saw the space and I ran behind" (Can, quoted by AFP via [Dawn](https://dawn.com/news/1330661)).
   - Severity: minor.
