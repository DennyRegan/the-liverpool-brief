Klopp spoke about a full pre-season and "the best shape since I have known him". This is acceptable, but tie it to the pre-season if a tighter attribution is wanted.

## Betting-rules case (not in the draft)

The draft does not mention the case. Nothing needs changing. If it is added, keep strictly to the FA's statement of 2 March 2020 ([thefa.com](https://www.thefa.com/news/2020/mar/02/sturridge-suspended-020320)):
- An Independent Regulatory Commission first dealt with the charges. Its written reasons are dated July 2019 (Wikipedia gives 18 July 2019). It imposed a six-week ban with four weeks suspended, an effective two weeks. The FA's statement implies a £75,000 fine, because the appeal doubled it to £150,000.
- The FA appealed. On 2 March 2020 the Appeal Board found two further charges proven that had originally been dismissed. It said the Commission had misapplied the rules on inside information and that the original penalty was "unduly lenient". It increased the effective ban "from two weeks to four months", to the end of 17 June 2020, and doubled the fine to £150,000. FIFA gave the ban worldwide effect.
- The breaches concerned the FA's betting rules and the use of inside information. **Avoid** words such as "match-fixing", "corruption" or "gambling addiction". Do not say he bet on matches he played in. The FA's findings support none of these.
- Wikipedia says the case involved him telling his brother to bet on his possible transfer to Sevilla. That detail is not in the FA statement I read, so treat it as Unconfirmed unless it is checked against the Commission's written reasons.
