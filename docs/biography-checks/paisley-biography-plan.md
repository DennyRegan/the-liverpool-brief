## Boundary cases and research cautions

- Peter McDonnell is an optional reserve-goalkeeper story, not one of the 44 first-team players. He made no competitive first-team appearance despite being an unused substitute 28 times. [LFChistory profile](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/496)
- Michael Robinson joined in August 1983 under Joe Fagan and is outside the playing-era queue. [Club profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/michael-robinson)
- Steve McMahon joined in September 1985 under Dalglish; later comments about him by Paisley do not make him a Paisley player. [Club profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/steve-mcmahon)
- Nicol's debut was on 31 August 1982; his four appearances make him eligible even though his regular first-team role developed later. [1982–83 statistics](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/statistics/23)
- The 1974–75 season table includes the 1974 Charity Shield, whereas LFChistory's current manager page starts Paisley's match record on 17 August. This does not change roster membership, but managerial totals must state the counting convention.
- Keep this commission within the existing biography content model and reader routes. Do not create a series, taxonomy, new automated publisher or other infrastructure merely because these biographies support a future expansive feature.
