## 1. Summary

The draft is largely reliable. Every playing statistic checked (475 apps / 76 goals; 331 / 50 league; 49 apps in 1970–71; league apps 1978–81) matches LFChistory exactly. The big-match details (Everton 1970, the 1971 and 1974 FA Cup finals, both legs of the 1976 UEFA Cup final, Rome 1977, Wembley 1978) all check out. The errors are small: one wrong date (the Kirkby Academy opening), a degree subject that is only half given, signing dates where sources disagree, and a first mention of Benítez with no introduction. The one direct quotation is verified word for word.

Errors and issues:
1. **Kirkby Academy opening year is given as 1999.** LFChistory and other sources give **1998**.
2. **Degree described as "an economics degree".** He studied **economics and politics** at the University of Warwick (graduated 1970). LFChistory says only "Economics", so the draft is incomplete rather than wrong.
3. **Signing dates (amateur "February 1970", professional "19 May").** These come from LFChistory alone. A 1970 contemporary piece (playupliverpool.com) says amateur forms in April 1970 and professional terms in July 1970. Safest wording: "in 1970", or "May 1970, according to LFChistory".
4. **"Benítez" appears with no first name or role** ("Benítez was himself calling…"). Editorial fix, not a factual one: "Rafael Benítez, then Liverpool manager".
5. **Minor framing: Owens and Ablett.** The 2007 Echo piece says the two "talk most days" now that the Academy has its own base. That is ongoing liaison under the new set-up, not liaison "over those calls" from the old Melwood days. Small rewording suggested.
6. **"The passes for two of their three goals" in Rome.** One of the two was a corner. That is accurate, but "set up two of their three goals" is cleaner. Optional.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

- **Alive.** No reports of his death were found. He is 78 and turns 79 on 25 November 2026 (born 25 November 1947, Dublin).
- **Retired.** Liverpool announced his retirement from his Academy coaching role on 2 December 2022, aged 75. Academy director Alex Inglethorpe said he "will still be at the Academy to watch games and share his expertise and wisdom". He holds no current coaching role at the club.
- The draft's closing status ("Liverpool announced Heighway's retirement from coaching in December 2022, shortly after his 75th birthday") is correct.
