## 1. Summary

The draft is broadly reliable. Every season-by-season figure, the career totals (329 apps, 81 goals) and nearly all the match facts match LFChistory exactly. That includes the debut alongside Neal, the Everton chip, the Hamburg hat-trick, the Forest game and the 42-match run, the Dundalk farewell and the 1982 League Cup final substitution. The errors are a disputed transfer fee, a disputed goal minute in Rome, and one honours claim that the official club contradicts. Some interpretive sentences (Hamburg "position" recollection, Johnston pressure, midfield rivals) have no source. **The draft contains no direct quotations.**

Errors and issues:
1. **Fee "£175,000" is disputed.** LFChistory gives £175,000. The contemporary report (13 Nov 1974) and most other accounts give **£170,000**. Safest: "about £170,000" or "a fee of around £170,000".
2. **"Finished in the 27th minute" (1977 European Cup final).** Sources disagree: LFChistory 24', Wikipedia 27', RSSSF 28'. Safest: drop the minute ("midway through the first half").
3. **"Did not receive a championship medal" (1975–76).** Liverpool FC's official site (both the past-player page and "Liverpool's Greatest") credits him with **five** league titles, including 1975–76. His 9 league appearances were below the usual 14 qualifying mark of that era, but no source found confirms he did not receive a medal. **Cut the clause.**
4. **Hamburg "position" recollection is unsourced.** The sentences "McDermott later identified..." (in part) and "Moved in from a wider role, he felt encouragement to regard the central area as his natural territory" have no source. LFChistory supports only that he called the hat-trick "the making of him at Liverpool". Cut or reduce to that point.
5. **Aberdeen 1980 description: no minute given, which is fine, but note the goal came in the 5th minute (LFChistory).** "Chipping from a narrow angle" is supported by McDermott's own account (FourFourTwo via inkl). No change needed. For information only.
6. **"He returned to Newcastle later that month."** His last game was 28 Sept 1982 and he left on 29 Sept (LFChistory). "Later that month" is technically true but reads oddly for the next day. Suggest "days later, in September 1982, for £100,000".
7. **Unsourced colour (low risk):** "Brian Hall and Jimmy Case were among the midfield alternatives" (1975–76), "becoming a regular towards the season's end" (1976–77), and "Johnston's growing involvement contributed to the pressure". These are plausible, but no source found names them. The club's own wording is "younger players challenging for his place". Soften or cut.
