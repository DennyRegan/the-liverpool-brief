## Corrections

No hard factual errors found. Two paraphrases overstate what the sources show:

1. **October 2018 Klopp paraphrase.** Draft: "the club believed in the player, but others were better prepared for the role at that moment. Fabinho needed time to catch up." Klopp's recorded words were "there is only one reason: because another player fits better in the moment" and "It's about the new player to close that gap. It always took time and will always take time." "Better prepared" and "catch up" are fair readings. "The club believed in the player" has no support in the text I retrieved. The nearest line is "he is very close, he is never far away."
2. **March 2021 Klopp paraphrase.** Draft: "helped Thiago too. The manager resisted the idea that one positional change could solve everything." The Wijnaldum point is supported. The Thiago point and the caution are unconfirmed. A similar caution exists, but it comes from a different press conference: Sky Sports, 13 May 2021, where Klopp said late goals conceded against Leeds and Newcastle had nothing to do with Fabinho's position (https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11095/12302250/fabinhos-role-in-liverpools-midfield-and-why-his-absence-affects-others-explained-with-jurgen-klopps-help).
