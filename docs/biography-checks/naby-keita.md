## Summary

The draft is very reliable. Every date, score, minute, provider and total I checked is correct: Newcastle 0–1 (19'), Southampton (36'), Porto (5'), the Barcelona adductor injury, Monterrey (12'), the Flamengo start, Chelsea 5–3 (23'), Old Trafford (5'), 40 games in 2021–22, 13 in 2022–23, and 129 games / 11 goals. I found no factual errors. The problems are small:

1. **Gerrard shirt timing (misleading).** The draft places the handover under "symbolism waiting at Anfield", which reads as if it happened on his arrival in 2018. In fact Gerrard handed him the No.8 at Melwood in **August 2017**, on Keïta's first visit after the deal was agreed. Keïta described it in his June 2018 unveiling interview.
2. **Honours list incomplete.** Liverpool's own farewell and departure articles also list the **FA Community Shield** (2022). The draft names five honours, not six. It doesn't have to list everything, but it shouldn't read as complete.
3. **Current status missing.** He left Werder Bremen in disgrace (suspended April 2024). He is now a permanent Ferencváros player (from 14 January 2026).
4. **Metadata.** `decade: "2010s"` for a 2018–2023 career is an editorial choice. Most of his Liverpool career (2020–23) falls in the 2020s.

There are no quotation marks in the draft, so there are no direct quotes to verify.

- **International:** One fan-news aggregator reports he turned down a Guinea call-up for World Cup qualifiers ([africasoccer.com tag page](https://africasoccer.com/tag/naby-keita/)). This is a single weak source with no dates: **Unconfirmed**. Don't use it.
