# Fact-check: Peter Thompson (Liverpool winger, 1963–1974)

Draft checked: `/home/claude/bios/gpt/peter-thompson.md` (GPT). Checked 10 November 2026.

## Sensitive events

- **Death**: the draft reports his death ("died in December 2018, aged 76"). It is accurate (see below).
- **Injury and personal difficulty**: knee operations, doubts about whether he could play again, and a painful breakdown in his relationship with Shankly. These come from Thompson's own account and are handled fairly. (The source interview also mentions cortisone injections and later health problems. The draft does not mention these and does not need to.)
- **Heysel / Hillsborough**: not mentioned.

## 1. Summary

The draft is very reliable. Every date, score, minute, appearance total and match detail I checked agrees with LFChistory, the official club site, DFB, England match records or contemporary obituaries. It contains no direct quotations. I found no outright factual errors in the text. The problems are a broken source link, a transfer fee that sources give differently, and two small points of wording.

Errors and issues:
1. **Wrong source link**: the Englandstats link (`player.php?pid=826`) goes to **Ralph Coates**, not Peter Thompson. Replace it.
2. **Transfer fee disagreement (not an error)**: £37,000 is supported by LFChistory and the PA obituary (Irish Times). Thompson's own 2009 interview (LFChistory/PNE) and the Lancashire Evening Post give **£40,000**, and the interview calls it a Liverpool club record. Keep £37,000. Do not add any "club record" claim.
3. **Bolton loan month (minor disagreement)**: LFChistory, Wikipedia and Bolton's obituary say December 1973. The Lancashire Evening Post says November 1973. Keep December.
4. **Hull 1968 (wording)**: the Echo piece says "at Hull on the Friday night" and does not use the word "pre-season". The timing (6 August 1968, before the season opened) makes it a pre-season game, so this is low risk. Optional softening below.
5. **Ramsey and wingers (wording)**: this is a widely held interpretation, but I found no direct source in this check that ties it to Thompson's omission. Soften it slightly.
6. **1970–71 count (note only)**: LFChistory's season page shows 40 matchday involvements. That is 32 starts, 7 as substitute and 1 unused on the bench, so **39 appearances**. The draft is correct.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

Dead. **Died 30 December 2018, aged 76** (LFChistory; Wikipedia). Club and media tributes appeared on 31 December 2018 (Liverpool FC, Bolton Wanderers, Sky Sports, PA/Irish Times). The draft's "died in December 2018, aged 76" is correct.

Later life (not in the draft, for reference): ran a caravan park in Knott End-on-Sea for about seven years, then an inn at Bowland Bridge in the Lake District, then a **hotel in Harrogate**, and moved to Portugal around 2006. Sources: Wikipedia, and the 2009 PNE interview reproduced on LFChistory (articles/3267), which mentions the caravan park, the Lake District inn and the Harrogate hotel.
