## Summary

The draft is reliable. Every checkable figure (354 games and 23 goals, dates, scores, assist totals, trophies, departure) matches at least one good source, and most match two. Nothing in it is plainly wrong. The issues are small:

1. The transfer timing is loosely worded. The fee was agreed and announced on 30 May 2025, and the move went through on 1 June. "Agreed a fee ... in June" can be read as wrong.
2. Who moved him to right-back is disputed. Liverpool's own profile credits the Academy coaches, including Alex Inglethorpe, and records him at right-back under Rodgers in 2015. LFChistory credits Klopp. The draft follows Liverpool's account, which is the better-documented one, but the sources do not agree.
3. The Henderson line is a fair paraphrase. Do not turn it into a quote unless the exact wording below is used.
4. The Barcelona sentence lists all four goals ("two goals from Origi and Wijnaldum's double overturned the deficit") and then says "For the fourth goal". That is a wording problem, not a factual one.
