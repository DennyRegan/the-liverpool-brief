## Corrections

1. **Villarreal, second goal.** Draft: "his deflected effort reached Sturridge for the second". Correct: Firmino put Sturridge through with a flicked pass, and Sturridge scored off the post in the 63rd minute (UEFA; Sky Sports). It was a pass, not a deflected shot.
2. **Current status.** Draft: "By August 2026 he was still playing for the Qatari club." Correct: he is still at Al Sadd as of October 2026 (last listed match 18 October 2026), with a contract to 30 June 2027.
3. **Klopp, January 2016 (paraphrase).** Draft: "when he should enter the penalty area, drop back to combine or push the defensive line towards its own goal". The source has "when he has to be in the box, when he has to not be in the box" and "push the last line back". "Drop back to combine" is the writer's reading. Suggest "when he should be in the box and when he should not, and when to push the defensive line back".
4. **2022 cups (clarification, not a hard error).** "He remained part of the squad that won the FA Cup and League Cup" is literally true. He missed the League Cup final through injury, though, so make sure the sentence does not suggest he played in the finals.
5. **Hoffenheim move (minor).** "moving to Hoffenheim in 2011". He signed in December 2010 and arrived on 1 January 2011. That's acceptable as written; "at the end of 2010" matches the club's own profile.

## Cut list

- **"By December, Klopp was describing..."** Keep the quote but drop the month, or write "During that season". The source page is undated and I could not independently confirm December 2016.
- No other claims need cutting. Lines like "His opening weeks in England were unsettled" and "Liverpool were increasingly finding ways to play without him" are editorial judgement, not factual claims. They fit the record (no goal until November 2015; 20 league appearances in 2021–22).

## Unverifiable or loosely supported claims

1. **"Three days later, in the final against Flamengo, he took Mané's pass in the box, waited for the goalkeeper to commit and scored in extra time."** Sources do not say he waited for the keeper to commit. 2. **"Michael Cox of FourFourTwo noted that week..."** Cox's piece is dated 18 January 2016 and is about the 1–0 home defeat by Manchester United, five days after Arsenal (https://www.fourfourtwo.com/features/michael-cox-how-firminos-excellent-false-nine-display-almost-led-liverpool-victory). - **"With Mohamed Salah and Sadio Mané either side of him, that movement became the engine of Jürgen Klopp's team."** "The engine" is a cliché and an overstatement. Suggest: "With Mohamed Salah and Sadio Mané either side of him, that movement was central to how Jürgen Klopp's team attacked."
