## Errors

1. **Excerpt:** "He refused, stayed 12 seasons, and lifted the European Cup and the league title as captain."
   Problem: 12 seasons is his whole Liverpool career (2011–12 to 2022–23). From the 2012 refusal he stayed 11 more seasons. The body gets this right ("12 seasons" in total).
   Fix: "He refused, stayed another 11 seasons, and lifted the European Cup and the league title as captain." Or: "He refused, and went on to lift the European Cup and the league title as captain."
   Source: https://www.lfchistory.net/players/1233

2. **Para 7:** "Fabinho could sit deeper, and Henderson could make runs into the box."
   Problem: the source does not mention runs into the box. Henderson said Fabinho suited the deeper role and that the change "might give me a bit more license to get forward more".
   Fix: "Fabinho could sit deeper, and Henderson could get forward more."
   Source: https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/345384-henderson-klopp-midfield-talks-liverpool

3. **Para 9 (Klopp on his departure):** "...and his ability to play in different midfield positions."
   Problem: Klopp said "had to play different positions" and in the same tribute mentioned that he "even played centre-half". He did not limit it to midfield.
   Fix: "...and his ability to play in different positions."
   Source: https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/jurgen-klopp-jordan-henderson-departure-we-will-miss-him

Everything else checks out. That includes all the figures (492/360/33/29, 48 in 2011–12, 35 league starts in 2013–14, 43 in 2022–23, 99 points, 26 wins from 27), every date and score, the seven major trophies plus the Shield, and the later clubs: Al-Ettifaq on 27 July 2023, Ajax in January 2024, Brentford in July 2025 and Chelsea on 3 August 2026 on a two-year deal (Reuters via The Star: https://www.thestar.com.my/sport/football/2026/08/03/soccer-chelsea-sign-england-midfielder-henderson-from-brentford).

I re-checked the claims the fact-check had from only one source. All of them hold up:
- the Coventry loan in 2009 (LFChistory, plus a Coventry Telegraph report)
- the red-card acceptance, "I couldn't have expected anything else really" (liverpoolfc.com)
- Players Together (FWA: "rallying fellow club captains and establishing the #PlayersTogether collective")
- the Klopp tribute
- Kop Outs condemning the move (Sky, 27 July 2023), with Henderson "urged to 'stand by your words'" (https://uk.news.yahoo.com/jordan-hendersons-saudi-arabia-move-125600691.html)
