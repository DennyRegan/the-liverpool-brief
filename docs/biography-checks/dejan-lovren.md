## Corrections

There are no outright errors. These two points are accurate but incomplete or loosely worded:

1. **Tottenham goals.** The draft says: "A missed aerial interception helped create the hosts' second goal." That is correct, but he was also at fault for the first. Sky Sports says he "failed to head clear the ball, letting Kane through"; This Is Anfield says he stepped out of the line, leaving Kane free. Saying only the second goal understates his part. It is fine to keep the sentence if the owner prefers restraint, but it should not imply that the second goal was his only error.
2. **Klopp's explanation.** The draft says the change "was intended to improve its protection and stability". Klopp said: "We had to change something... it was more that we needed more protection in the half-space" (IBTimes). "Stability" is not his word. Suggested accurate paraphrase: "more protection in midfield areas".

Two minor sourcing notes:

- The draft links Klopp's assessment to `liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/279136-...`. That page would not open (neither the main nor the legacy version), so I could not check the club's wording. The substance is confirmed elsewhere. The owner may want to test the link before publishing.
- Sky Sports dates the signing to Monday 28 July 2014, but the club's announcement and LFChistory give 27 July. 27 July is correct.
