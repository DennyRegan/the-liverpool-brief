## Source limits and cautions

All sources used carry a 200-word derivation ceiling. No quotation is reproduced. Approximate allocation including compact claim-map phrasing: LFC profile 170 words; LFChistory profile 155; Bowen 100; LFC signing anniversary 110; Sporting Heroes 110; LFChistory 1977 campaign 100; UEFA 1977 account 120; UEFA 1984 retrospective 75; LFChistory 1984 match 45; Bolton 95; FA 35. Remaining language is independent football assessment or cross-source synthesis. Duplicate versions of the official profile are treated as the same source. These estimates leave margin and are not permissions for further reuse.

- LFChistory's prose says the debut came days after arrival, contradicting its own precise date fields and LFC's one-month account. The biography follows the dates and says October signing, November debut.
- Northampton career-start fields vary between 1967 and 1968, reflecting joining versus senior playing. The text says senior career in 1968, supported by Bolton and the seasonal playing record; it does not assert a joining date.
- Fee reports elsewhere vary (£60,000/£65,000/£66,000). £66,000 follows both LFC and LFChistory.
- The 365 and 417 records are explicitly distinguished. The latter does not mean 417 consecutive league games, nor does it run from December 1974. No claim is made that such records cannot be surpassed.
- Published timings for the 1984 opener vary (13/14/15 minutes); no minute is used.
- The eighth league title is properly assigned to 1985–86 and explained as a season he began before leaving. No claim of an FA Cup medal for that Double season. Charity Shields and a grand-total trophy count are omitted because sources use different inclusion conventions.
- Bolton's retrospective is used for the later playing career and 1968 origin only. Its first Liverpool season appearance count and Charity Shield total are not used.
- No imagined scenes, direct dialogue, private thoughts, assist total, health speculation or discussion of Heysel/Hillsborough. No claims about current health or current employment.
