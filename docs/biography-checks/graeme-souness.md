## Conflicts, boundaries and source limits

- National Football Museum gives 1972 for Middlesbrough arrival; Scottish Football Museum explicitly gives January 1973, corroborated by LFChistory's 1973 transfer account. Biography follows the latter. Avoid treating six calendar years touched as six full seasons.
- National Football Museum misdates the captaincy to the start of 1981–82 and gives 358 appearances/56 goals. The biography follows the official Liverpool profile and LFChistory: January 1982 and 359/55.
- Scottish Football Museum says three Liverpool League Cups; official Liverpool and LFChistory list four. Biography uses four. Avoid the inaccurate 2017 LFC short profile that also undercounts league titles.
- £350,000 is a common rounded Liverpool fee; the £352,000 used here is LFChistory's precise figure. Departure fee omitted because reported totals differ.
- The 1978 winner is dated 64/65 minutes by different UEFA retrospectives. No minute appears. No claim of an instant first-team breakthrough at Tottenham.
- The 1984 injury is included plainly and proportionately. No speculation about motive, invented dialogue, celebration of retaliation or detailed reconstruction. No claim that dangerous play was necessary to Liverpool's success.
- No direct quotes, fabricated scenes, unrecorded private feelings, assist totals or present-day employment claims. No discussion of Heysel/Hillsborough.
- Every retrieved page carried a 200-word derivation ceiling. Conservative allocation across biography and register: A approximately 180 words; B 100; C 170; D 150; E 90; F 55; G 135; H 120; I 55; J 180; K 75; L 65. These account for factual paraphrase and brief claim-map coverage, with independently expressed interpretation separated from sourced factual language. Duplicate URLs are one source. No source is treated as unlimited and no verbatim quotation is reproduced.

## Re-review and restoration — 13 November 2026

The biography was removed from the site on 10 November and restored on Denny's instruction of 13 November 2026, after independent review by fresh reviewers who had not seen the piece or this register.

- Fact-check: no outright factual error; findings were date, source and wording points, all applied. The captaincy date is a source conflict (Liverpool's page says January 1982; LFChistory says Paisley made him captain at Christmas 1981, with the Swansea match on 2 January 1982 as his first as outright captain), so the text now gives LFChistory's first-match date and notes Liverpool's own dating. The Middlesbrough signing date is not documented precisely (the Scottish Football Museum says January 1973; his debut was 6 January 1973), so the text attributes it to the museum. "After a 1–1 draw" now says the 1984 final was 1–1 after extra time. The League Cup 1983 gesture now says he "gave up the captain's right to lift" it (the cited club page does not say he "asked"). The unsupported "wider transition, with younger players taking larger roles" was cut. "Fierce first-time strike" became "volley" (sources say volley); "his third completing the scoring" became "his third was Liverpool's fifth" (CSKA scored once). Confirmed with sources: birth, Tottenham's one substitute appearance (Keflavík, 14 September 1971), Montreal, Middlesbrough Second Division title 1973–74, £352,000 on 10 January 1978, first goal 25 February 1978 (3–1 v Manchester United), the 1978 final and pass, 41 league games in 1978–79 and 1979–80, the CSKA hat-trick (16', 51', 80'; 5–1; 6–1 aggregate), Boxing Day 1981 defeat, titles and League Cups, 1983 League Cup final, the 1984 replay goal at Maine Road, the Dinamo Bucharest incident (as recorded by LFChistory), the 1984 final and shoot-out, career totals (359 appearances, 55 goals, all competitions), Sampdoria, Rangers, Liverpool manager 1991–94, FA Cup 1992.
