## Conflicts, boundaries and source limits

- National Football Museum gives 1972 for Middlesbrough arrival; Scottish Football Museum explicitly gives January 1973, corroborated by LFChistory's 1973 transfer account. Biography follows the latter. Avoid treating six calendar years touched as six full seasons.
- National Football Museum misdates the captaincy to the start of 1981–82 and gives 358 appearances/56 goals. The biography follows the official Liverpool profile and LFChistory: January 1982 and 359/55.
- Scottish Football Museum says three Liverpool League Cups; official Liverpool and LFChistory list four. Biography uses four. Avoid the inaccurate 2017 LFC short profile that also undercounts league titles.
- £350,000 is a common rounded Liverpool fee; the £352,000 used here is LFChistory's precise figure. Departure fee omitted because reported totals differ.
- The 1978 winner is dated 64/65 minutes by different UEFA retrospectives. No minute appears. No claim of an instant first-team breakthrough at Tottenham.
- The 1984 injury is included plainly and proportionately. No speculation about motive, invented dialogue, celebration of retaliation or detailed reconstruction. No claim that dangerous play was necessary to Liverpool's success.
- No direct quotes, fabricated scenes, unrecorded private feelings, assist totals or present-day employment claims. No discussion of Heysel/Hillsborough.
- Every retrieved page carried a 200-word derivation ceiling. Conservative allocation across biography and register: A approximately 180 words; B 100; C 170; D 150; E 90; F 55; G 135; H 120; I 55; J 180; K 75; L 65. These account for factual paraphrase and brief claim-map coverage, with independently expressed interpretation separated from sourced factual language. Duplicate URLs are one source. No source is treated as unlimited and no verbatim quotation is reproduced.
