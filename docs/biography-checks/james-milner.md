## Corrections

1. **Sky interview attribution.** The draft says: "In the Sky interview, he acknowledged that he had not enjoyed the role at the time." In the Sky article, "It was not an enjoyable role at the time" is the reporter's narration, not something Milner is quoted saying. His own quoted words are "It was what the manager needed at the time", and he says he chose to learn the role rather than leave. The fix is either to attribute the judgement to Sky ("Sky Sports described it as…") or to say he accepted it because the manager needed it.
2. **Dortmund scene.** The draft says: "Milner had a free-kick and a penalty area full of players waiting for the delivery." The free-kick was near the halfway line, and he played it short to Sturridge, so there was no delivery into a waiting box. The sources are the LFC legacy pieces and LFC's "five memorable moments" article. The rest of the Dortmund passage is correct.
3. **Early career (minor, not an error).** The draft says: "Newcastle United, Aston Villa and Manchester City followed." This leaves out his 2003 loan to Swindon Town. It also folds two Villa spells into one: a loan from Newcastle in 2005–06, then a permanent move in 2008 (Wikipedia). It is acceptable as a summary.
