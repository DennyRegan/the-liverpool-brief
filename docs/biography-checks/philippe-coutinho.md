## Corrections

1. **Spartak.** The draft says "scored his first club hat-trick". Correct: his first **Liverpool** hat-trick (liverpoolfc.com match report and profile).
2. **Persuasion.** The draft says "Liverpool's attempts to persuade him had failed." This is the club's version stated as fact, so attribute it. For example: Klopp said the club had kept him beyond the summer window in the hope he would stay.
3. **Later trophies.** The draft says "the European Cup and league title that followed belonged to the side built after his departure." This is loose as fact and partisan in effect. Most of the trophy-winning core played with him. The line implies his sale was the turning point, which is the contested supporter argument. Suggested neutral alternative: the trophies came after he left; he was not part of them.
4. **Middlesbrough.** The draft says "his free-kick contributed to the 3–0 win". It was a goal. Saying so is clearer and avoids underselling him.
5. **The 2017 dispute is missing.** The draft says only that he "had wanted the move since the previous summer". For a balanced account it should say plainly that he pushed to leave (Klopp: "insistent with me, the owners and even his teammates") and that Liverpool refused to sell in summer 2017. Reports at the time also said he submitted a formal transfer request in August 2017. I could not open a source for that this session, so verify it before use (BBC or Guardian, around 11 Aug 2017).

## Neutrality review of the Barcelona section (requested)

- **Neutral and accurate:** "he left for Barcelona in January 2018". So is the Klopp attribution ("Klopp's farewell statement made clear..."), although "made clear" quietly endorses the statement; "said" would be more neutral. Mentioning both his wish to leave and his professionalism afterwards gives both sides.
- **Takes the club's side by stating it as fact:** "Liverpool's attempts to persuade him had failed."
- **Takes the "we were better off" side:** "The separation preceded Liverpool's greatest achievements... belonged to the side built after his departure". The closing lines are then softer and fair ("His Liverpool career remains unfinished... no great trophy to accompany the talent").
- **Softened by leaving things out (the opposite side):** no transfer request, no refused bids, and no mention of the friction in summer 2017. A reader who remembers that summer could see this as whitewashing. One factual sentence fixes it.
- **Not in the draft, and should stay out:** fee, add-ons, claims that his fee "paid for" Van Dijk or Alisson, and his reasons or motives (for example the back injury in August 2017). None of these appear, and that is good.
