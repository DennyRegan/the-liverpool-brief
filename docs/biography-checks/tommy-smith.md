## 1. Summary

The draft is largely reliable. All the main figures check out against LFChistory and at least one other source: dates, the debut, 638 appearances and 48 goals, the captaincy, honours, the finals, the England cap and the Swansea record. There are no outright factual errors in the headline claims. The problems are a misleading sequence around losing the captaincy, an unverified detail in the 1974 third goal (Heighway's role), a weakly sourced 34-appearance figure for 1977-78, and an unopened Guardian source for the Gayle paragraph. The draft also leaves out the 1977 FA Cup final and his testimonial, both of which the brief asked about. The draft contains no direct quotations.

Errors and weaknesses:
1. **Captaincy sequence is misleading.** The draft treats Hughes succeeding him and "a dispute with Shankly over being left out against Arsenal" as two separate things in 1973. In fact the row (November 1973, at Highbury) is what cost him the captaincy.
2. **1974 third goal:** "Heighway found Smith" is not confirmed. Sources confirm only that Smith supplied the pass or cross from the right for Keegan (88').
3. **"His final season still produced 34 competitive appearances":** only a weak source (LFC fandom wiki: 22 league, 6 League Cup, 6 other). The 22 league appearances are confirmed by LFChistory.
4. **Source list:** the Guardian Gayle URL could not be verified (the fetch was blocked). Some liverpoolfc.com URLs in the source list also failed to load (UEFA Cup 1973 piece). They may still be valid, so check before publishing.
5. **Omission (not an error):** the draft never mentions the **1977 FA Cup final**. Smith played in it, a 2–1 defeat to Manchester United (LFC official feature: he "played every remaining fixture that season, including the FA Cup and European Cup finals"). The closing line about "disappointment at Wembley" currently refers only to 1971.
6. **Omission:** the **testimonial** is not mentioned. LFChistory says it was held at Anfield two days after the Rome final, with the European Cup paraded (single source).
7. **Minute of the Rome header** is not stated. "After the hour" is safe. If a minute is wanted, use 64th (LFChistory, liverpoolfc.com). One LFC feature says "around 25 minutes from time".

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

Deceased. Died 12 April 2019, aged 74, at a nursing home in Crosby, Merseyside (ITV, Wikipedia). He had dementia (Alzheimer's/vascular dementia per Liverpool Echo and Alzheimer's Society).

## 4. Corrections

1. **Quoted:** "Smith's position changed in 1973. Emlyn Hughes succeeded him as captain, while the year also brought a dispute with Shankly over being left out against Arsenal."
   **Safe replacement:** "Smith's position changed in November 1973. Left out of the team to face Arsenal at Highbury, he argued with Shankly before kick-off, and soon afterwards Emlyn Hughes replaced him as captain."

2. **Quoted:** "For the third goal, Heighway found Smith advancing on the right of the penalty area. Smith sent the ball across for Kevin Keegan to finish."
   **Safe replacement:** "For the third goal, two minutes from time, Smith advanced on the right and sent the ball across for Kevin Keegan to finish." (Drop Heighway unless a match account confirms his part.)

3. **Quoted:** "His final season still produced 34 competitive appearances."
   **Safe replacement:** "He still made 22 league appearances in that final season." (Confirmed by LFChistory.) Keep 34 only if LFChistory's 1977–78 season appearance page confirms it.

4. **Optional (omission), para 44 or 56:** add the 1977 FA Cup final. For example, after "Liverpool won the league again": "He also played in the FA Cup final, lost 2–1 to Manchester United four days before Rome." The closing "disappointment at Wembley" then covers both 1971 and 1977.

5. **Gayle paragraph:** wording is acceptable as is. Replace the unverified Guardian link with a source that has been checked (e.g. the Football Faithful piece or a publisher page for *61 Minutes in Munich*), or open and confirm the Guardian URL before publishing.
