# Fact-check: Mohamed Salah biography draft

Draft checked: `/home/claude/bios/mohamed-salah.md` (research cut-off stated as 27 September 2026)
Checked: 6 November 2026, by independent web research. The earlier AI research record and publication notes were used only as pointers; nothing below is taken from them unverified.

## Summary

The draft is reliable. I found no outright wrong figure in the body: the career totals (442 apps, 257 goals, 120 assists), the 191 Premier League goals, the 93 Premier League assists, the fee, the dates and the records all match club, UEFA, Premier League, LFChistory and news-agency sources. The problems are small:

1. One description is misleading. The 2023-24 paragraph implies the Africa Cup of Nations injury is why he missed the 2024 League Cup final. In fact he came back, played and scored at Brentford, then picked up a new muscle problem.
2. One cited source is wrong. The "Liverpool 2-2 Manchester City" link now opens a report of an October 2026 City win at Anfield, not the October 2021 game.
3. Two claims needed a caveat. The 47 goal involvements equalled the all-time Premier League record (Shearer and Cole set it in 42-game seasons), so "a record for a 38-game season" is true but undersells it. Some outlets give 193 Premier League goals; that figure includes his 2 Chelsea goals, and 191 is the correct Liverpool figure.
4. The draft is dated to 27 September 2026 and does not cover his time at Trabzonspor, which is going well (see below). The "Research cut-off" line should not appear in published text.

The draft has no direct quotes and does not mention Heysel or Hillsborough.

## Corrections

1. **2023-24 injury (paragraph beginning "His durability…").** The draft says: "A muscle injury suffered with Egypt at the Africa Cup of Nations disrupted 2023-24, and he was unavailable for Liverpool's League Cup final victory over Chelsea." He did injure a hamstring at AFCON in January 2024. But he came back, played and scored at Brentford on 17 February, then had a fresh muscle problem that ruled him out of the final on 25 February. The sentence should not suggest the AFCON injury itself kept him out. Sources: [Sports Mole](https://www.sportsmole.co.uk/football/liverpool/injury-news/news/salah-ruled-out-of-luton-clash-doubt-for-efl-cup-final_537093.html); [LFC, 27 Feb 2024](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/lfc-injury-update-gravenberch-endo-nunez-salah-and-szoboszlai); [Ahram (AFCON tear)](https://english.ahram.org.eg/News/516388.aspx).
2. **Sources list: "Match report: Liverpool 2-2 Manchester City" (liverpoolfc.com/…/445586-…).** That URL now opens a report of a 3-0 City win at Anfield on 11 October 2026 and does not mention Salah. Replace it with a report of 3 October 2021, for example [AP via News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/sports/2021/10/03/salah-brilliance-not-enough-as-liverpool-draws-2-2-with-city/) or [PL Goal of the Season](https://www.premierleague.com/news/2635573).
3. **47 goal involvements.** The draft says: "Those 47 combined goals and assists set a record for a 38-game Premier League season." This is accurate, but the Premier League says 47 **equals the all-time single-season record** shared with Shearer and Cole, who did it in 42-game seasons. Keep "38-game" if the wording stays, or say he equalled the overall record.
4. **"Research cut-off: 27 September 2026"** (line 23). This is an internal note. It is out of date (see Current status) and should not appear in published text.
