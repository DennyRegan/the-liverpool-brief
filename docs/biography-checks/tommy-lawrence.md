# Fact-check: Tommy Lawrence (draft: /home/claude/bios/gpt/tommy-lawrence.md)

Checked 10 November 2026. This is an independent check. The draft's own source list was used only as pointers.

## SENSITIVE FLAGS (read first)

- **Death:** the draft says he died "aged 77 in January 2018". That is confirmed. Do not add a cause of death. LFChistory says he died "after a battle with cancer and dementia", and Roy Evans (liverpoolfc.com) says he "had been unwell for some time". The draft leaves both out, which is the safest choice. Keep it that way unless the editor decides otherwise.
- **The exact date of death differs between sources:** LFChistory gives **9 January 2018**, while Wikipedia gives 10 January 2018 (the day the club announced it). The draft's "January 2018" is safe. **Do not add a day.**
- **Injury (not in the draft):** in his last Scotland cap, against Wales in 1969, he broke his nose and was carried off (Telegraph, Wikipedia). Nothing to flag in the draft itself.
- **Heysel / Hillsborough disaster:** neither is mentioned. The 1963 FA Cup semi-final against Leicester was played at Hillsborough, but the draft does not name the venue. No action needed.

## 1. Summary

The draft is highly reliable. All the major statistics are confirmed by LFChistory, the club, the Telegraph obituary and contemporary reports, as are the key match facts and the career milestones. These include the 390 appearances, the debut on 27 October 1962, the 1964 Eastham penalty save at 1–0, the 1965 Cup run and final, Inter 1965, all 42 league games in 1965–66 with 34 conceded, 24 conceded in 1968–69, the Watford defeat on 21 February 1970, the last game on 26 April 1971, three Scotland caps, Tranmere in November 1971 with 80 league appearances, and Chorley. The draft contains no direct quotations, so there are no wording risks there. The problems are one spelling, two unsourced details and one slightly embellished paraphrase.

Errors and issues:
1. **"Sigfried Held"**: the usual spelling is **Siegfried Held**. The DFB data centre does use "Sigfried", but use "Siegfried" for consistency with the Ron Yeats check and standard English-language usage.
2. **"His passing was another useful quality."**: no source found. Cut it.
3. **"Lawrence later remembered the replay for the extraordinary noise."**: the cited club article (road to Wembley 1965) could not be opened, and no other source was found. Soften it or cut it.
4. **"Evans remembered an alert player adapting to a side which pressed opponents farther up the field."**: Evans said Lawrence worked out for himself that he had to be alert when the team "pushed up". "Pressed opponents" is a modern gloss. Reword it.
5. **"He was one of five players who started every league game" (1965–66)**: this is supported only by the Wikipedia season page (Byrne, Callaghan, Lawrence, Smith, Yeats). It is probably right, but it has no second source. Soften it or verify it against the LFChistory season stats before publishing.
6. Minor: **"tried local football with Stockton Heath Albion"** has a single source (LFChistory), which says he had a trial there *as an outfield player* and did not impress. The draft's wording is acceptable.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

Died January 2018, aged 77. LFChistory gives 9 January 2018 and Wikipedia gives 10 January 2018 (in Warrington). Liverpool announced the death on Wednesday 10 January 2018 and held a minute's applause before the home match against Manchester City on 14 January 2018.
