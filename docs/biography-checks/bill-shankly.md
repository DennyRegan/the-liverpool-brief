# Fact-check: Bill Shankly (draft: /home/claude/bios/gpt/bill-shankly.md)

Checked 10 November 2026. This is an independent check. The GPT draft and its source list were used only as pointers.

## Sensitive events flag

- **Death:** the draft says "Shankly died on 29 September 1981, aged sixty-eight." This is accurate and handled plainly. The cause (a heart attack followed by cardiac arrest at Broadgreen Hospital) is not mentioned, and does not need to be.
- **Family / personal:** the retirement paragraph says "Shankly's wife Ness wanted the family to see more of him". This is sourced (LFC) and the wording is fine.
- **Post-retirement unhappiness:** "His relationship with the club became strained". This is accurate and kept general. If it is expanded, the details (feeling unwelcome at Melwood, the separate hotel at Bruges in 1976) are sourced mainly from Wikipedia and should be checked against a second source first.
- **No mention of Heysel or the Hillsborough disaster.**

## 1. Summary

The draft is highly reliable. Every date, score, scorer and honour checked is correct: the first match, the 1961–62 figures (99 goals, 62 points, eight clear, Hunt 41 in 41), Kevin Lewis's two goals against Southampton, the 5–0 title-clincher against Arsenal, the 1965 and 1974 FA Cup finals, Inter, Dortmund, Ajax, Watford, the 1973 UEFA Cup run and final, the retirement on 12 July 1974, the Charity Shield, and the death. The problems are small: one misleading wording of the appointment date, one sentence that attributes an admission to Shankly which the sources make on his behalf, and a source disagreement over the "fourteen players" figure. **The draft contains no direct quotations.**

**Errors and issues:**
1. **"He had arrived on 1 December 1959":** 1 December 1959 is the date Liverpool announced the appointment. He resigned from Huddersfield that day and actually arrived at Anfield on Monday 14 December 1959 (LFC Boot Room: "announced on December 1"; Wikipedia: arrived 14 December). Say "was appointed".
2. **"Shankly later acknowledged that loyalty had complicated the task of replacement":** the sources say he kept ageing players too long out of loyalty (LFC, "How Shankly changed the team"). Wikipedia attributes this judgement to Bob Paisley, who called it Shankly's one failing because he was "a softie at heart". No source found in which Shankly himself acknowledges it. Re-attribute it or make it neutral.
3. **"achieved with only fourteen players used in the league":** this is supported by the 1965–66 league appearance table (14 players with league appearances; Phil Chisnall is on the squad list but made none). However, the LFC Boot Room says "Liverpool used 15 players" in the title season, apparently counting all competitions. Keep "in the league" as written. Do not shorten it to "fourteen players".
4. **Record (not in the draft):** if a match record is added, the sources disagree. LFChistory gives 784 games (408 W, 198 D, 178 L) and LFC gives 784 matches. Wikipedia gives 783 (407 W, 198 D, 178 L). The one-game difference is probably the 1974 Charity Shield (1–1, won on penalties). Use "784 matches" (club and LFChistory figure). If wins are given, use 407 or 408 only with a note on how the penalty shoot-out is counted, or avoid the win total.
5. **Minor, optional:** the 1971 FA Cup final was lost 2–1 **after extra time**. The UEFA Cup final first leg was abandoned after about 25–27 minutes (sources differ, and also on whether that was 8 or 9 May). The draft gives neither the minute nor the date, which is the safe option.

## 2. Current status (as of 10 November 2026)

Deceased. Died 29 September 1981 at Broadgreen Hospital, Liverpool, certified dead at 01:20 after a cardiac arrest at 00:30, following a heart attack a few days earlier. Born 2 September 1913, so aged 68.
- LFChistory: 29 September 1981.
- Play Up Liverpool (contemporary report, 29 Sept 1981): 29 September, 1:20am, Broadgreen. That report gives his age as 67, but by arithmetic it was 68.
- Wikipedia: 29 September 1981, aged 68.
- Spartacus Educational gives 28 September, which is wrong. Ignore it.
- The draft's date and age are correct.

## 4. Corrections

1. **"He had arrived on 1 December 1959 with Liverpool in the Second Division."**
   Replace with: "He was appointed on 1 December 1959, with Liverpool in the Second Division."

2. **"Shankly later acknowledged that loyalty had complicated the task of replacement."**
   Replace with: "Loyalty complicated the task of replacement; Bob Paisley later called it Shankly's one failing, that he was 'a softie at heart'." Alternatively, neutral wording: "Loyalty to those players complicated the task of replacing them."

3. **"A second championship followed in 1965–66, achieved with only fourteen players used in the league."**
   This is correct as written. Do not shorten it to "fourteen players" without "in the league": the LFC Boot Room says 15 were used across the season.

4. **Optional:** "Liverpool lost the 1971 FA Cup final 2–1 to Arsenal". Could add "after extra time". This is not an error.
