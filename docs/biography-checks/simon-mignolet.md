## Corrections

1. **January 2018, Klopp's reasoning.** Draft: "His public explanation was that the German had earned the opportunity through his performances and training." I found no source in which Klopp says this. The reports of 14–15 January 2018 (FourFourTwo, SI, This Is Anfield) record only "He's the number one, he might make mistakes but this is life" and "When it comes to goalkeepers you have to make a decision and stick with it. He will stay in goal." The Independent link in the draft could not be opened in this session. Unless Denny can confirm that page says it, reword to something like: "Klopp named Karius his number one, saying that with goalkeepers you have to make a decision and stick with it."

2. **December 2016, "Klopp praised his readiness".** The transcript does not say this. Klopp said Mignolet "played really well at the beginning of the season" and "Now it is Simon's chance", and that the change was made because Karius had not shown his quality in recent games ("that's all, nothing else"; "no timeframe" for his return). "Without becoming a final judgement on either goalkeeper" is a fair reading of "that's all, nothing else" and "no timeframe". "Readiness" is not supported. Suggested fix: "Klopp said Mignolet had played really well at the start of the season and that it was now his chance."

3. **Stoke 2017, the Berahino chance.** Draft: "Saido Berahino met a low ball across the penalty area." Both reports describe a cross from Arnautovic: "to the back post" (LFC) and "teasing" (Sky). Neither says it was low. Suggested fix: "met Marko Arnautovic's cross at the back post".
