## 1. Summary

The draft is largely reliable. Every career total, season appearance figure, date, final, scoreline and honour I checked against LFChistory, the official club and UEFA is correct, and the one direct quotation ("safety net") is word for word. The problems are one unsourced family detail, two pieces of reported speech I could not find, a substitution minute on which the sources disagree, a slight overstatement of the 2013 Premier League finding, and the toe/foot wording. Separately, the frontmatter does not fit the site's strict `BiographySchema` (see the note at the end).

Errors and weak points:
1. "His father coached": no source found. Cut.
2. "His brother played at TSV 1860 Munich": the only source is Wikipedia (Matthias Hamann, mainly with 1860 Munich). Soften or cut.
3. 2006 FA Cup final, "He came on after 70 minutes": LFChistory says 67 minutes, 11v11 says 71. Both are after West Ham's 64th-minute goal, so "with Liverpool behind" is right. Avoid giving a minute.
4. Istanbul injury: at the time it was reported as a broken toe (UEFA, 9 June 2005). The club now calls it "a broken foot" (Liverpool's Greatest No.74). The Voetbal International recollection (pain in the right big toe late in extra time) could not be opened, so it is unverified. Recommend neutral wording.
5. "Benítez had told him to expect fewer games, and that he already felt he had played less than he wanted": I could not find this as Hamann's own account. The Manchester City interview it points to could not be opened. Secondary sources say only that he had fallen down the pecking order behind Gerrard, Alonso and Sissoko. Rephrase.
6. "A subsequent Premier League inquiry confirmed his Bolton registration and found the transfer in order": the inquiry was in 2013, after Phil Gartside's talkSPORT claim. It found that Bolton had registered Hamann. "Found the transfer in order" goes slightly beyond the sources, and "subsequent" hides a seven-year gap.
7. Minor: at Bayern he also won the German League Cup (LFChistory). The draft leaves it out, which is not wrong.

## 4. Corrections

1. "His father coached and his brother played at TSV 1860 Munich."
   → "His younger brother, Matthias, also became a professional footballer." Better still, cut the sentence: the father claim is unsourced, and the 1860 detail rests on Wikipedia alone.

2. "Contemporary reports described the injury as a broken toe." (and the preceding Voetbal International sentence)
   → "He had broken a bone in his foot, reported at the time as a broken toe, and did not realise until after the match." Drop the "right big toe late in extra time" detail unless the Voetbal International article can be checked.

3. "He came on after 70 minutes, with Liverpool behind, …"
   → "He came on midway through the second half, with Liverpool behind, …" (The sources give 67' and 71'.)

4. "Hamann later explained that Benítez had told him to expect fewer games, and that he already felt he had played less than he wanted."
   → "With Gerrard, Alonso and Sissoko ahead of him, he had slipped down Benítez's pecking order and made only 17 league appearances that season." (Supported by inkl and LFChistory.) Keep the original only if the Manchester City interview is checked and says this.

5. "A subsequent Premier League inquiry confirmed his Bolton registration and found the transfer in order."
   → "When Bolton's chairman later claimed the club had never signed him, a Premier League investigation in 2013 found that Bolton had registered him."

6. "Hamann explained at his unveiling that he had quickly concluded the Bolton move was wrong for him and had obtained permission to speak to other clubs."
   Supported by Hamann's quoted words ("I quickly realised that going to them would not be the right move for me") and LFChistory (Bolton allowed him to talk to other clubs). No change needed.
