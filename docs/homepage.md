# Concise homepage

Home preserves the shared masthead, Search link, six navigation links, fonts, colours and footer. Its only content areas, in order, are one editorial lead, optional timely match coverage, an optional compact Brief, and the wrapping `Explore more: Articles · Analysis · History · Match Centre` line. Other reading and History discovery remain available on their existing pages.

## Selection and publication boundaries

`getWriting()` remains the source of published Opinion/Analysis, including legacy canonical `/archive/<slug>` writing. Factual History is excluded. Future publication dates are excluded using today's Europe/London date. Order is publication date descending, then canonical URL ascending. The standfirst is a compact plain-text excerpt; source prose and metadata are never edited. The newest article remains visible beyond 14 days with its actual date and no freshness claim. Without an eligible article, a neutral heading and Articles link appear.

`lib/content/homepage-override.json` is `null` by default. To pin already-published eligible writing, use `{ "href": "/articles/existing-slug", "startsAt": "2026-10-10T09:00:00+01:00", "expiresAt": "2026-10-12T09:00:00+01:00" }`. These example dates are illustrative, not an active selection. Only canonical Articles or legacy individual Archive URLs are accepted. Both timestamps need explicit offsets; expiry must follow the start by no more than seven elapsed days. The active interval includes start and excludes expiry. An expired, unavailable, factual or future-dated target falls back to newest eligible writing. Malformed configuration fails validation. This configuration cannot publish writing or alter its canonical URL.

Match coverage reuses `getMatchCentre`, stable fixture IDs and `selectMatches` without changing fixture selection or data. Only its selected next scheduled fixture can supply the `/match-centre#match-preview` link, ensuring the destination actually renders that preview. A stored approved preview and confirmed kick-off are required. Eligibility starts exactly 48 elapsed hours before kick-off and ends exactly at kick-off. A preview timestamp in the future cannot qualify. Postponed, cancelled, undated or time-TBC fixtures do not qualify for previews; elapsed scheduled fixtures never become reviews.

A review requires the selected latest explicitly completed fixture and its existing available published factual report referenced by `reportSlug`. Future match/report dates are excluded. It expires at Europe/London midnight starting the third day after matchday: a Friday match expires at the start of Monday. Missing kick-off is safe because completed fixtures have a verified date and score. Delayed results/report publication cannot restart the window, and an unavailable report on the latest completed fixture does not revive an older report. A qualifying preview takes precedence over a qualifying review. Only the heading, match date/time and reading link are shown; no match prose, fixture list or table is reproduced. Rescheduling uses the same record with its revised date/time. No qualifying coverage means no block.

Brief requires `status: published`, an explicit-offset valid `lastUpdated`, and age from zero inclusive to 48 elapsed hours exclusive. Empty, stale, invalid and future-dated updates are hidden entirely. Show at most three nonempty distinct headlines in source order, deduplicating normalised whitespace/case, with the genuine timestamp and one `/brief` link. No summary or “today/current” claim is added. PR #64's all-headlines proposal remains separate; this approved limit supersedes it for the homepage.

The homepage retains `dynamic = 'force-dynamic'` under the installed Next.js 16.2.11 configuration (Cache Components are not enabled). One server clock is passed into pure selectors per request. Do not cache the timed projection or statically generate Home. Fresh requests evaluate expiry without scheduled redeployments. Already-open documents update on reload/navigation; new coverage/data still follows normal approved content deployments. All calendar display and date boundaries use Europe/London, including BST/GMT; elapsed-hour windows compare absolute instants. Selection automates neither writing nor publication.
