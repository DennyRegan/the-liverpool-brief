# Independent factual cross-review: 1963–64 to 1965–66

Reviewed on 9 September 2026 by the researcher who authored the 1959–63 batch. The three JSON records were read separately from the original author's QA notes, and the sources below were retrieved for this check. The review covers manager, league position, competitive scoring, cups, transfers, season-specific key players, important dates, venues and chronology. No changes were made to the other author's season files.

## Findings and resolution

1. **Resolved: Furnell newspaper attribution in 1963–64.** The source named `mirror-furnell` and its research note call the report a Daily Mirror transcription. The retrieved [Furnell to the Gunners](https://playupliverpool.com/1963/11/21/furnell-to-the-gunners/) explicitly credits the **Daily Express, 22 November 1963**. Its £16,000 Arsenal amount does disagree with LFChistory's £15,000, so omitting the fee is right. Root corrected the source ID to `express-furnell`, the source claims and the research prose. This is an attribution correction, not a new dispute about the amount.
2. That same report recalls Furnell's earlier Burnley arrival as £15,000, versus LFChistory's £18,000. The reviewer has already removed the incoming fee from the owned 1961–62 entry and added the conflicting source. This follow-up is recorded here to keep the two batches consistent.

No incorrect league finish, competitive top-scorer total, cup result or major event date was found in the reviewed 1963–66 records. The original author has correctly distinguished shared Shields, the two stages of the kit change, and the corrected Hunt goal total.

## 1963–64

**League and manager:** RSSSF confirms Liverpool first on 57 points, four ahead of Manchester United, with 92 goals scored. The narrative correctly places the title in Shankly's second season back in the First Division. The 17-year gap follows the 1947 title. The seven successive wins after the 14 March defeat at Fulham and the 18 April Arsenal clincher match the fixture sequence; three league fixtures remained.

**Scoring and cups:** The specialist scoring breakdown and Liverpool's official 30-goal article support Hunt's 33 competitive goals, not just his 31 league goals. The additional figures for St John and Arrowsmith are consistent with the season table. Swansea Town's 2–1 win at Anfield on 29 February was round six, correctly presented as the quarter-final. No League Cup, European or Charity Shield entry should be added.

**Players and transfers:** The appearance table supports Thompson playing all 42 league games, and the core selection has a clear basis in the season. Arrowsmith's 24 competitive appearances and substantial scoring contribution justify inclusion. Melia is represented in the narrative and transfers despite not being in the key-player array; no arbitrary quota is imposed. Thompson from Preston and Chisnall from Manchester United agree with the retrieved transfer table and club accounts. Chisnall's April signing and August competitive debut are correctly separated. Lewis's August departure followed Thompson's arrival; Furnell left in November; Melia's £48,000 March transfer is independently supported by the reproduced contemporary report. The table includes all four listed departures and deliberately omits developmental John Sealey.

## 1964–65

**League and manager:** Seventh place with 44 points agrees with RSSSF. The fixture table supports the autumn slide, reaching 21st after the September Everton defeat and remaining in the lower part of the table into November. Shankly remains the manager throughout.

**Scoring and competitions:** Hunt's 37 is independently confirmed by the official club article. The national-cup route, replays against Stockport and Leicester, 1 May final, extra time, Hunt/Bremner/St John sequence, Callaghan's winning assist and Byrne's injury are corroborated by the official FA Cup history. The European sequence agrees with RSSSF: Reykjavik 11–1 overall, Anderlecht 4–0, three drawn Cologne games decided by a coin toss, then 3–4 overall against Internazionale. The shared 2–2 Shield against West Ham was at Anfield. No League Cup is appropriate.

**Dates and players:** The European debut was 17 August 1964. The kit article explicitly distinguishes red shorts on 25 November from red socks completing the kit on 16 December; the JSON preserves this. Cologne's Rotterdam replay date is 24 March. The FA Cup final on 1 May and Anfield semi-final on 4 May are three days apart; the Milan return was 12 May. Smith and Lawler's appearance totals support their emergence as regulars. Strong's 16 appearances and FA Cup final role justify inclusion without pretending he was an ever-present.

**Transfers:** Strong's Arsenal origin, £40,000 amount and November date are supported by both the specialist register and official obituary. A'Court's final September Reykjavik appearance and October Tranmere departure agree with the official account. George Scott, who did not play a competitive senior game, is appropriately omitted. The £9,000 A'Court amount is in the specialist register; the retrieved official article corroborates the move but not that amount.

## 1965–66

**League and manager:** RSSSF confirms champions on 61 points, six ahead of Leeds, scoring 79 and conceding 34. The fixture sequence confirms first place from 17 November onwards, with the Chelsea win on 30 April followed by one remaining league fixture. Shankly is consistently identified by the club histories.

**Scoring:** The accepted 32 competitive goals are correct. Liverpool's official article independently states 32, and LFChistory explains the old extra goal: the 12 March Tottenham winner was a Ted Clayton own goal, not a Hunt goal. The resulting 29 league, one FA Cup and two European goals are the right breakdown. The note explicitly investigates the discrepancy instead of selecting a convenient total.

**Cups and dates:** The FA Cup defence ended against Chelsea in round three, at Anfield. The Cup Winners' Cup sequence is confirmed by RSSSF, including overturned first-leg deficits against Juventus and Celtic. The Celtic second leg was 19 April, and the final was at Hampden on 5 May. Hunt's equaliser preceded Libuda's extra-time winner. The shared 2–2 Charity Shield was at Old Trafford. The September 5–0 derby and Anfield venue match the fixture register.

**Players and transfers:** The appearances table confirms 14 league players, 15 across all competitions and the five named players making 53 appearances each. Chisnall's Europe-only appearance explains the different squad counts. This is a sound season-specific key-player selection. Aberdeen independently confirms Ogston's September arrival as reserve cover but supplies a different exact date and amount from LFChistory; omission of both is appropriate. Thomson's Luton destination and eight Liverpool appearances agree with the official and specialist profiles retrieved during the first-batch research. The Ferns source was blocked by a browser challenge on this review pass, so its £5,000 corroboration is the original author's retrieval, not a newly verified document in this review.

## Limits and editorial considerations

Core outcomes and major dates have strong corroboration. Some lower-profile fees, such as Jones's £5,000 and A'Court's £9,000, are stated by the specialist transfer register but not repeated in the corroborating biography. This is weaker than the contemporary evidence available for Melia or the independent club confirmation for Strong; do not describe every fee as independently established by two sources. No contradictory amount was found for those examples in this review.

The review is factual and editorial, not a build or browser test. It does not certify every sentence in every linked biography: several biographies contain unrelated mistakes, which are not imported into these entries. The Furnell attribution correction is resolved. No additional change to these three published season records is required by the evidence retrieved here.

Root also replaced the early-batch Fred Morris legacy source URL with the current official player profile after direct retrieval. The source change does not alter the accepted summer 1960 move to Crewe.
