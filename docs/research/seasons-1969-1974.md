# Seasons 1969–70 to 1973–74: research and claim audit

Research completed 13 September 2026 before drafting the summaries. Scope: the next five records in the existing season system. Starting source revision: `53372bc` (origin/main); working branch: `feature/history-seasons-1969-1974`.

## Method and verified baseline

Retrieved each season’s LFChistory results, scoring breakdown, appearances and transfer ledger, RSSSF league table and European results, and Football Club History Database FA Cup results. Cross-checked League Cup outcomes against RSSSF’s competition history. Official Liverpool accounts corroborate the major signings, managerial chronology and milestones; UEFA corroborates the 1973 final. 11v11’s retrieved all-competition tables independently corroborate the leading totals for 1970–71, 1971–72 and 1973–74. Sources are scoped to the claims actually checked, not endorsed wholesale.

## Conflicts, exclusions and boundary decisions

- **Keegan fee:** LFChistory and Liverpool’s *Second Coming* article say £33,000; Liverpool’s [signing anniversary](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/219441-keegan-joins-reds-for-35-000) says £35,000. No fee is published. His 3 May 1971 signing is allocated to 1971–72, the incoming campaign, consistent with the existing St John convention. It is not duplicated in 1970–71.
- **Whitham:** the LFChistory 1969–70 ledger gives 28 April 1970, after Liverpool’s last league game on 18 April. Included as a preparatory arrival for 1970–71. Liverpool’s profile loosely says six months after Hunt’s departure; that interval is not reproduced. The exact signing day and fee are omitted from the page.
- **Heighway:** LFChistory marks the move “Free” with an explanatory asterisk. Without resolving compensation terminology, describe a permanent move from Skelmersdale United and omit the fee; do not infer a zero cost.
- **St John:** the ledger records a free transfer on 18 May 1971, rather than a direct move to Coventry. The page records departure into free agency; it makes no claim about the intervening Hellenic spell or its exact terms.
- **Case:** the 1972–73 ledger includes May 1973, but Liverpool’s retrospective explicitly places him among the summer additions for 1973–74. Allocate once to 1973–74. He is an incoming young player, not a key player in the first team that season. His competitive debut was in 1975; no invented 1973–74 appearance.
- **Spiring:** the 1973–74 ledger contains a 6 March 1973 signing date. This is not evidence of a 1973–74 arrival. Omitted from the selected significant senior moves; he does not appear in either season’s first-team appearance ledger. A future fuller transfer inventory should research the original March 1973 move and allocate it to 1972–73.
- **Peter Thompson:** the official profile’s “1963–1972” heading describes playing appearances, not the end of his contract. It separately confirms a 1973 Bolton loan followed by a permanent move. The January 1974 transfer is included once, with that loan context and without an uncorroborated fee or precise loan day.
- **1973 UEFA Cup:** Liverpool’s [2018 title anniversary](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/301341-liverpool-0-0-leicester-april-28-1973) incorrectly says 5–0 on aggregate. The verified results are 3–0 and 0–2, hence 3–2, supported by UEFA, RSSSF, LFChistory and Liverpool’s later *Second Coming* account. The completed first leg was on 10 May, after the 9 May abandonment. No goals or appearance from the abandoned game are added.
- **1974 managerial chronology:** the FA Cup final on 4 May was not Shankly’s last competitive fixture: the 1–1 draw at Tottenham followed on 8 May. Retirement was announced on 12 July, after the campaign. Shankly remains the sole manager for this record; Paisley and the 1974 Charity Shield belong to the next batch. Ray Kennedy’s July 1974 signing also belongs there.
- **Other unused narrative claims:** no invented motivation, atmosphere, tactical turning point, private conversation or causal claim about a single defeat forcing the rebuild. The official Graham obituary has problematic injury/return wording outside its sound 1969–70 total and March 1972 departure claims; those passages are not used. Callaghan’s official profile contains a questionable 492-appearance record statement; only its 1974 Football Writers’ award claim is used. The Arsenal retrospective’s final equaliser attribution is not relied on.
- **Retrieval limits:** direct 11v11 requests returned 403, but the full relevant tables were retrieved through web browsing. Some old Liverpool history/soccerschools URLs redirect to the homepage or membership pages; those redirects are not treated as historical evidence. UEFA timed out through the direct downloader but its full article was retrieved through web browsing. An indexed Chelsea programme was investigated but is not needed or cited in the records.
- **Selection:** key-player lists follow current-season competitive involvement, with important cup contributors retained. They are not a fixed XI. Transfers are selected senior moves plus Case’s historically important external recruitment, not a complete registration ledger. Fees are optional; only the independently corroborated £40,000 Waddle fee is included in this batch. No omitted fee is presented as zero.
