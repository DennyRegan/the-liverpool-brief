## Scope and resolved discrepancies

- All competition outcomes were checked against the full fixture lists; aggregate scores are arithmetic from the legs. Key players are editorial selections supported by appearances, not an objective ranking.
- The 1985–86 Football League Super Cup final was played in September 1986. Reaching it is recorded in 1985–86; winning it is recorded in 1986–87. The edition and playing season are explicitly distinguished.
- Rush's 1985–86 total is 33 including two Super Cup goals. 11v11 lists 31 without those games; the Guardian account independently describes his two goals in the final group match at Tottenham. The difference is competition scope.
- The official Dalglish profile calls 1986–87 empty-handed, but the official Shield history and the Super Cup results establish two honours. The broad statement is not repeated.
- The Guardian Super Cup article is a credited republication on LFChistory. It has some incidental chronology discrepancies, so its scope is restricted to competition context, the two Rush group goals and the delayed final scores, checked against fixtures.
- The 1988 short-format centenary tournament is distinct from the 1988–89 full-match Centenary Trophy. The latter is included in the competitive record.
- Liverpool and Arsenal ended 1988–89 on 76 points and +37 goal difference. Arsenal won on goals scored, 73 to 65, not goal difference.
- Hillsborough's 97 is the eventual death toll, not the number who died on 15 April. The official memorial supplies this fact. A secondary FA Cup season account corroborates abandonment and rescheduling only; the completed match result and venue come from the fixture archive. No redemption narrative links the disaster to later results.
- Heysel's deaths and circumstances are supported by the official memorial and Reuters. Fagan's retirement decision preceded the disaster according to the club profile.

## Transfer boundaries and exclusions

Walsh's May 1984 arrival is included in 1984–85 as promised in the prior audit. Rush's physical July 1987 departure is placed in 1987–88, without asserting contract or loan-back terms; his August 1988 return is recorded in 1988–89. Aldridge's September 1989 exit belongs to the next batch. Unverified retirement dates, post-release destinations, fees and motives are omitted. Senior moves are selected, not an exhaustive youth and reserve ledger.

Publication is authorised by Denny's instruction to publish five, verify them live and immediately continue. No schedule is used.
