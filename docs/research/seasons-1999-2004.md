## Conflicts, exclusions and boundaries

- LFChistory season IDs are 43, 42, 44, 45 and 46. Headings were checked because the first pair is not chronological.
- The official Houllier profile describes six final winless fixtures in 1999–2000. The dated record shows a win at Wimbledon on 16 April followed by five scoreless league games. The summary uses the fixture record and the discrepancy remains documented.
- The three-cup treble belongs to 2000–01. The Charity Shield and UEFA Super Cup were won in August 2001, during 2001–02. Five trophies in calendar 2001 must not be recorded as five trophies in one season.
- The 2001 League Cup final was 1–1 after extra time and 5–4 on penalties. The UEFA Cup final was 5–4 after extra time, settled by Geli’s golden own goal. Shoot-out kicks are not included in scorer totals.
- Owen’s 2001–02 total includes qualifying games and both August trophies. His European Footballer of the Year award concerns calendar 2001 and was received during 2001–02.
- Houllier remained the appointed manager during his health absence. Thompson is included as temporary cover. The illness during the 13 October Leeds match and the 19 March Roma return are verified separately.
- The existing european-cup competition identity is reused for the Champions League, with its contemporary name stated in result text and summaries. The existing charity-shield identity likewise covers the renamed Community Shield. No duplicate competition system or identities were introduced.
- The 2002–03 opening 12-match unbeaten league run and subsequent 11-match winless league run were counted from fixtures. Cup wins occurred during the latter: it is not an all-competition winless run.
- RSSSF’s European table confirms Liverpool finished third in their first Champions League group in 2002–03 and transferred to the UEFA Cup. The UEFA Cup fifth round was the quarter-final.
- The Gerrard captaincy retrospective has a 14 October 2014 publication timestamp but explicitly states 15 October 2003 in its body. The summary uses October, keeping publication and historical event dates separate.
- McManaman’s physical summer 1999 departure is recorded here. Hyypiä and Šmicer’s May 1999 arrivals and Rob Jones’s May departure belong to the incoming campaign.
- McAllister’s 13 May 2002 Coventry departure is allocated to 2002–03. Anelka’s May 2002 loan ending remains with his 2001–02 loan spell. No move is duplicated.
- Le Tallec and Sinama-Pongolle are recorded joining the squad in 2003 without making an unverified claim about earlier agreements. Babbel’s Blackburn loan is verified by his club profile; its compressed headline playing years are not used as departure dates.
- Heskey’s May 2004 departure and Owen’s summer 2004 transfer are reserved for 2004–05. Houllier’s departure is described as following his final campaign; Benítez did not manage a competitive match in 2003–04.
- Selected senior transfers are not an exhaustive youth/reserve ledger. Unverified fees, motives, quotations and atmosphere are omitted.

Publication is explicitly authorised by the continuing five-season instruction. No schedule is used.
