## Decisions and boundaries

- LFChistory archive IDs are 36, 37, 39, 40 and 41 respectively. They were confirmed from page headings and navigation, not assumed to be consecutive.
- Fowler’s exact Arsenal hat-trick time is omitted: the official profile states four minutes 35 seconds, while differing timings circulate. The date, opponents, three goals and 3–0 result are verified without a speed-record claim.
- All leading-scorer totals cover competitive matches. Owen’s 18 league goals in each of the final two seasons are distinct from his 23 competitive goals. Both Golden Boots were shared, as confirmed by the Premier League’s full award register.
- Cup aggregates are calculated from both fixture legs. No penalty shoot-out goals or friendly goals enter the player totals.
- Grobbelaar and Whelan’s ledger entries show free transfers. Their LFChistory profiles establish Southampton and Southend United as destinations. No unverified Whelan managerial appointment date is included.
- Rush’s 20 May 1996 Leeds move followed the previous campaign’s final competitive match and is allocated to the incoming 1996–97 season. Collymore’s 13 May 1997 Villa move followed the 11 May finale and is similarly allocated to 1997–98. Each page retains the prior ledger needed to audit this boundary. Neither move is duplicated.
- Kvarme’s profile identifies Rosenborg as his previous club and confirms the free transfer in January 1997. Staunton’s official profile confirms his return from Aston Villa in summer 1998.
- Houllier and Evans became joint managers on 16 July 1998; Evans left on 12 November. These are separate from the 10 November Tottenham League Cup defeat. The club’s narrative and the dated managerial register agree.
- McManaman’s physical summer 1999 departure is reserved for 1999–2000. General accounts of Houllier’s rebuilding compress transfers across dates; dated ledgers determine allocation.
- Transfers are selected senior moves. Unverified fees, motives, atmosphere, injury explanations and player-attitude narratives are omitted.

Publication is explicitly authorised by the user’s instruction to publish five, verify them live and immediately continue. No schedule is used.
