## 1974-75

### Claim mapping and editorial decisions

- Liverpool FC attributes the 1974 Charity Shield to Paisley; LFChistory attributes it to Shankly and gives a later formal handover. The match is not attributed to either manager in the overview. Paisley’s league-season management is undisputed.
- The Charity Shield is included as a trophy, so this campaign is not described as trophyless. Transfer fees are omitted. Storton’s destination is styled Chester, the contemporary club name, rather than the archive’s later Chester City label.
- Overview evidence: paragraph 1 — lfc-paisley, rsssf-league, lfc-shield and lfch-shield-match; paragraph 2 — lfch-transfers, lfc-ray, lfc-neal, lfc-mcdermott and lfch-scorers; paragraph 3 — lfch-games.
