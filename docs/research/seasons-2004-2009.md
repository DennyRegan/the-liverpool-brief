# Season research audit: 2004–05 to 2008–09

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Research was completed before drafting. Publication is authorised by the user’s instruction to publish five seasons and immediately continue.

## Method

Checked full LFChistory fixtures, scoring, appearances and transfer ledgers against RSSSF league tables and Liverpool FC profiles and honours pages. Contemporary Reuters reporting supports the ownership agreement. All competitive scoring includes qualifying rounds and standalone competitive trophies, excluding friendlies and shoot-out kicks. Transfers are selected senior moves, with preparatory summer moves assigned to the incoming campaign. Existing season schema, routes and design are retained.
