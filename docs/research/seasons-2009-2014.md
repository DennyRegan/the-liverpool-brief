# Season research audit: 2009–10 to 2013–14

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Research completed before drafting. Publication authorised by the user’s instruction to publish five and immediately continue.

## Method

Read full LFChistory fixture lists, scoring, appearances, transfer ledgers and final tables. Corroborated leading scorers and key events with Liverpool FC profiles, UEFA contemporary reports, Sky Sports and Reuters. Consulted the primary 2013 FA decision and the Hillsborough Independent Panel report. RSSSF pages for these years were unavailable; none is cited as evidence. Existing schema, canonical identities, routes and design are retained.
