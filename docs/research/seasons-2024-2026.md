# Season research audit: 2024–25 and 2025–26

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Research completed before drafting. Publication authorised by Denny’s repeated instruction to continue. These are the final two completed campaigns in the existing Shankly-to-present scope; no incomplete 2026–27 page is created to fill a five-season batch.

## Method

Read both complete competitive fixture lists, final league tables, goalscorer tables, appearance records and transfer ledgers. Corroborated titles, managerial tenure, cup stages and farewells against official Liverpool FC records, and shoot-outs against Reuters. Used the club’s own announcements for Jota’s death and tribute, and the contemporary emergency-services statement for the parade incident. Retained the existing schema, design and routes.
