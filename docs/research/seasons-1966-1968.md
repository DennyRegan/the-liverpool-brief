# Seasons research and editorial QA: 1966–67 to 1968–69

Reviewed 9 September 2026. These three entries were researched individually, using retrieved club histories, season-specific LFChistory records, independent RSSSF competition tables and Football Club History Database cup results. 11v11 provides independent scorer cross-checks for 1966–67 and 1968–69; Liverpool FC independently confirms the 1967–68 total. No existing Archive text was copied or altered.

This log distinguishes a strong source for a particular claim from blanket confidence in everything on that website. Source descriptions and confidence ratings are also carried in each JSON file. Statistics and contemporary match records take precedence over retrospective prose where they conflict.

Editorial choices and limitations:

- The £65,000 Hughes fee is supported by a newly retrieved current official article, [Liverpool’s Greatest: Emlyn Hughes](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/liverpools-greatest-no17-emlyn-hughes). An older Soccer Schools profile was retrieved through web indexing but its live URL redirects to the home page; it has been replaced in the published source list. The debut date is separately supported by [LFChistory’s Hughes profile](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/324).
- Peter Wall and Stuart Mason arrived together from Wrexham. LFChistory flags their fees as requiring linked-deal information. Wall is included because he went on to appear in the first team within the covered period; Mason never did and is omitted under the instruction to avoid insignificant youth/reserve registrations. Wall’s individual fee is not published.
- Wilson is a senior signing, though unsuccessful, and therefore belongs in the arrival record. His [official profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/dave-wilson) incorrectly describes his sole appearance as away at Blackpool. The [specialist profile](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/446) and fixture record establish Anfield, 13 May 1967. The published entry does not reproduce the wrong venue.
- The June 1966 departures of Robert Lowry and Mike Sealey were considered in coordination with the preceding-season researcher. Both were fringe players and are omitted from this concise senior-transfer selection. Alan Hignett’s August departure is also excluded because he had no Liverpool competitive first-team appearances. This is not represented as a complete registration ledger.
- Clemence’s June 1967 move belongs to 1967–68 under the brief’s summer convention, despite appearing in LFChistory’s 1966–67 transfer register. Billy Molyneux’s June 1967 departure, a reserve goalkeeper with one earlier competitive appearance, is not a significant transfer for the new first-team season.
- 11v11 and LFChistory agree on Hunt’s 19 but differ on individual attribution of one other goal: Callaghan/Strong are 5/11 in 11v11 and 4/12 in LFChistory. Their individual goal totals are not published here. This does not affect the top-scorer conclusion.

Disagreements requiring preservation:

1. **Hunt’s overall scoring-record match.** The [official profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/roger-hunt) associates the record with his brace against TSV Munich on 7 November 1967; the [current LFChistory profile](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/326) identifies Manchester United on 11 November. [LFChistory’s correction article](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/3453) explains why the 12 March 1966 winner against Tottenham was reassigned from Hunt to a Ted Clayton own goal, using television and contemporary reporting. This changes career totals and milestones. The published event uses **November 1967**, leaves `date` absent, and tells the reader why the precise match is disputed. It does not silently adopt the uncorrected club chronology.
2. **Cross-season implication of that correction.** The corrected 1965–66 Hunt tally is 29 league goals and 32 competitive goals, not the older 30/33. The 1965–66 author and root reviewer were notified. The correct final career total is 285, not 286. This does not alter the independently checked 1967–68 total of 30. The separate league-record event belongs on 1 February 1969 under both LFC’s current specific article and LFChistory’s corrected chronology.
3. **Clemence’s signing day.** The [transfer register](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/transfers/8) says 12 June; an [LFChistory interview](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/846) says 24 June; the [LFC anniversary article](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/121211-years-ago-clemence-arrives), published 21 June 2012, implies 21 June. All agree on the relevant summer, origin club and £18,000 fee. No exact day is published. These specific sources are graded medium for their conflicting chronology rather than their agreed transfer facts.
4. **Chisnall/Wallace fees.** Retrieved [LFChistory book extract](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/2835/page/3) gives Chisnall’s fee as £12,000; discovery accounts give other figures. [LFChistory’s Crewe transfer history](https://www.lfchistory.net/transfers/by-club/134) gives Wallace £5,000, while other transfer listings describe a free move. Neither fee is published. The departures themselves are well supported.
5. **Stevenson’s last appearance.** Both retrospective profiles use wording implying the Wolves league game on 25 November was his only final appearance; the [specialist profile’s own statistics](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/420) and season records show the Ferencváros match on 28 November afterwards. The entry says only that he left in December. His source confidence is limited where the retrospective text is concerned.

Editorial choices and limitations:

- [LFC’s current Hunt milestone article](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/day-roger-hunt-scores-set-liverpool-fc-record) states 1 February 1969, the Sheffield Wednesday winner, and 234 league goals. The published event follows that account and distinguishes the league record from the November 1967 overall record.
- Lindsay and Lloyd were significant senior signings but neither made a Liverpool competitive appearance this season. Clemence made one cup appearance. None appears in the key-player list because of future fame. Evans, by contrast, played 33 league games and 39 overall, and belongs in it.
- Peter Wall’s 17 appearances and Bobby Graham’s 16 were considered; the key list concentrates on the regular XI. Gerry Byrne made three appearances; his later retirement is not moved backwards into this season. John Ogston’s reserve-goalkeeper loan is omitted as insufficiently significant to this first-team narrative.
- Exact ages are unnecessary to the Evans entry. The [official profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/alun-evans) and specialist prose call him 19, while the [specialist profile’s birth date](https://www.lfchistory.net/players/295) is 30 September 1949, making him 18 at the September signing and debut. The [season statistics](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/statistics/10) calculate 18 years 357 days at debut. Only the move, fee and performances are published. The official profile also mixes some league and overall totals for later seasons, which are not drawn into this entry.
- Lloyd’s precise signing date differs between acceptance-of-offer and completed-transfer accounts. The published transfer note says April 1969, with no exact event date. The origin club and fee are consistently supported.
- Athletic Club’s English translation contains an unrelated geographical slip in the surrounding retrospective text. Its core claim about the Liverpool tie and coin toss is independently supported by RSSSF; no geographical aside has been reproduced.

## Source access and structural checks

All listed sources were actually retrieved through web tools during this research, rather than generated from memory. Some sites restrict direct automated HTTP requests: root’s link audit received a 403 bot challenge from the 11v11 1967 table despite successful web retrieval. This is an access restriction, not a claim that the underlying reference is missing; universal automated accessibility is not asserted.

Only these three new season records, this QA log and the merged entity additions are owned by this author. Shared registry updates are delegated to root. IDs use the existing canonical person/competition model. All source IDs are local to their season; no undated milestone is assigned an invented ISO date. Related seasons connect the preceding title and the sequence of squad renewal, and are restricted to the ten available records.

The three JSON files are checked against `SeasonSchema` for required fields, valid dates, season identifiers, unique IDs/URLs, referenced sources, and trophy/competition consistency. Root performs final collection/entity, route, build and browser checks after merging entity proposals.
