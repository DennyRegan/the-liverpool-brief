# Season research audit: 2019–20 to 2023–24

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Research completed before drafting. User authorised publication in batches of five followed immediately by the next batch.

## Method

Read each full competitive fixture list, final league table, scoring table, transfer ledger and appearance record at LFChistory. Corroborated major developments with Liverpool FC profiles, contemporary reporting and the primary independent review of the Paris final. Retained the existing schema, competition identities, routes and design. Competitive goals include all competitive fixtures, excluding friendlies and shoot-out kicks. Transfers are selected senior moves, with preparatory summer moves assigned to the incoming campaign.
