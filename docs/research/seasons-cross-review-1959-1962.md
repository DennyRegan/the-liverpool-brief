# Independent cross-review: 1959–60 to 1962–63

Reviewed 9 September 2026 by the researcher responsible for the later 1966–69 batch, independently of the author of these four entries. The JSON narratives, structured facts, event dates, competition lists, key-player selections and transfer references were read against separately retrieved sources. The reviewer did not edit these four content files; corrections were sent to the author through the coordinating task.

## Findings resolved during review

1. **1960–61, Liddell’s final appearance:** the original event said the 31 August 1960 defeat was “at Southampton”. It was at **Anfield**; the away defeat at The Dell was on 24 August. The [season fixture record](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/games/2) distinguishes them. The revised file now says “1–0 home defeat by Southampton”.
2. **1962–63, November Burnley defeat:** the original overview placed it away. The 3 November defeat was at **Anfield**, as shown in the [season fixture record](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/games/4). Revised text verified.
3. **1962–63, December Blackburn victory:** the original event said “at Blackburn”. The 22 December match was at **Anfield**. Revised event verified against the same record.
4. **1962–63, Dick White transfer source:** the earlier source object pointed to the preceding season’s transfer page, which did not contain his departure. The [1962–63 register](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/transfers/4) does. The corrected transfer row cites that register and his profile; the misleading unused source object has been removed.
5. **1960–61, Kevin Lewis transfer reference:** his June 1960 arrival is in the [1959–60 register](https://www.lfchistory.net/season-archive/transfers/1), not the 1960–61 register. The existing `previous-transfers` source now correctly supports both his transfer and event. The allocation to 1960–61 is unchanged and follows the summer convention.
6. **1959–60, Slater/Younger references:** the irrelevant transfer-table reference was removed. Their specialist profile and [Falkirk’s club history](https://falkirkfc.co.uk/throwback-thursday-navy-blue-keepers/) remain as direct evidence for the exchange.
7. **1959–60, caretaker source label:** the 21 November fixture label was reversed. It now reads Liverpool v Leyton Orient, consistent with Anfield in the match record.

The three venue errors are corrections, not historical uncertainties. Source-reference refinements do not alter the underlying transfer facts.

## Remaining editorial matters and review boundary

No unresolved error was found in the four league finishes, leading scorer identities/totals, entered competitions, cup exit rounds or trophy outcomes. The original date/venue errors and citation mismatches listed above were sent for correction and their corrected text was inspected in the shared files. The two newly retrieved 11v11 references were sent to root for incorporation in the source arrays.

The author’s deliberately qualified Reid fee, Leishman fee, Liddell retirement date and minor transfer-fee limitations remain review notes rather than unsupported numbers. This review checked every significant transfer against the retrieved register and sampled the most important primary and contemporary confirmations; it does not claim a fresh archival adjudication of every historical fee dispute. High confidence applies to the league, scoring and cup conclusions, and to the corrected match chronology. Lower confidence concerns explicitly omitted peripheral fees/dates, not a weakly researched season.

The web reader successfully retrieved both 11v11 player tables. The site may impose direct HTTP bot restrictions, as noted in the wider source-link audit. No content source is replaced with an invented URL because of those restrictions.
