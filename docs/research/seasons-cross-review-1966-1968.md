The [current official profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/ronnie-moran) ends his first-team years in 1965 but does not supply a precise retirement/coaching date. It does not establish a required transfer entry in any one of these three seasons, and this reviewer has not treated its absence as a factual error. No new date should be inferred from the first-team career heading alone.
