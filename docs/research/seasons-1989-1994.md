## Resolutions and boundaries

- LFChistory season IDs are not wholly chronological: 34 is 1991–92 and 33 is 1992–93. Every URL was checked by its page heading, not assumed from its number.
- The official Dalglish profile says the 1990–91 opening run was ten league wins. It was eight league wins plus two League Cup wins. The fixture list and official Rosenthal profile establish eight; the incorrect claim is excluded.
- Dalglish resigned on 22 February 1991, two days after the Everton replay. The LFChistory Moran narrative calls it the day after, contradicting its own appointment date. The dated registry and official Dalglish account agree on the two-day interval.
- Arsenal’s final 1990–91 total of 83 already accounts for the two-point deduction, leaving a seven-point gap to Liverpool.
- Moran is recorded as caretaker in 1991 and as deputy during Souness’s health absence in 1992. Accounts differ on the precise allocation of duties during the 1992 final, so no exclusive match-management claim is made.
- The Souness narrative at LFChistory skips Kuusysi when describing the European return. The complete fixtures and dedicated European history establish Kuusysi in round one, Auxerre in round two and Genoa in the quarter-final (the fourth round).
- Rosenthal’s spring 1990 loan is supported by the club profile even though the permanent-transfer ledger omits it. The June 1990 permanent transfer is recorded separately in 1990–91. His seven goals and eight league appearances are checked against the scoring and appearance ledgers.
- Saunders’s total in 1991–92 is 23, not his full Liverpool career total of 25. Rush’s 1992–93 total includes his Charity Shield goal.
- The December 1993 Wimbledon replay finished 2–2 after extra time; Wimbledon won the shoot-out 4–3. Shoot-out kicks are not added to player totals.
- Souness’s last match and Evans’s appointment were separate dates. Evans’s 31 January 1994 appointment is verified; the summary does not collapse it into the Bristol City match date.
- The final standing-Kop match on 30 April 1994 is distinct from the later opening of the seated replacement. No atmosphere, crowd behaviour or supporter quotations have been invented.
- Transfers are selected senior moves, not an exhaustive reserve/youth ledger. No unverified fees or motives are included. Aldridge’s September 1989 exit is now recorded; Babb and Scales belong to 1994–95.
