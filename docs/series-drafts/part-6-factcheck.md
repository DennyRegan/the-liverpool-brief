Draft: docs/series-drafts/part-6-kings-of-europe.md

Checker: independent fact-checker agent, web sources only, no access to research notes

Heysel/Hillsborough: not mentioned.

**Summary:** I checked 177 claims. 123 are CORRECT, 8 are WRONG, 39 are UNVERIFIED and 7 are SOURCES CONFLICT. Paragraphs are numbered from "On Sunday 12 May 2019…" as 1, through "On Sunday 2 June…" as 39. Every weekday in the piece is right: 12 May 2019 was a Sunday, 1 June a Saturday, 2 June a Sunday and 6 May a Monday.

**The 8 WRONG findings:**
- the Klopp "interruptions" quote (row 33)
- van Dijk's "last-man challenge" on Mertens (row 41)
- Klopp's "lifesaver" quote (row 44)
- "three shots on target" in the 0-0 with Bayern (row 55)
- "By then City were a point clear" (row 92)
- the "impossible… because it's you" quote, which is Lovren's recollection, not Klopp's account afterwards (row 110)
- "around 33 degrees" in Madrid (row 144)
- the Henderson "got over the line" quote (row 166)

The other finding to fix: Cannonier was probably 15, not 14, on 7 May 2019 (row 117, left UNVERIFIED because only Wikipedia gives his birth date).
