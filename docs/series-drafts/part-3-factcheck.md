How paragraphs are counted: there is no YAML frontmatter, so I left out the heading and the italic working-title/excerpt line. P1 is "The first thing Liverpool did…" and P37 is "Within three weeks of the final day…". The `* * *` breaks are not counted.

**The seven WRONG rows, most important first:**
- **#93 (P28–29): order of events.** The Bournemouth draw (5 April) came before Klopp ruled Mané out (7 April). It was not "the first of" the seven remaining games.
- **#78 (P23): Chelsea goal order.** David Luiz scored first (24'). Wijnaldum equalised (57').
- **#84 (P25): save percentage.** The Ringer's "7 points below average" figure is for all opponents, not just weaker ones.
- **#90 (P27): Henderson's appearances.** He made six appearances in all competitions in 2017, but no more than five in the league.
- **#24 (P6): Sakho date.** UEFA dismissed the case on 8 July, not 9 July.
- **#31 (P8): "A week later".** It was ten days: 27 July to 6 August.
- **#32 (P8): Mascherano own goal.** It came from Mané's cross under pressure from Henderson, not from a Henderson cross.
