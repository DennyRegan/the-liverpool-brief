## Alan Kennedy — 946 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The opening explicitly resists reducing Kennedy to Paris and Rome. Recruitment, overlapping play, regular league appearances, the defensive record of his first championship season and all 67 matches of the treble campaign give those famous interventions a substantial career frame. The 1981 injury and selection story links the Paris goal to Paisley's judgement. The League Cup goals of 1981 and 1983 broaden the account beyond European finals. Beglin's emergence and the timing of departure are covered, with a useful distinction between his early contribution to 1985–86 and the later Double.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

His routine defending and relationships within the left-sided unit are described less concretely than his forward movement. The phrase that Liverpool needed a settled left-back is not developed into an explanation of the position before his arrival. The league championships after his first two seasons are also largely represented by the treble's appearance total rather than his work in those campaigns.

If this article is revisited, research could ask: what problem did Paisley identify at left-back; how did Kennedy combine with the midfielder ahead of him and the centre-back inside him; and is there a representative non-final match that demonstrates his defending and overlap together? Those are optional avenues, not proof that the biography currently lacks an essential episode.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Keep; low priority.** The article already fulfils its own promise to recover the career behind the two famous finishes. Do not commission more material simply to make it longer. One unusually revealing researched example could eventually replace a general sentence, but expansion is not presently necessary.

## Bob Paisley — 1,405 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

This is the fullest piece in this group and its space is purposeful. It connects playing, physiotherapy, Shankly's staff and the Boot Room to senior management. It explains decisions through Ray Kennedy's conversion, Keegan's movement against Vogts, Dalglish/Hansen/Souness recruitment, the continental passing approach, the Souness captaincy and renewal through Whelan and Rush. It covers the principal winning seasons and includes defeats, the absence of an FA Cup win, reluctance to succeed Shankly, delegation and personal compassion. The handover to Fagan and later assistance to Dalglish complete the institutional arc.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The 1974 handover and second-place first season are brief; the manuscript does not explore the practical adjustment from assistant to final decision-maker at length. Scouting and collective expertise are named but not illustrated with a detailed recruitment process. These are possible specialist enrichments, not large omissions from an already explanatory managerial biography.

Future research might ask whether a specific first-season selection decision or a documented scouting collaboration adds something not already shown by the later recruitment and renewal examples. Avoid repeating the same argument with additional trophy descriptions.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Keep; very low priority.** The central claim—that Paisley repeatedly changed a successful side without losing its competitive standard—is demonstrated rather than asserted. Preserve the prose. Neither his stature nor comparison with another manager justifies an automatic expansion.

## David Fairclough — 845 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The supersub label is treated as the organising problem, not as the complete story. Starts versus substitute appearances and goals, the 1976 contribution, the derby run, Saint-Étienne, exclusions from the 1977 finals and substantial involvement in 1977–78 give the argument evidence. The 1978 final provides a clear counterweight to the remembered substitute goal. Selection competition and injuries explain uneven opportunities, and the text distinguishes Fairclough's retrospective frustration from proof that a different policy would have changed his career.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The period between his productive 1979–80 season, absence from competitive first-team football in 1981–82 and departure in 1983 is compressed. Individual injury chronology, any attempted recovery and the circumstances of the final move are not developed. Research could ask whether these years contain a consequential return, selection decision or loan spell that changes the present explanation of his marginalisation. Such details would need to justify their inclusion rather than fill the calendar.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Keep; very low priority.** The biography convincingly recovers a career from a nickname and a single remembered night. It is shorter because its arc is focused, not because it is only a highlights list. No substantive expansion is indicated by the current coverage.

## Gérard Houllier — 961 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The full managerial chronology is present: joint management, sole control, defensive recruitment, 2001 treble, Champions League entry, illness and Thompson's interim work, second place, decline and departure. Discipline, Melwood redevelopment and recollections from Murphy and Šmicer prevent it becoming solely a trophy inventory. Gerrard's debut and captaincy give the developmental argument a consequential example. The FA Cup and UEFA Cup finals are narrated clearly, and the opening acknowledges both reconstruction and failure to achieve the championship.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The article's main explanatory claim is stronger than its evidence of the team's football. Hyypiä/Henchoz/Hamann establish the defensive spine, but the wider turnover from the inherited team, recruitment logic and resulting attacking organisation remain largely implicit. The finals say what happened more than how Houllier's choices made his team competitive. The 2001–02 peak is represented by the Roma return and final points total; the subsequent reversal is attributed briefly to three unsuccessful arrivals and a winless run.

Research should ask: which departures and acquisitions materially altered the inherited squad; how were defensive security, midfield shape and the forwards' roles connected; what explains the progression from fourth to third to second; and what evidence supports the causes of the 2002–04 stagnation? The Champions League quarter-final and the limits of the title challenge could supply useful context if researched. Do not assume that recruitment alone explains decline, or retrofit later reputations as contemporary evidence.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Substantive expansion, selectively; high priority.** Add meaningful explanatory depth to rebuilding, the football of the successful side and the failure to take the next step. Retain the existing chronology, illness account and measured conclusion. The commission should be question-led and evidence-led; it does not require doubling the biography, longer foreign-career background or retelling every final.

## Ian Callaghan — 939 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The account covers the Second Division start, establishment on the right, first title team, defining 1965 cross, injury, central-midfield conversion and renewed importance through the 1970s. Appearance totals for 1972–74 support the argument that adaptation prolonged his usefulness. England's limited use is proportionate. The 1977 final and nuanced account of his unused-substitute status in 1978 distinguish service from participation in a particular final. The Swansea move is explained through his stated desire for regular football.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The mechanics of his central-midfield role are less specific than the winger description: energy and reading of the game are supplied, but distribution, defensive work and partnerships are not illustrated by a particular passage of play or contemporary account. The 1973 and 1976 UEFA Cup successes are mentioned rather than developed through his contribution.

A possible later research question is whether a documented example can show how his midfield function differed from the younger teammates around him. That would enrich an already clear conversion story; it is not necessary to add complete European campaign narratives.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Keep; very low priority.** The biography makes the appearance record meaningful through changing work, rather than treating longevity as sufficient explanation. Its span is long, but the transitions and representative achievements are already well chosen.

## Ian Rush — 920 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The piece combines scoring records with pressing and off-ball running, supported by the Dalglish partnership and a recollection from Sammy Lee. It covers the slow start, breakthrough, derby record, 1983–84 peak, 1986 Double, Juventus arrangement, return difficulties and the 1989 final. The conclusion's distinction between two Liverpool careers is effective. Captaincy, Fowler and the 1995 League Cup ensure that the second spell is not omitted altogether.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The second spell receives substantially less explanation than the first. After the 1989 final, the 1990 championship, 1992 cup, record, captaincy, Fowler relationship and final-season marginalisation follow rapidly. Supporting Fowler is stated rather than demonstrated. The changing Liverpool sides under Souness and Evans are named, but what changed in Rush's responsibilities, selection and contribution is only lightly described. Juventus is proportionate to a Liverpool biography; the larger gap is the years after his return.

Research should ask: what sustained his scoring usefulness through the early 1990s; what responsibilities came with the captaincy; is there documented evidence of how he helped Fowler; and how did Evans use him before the Fowler/Collymore partnership displaced him? Select one or two concrete examples. Do not manufacture a mentor narrative from mere coexistence or assume an altered style without evidence.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Small targeted change; medium priority.** Strengthen the second-career section so that the strong closing distinction is earned more evenly. The first spell is already explained, not merely counted. Preserve it and avoid adding more celebrated goals in place of the missing later-career context.

## Jimmy Case — 825 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The apprenticeship and semi-professional route give his entry an individual character. His establishment in 1975–76, UEFA Cup final intervention, right-midfield function, 1977 FA Cup technique and acknowledged European-final mistake are specific and balanced. The second European Cup campaign, regular league appearances in 1978–80, Lee's emergence and the final Liverpool appearance all appear. The departure account carefully treats Case's own retrospective explanation as context, not as a proven single cause.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

His repeated importance in the 1978–80 title teams is supported chiefly by appearance and goal totals. The article does not show his relationship with the changing midfield or how his right-sided role complemented the attacking and defensive structure. That ordinary work is less vivid than the cup goals. Competition with Lee is clear in outline but not developed into a football explanation.

Research could ask for a contemporaneous description or ordinary-match example of his positioning, ball use and defensive contribution, and whether there is evidence explaining how Lee's selection changed that role. Do not turn the departure into an unsupported morality tale or add off-field detail simply for colour.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Small targeted change; low priority.** One researched illustration of his regular midfield work would deepen the middle years. The current article is already substantial for its six first-team seasons, and the absence of that illustration does not justify wholesale expansion.

## John Aldridge — 922 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The career is organised clearly around replacing Rush and then competing with his return. Earlier scoring and cup experience explain the purchase; the first half-season's limited starts prevent the story skipping straight to success. His attacking fit with Barnes and Beardsley, scoring streak, Forest performances, Wimbledon penalty, continued output in 1988–89, Hillsborough recollection and 1989 final are all developed. If a later review seeks refinement, research could ask how often they played together and what documented selection reasoning explains Aldridge remaining prolific yet departing. This is an optional refinement, not an unexplained hole in the career arc.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Keep; very low priority.** A two-and-a-half-year career does not need the space of a decade-long one. The present piece is already rich in Liverpool context and consequence. Preserve its structure and avoid expanding Oxford or post-Liverpool material to satisfy a generic length expectation.

## John Barnes — 961 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

Barnes's attacking role is explained through interchange with Beardsley, balance from Houghton, creation as well as scoring and representative matches. The championships and 1989 losses are integrated into the story, with his own interpretation of the Arsenal ending. Racism is handled through his experiences and argument rather than a congratulatory acceptance story. The injuries, absence from the 1992 final, central-midfield adaptation alongside Redknapp and McManaman, 1995 trophy and eventual departure give the later half of the career a distinct purpose.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The captaincy first appears when he leads the team into the 1996 final; its appointment and responsibilities are not set out. His productive midfield adaptation is described well in outline, but how important he was to Evans's recovering side is demonstrated principally by one final assist. The passage from post-injury return to established central midfielder is also brisk.

Research could ask when and why he became captain, whether a contemporary account clarifies his responsibility in Evans's side, and whether one representative performance would sharpen the midfield description. Those details should serve the existing two-role argument, not create a separate leadership tribute.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Small targeted change; low priority.** Clarify captaincy chronology and, if evidence is strong, add one example of the later role. There is no case for rebuilding the article: its early peak, difficult transition and surviving technique are already convincingly covered.

## Jordan Henderson — 968 prose words

### Coverage strengths — file observations

The article offers a career arc rather than a captain's trophy list: right-midfield beginnings, refusal of the Fulham move, central work under Rodgers, personal responsibility for the 2014 red card, succession to Gerrard and role changes under Klopp. The 2019 move forward is supported by Southampton and Barcelona examples. The pandemic work, centre-back adaptation and Saudi departure criticism connect leadership to actions, while Klopp's assessment provides a football counterweight to ceremonial captaincy. It avoids claiming that his 2014 availability would necessarily have won the title.

### Missing or compressed context — editorial judgement and research questions

The 2016–18 deep-midfield period is much less concrete than the 2019 shift forward. The 2019–20 championship is illustrated by team results and his award, not a distinct example of his contribution. The 2021–22 cup-winning side and 2022–23 decline are compressed into honours and an appearance total, with no explanation of the evolution of his place in midfield. This leaves the last four years of the Liverpool career less differentiated than its formative years.

Research should ask what his deeper role required in Klopp's developing side, how his role and midfield partnerships changed around the championship and 2022 campaigns, and what the final season meant for his selection and departure. Choose concrete role evidence rather than more celebrations. The closing paragraph's post-Liverpool moves and linked claims were read but not verified; they are outside this depth review and should not be treated as independently confirmed by it.

### Disposition and priority — editorial judgement

**Small targeted change; medium priority.** A bounded addition to the middle/later Liverpool years would make the twelve-year span more even. Keep the useful 2012 decision, 2014 qualification, 2019 examples and balanced departure account. Substantive wholesale expansion is not needed when the principal role changes and honours are already present.
