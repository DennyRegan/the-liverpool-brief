## Ronnie Moran

**Prose count:** 884. **Disposition:** Keep. **Priority:** Low.

**Coverage strengths — manuscript observation.** The biography connects the Second Division player, championship-winning defender, reserve-team manager, trainer, chief coach and two caretaker spells. The playing career is not merely a preamble: it includes relegation, captaincy, injuries, participation in the two league-winning campaigns, selection changes and the final European appearances. Coaching work receives unusually useful detail through diaries, marking instructions, Rush's recollections and the complementary temperaments of the staff. The diary and player-development passages make this substantially more informative than a general Boot Room tribute.

**Research question only, if revisited.** Can a well-attributed account illustrate a specific change in Moran's responsibilities or training work during the 1990s? Add it only if it reveals something different from the existing Rush/Hansen material. There is no coverage-based reason to expand merely to enumerate more honours or managers.

**Reason for priority.** The central claim of a lifetime at Liverpool is already supported across both careers. More length is unlikely to materially improve the balance.
