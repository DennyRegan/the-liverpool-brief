## Scope and method

Read-only substantive coverage review of the ten current manuscripts named below, read in full from `docs/editorial/drafts/biographies/`. This is an editorial depth assessment, **not** a factual audit. No broad web research was undertaken. A fact's presence in the manuscript does not constitute independent verification of it; dates, totals, recent events, allegations and causal accounts still belong to the separate sourcing/audit process.

Counts are whitespace-delimited narrative-body words, excluding JSON frontmatter, all heading lines, the final source list (whether introduced by `Sources` or `## Sources`), and standalone editorial research-date notes. Hyphenated terms and numbers are counted as single whitespace tokens. Dalglish's six-word research-completion note and Salah's five-word cut-off note are excluded; retaining those notes would give 2,300 and 1,519 respectively. These are reproducible length measures, not targets.

Confidence is high in the observations about what the current manuscripts contain or omit. Dispositions and priorities are explicitly editorial judgements. Proposed additional context is expressed as a research question unless it is already stated in the manuscript. A research question does not authorise inserting an assumed answer.

Only this review file was written. Manuscripts, canonical metadata, calendar, public files and Git state/publication operations were not changed.

## Executive judgement

These ten are not a set of skeletal biographies needing to be doubled. All already explain a distinctive contribution, supply match-level evidence and follow a recognisable career arc. Most of the shorter pieces are around 850–1,000 narrative words and are effective at that length. The longer Dalglish and Salah manuscripts already justify their extra space through broader responsibilities or longer evolving careers.

**No manuscript in this group presently warrants a wholesale substantive expansion.** Selective researched paragraphs could improve Kennedy, Fowler, Firmino and Salah; Beardsley and Clemence offer smaller, optional contextual opportunities. Preserve the existing strong prose and its proportions. A new passage should answer a real question left by the current story, not make room for another list of honours or famous matches.

Priority refers to usefulness within this group's depth work, not publication urgency or a finding of factual error.

## Kenny Dalglish

**Current strengths.** The manuscript has an appropriate breadth for a player, two-spell manager and central figure in the club's relationship with its people. It explains replacing Keegan; the Rush partnership; the inherited side and 1986 recovery; the construction of the Barnes–Beardsley–Aldridge attack; and the difference between winning a league and losing a final. Its Hillsborough section contains concrete acts of support rather than generic reverence, gives Marina and the collective response space, and properly puts families and survivors at the centre of the continuing struggle. The resignation is explained through Dalglish's account rather than attributed simplistically to the Everton draw. The second managerial spell recognises the cup success, league limitations and the Suárez affair rather than ending in unqualified praise.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The 2011–12 personnel changes and footballing approach are compressed; the narrative also gives less detail to the everyday mechanics of Dalglish's playing craft than to his principal partnerships and results. Those are choices of emphasis, not evidence that the piece lacks depth. The first-season replacement of Keegan and the reinvention of the 1987 attack already answer the major sporting questions.

**Research questions, only if revisiting.** Would a concise explanation of the 2011–12 recruitment/playing plan materially clarify why the league recovery stalled, using contemporary evidence rather than retrospective blame? Could one supported playing example make his ball protection or spatial judgement more vivid without repeating the Rush account?

**Disposition: keep. Priority: low.** It is the most developed biography in this group. More length risks weakening the balance between sporting achievement and human responsibility. No expansion should be undertaken merely because other biographies are being revisited.

## Kevin Keegan

**Current strengths.** This is a strong concise biography, not a thin résumé. It describes the move from Scunthorpe, the initial midfield possibility, the Toshack partnership and why that partnership worked. The abandoned/rearranged Mönchengladbach first leg gives tactical context to the 1973 achievement. The FA Cup final identifies two different kinds of finishing contribution. The Charity Shield incident acknowledges a setback. Both Brugge legs and the Vogts confrontation in Rome show Keegan's importance beyond a trophy list. The final argument earns its claim that he helped take Liverpool from reconstruction to European champions.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The league campaigns are sketched more lightly than the finals. The move abroad is introduced as an intention already known during his last season, without much explanation of its origin or Liverpool's response. Keegan's relationship with Shankly and the difference between his role under Shankly and Paisley are not individually developed. None of these omissions prevents the existing six-season argument working.

**Research questions, only if revisiting.** What did Keegan himself say contemporaneously about wanting to leave, and can that be distinguished from later recollection? Is there an especially useful league-season example that would add something the existing finals do not already establish? Did the managerial change alter his role in a documented way?

**Disposition: keep. Priority: low.** At 887 words it already supplies character, tactical explanation, setbacks and decisive evidence. A small departure-context clarification could be worthwhile if supported, but there is no case for substantive expansion on length alone. The manuscript's recent-life/death statement is outside the depth assessment and is not independently verified here.

## Mark Lawrenson

**Current strengths.** The recruitment story is unusually specific: Paisley's account of a clean tackle on Dalglish illustrates what Liverpool valued. The piece then connects speed, timing and versatility to the initially crowded centre-back position. It avoids implying that the Hansen partnership appeared fully formed on arrival, gives the 1982 Tottenham contribution its full importance, and distinguishes injury pressure in 1986 from the career-altering Achilles injury. The last playing chapter makes the loss of mobility meaningful because the opening has already explained its value.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The Hansen partnership is described rather than demonstrated through an individual defensive episode. The 1984 European Cup final passage mainly records participation and result, not Lawrenson's specific work. Midfield deployment is mentioned without explaining which situations or team needs occasioned it. Those are potential refinements, not a missing career phase.

**Research questions, only if revisiting.** Can a credible match report or teammate account demonstrate the complementary Hansen–Lawrenson roles in action? Is there a documented example of midfield use that explains Liverpool's tactical needs more clearly than the current general description?

**Disposition: keep. Priority: low.** The biography already has a coherent causal structure from recruitment qualities to achievement and early decline. If new evidence emerges, replace a generic description with the sharper example rather than append several hundred words.

## Mohamed Salah

**Current strengths.** The manuscript treats Salah as both scorer and creator and explains his right-sided position within a coordinated attack. Firmino, Mané and Alexander-Arnold are integrated into that explanation rather than simply named. The first Roma match demonstrates the range of his contribution. The season-by-season account differentiates winning the league, sustaining production in the difficult title defence, the 2021–22 near-misses and the later Slot peak. Records and awards reinforce an argument already established by footballing examples. The treatment is not confined to the Klopp era.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The transition from the original front three to later attacking colleagues receives little direct explanation. The Slot championship has excellent numerical evidence but less on how Salah's role or combinations changed. Most noticeably, public criticism of the playing style, the March departure announcement, a fifth-place finish and the final appearance are concentrated into one paragraph. Given their importance to the ending, the connection between these events is less clear than the rest of the career. Supporters' relationship with him and the broader local significance of his presence are also largely absent, but should not be inferred from fame or nationality.

**Research questions for a targeted revision.** What specific, evidenced footballing changes under Slot explain the 2024–25 balance of scoring and assisting? What is the documented timeline and attributed explanation of the final-season rupture and exit? Can supporter or community significance be demonstrated through reliable evidence without making sweeping claims about cultural impact?

**Disposition: small targeted change. Priority: medium.** The aim should be a clearer bridge and ending, not a second full statistical chronology. Recent 2025–26 events and the stated post-Liverpool move require a separate verification pass before any addition; this review does not endorse those claims. Avoid treating an unattributed causal explanation of departure as established history.

## Peter Beardsley

**Current strengths.** The four-season scope is well judged. It explains the reconstruction after Rush, Beardsley's deeper role, his settling-in period and individual control in adverse Watford conditions. The Forest performance and title-clincher illustrate creation and scoring. The later selection difficulties are handled carefully: his dissatisfaction is documented without inventing a feud with Dalglish. The move to Everton is placed after Souness's arrival and the Saunders signing. The later disciplinary finding is acknowledged rather than omitted from an admiring account.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The manuscript repeatedly places Beardsley in a great attacking side but never explains why his Liverpool years offered no European stage. Hillsborough appears only in the phrase introducing the 1989 FA Cup campaign. Rush's return is demonstrated by the final's personnel but its effect on regular roles is not developed. These are small context gaps, not a deficient main narrative.

**Research questions for a targeted revision.** Can one brief, checked sentence connect his Liverpool years to the continuing European exclusion? What concise context is necessary for the resumed 1989 campaign, without importing a full disaster history into this biography? Did Rush's return materially change Beardsley's position, selection or partnerships over the season, and what evidence supports that account?

**Disposition: small targeted change. Priority: low.** The first question is the clearest addition. Protect the existing compact four-season arc. Neither another match catalogue nor a long Hillsborough passage is required.

## Phil Neal

**Current strengths.** Neal's career is unusually well covered in 870 words. The move from the fourth tier and Paisley's first signing are established. The initial left-back spell prevents the biography from flattening him into an unchanging right-back. Thompson's account describes anticipation and use of the ball; the consecutive-match run is therefore attached to useful football qualities rather than treated as availability alone. The open-play/penalty distinction and detailed 1984 final recover his contribution from the more familiar Grobbelaar and Alan Kennedy memories. Captaincy, Heysel, displacement and the qualified 1985–86 title attribution all receive proportionate treatment.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** Domestic League Cup victories are grouped rather than individually narrated, and his everyday attacking relationship with right-sided teammates is less developed than his defensive relationship with Thompson. His captaincy year is brief, though its ending is given the appropriate gravity. These are understandable selections: a biography need not reproduce every medal campaign to explain importance.

**Research questions, only if revisiting.** Would a documented overlapping or combination-play example reveal a quality not already conveyed by his goals? Is there a reliable account of his leadership that adds something beyond the captaincy date and final-season chronology?

**Disposition: keep. Priority: low.** This is a model case for retaining a strong shorter biography. The essentials are present, including qualifications that a longer but less disciplined piece might omit. No substantive expansion is justified.

## Ray Clemence

**Current strengths.** The two Mönchengladbach saves provide an effective through-line without reducing him to those moments. The manuscript explains the long apprenticeship behind Lawrence, the move into Shankly's reconstructed side, durability, the 1973 penalty's aggregate significance, distribution in the 1974 final and audible leadership through Thompson's recollection. The concentration paragraph makes clear why dominant teams still need an exceptional goalkeeper. The 16-goal league campaign is correctly framed in the draft as collective work rather than Clemence alone. The return to Anfield gives concrete evidence of affection.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The 1981 European Cup final leads directly to Tottenham, without an explanation of why a goalkeeper still central to a successful side left. Liverpool's transition at goalkeeper is also absent. The manuscript tells readers how his career began and why it mattered more fully than why it ended.

**Research questions for an optional targeted revision.** What did Clemence say about the 1981 decision, and how does contemporary reporting distinguish his reasons from Liverpool's succession planning? Would introducing the replacement clarify the transition without making an unsupported claim that it caused his departure?

**Disposition: small targeted change, optional. Priority: low.** One well-sourced departure paragraph could close the only significant narrative question. The existing biography already stands on its own; the proposal is not a reason to expand the saves, honours or England rivalry at length.

## Ray Kennedy

**Current strengths.** The positional conversion is already the centre of the story, with its timing, opening and practical benefits explained. The 1976 Brugge/Wolves sequence, Saint-Étienne scorer-and-provider contribution and Munich away goal demonstrate why the change mattered. The 205-of-210 league availability figure supports importance more persuasively than a vague claim to being underrated. Departure pressure from Whelan and the later championship medal qualification are included. The account retains Kennedy's agency as a skilled, dependable footballer.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** Day-to-day midfield relationships receive less attention than his decisive European interventions. His later life is more substantially compressed: Parkinson's disease and the 1991 testimonial occupy a single sentence, even though the opening and body establish a player whose contribution and continuity were exceptional. The draft does not explain whether or how his relationship with Liverpool endured through illness. This is an opportunity for human and club context, not for speculative medical explanation.

**Research questions for a targeted revision.** What reliable accounts document Liverpool teammates' or supporters' practical support and Kennedy's own perspective? What did the 1991 testimonial represent beyond its occurrence? Is there an attributable account of how Kennedy experienced the end of his playing career, and can it be included without suggesting an unverified medical cause for earlier selection or form changes?

**Disposition: small targeted change. Priority: medium.** A dignified, Liverpool-connected post-playing paragraph is more useful than doubling the already strong football narrative. Avoid turning the biography into a medical case history or letting illness eclipse the achievements already explained.

## Robbie Fowler

**Current strengths.** The opening scoring run, emergence from the youth system, range of finishing and two Newcastle matches give substance to the reputation. The dockers gesture establishes a Liverpool-city connection while the following paragraph acknowledges misconduct. Injuries are linked to reduced availability rather than simply presented as bad luck. The 2001 section is particularly effective: it distinguishes limited selection from valuable contribution, covers all three cups and includes the essential Charlton qualification match. The second spell is measured against Fowler's changed capabilities rather than written as a sentimental restoration of his peak.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** Collymore is principally the scorer of a winner, McManaman principally a cup-final scorer, and the structure of Evans's attack is not explained. The reader sees prolific Fowler and exciting Liverpool but little of how the team created his chances or why its promise failed to become a title. The first departure is stated after the successful 2001 season without much account of the intervening selection or club decision. The famous 1996 FA Cup final and its surrounding public image are absent, but adding them requires a defensible purpose, not automatic inclusion of every famous episode.

**Research questions for a targeted revision.** Which partnership or team-shape evidence best explains Fowler's peak scoring? Can the team's title limitations be described fairly without repeating the simplistic, personality-led labels attached to that generation? What did Fowler and Houllier say, with proper attribution, about the 2001 move? Would the 1996 final genuinely sharpen that explanation or merely add a familiar set piece?

**Disposition: small targeted change. Priority: medium.** Prioritise a team-context bridge and, if necessary, a clearer exit transition. Existing early-career and treble passages are strong enough to retain. A substantive rewrite would risk displacing the biography's best evidence.

## Roberto Firmino

**Current strengths.** The opening makes the tactical mechanism intelligible in plain language. The 2015 Manchester City game demonstrates rather than merely labels his suitability for the central role. The false-nine explanation recognises an early limitation against deep defences; the pressing evidence stops the argument becoming solely about stylish passing. Roma and the 27-goal season prevent an artificial contrast between selfless creation and scoring. Qatar, away league winners and later changes in selection provide a persuasive career arc through to departure.

**Absent or compressed Liverpool context.** The account moves quickly from Klopp's arrival and the first City example to Salah completing the front three in 2017. The intervening development is largely missing: how Liverpool's attack operated before Salah, how Firmino's role became established and what transitional European experience mattered. The later attack's succession is described through Jota/Díaz and injuries but not deeply analysed. The final Anfield goal conveys a farewell moment, yet the distinctive supporter relationship is not directly explored.

**Research questions for a targeted revision.** Which 2015–17 developments show the central role becoming established rather than merely appearing in one match? Would a carefully chosen example from the first Klopp European campaign add a missing phase? Can a supported account of the supporter song, farewell or teammate appreciation explain the relationship without generic claims that everybody loved him? What evidence distinguishes tactical succession from injury-limited availability?

**Disposition: small targeted change. Priority: medium.** Add a short developmental bridge if research produces a clear explanation. Fan context is a secondary optional addition. The current tactical prose and decisive match examples should remain; another inventory of assists, goals and honours would not supply the missing depth.
