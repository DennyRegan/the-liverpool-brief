Robbie Fowler scored every goal in Liverpool's 5–0 victory over Fulham at Anfield on 5 October 1993. Two came before half-time and three after it, completing an 8–1 aggregate win in the second round of the League Cup.

The 18-year-old had already scored on his senior debut in the first leg at Craven Cottage. This was not his first appearance at Anfield either: he had played in the goalless league match against Arsenal a few days earlier. The return against Fulham gave a crowd of 12,541 a different introduction to his finishing.

Fowler opened the scoring after 13 minutes, following up when Jim Stannard could not hold Rob Jones's shot. His second came from a free-kick situation, leaving Liverpool two ahead at the interval. Fulham had threatened too, with Bruce Grobbelaar making a double save from Jeff Eckhardt and Udo Onwere.

Two minutes into the second half, Fowler completed his hat-trick after further work by Jones. Julian Dicks then supplied a cross for Fowler to head in his fourth. The fifth followed with about 20 minutes remaining, a finish past Stannard that completed the night's scoring.

Fowler had scored with both feet and his head. Ian Rush, starting alongside him, did not add to the total. Graeme Souness's team advanced with its new forward responsible for six of the eight goals across the tie.

The first leg had established Fowler as a goalscoring debutant. The second supplied a separate achievement: all five goals in a senior match, against the same opponents, less than a fortnight later. Liverpool's progress was comfortable by the end; Fowler's individual total made the evening distinctive.
