## Conflicts, exclusions and limits

- Agreement on 6 October and completed purchase on 15 October are separate events. The 13 October court ruling was not the ownership transfer.
- Do not say “debt free”. Financial claims are attributed to the announcement and distinguish acquisition debt from other debt or continuing servicing costs. £300m is the stated transaction valuation, not money said to have been paid to Hicks and Gillett personally.
- Source 3 discusses a withdrawal of the Texas lawsuit; source 2 includes an earlier intention to seek damages. No permanent abandonment of all legal claims is inferred. Disputed accusations, damages figures and moral verdicts are excluded.
- Source 3 initially reported that Henry would not attend the forthcoming derby; source 6 records Goodison as his first live match. This forecast is not used as an account of what subsequently happened. Existing 17 October 2010 match manuscript is preserved; no match report is duplicated here.
- Source 3 carries a 01:00 timestamp while describing events that afternoon. No precise completion time is inferred from that timestamp. Sky publication times are not presented as completion times either.
- **UNVERIFIED and omitted:** original court orders and full transaction contracts, private negotiations, exact ownership stakes, audited post-acquisition balance sheet, tax consequences, precise settlement mechanics and financial promises not contained in retrieved text.
- Archived official timeline redirects were already documented in earlier notes; the successfully retrieved official retrospective is used instead. No failed or search-only source is represented as a retrieved original announcement.
- NESV is the contemporary name. No present-day ownership assessment, future trophies or claim that the takeover guaranteed sporting recovery.
- Salah/Rangers work is outside this assignment and is untouched.
