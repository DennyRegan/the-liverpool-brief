Fernando Torres scored both goals as Liverpool beat Everton 2–0 at Goodison Park on 27 September 2008. Two finishes around the hour mark settled a match that had been goalless at half-time and gave Rafael Benítez’s side a third successive Merseyside derby victory.

Robbie Keane started alongside Torres, with Steven Gerrard and Xabi Alonso in midfield and Albert Riera and Dirk Kuyt also in the side. Liverpool had more of the ball before the interval but did not turn that possession into a lead.

Everton had opportunities of their own. Tim Cahill failed to make proper contact with Mikel Arteta’s corner from close range. Later, Pepe Reina dropped a cross and Marouane Fellaini’s attempt was blocked on the line by Jamie Carragher, although referee Mike Riley awarded Liverpool a free-kick. Joseph Yobo blocked a Torres shot at the other end.

The breakthrough came just before the hour. Keane reached the byline on the left and crossed towards the far post, where Torres arrived to volley past Tim Howard. The two Liverpool forwards had combined to produce the opening goal after a first half without a breakthrough.

Soon afterwards, Torres struck again. Phil Jagielka’s tackle on Kuyt sent the ball loose inside the penalty area, and Torres drove it into the roof of the net. Liverpool had gone from level to two goals ahead in a brief spell either side of the hour.

Torres subsequently put the ball in the net once more, but Riley ruled the effort out. His contribution to the score remained a double, enough to give Liverpool a clear advantage before Everton lost a player.

Cahill was sent off in the 80th minute for a challenge on Alonso. Both Liverpool goals had come while the sides still had 11 men; the dismissal left Everton to play the closing stages with ten.

The win kept Liverpool unbeaten through six Premier League matches: four victories, two draws and 14 points. They briefly led the table after the lunchtime fixture, but Chelsea’s later win at Stoke City put them ahead on goal difference. Liverpool ended the day second, having turned a goalless first half at Goodison into another derby win through Torres’ two finishes.
