# Nottingham Forest 2–0 Liverpool: Barrett doubles the damage

Liverpool began their attempt to win a third successive European Cup with a 2–0 defeat at Nottingham Forest on 13 September 1978. Garry Birtles scored in the first half, but Colin Barrett's goal three minutes from time substantially changed the task awaiting the holders at Anfield.

The first-round draw had paired Europe's champions with England's. Brian Clough's Forest had taken the league title and beaten Liverpool in the League Cup final replay the previous season. Liverpool arrived at the City Ground with five wins from five league games in the new campaign, including a 7–0 victory over Tottenham Hotspur.

Birtles put Forest ahead after 26 minutes. Liverpool reached half-time a goal down, and Bob Paisley replaced Terry McDermott with David Johnson early in the second half. The equaliser did not come. Instead, Barrett made it 2–0 in the 87th minute.

Graeme Souness later remembered that second goal as a lesson in the cost of chasing a game. In an account reproduced by LFChistory from a 2016 Sunday Times recollection, he described leaving his central midfield position and Forest exploiting the space. Joe Fagan rebuked him afterwards: Liverpool could have accepted a single-goal defeat with the home leg still to come.

They now had two goals to recover. A fortnight later, Forest held Liverpool to a goalless draw at Anfield. The damage done at the City Ground proved sufficient to end the champions' defence at the first hurdle.
