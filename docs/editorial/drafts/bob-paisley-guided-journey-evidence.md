## Conflicts, exclusions and precision

- LFChistory records 26 July 1974 as the announcement and 13 August as official takeover; Liverpool FC commemorates 26 July as the managerial anniversary ([retrieved club anniversary](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/189674-in-quotes-paisley-s-words-of-wisdom)). Reader copy says only July. The optional 1974 Shield report is retained, but the chapter does not attribute touchline control or call it Paisley’s first match. Do not describe 1974–75 as unqualified ‘trophyless’ without this context.
- LFChistory’s Paisley profile places Souness ‘two months’ after Dalglish. UEFA explicitly says January 1978; use that. The profile’s 14 trophies and club’s 13 major trophies use different baskets: neither total is used. Six league titles and three European Cups agree.
- The 1977 title margin is one point (57–56), not an effortless runaway. In 1978 Forest led 64–57; in 1979 Liverpool led 68–60; in 1980 60–58; in 1983 82–71.
- UEFA’s Rome page has an erroneous image caption naming 1977/78. Its text, date, result and club record establish 25 May 1977; the caption is ignored.
- Ray Kennedy’s club profile header ends his years at 1981 but the body explicitly records January 1982 departure. Use the body. Whelan’s header starts in 1981 and is not used as his signing date; the copy gives his debut only.
- Fagan’s club profile conflates personnel moves across his tenure. It is used only for 1983 succession. No assertion that Souness left before Fagan’s first season or that Fagan signed Beglin is made.
- No exact transfer fees, precise tactical formation, private motivation, fabricated dialogue, match atmosphere or claim to an exclusive all-time managerial record appears.
- 1980 chronology is explicit: the Arsenal semi-final exit was 1 May, before the 3 May title-clinching result. The prose says ‘also’, not ‘then’.
- Chapters include the 1974–75 cup exits, 1977 FA Cup final loss, Forest’s domestic wins, 1978/1979 European exits, fifth in 1981, Tokyo/Boxing Day setbacks, CSKA, Brighton/Widzew and the winless 1983 run. Success is not presented as uninterrupted.
