Daniel Sturridge returned from injury to create a goal and score another as Liverpool won 5–3 at Stoke City on 12 January 2014. The eight-goal match brought Liverpool their first Premier League away victory over Stoke, after a two-goal lead had disappeared before half-time.

Liverpool went ahead when Aly Cissokho’s shot deflected off Ryan Shawcross for an own goal. Suárez added the second after a Stoke defensive mix-up. Those early advantages did not survive the half: Peter Crouch headed in Marko Arnautović’s cross and Charlie Adam scored from distance for 2–2.

Gerrard restored the lead after the interval by converting the penalty awarded following Sterling’s challenge with Marc Wilson. Liverpool were 3–2 ahead when Sturridge came on for Coutinho in the 66th minute, ending his absence with an immediate opportunity to contribute.

Sturridge supplied Suárez with a reverse pass for Liverpool’s fourth. The returning forward had been out with an ankle injury, but his first part in the scoring was to create the chance that opened a two-goal gap again.

Jonathan Walters reduced the deficit late on, bringing Stoke back to within one. Sturridge then met a Suárez cross, saw his first attempt saved and finished at the next opportunity to make the score 5–3.

The win left Liverpool fourth with 42 points from 21 league games. Sturridge had helped open a two-goal gap with his assist, then restored that margin with Liverpool’s fifth after Walters’s reply.
