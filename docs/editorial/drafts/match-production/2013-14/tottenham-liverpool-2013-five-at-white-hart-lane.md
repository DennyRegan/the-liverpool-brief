Liverpool beat Tottenham Hotspur 5–0 at White Hart Lane on 15 December 2013, with Luis Suárez scoring twice in his first Premier League match as captain. Henderson, Flanagan and Sterling supplied the other goals as Liverpool moved into second place.

Suárez opened the scoring in the 18th minute after Henderson played him through. The midfielder later made it 2–0, following up after Hugo Lloris had saved attempts during the move. Liverpool took that two-goal lead into half-time.

Tottenham were reduced to ten men in the 62nd minute when Paulinho was sent off after a high challenge on Suárez. Liverpool’s advantage then increased through Flanagan, who met Suárez’s cross with a volley that went in off the underside of the crossbar.

It was Flanagan’s first senior Liverpool goal. The defender’s finish made the score 3–0 and added a separate milestone to an afternoon in which Suárez had already scored and helped create the other two goals.

Suárez scored his second late on, lifting the ball over Lloris after Luis Alberto’s pass. He then supplied Sterling for the fifth. Liverpool’s five goals came from four different players, with the captain involved in the scoring and the creation.

The victory left Liverpool on 33 points from 16 league games, two behind Arsenal. The captaincy landmark was specifically in the Premier League; the result itself was an emphatic away win that moved Rodgers’s side up to second.
