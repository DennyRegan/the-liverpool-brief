Steven Gerrard scored his 100th Premier League goal as Liverpool twice came from behind to draw 2–2 with Newcastle United at St James’ Park on 19 October 2013. His penalty cancelled out Yohan Cabaye’s opener, but Daniel Sturridge had to supply a second equaliser after ten-man Newcastle regained the lead.

Cabaye gave the home side the advantage with a shot from distance in the 23rd minute. Liverpool’s response came before half-time when Luis Suárez was fouled by Mapou Yanga-Mbiwa inside the penalty area. The defender was sent off and Liverpool were awarded a penalty.

Gerrard converted to bring both the score and his personal tally to their landmarks: 1–1 in the match and 100 goals in the Premier League. The century was a league milestone, distinct from his larger total in all competitions.

Newcastle had lost a player, but they took the lead again after the interval. Substitute Paul Dummett scored in the 56th minute, putting Liverpool behind for a second time. The visitors still had to find another goal despite their numerical advantage.

Suárez created that opening with a cross for Sturridge, whose diving header made it 2–2 in the 72nd minute. Suárez also struck the crossbar later, but Liverpool could not turn the second recovery into a win.

The draw briefly put Liverpool on top after the early kick-off. Wins for Arsenal and Chelsea later that day left Rodgers’s team third with 17 points from eight league matches. Gerrard had reached his century, while the afternoon’s result remained a point gained through two separate equalisers.
