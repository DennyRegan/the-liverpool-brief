Daniel Sturridge’s late header earned Liverpool a 3–3 draw with Everton at Goodison Park on 23 November 2013. Liverpool had led twice before Romelu Lukaku’s two second-half goals put Everton in front, leaving the substitute to produce the derby’s sixth goal in the 89th minute.

Philippe Coutinho opened the scoring after five minutes from a corner sequence. Everton replied through Kevin Mirallas three minutes later, but Suárez restored Liverpool’s lead with a direct free-kick in the 19th minute. The visitors reached half-time 2–1 ahead.

The first half also brought a high Mirallas challenge on Suárez, for which the Everton player was booked. After the break Liverpool had a chance to extend their advantage, but Joe Allen sent his effort wide. The score stayed close enough for Everton’s later goals to reverse the lead.

Simon Mignolet saved efforts from Gerard Deulofeu and Lukaku as Everton pursued an equaliser. Lukaku eventually scored following a free-kick sequence to make it 2–2, then headed in from a corner to give the home side their first lead of the match.

Sturridge had entered in place of Lucas Leiva with Liverpool still level. When Gerrard delivered a late free-kick, the forward met it with a header and made the score 3–3. His contribution recovered the point after Everton had overturned the earlier Liverpool advantages.

The result left Liverpool second on 24 points from 12 league games, four behind Arsenal in the contemporary table. Its immediate story belonged to the changing score: two Liverpool leads, an Everton recovery and finally a substitute’s equaliser that prevented a derby defeat.
