Sterling scored twice as Liverpool beat Norwich City 3–2 at Carrow Road on 20 April 2014. The eleventh successive league win secured Liverpool’s return to the Champions League, and Suárez reached 30 Premier League goals for the season with their second.

Liverpool took the lead after four minutes when Sterling’s long-range shot beat John Ruddy. He then created the second, providing the delivery that Suárez finished in the 11th minute. The visitors reached half-time 2–0 ahead.

Norwich reduced the gap after the break when Mignolet could not gather a cross and Gary Hooper scored from the loose ball. Sterling restored Liverpool’s two-goal advantage with a shot that deflected into the net in the 62nd minute.

The contest remained unsettled. Robert Snodgrass headed Norwich’s second later in the half, and Mignolet then saved from Ricky van Wolfswinkel. Liverpool held their 3–2 lead through the closing stages.

The three points put Liverpool on 80 from 35 games, five ahead of second-placed Chelsea. City had 71 from 33, so their two games in hand still mattered despite Liverpool’s larger points total.

With Champions League qualification now secure, Liverpool had three league fixtures left in the title race. The winning sequence had reached eleven league games, and Sterling’s two goals had supplied the difference on an afternoon when Norwich twice reduced the lead.
