Simon Mignolet marked his Liverpool debut by saving a late penalty as Brendan Rodgers’s side began the Premier League season with a 1–0 victory over Stoke City at Anfield on 17 August 2013. Daniel Sturridge’s first-half goal stood as the winner after the goalkeeper stopped Jonathan Walters’s spot-kick and the follow-up.

Sturridge provided the breakthrough in the 37th minute, striking from outside the penalty area to beat Stoke’s goalkeeper. Liverpool took that single-goal advantage into the interval.

Other Liverpool attempts brought a disallowed goal and two efforts against the woodwork. Mignolet also had work to do before the interval, stretching to save a Walters shot. The goalkeeper’s first-half intervention preceded the more decisive double save near the end.

Liverpool were approaching a winning start when Daniel Agger handled inside his own area and Stoke received a penalty. Walters had the opportunity to turn the result into a draw with very little time remaining.

Mignolet parried the penalty, then got up to stop the follow-up. Liverpool held the 1–0 lead and took their first opening-day league victory since 2008, with a debutant’s late intervention protecting Sturridge’s earlier finish.
