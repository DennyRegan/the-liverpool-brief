Luis Suárez scored four goals and supplied another as Liverpool beat Norwich City 5–1 at Anfield on 4 December 2013. His first three came before half-time, giving him a third league hat-trick against Norwich, and his later free-kick completed the four-goal performance.

The opening goal came in the 15th minute when Suárez struck a volley from distance over John Ruddy. Norwich had survived the opening exchanges without conceding, but the long-range finish began a scoring sequence that would settle the match before the interval.

For the second, Philippe Coutinho delivered a corner and Gerrard flicked it on. Suárez met the ball with a volley to make it 2–0 in the 29th minute. Six minutes later he lifted the ball over his marker before driving another shot beyond Ruddy for his third.

Liverpool went in 3–0 ahead. Ruddy saved further attempts after the break, but Suárez added his fourth with a direct free-kick. The forward had now produced the first Liverpool sequence of three league hat-tricks against the same club.

Norwich found a reply when Nathan Redmond crossed for Bradley Johnson to head past Simon Mignolet. Liverpool then completed the scoring through Sterling, who finished from Suárez’s supply in the 88th minute. The creator of four goals had also helped produce the fifth.

The result left Liverpool fourth with 27 points from 14 league matches. Its central achievement was individual: a first-half treble expanded into four goals, followed by an assist that made Suárez’s contribution run through every Liverpool entry on the scoresheet.
