Daniel Sturridge scored twice as Liverpool beat Everton 4–0 at Anfield on 28 January 2014. Gerrard’s header opened the scoring and Suárez completed it after half-time, giving Brendan Rodgers his first derby victory as Liverpool manager.

The opener came in the 21st minute when Gerrard headed in Suárez’s corner. Liverpool then added two goals in quick succession through Sturridge, turning the lead into a three-goal half-time advantage.

Coutinho’s pass released Sturridge for the first of his double in the 33rd minute. Two minutes later Kolo Touré’s ball gave the forward another opening, and he lobbed Tim Howard for 3–0. The two goals came from different deliveries but the same finisher.

Suárez added the fourth early after the break, running through to score in the 50th minute. Liverpool also received a penalty after Howard’s challenge on Sterling, but Sturridge sent the kick over the bar and missed the opportunity to complete a hat-trick.

The penalty miss left the score unchanged. Liverpool’s advantage had been established by Gerrard and Sturridge before the interval and enlarged by Suárez’s second-half run, producing four unanswered goals in the league derby.

Liverpool stayed fourth with 46 points from 23 matches, four points above Everton in sixth. Beyond Rodgers’s first win in the fixture, the result widened the gap between two clubs competing near the Champions League places.
