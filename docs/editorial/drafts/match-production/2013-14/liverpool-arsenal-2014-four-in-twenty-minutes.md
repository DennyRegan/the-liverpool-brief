Liverpool scored four goals inside the opening 20 minutes as they beat Premier League leaders Arsenal 5–1 at Anfield on 8 February 2014. Skrtel and Sterling each finished with two goals, while Sturridge supplied the other in a victory that began an eleven-match league winning sequence.

The first goal arrived within a minute. Skrtel met Gerrard’s free-kick to put Liverpool ahead, then headed in the captain’s corner for his second in the 10th minute. Arsenal were already two goals behind when Suárez struck the post with a further attempt.

Sterling made it 3–0 in the 16th minute, meeting Suárez’s low cross. Coutinho then played Sturridge through to finish the fourth in the 20th. Four separate scoring moves had produced a lead that Liverpool carried into half-time.

Sterling added his second after the break. His initial effort was saved, but he followed up to make it 5–0 in the 52nd minute. Arsenal’s reply came through Mikel Arteta’s penalty after Gerrard’s challenge on Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain.

Liverpool remained fourth with 50 points from 25 games. Arsenal had arrived as leaders; the contemporary table after the day’s fixtures showed Chelsea above them. For Liverpool, the significance would extend beyond this afternoon: it was the first of eleven consecutive league wins, ending at Norwich in April.
