Gerrard’s stoppage-time penalty completed Liverpool’s 3–2 victory over Fulham at Craven Cottage on 12 February 2014. Touré’s own goal and a later Richardson finish had given the home side two separate leads, but Liverpool recovered both before their captain scored the winner.

The first setback came when Touré turned Kieran Richardson’s cross into his own net. Liverpool levelled before half-time through Sturridge, who collected Gerrard’s pass and finished with a shot that went in off the post. The interval arrived with the score 1–1.

Suárez struck the post after the break, but Fulham took the lead again. An unsuccessful clearance by Skrtel left Richardson with the opening to score in the 63rd minute. Liverpool now had to respond to a second deficit.

Coutinho supplied that response in the 72nd minute, curling in the equaliser. His goal brought the score to 2–2, leaving the match open for the late penalty that would settle it.

Fulham’s Sascha Riether fouled Sturridge inside the area and Liverpool were awarded a spot-kick in added time. Gerrard converted to make it 3–2. The captain had helped create the first equaliser and now supplied the finish that turned the two recoveries into a win.

The three points left Liverpool fourth with 53 from 26 league games, four behind Chelsea. Coming straight after the 5–1 home win over Arsenal, the Fulham result extended the league winning sequence through a markedly different contest: two equalisers followed by an added-time winner.
