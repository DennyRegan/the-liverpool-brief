Liverpool came from behind to beat Newcastle United 2–1 at Anfield on 11 May 2014, but finished second as Manchester City won on the final day. Agger and Sturridge scored from Gerrard free-kicks to overturn Skrtel’s first-half own goal.

Newcastle took the lead in the 20th minute when Gouffran’s cross was turned into his own net by Skrtel. The visitors retained that advantage until half-time, leaving Liverpool behind in their final fixture of the championship challenge.

Liverpool’s recovery came through two similar deliveries within two minutes. Agger met Gerrard’s free-kick to equalise in the 63rd minute. Another Gerrard set-piece then reached Sturridge, who finished in the 65th to put Liverpool ahead.

Newcastle were reduced to ten men when Shola Ameobi received two bookings for dissent soon after the second goal. Substitute Paul Dummett was also sent off later following a challenge on Suárez, and Liverpool finished the match against nine men.

The victory took Liverpool to 84 points from 38 games, with 26 wins, six draws and six defeats. They scored 101 league goals and conceded 50. City’s concurrent victory meant the champions finished on 86 points, two ahead.

The final result therefore completed a Liverpool recovery on the afternoon without changing the championship winner. Rodgers’s side ended the league season as runners-up, having reached the last day of the title contest and secured a return to Champions League football.
