Suárez scored a hat-trick as Liverpool won 6–3 at Cardiff City on 22 March 2014. The visitors twice fell behind before drawing level by half-time, then scored four more after the break to complete a nine-goal match.

Jordon Mutch put Cardiff ahead in the ninth minute after Fraizer Campbell’s pull-back. Liverpool equalised through Suárez, who finished Glen Johnson’s cross, but Campbell then scored himself to restore Cardiff’s lead. Skrtel met Coutinho’s delivery late in the half to make it 2–2.

Skrtel added his second after the interval, heading in another Coutinho delivery, this time from a corner. Liverpool had the lead for the first time, and Suárez soon widened it by finishing after Sturridge’s backheel.

The attacking partners combined again for the fifth goal. Suárez provided the pass and Sturridge finished in the 75th minute, taking Liverpool to 5–2 after their two first-half recoveries.

Mutch headed Cardiff’s third late on after a Kenwyne Jones knock-down. Liverpool nevertheless had one more goal to add: Suárez scored again in stoppage time to complete his treble and the 6–3 result.

It was his sixth Liverpool hat-trick, and his 28 league goals equalled Robbie Fowler’s existing club Premier League season mark. Liverpool’s 82 goals after 30 games had also already overtaken their previous highest Premier League season tally of 77.

The win left Liverpool second with 65 points from 30 matches, four behind Chelsea with a game in hand. Their scoring landmarks were accompanied by a result that required two recoveries before the second-half lead was built.
