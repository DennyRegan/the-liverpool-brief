Liverpool lost a three-goal lead in the closing stages as Crystal Palace recovered to draw 3–3 at Selhurst Park on 5 May 2014. Delaney’s long-range strike and two goals from substitute Gayle turned the score from 3–0 to 3–3 between the 79th and 88th minutes.

Liverpool entered level on points with Manchester City, but with an inferior goal difference and one more match played. Allen gave them the lead in the 18th minute, heading in Gerrard’s corner, and they reached half-time 1–0 ahead.

The advantage grew soon after the restart. Sturridge’s shot deflected off Delaney for the second goal, which is now credited as a Delaney own goal. Suárez then exchanged passes with Sterling and finished to make it 3–0 in the 55th minute.

Palace’s first reply came in the 79th minute when Delaney’s shot deflected from Johnson and went past Mignolet. Two minutes later Bolasie advanced on a counter-attack and supplied Gayle, who finished to reduce the deficit to one.

Gayle completed the recovery in the 88th minute. Glenn Murray chested down a long ball and the substitute scored again, bringing Palace level after Liverpool had held a three-goal lead for much of the half.

The draw put Liverpool back on top with 81 points from 37 games, but City had 80 from 36. Liverpool had one fixture left, while City still had two. The late goals had cost Liverpool two points without mathematically deciding the title that evening.
