Gerrard converted two penalties as Liverpool won 2–1 at West Ham United on 6 April 2014 and returned to the top of the Premier League. The ninth consecutive league victory came through two spot-kicks, either side of Guy Demel’s disputed equaliser.

The first penalty followed Suárez’s run into the area after Gerrard’s pass. James Tomkins handled, and Gerrard converted in the 44th minute to give Liverpool the lead.

West Ham replied before half-time. Mignolet dropped a corner while challenged by Carroll, and Demel scored from the loose ball. The officials consulted before allowing the goal to stand, leaving the interval score 1–1.

Lucas replaced Coutinho at the break and later supplied Flanagan with a pass into the area. Adrián challenged the defender, and Liverpool were awarded their second penalty. The goalkeeper had touched the ball before the collision, a point described in the contemporary independent account of the disputed decision.

Gerrard converted again to restore Liverpool’s lead. Carroll headed against the crossbar during the second half, and Suárez also struck the woodwork twice, but there were no further goals.

Liverpool finished the day on 74 points from 33 games, two ahead of Chelsea. Manchester City had 70 from 31, retaining two games in hand, and would visit Anfield next. The West Ham victory restored Liverpool’s first place before that direct meeting.
