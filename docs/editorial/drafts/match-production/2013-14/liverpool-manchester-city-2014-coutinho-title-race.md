Coutinho’s second-half winner gave Liverpool a 3–2 victory over Manchester City at Anfield on 13 April 2014. The visitors had recovered Liverpool’s two-goal half-time lead, but the Brazilian’s finish restored the advantage in the direct meeting between title contenders.

Before the match, a minute’s silence marked the approaching 25th anniversary of the Hillsborough disaster. Liverpool then took the lead after six minutes when Suárez released Sterling, who moved past Joe Hart and Vincent Kompany before finishing.

Skrtel headed in Gerrard’s corner to make it 2–0 in the 26th minute. City also lost Yaya Touré to injury before the interval, with Javi García coming on, and reached half-time two goals behind.

The visitors changed the score after the break. Substitute James Milner supplied David Silva for City’s first, and another Silva effort deflected from Glen Johnson into his own net for the equaliser. Liverpool’s lead had disappeared with more than a quarter of the match remaining.

Coutinho supplied the winning goal in the 78th minute. Kompany’s attempted clearance fell to him, and he finished to put Liverpool 3–2 ahead. Henderson was sent off in added time for a tackle on Samir Nasri, but Liverpool retained the lead.

It was Liverpool’s tenth successive league win. They stood on 77 points from 34 games, two ahead of Chelsea and seven above City, who had played 32. Four league fixtures remained for Liverpool, and City’s games in hand remained part of the championship calculation.
