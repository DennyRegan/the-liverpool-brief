Chelsea ended Liverpool’s eleven-match league winning run with a 2–0 victory at Anfield on 27 April 2014. Ba scored in first-half added time and Willian added the second near the end, giving Liverpool their first league defeat of the calendar year.

The opening goal followed Gerrard slipping while receiving the ball. Ba took possession, ran through and finished past Mignolet. The incident gave Chelsea a 1–0 lead at the interval, leaving Liverpool to seek an equaliser after the break.

Mark Schwarzer saved efforts from Allen and Gerrard during the second half. Liverpool also brought Sturridge on from the bench, but the equalising goal did not arrive.

Chelsea completed the scoring in stoppage time. Substitute Torres supplied Willian, who finished to make it 2–0. Both goals had come in added time, the first before the interval and the second before the final whistle.

Liverpool remained top with 80 points from 36 matches. Chelsea had 78 from 36, while City stood on 77 from 35 and held a better goal difference. Liverpool still led on points, but City’s game in hand could now bring them level.

Two league fixtures remained for Rodgers’s side. The defeat had ended both the eleven-win sequence and their unbeaten league record in 2014, altering the championship positions without yet settling the final outcome.
