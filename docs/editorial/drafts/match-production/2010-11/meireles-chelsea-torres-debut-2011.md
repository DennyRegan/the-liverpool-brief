Liverpool won 1–0 at Stamford Bridge on 6 February 2011, with Raul Meireles scoring the only goal on Fernando Torres’s Chelsea debut. It was Kenny Dalglish’s fourth consecutive league victory since returning to the Liverpool dugout.

Torres started against the club he had left in the January transfer window. Jamie Carragher blocked one of his first-half shots after Didier Drogba’s pass. Liverpool also came close before the interval, when Maxi Rodríguez struck the crossbar from Steven Gerrard’s delivery.

The teams went in level at half-time. Chelsea replaced Torres with Salomon Kalou in the 66th minute, three minutes before Liverpool took the lead.

Gerrard supplied the cross that created the winner. Neither Petr Čech nor Branislav Ivanović cleared the delivery, and Meireles finished from close range. The midfielder had scored four times in his last five league appearances.

Liverpool kept their fourth successive league clean sheet, having already beaten Wolves, Fulham and Stoke City. Luis Suárez, who had scored on his debut against Stoke, remained an unused substitute at Chelsea.

The win left Liverpool sixth on 38 points, six behind Chelsea, who had played one match fewer. Torres’s debut had ended before the decisive goal, while Meireles extended the run that was carrying Liverpool up the table.
