Liverpool lost 3–1 at Blackburn Rovers on 5 January 2011 in what proved to be Roy Hodgson’s final match in charge. Martin Olsson opened the scoring and Benjani added two more before Steven Gerrard’s late reply.

Blackburn went ahead in the 32nd minute when Mame Biram Diouf supplied Olsson, who finished past Pepe Reina. Benjani then controlled Morten Gamst Pedersen’s delivery, turned and scored to make it 2–0 before half-time. Liverpool’s deficit grew after the restart as Junior Hoilett reached the byline and pulled the ball back for Benjani’s second.

Gerrard eventually drove home a loose ball to reduce the gap. Liverpool then received a penalty following a challenge by Michel Salgado, but Gerrard put his kick over the bar. The late chances produced no further goal.

This was Liverpool’s fourth successive away league defeat, following losses at Stoke City, Tottenham Hotspur and Newcastle United. They were twelfth with 25 points from twenty matches, having lost nine of them.

On 8 January, Liverpool announced Hodgson’s departure by mutual agreement and Kenny Dalglish’s appointment until the end of the season. Dalglish’s first game would be the FA Cup tie at Manchester United the next day. The Ewood Park defeat therefore closed Hodgson’s Liverpool match record; the managerial change came three days afterwards.
