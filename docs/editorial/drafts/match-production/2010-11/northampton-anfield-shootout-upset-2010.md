Northampton Town knocked Liverpool out of the League Cup at Anfield on 22 September 2010, winning 4–2 on penalties after a 2–2 draw. The League Two visitors recovered from an early deficit, then came within four minutes of winning in extra time.

Roy Hodgson changed all eleven starters from the preceding league defeat at Manchester United. Brad Jones and Danny Wilson began their Liverpool careers in the starting side, while Jonjo Shelvey and Tom Ince made their debuts from the bench.

Milan Jovanović put Liverpool ahead in the ninth minute, finishing after Daniel Agger’s pass. Billy McKay equalised in the second half, and the match reached ninety minutes with the sides level.

Michael Jacobs then gave Northampton the lead in the eighth minute of extra time. David N’Gog’s equaliser in the 116th minute kept Liverpool in the competition and forced the shootout.

Northampton’s Steve Guinan and Liverpool’s N’Gog missed the opening penalties. Shelvey and Agger converted Liverpool’s next two, but Nathan Eccleston missed their fourth. Northampton scored four consecutive kicks after Guinan’s miss, with Abdul Osman converting the decisive penalty.

Liverpool’s first League Cup match of the season was also their last. Their next domestic cup appearance would come in January, when Manchester United eliminated them from the FA Cup at Old Trafford.
