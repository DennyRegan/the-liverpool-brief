Kenny Dalglish’s first match after returning as Liverpool manager ended in a 1–0 FA Cup defeat at Old Trafford on 9 January 2011. Ryan Giggs scored an early penalty, and Steven Gerrard’s first-half dismissal left Liverpool to play most of the tie with ten men.

Dalglish had been appointed the previous day following Roy Hodgson’s departure by mutual agreement. The change came between the league defeat at Blackburn Rovers and this third-round cup match against Manchester United.

United were awarded a penalty in the opening minute following Daniel Agger’s challenge on Dimitar Berbatov. Giggs converted in the second minute, giving Liverpool an immediate deficit.

Gerrard was sent off in the 32nd minute after a challenge on Michael Carrick. United almost added a second before half-time when Jonny Evans headed against the post, but the interval score remained 1–0.

Liverpool still threatened after the break through Fábio Aurélio’s free-kick, which Tomasz Kuszczak saved. They could not find an equaliser, and the early penalty remained the only goal.

Dalglish’s return did not produce an immediate winning run: Liverpool lost at Blackpool and drew the home derby against Everton in his next two matches. His first victory followed at Wolverhampton Wanderers on 22 January, a 3–0 league win. At Old Trafford, the renewed managerial spell had begun with a cup exit and two decisive incidents before half-time.
