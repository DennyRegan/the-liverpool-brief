Andy Carroll scored his first two Liverpool goals in a 3–0 victory over Manchester City at Anfield on 11 April 2011. Dirk Kuyt scored between them, with all three goals coming before half-time.

Kenny Dalglish also gave Jon Flanagan his senior Liverpool debut, starting him at right-back. Luis Suárez came close early on when Joe Hart tipped his low shot onto the post.

Carroll opened the scoring in the 13th minute, driving a shot past Hart from outside the penalty area. City lost Carlos Tévez to injury shortly afterwards, with Mario Balotelli taking his place.

Kuyt doubled Liverpool’s lead in the 34th minute after the ball reached him across the penalty area. Within a minute Carroll had his second, heading in Raul Meireles’s cross. Liverpool went to the interval three goals ahead.

They had further openings after the restart, with Suárez volleying wide and Kuyt sending a shot across the face of goal. City could not reduce the deficit, and the first-half score stood at full-time.

Liverpool remained sixth, five points behind Tottenham Hotspur, who had played one game fewer. They had lost at West Bromwich Albion in their previous match, and the visit to Arsenal would follow this home win. Carroll’s first goals and Flanagan’s debut had arrived together in a league victory over fourth-placed opposition.
