Dirk Kuyt scored a hat-trick as Liverpool beat Premier League leaders Manchester United 3–1 at Anfield on 6 March 2011. Luis Suárez helped create two of the goals, and Andy Carroll made his Liverpool debut from the bench.

Liverpool went ahead in the 34th minute. Suárez worked his way through United’s defence and supplied the close-range opportunity that Kuyt finished. The second followed five minutes later when Nani’s misdirected header fell to Kuyt, who headed past Edwin van der Sar.

Liverpool led 2–0 at half-time, and Kuyt completed his hat-trick in the 65th minute. Van der Sar could not hold Suárez’s free-kick, leaving Kuyt to score from the rebound. The three finishes came from close to goal, following different openings created by Suárez or United’s attempted clearance.

Carroll replaced Raul Meireles in the 74th minute for his first competitive Liverpool appearance. Javier Hernández scored a late consolation for United, but Liverpool’s three-goal lead had already decided the match.

Liverpool remained sixth after the victory, while United still led the league. It was Dalglish’s first league meeting with United since his January return; his opening match had been the FA Cup defeat at Old Trafford.

European disappointment followed, with a defeat at Braga and a goalless return leg ending Liverpool’s Europa League campaign. Against United at Anfield, however, Kuyt’s finishing and Suárez’s creation had produced a league victory and a debut for the other January striker.
