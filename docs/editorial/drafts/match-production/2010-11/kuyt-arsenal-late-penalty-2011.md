Liverpool drew 1–1 at Arsenal on 17 April 2011 after both teams scored penalties deep into second-half added time. Robin van Persie put Arsenal ahead, but Dirk Kuyt answered before the final whistle.

Kenny Dalglish again started Jon Flanagan, who had made his debut against Manchester City six days earlier. Jack Robinson joined him in the defence when an injury forced Fábio Aurélio off after 22 minutes.

Liverpool lost another defender when Jamie Carragher suffered a head injury in the second half and was replaced by Sotirios Kyrgiakos. Andy Carroll also came off, with Jonjo Shelvey replacing him. The disruption contributed to a prolonged period of added time, and the match was still goalless at the end of ninety minutes.

Arsenal received the first penalty following Jay Spearing’s challenge on Cesc Fàbregas. Van Persie converted in the eighth minute of added time.

Liverpool then won a penalty when Emmanuel Eboué challenged Lucas Leiva. Kuyt converted in the twelfth added minute to restore parity. Arsenal’s late lead had lasted only until the next penalty.

Liverpool remained sixth after the draw. They returned to Anfield the following weekend and beat Birmingham City 5–0.
