Liverpool’s first match after the change of ownership ended in a 2–0 derby defeat at Goodison Park on 17 October 2010. Tim Cahill and Mikel Arteta scored for Everton, leaving Roy Hodgson’s side nineteenth in the Premier League.

NESV had completed its takeover on 15 October, and the new owners attended the derby. On the pitch, Liverpool had won only one of their opening eight league matches by the end of the afternoon.

Everton went ahead in the 34th minute. Seamus Coleman carried the ball down the right and supplied a cutback for Cahill, who drove his finish into the roof of the net. Liverpool reached the interval a goal behind.

Arteta doubled the lead five minutes into the second half. A Leighton Baines corner was cleared out to the midfielder, whose swerving shot beat Pepe Reina.

Liverpool could not reduce the deficit, and the defeat left them with six points from eight matches. The ownership change had arrived between two league defeats: Blackpool had won at Anfield before the international break, and Everton now took the derby.

Hodgson’s side followed the Goodison defeat with a draw at Napoli and three successive league wins over Blackburn Rovers, Bolton Wanderers and Chelsea. That recovery lifted Liverpool out of the relegation places, but it came after a derby in which a new chapter off the pitch had begun with another defeat on it.
