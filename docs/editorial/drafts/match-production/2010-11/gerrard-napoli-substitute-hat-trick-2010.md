Steven Gerrard came off the bench at half-time and scored a hat-trick as Liverpool beat Napoli 3–1 at Anfield on 4 November 2010. All three goals arrived in the final quarter-hour of the Europa League group match.

Liverpool had drawn the away meeting in Naples two weeks earlier. This time Napoli took the lead through Ezequiel Lavezzi, who finished after Christian Poulsen’s misplaced header and Edinson Cavani’s flick. Roy Hodgson replaced Milan Jovanović with Gerrard at the interval, with Liverpool still trailing.

The equaliser came when former Liverpool defender Andrea Dossena’s backpass left goalkeeper Morgan De Sanctis under pressure. Gerrard challenged as De Sanctis tried to clear, and the ball rebounded into the goal.

Liverpool were awarded a penalty late in the match after Salvatore Aronica’s challenge on Glen Johnson. Gerrard converted it in the 88th minute to put the home side ahead for the first time.

He completed the hat-trick a minute later, running on to Lucas Leiva’s pass and lifting his finish over De Sanctis. Napoli’s lead had become a two-goal Liverpool victory before the final whistle.

Liverpool now had eight points in Group K, three clear at the top. Three days later they beat Chelsea 2–0 in the league, extending a run of improved results after the October derby defeat.
