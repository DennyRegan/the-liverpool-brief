Luis Suárez scored on his Liverpool debut as Kenny Dalglish’s side beat Stoke City 2–0 at Anfield on 2 February 2011. Raul Meireles had put Liverpool ahead before the new signing came on to add the second.

The match followed a January transfer window in which Fernando Torres left and Suárez and Andy Carroll joined Liverpool. Carroll was unavailable through injury, while Suárez began on the bench.

Neither side scored in the first half. Asmir Begović saved Glen Johnson’s header from Martin Kelly’s cross as Liverpool sought an opening. Meireles provided it two minutes after the restart, driving in after Steven Gerrard’s free-kick reached him via Sotirios Kyrgiakos.

Dalglish introduced Suárez for Fábio Aurélio in the 63rd minute. Sixteen minutes later Dirk Kuyt played him through, and Suárez took the ball around Begović. His shot went in despite Andy Wilkinson’s attempted clearance, giving him a goal on his first appearance.

Liverpool had now won three consecutive league matches without conceding, following victories at Wolves and at home to Fulham. Their next match brought a fourth win and clean sheet, away at Chelsea. At Anfield, the opening match after the transfer window had ended with Meireles continuing his scoring run and Suárez beginning his Liverpool account.
