Fernando Torres scored both goals as Liverpool beat Chelsea 2–0 at Anfield on 7 November 2010. The defending champions arrived as league leaders, but Liverpool completed a third consecutive Premier League win and finished the day ninth.

Torres’s first goal came after eleven minutes. Dirk Kuyt sent a diagonal pass into his path, and Torres controlled the ball before finishing past Petr Čech. Liverpool had the advantage without needing to wait for the late turnaround that had decided their Europa League match against Napoli three days earlier.

The second goal arrived just before half-time. Raul Meireles won possession from Ashley Cole and found Torres on the left. The striker came inside and curled his shot into the far corner, giving Liverpool a two-goal interval lead.

Chelsea threatened after the restart, with Pepe Reina saving from Florent Malouda. Nicolas Anelka also struck the crossbar, but neither opportunity reduced Liverpool’s lead. At the other end Čech denied Kuyt, and the score remained unchanged.

Liverpool had followed the October defeat at Everton with league victories over Blackburn Rovers and Bolton Wanderers before beating Chelsea. The improvement did not become an uninterrupted climb: a draw at Wigan Athletic and defeat at Stoke City followed. At Anfield, though, Torres’s double had delivered a league win over the champions during Roy Hodgson’s brief tenure.
