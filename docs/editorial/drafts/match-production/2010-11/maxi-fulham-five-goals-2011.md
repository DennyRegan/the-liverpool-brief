Maxi Rodríguez scored a hat-trick as Liverpool won 5–2 at Fulham on 9 May 2011. The victory temporarily lifted Kenny Dalglish’s side to fifth, while Jamie Carragher’s 666th Liverpool appearance moved him outright second on the club’s list.

Liverpool scored after just 32 seconds. Luis Suárez reached Lucas Leiva’s pass and crossed, with the ball deflecting off Carlos Salcido before Maxi finished. Maxi added his second in the seventh minute, volleying in Glen Johnson’s cross at the back post.

Dirk Kuyt made it 3–0 after sixteen minutes, his shot beating Mark Schwarzer after Johnson’s pass. Liverpool held that lead at the interval, but Mousa Dembélé pulled a goal back for Fulham in the 57th minute.

Maxi completed his hat-trick with a shot from distance in the 70th minute. Five minutes later Jonjo Shelvey played Suárez through, and the striker rounded Schwarzer to score Liverpool’s fifth. Steve Sidwell’s late reply left the final score at 5–2.

It was Maxi’s second hat-trick in three league matches, while Kuyt had scored in five consecutive league games. Liverpool had 58 points and a two-point lead over Tottenham Hotspur, who had played one game fewer.

The two teams met at Anfield six days later, when Tottenham won 2–0. A further defeat at Aston Villa left Liverpool sixth in the final table, four points behind Tottenham.
