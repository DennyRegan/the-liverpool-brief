Fernando Torres finished his first Liverpool season with a Premier League record on 11 May 2008. His goal in a 2–0 victory at Tottenham Hotspur took him to 24 league goals, surpassing the previous best by an overseas player in a debut Premier League campaign.

Liverpool's fourth-place finish was already settled before the visit to White Hart Lane. The match therefore carried an individual landmark alongside the final opportunity to add to their points total.

Neither side scored in the first half. Pepe Reina saved a Dimitar Berbatov header, while Liverpool had to wait until the closing quarter of the match to take the lead. Torres helped create the opener, directing a header into Andriy Voronin's path for the Ukrainian to finish.

Torres then scored himself. Yossi Benayoun supplied the pass, and the striker went beyond Michael Dawson before sending a low shot past Radek Černý. Liverpool had scored twice in a short period and retained that advantage to the end.

The finish took Torres past Ruud van Nistelrooy's total of 23 in his first Premier League season.

Liverpool ended the league season with 76 points. Torres finished with 33 goals in all competitions, 24 of them in the league: the final one at Tottenham supplied the record with which his first campaign closed.
