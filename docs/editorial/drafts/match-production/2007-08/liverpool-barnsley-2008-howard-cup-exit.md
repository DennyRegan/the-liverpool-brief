Brian Howard scored in stoppage time to eliminate Liverpool from the FA Cup at Anfield on 16 February 2008. Barnsley's 2–1 fifth-round victory was secured after a series of saves by goalkeeper Luke Steele had denied Liverpool a second goal.

The Championship side had fallen behind in the first half. Xabi Alonso helped move Liverpool forward, Ryan Babel reached the left side of the penalty area and his cutback found Dirk Kuyt. The striker finished in the 32nd minute, giving Rafael Benítez's side a lead they held until the interval.

Benítez had begun with Charles Itandje in goal and Steven Gerrard among the substitutes. Fernando Torres was not in the match squad. Steele kept Liverpool’s lead to one goal when he saved a Peter Crouch header just before half-time.

Barnsley equalised after the break. Martin Devaney crossed and Stephen Foster headed towards goal; Itandje got a hand to the ball but could not keep it out. The visitors now had a result to defend, and Steele's work became central to the contest.

Liverpool pressed for the goal that would restore their lead. Steele turned a Lucas Leiva header against the bar and saved from Yossi Benayoun. Harry Kewell had shots blocked and another effort struck the bar. Gerrard replaced Lucas in the closing stages, but Liverpool could not find a way past the visiting goalkeeper again.

The decisive finish came at the opposite end. Howard struck from outside the penalty area in stoppage time, giving Barnsley a lead too late for Liverpool to recover.

The defeat removed the FA Cup from Liverpool's season three days before Inter Milan were due at Anfield in the Champions League. A tie Liverpool had led at half-time ended with the visitors through to the quarter-finals, their goalkeeper's resistance rewarded by Howard's last intervention.
