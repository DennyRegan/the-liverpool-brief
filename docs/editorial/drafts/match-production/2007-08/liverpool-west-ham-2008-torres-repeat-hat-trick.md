Fernando Torres followed his hat-trick against Middlesbrough with another in Liverpool's next home league match. West Ham United were beaten 4–0 at Anfield on 5 March 2008, a result that brought Liverpool level with Everton on points and above them on goal difference in fourth place.

Liverpool had won away at Bolton between the two Anfield fixtures. The West Ham match was their game in hand on Everton, and Torres gave them an early advantage when he turned in Dirk Kuyt's low cross in the eighth minute.

The first goal stood alone at the interval. Liverpool had to wait until the second half to establish a margin, with Kuyt again supplying the delivery. This time Torres met the cross with a header to put the home side two ahead.

The striker completed his hat-trick late in the match. John Arne Riise's headed contribution found him in the penalty area; Torres moved past Lucas Neill and finished. It was his sixth goal across two successive Premier League appearances at Anfield and his eighteenth league goal of the season.

Steven Gerrard added the fourth with a shot from distance shortly afterwards. Liverpool's narrow half-time lead had become a comprehensive win, with Torres responsible for the first three goals and the captain completing the scoring.

The result mattered in the contest for Champions League qualification as well as in Torres' individual season. Liverpool had entered the evening three points behind Everton with a match in hand. They finished it in fourth, having used that fixture to erase the points deficit and put their superior goal difference to use.
