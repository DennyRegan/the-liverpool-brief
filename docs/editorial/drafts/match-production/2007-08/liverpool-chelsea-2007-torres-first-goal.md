Fernando Torres needed 16 minutes of his first competitive appearance at Anfield to score his first Liverpool goal. Chelsea left with a point on 19 August 2007, however, after Frank Lampard converted a penalty that referee Rob Styles subsequently acknowledged he should not have awarded.

Liverpool had opened their Premier League campaign with victory at Aston Villa. Their first home league fixture brought José Mourinho's Chelsea and an early examination of the side Rafael Benítez was building with Torres in attack.

Steven Gerrard supplied the pass for the opener, sending Torres into the left channel. The striker took the ball beyond Tal Ben Haim on the outside before directing a low right-footed finish across Petr Čech and into the far corner. It was a goal made by the captain's delivery and completed by the new forward's pace and accuracy.

Liverpool carried that advantage into the second half. Chelsea's equaliser came after Shaun Wright-Phillips crossed and Florent Malouda went over in an incident involving Steve Finnan. Styles gave a penalty; Lampard converted it in the 62nd minute.

The decision became central to the match's aftermath. Styles accepted the following day that he had made a mistake in awarding the penalty and was withdrawn from the next weekend's Premier League appointments. The acknowledgement did not alter the result: Liverpool's lead had become a 1–1 draw.
