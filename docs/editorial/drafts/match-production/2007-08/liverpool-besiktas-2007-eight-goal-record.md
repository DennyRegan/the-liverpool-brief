Liverpool began their fourth Champions League group match with one point. They ended it with eight goals, a record winning margin for the competition at the time and a route back into contention for the knockout rounds.

The 8–0 defeat of Beşiktaş at Anfield on 6 November 2007 was led by Yossi Benayoun, who scored a hat-trick and supplied further goals. Peter Crouch and substitute Ryan Babel each scored twice, with Steven Gerrard also on target.

Liverpool had lost 2–1 in Istanbul two weeks earlier. Another failure would have left little room to recover, but Crouch gave them the lead in the 19th minute. Hakan Arıkan stopped his first attempt; the striker followed up to score.

Benayoun added the second before half-time, controlling Andriy Voronin's cross and finishing. A 2–0 interval lead gave Liverpool the advantage, although the scale of the eventual result was established only after the restart.

Twice in quick succession Benayoun reacted to balls coming back from Arıkan. First the goalkeeper could not hold John Arne Riise's shot; then a Gerrard free-kick produced another rebound. Benayoun converted both, completing his hat-trick and putting Liverpool four ahead.

Gerrard exchanged passes with Voronin before scoring the fifth through a deflection. Babel's introduction brought two more: a backheeled finish from Benayoun's delivery, followed by a clearance that struck the substitute and looped into the net. Crouch headed in another Benayoun cross late on to complete the scoring.

The win did not secure qualification, and Liverpool still needed victories against Porto and Marseille. It did, however, change the shape of the campaign: the side with one point from three games had won their fourth by the biggest margin yet recorded in the Champions League.
