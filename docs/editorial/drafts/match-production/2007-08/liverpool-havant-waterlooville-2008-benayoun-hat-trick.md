Havant & Waterlooville twice led at Anfield before Yossi Benayoun's hat-trick helped Liverpool through the FA Cup fourth round on 26 January 2008. The 5–2 result settled the tie in the Premier League side's favour, but the visitors from the sixth tier had reached half-time level at 2–2.

The gap between the clubs was five divisions. Rafael Benítez selected a side that included Benayoun, Peter Crouch, Sami Hyypiä and Javier Mascherano, with Charles Itandje in goal. Steven Gerrard and Fernando Torres began on the bench.

Havant scored first. Richard Pacquette met a corner with a header early in the match, leaving Liverpool to respond to a deficit against opponents from the Conference South. Lucas Leiva supplied the equaliser with a curling shot from outside the penalty area, his first goal for Liverpool.

The visitors then took the lead again. Alfie Potter's attempt struck Martin Škrtel and went in, the goal being recorded as an own goal by the Liverpool defender. The equaliser had lasted only a few minutes, and Liverpool again had to work from behind.

Benayoun made it 2–2 just before the interval. His finish prevented Havant from taking an advantage into half-time and provided the first of three goals that would decide the match.

Jermaine Pennant crossed from the right for Benayoun to turn and score his second after the break. The hat-trick followed when goalkeeper Kevin Scriven stopped Ryan Babel’s shot and Benayoun converted the rebound. Liverpool now led 4–2; Crouch added the fifth late on after a contribution from substitute Gerrard.

Benayoun’s three goals had turned the tie Liverpool’s way, taking them through to the fifth round.
