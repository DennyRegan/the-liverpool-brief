Liverpool recovered from their FA Cup defeat by Barnsley with a 2–0 victory over Inter Milan at Anfield on 19 February 2008. Three days after that cup exit, late goals from Dirk Kuyt and Steven Gerrard established a substantial advantage in the Champions League last sixteen.

Inter arrived as Italian champions and Serie A leaders. Liverpool's immediate task was to establish a lead before the return at San Siro, but for much of the evening they struggled to turn their pressure into goals.

The visitors lost Marco Materazzi around the half-hour. Both of his bookings followed challenges on Fernando Torres, and the second left Roberto Mancini's side to play the remainder with ten men. Inter reorganised and continued to frustrate Liverpool, with Júlio César saving from Torres and Sami Hyypiä unable to convert headed opportunities.

Rafael Benítez changed his attacking options in the second half. Peter Crouch replaced Lucas Leiva, then Jermaine Pennant came on for Ryan Babel. Inter also lost Iván Córdoba to injury as the match entered its final stages.

Pennant supplied the delivery for the breakthrough. His cross reached Kuyt at the far post, and the forward's shot took a deflection from Maicon on its way in. With only minutes remaining, Liverpool finally had the lead their numerical advantage had promised without producing.

Gerrard added a second before the end. From the right side of the penalty area, he drove a low shot towards the far corner; the ball went in off the post, doubling the margin Liverpool would take to Italy.

The two goals altered the tie's balance in a short space of time. Inter had been close to leaving Anfield with a goalless draw despite Materazzi's dismissal. Instead, Liverpool carried a two-goal lead into the return leg. Qualification still had to be secured, but the response to the Barnsley defeat had put Benítez's side in a strong position against the Italian champions.
