Liverpool's third Champions League semi-final against Chelsea in four seasons ended with a different outcome from the first two. At Stamford Bridge on 30 April 2008, Chelsea won 3–2 after extra time and progressed 4–3 on aggregate to their first final in the competition.

John Arne Riise's late own goal at Anfield had left the first leg level at 1–1, with Chelsea holding an away goal. Liverpool therefore needed to score in the return, and their task became harder when Didier Drogba put the home side ahead in the first half.

Pepe Reina saved Salomon Kalou's shot but could not stop Drogba's follow-up. Liverpool had already lost Martin Škrtel to injury, with Sami Hyypiä coming on in his place, and now trailed both on the night and across the tie.

Yossi Benayoun created the equaliser in the 64th minute. Carrying the ball in from the right, he found Fernando Torres, who finished low past Petr Čech. The goal brought the aggregate score to 2–2 and levelled the away-goal count. Neither side scored again before the end of normal time, sending the tie into an additional half-hour.

Chelsea took control during the first period of extra time. Hyypiä fouled Michael Ballack and Frank Lampard converted the penalty. Drogba then met Nicolas Anelka's cross to score his second, putting Chelsea 3–1 ahead on the night and leaving Liverpool two goals behind in the tie.

Rafael Benítez's side still threatened a late reversal. Babel struck from distance in the 117th minute, beating Čech and reducing the match score to 3–2. One further Liverpool goal would have changed the outcome on away goals, but Chelsea held their lead through the remaining minutes.

Liverpool had won their previous Champions League semi-finals against Chelsea in 2005 and 2007. This time the London club advanced to face Manchester United in Moscow. Torres had prolonged the contest and Babel had kept it unresolved until the final stages, but Chelsea's two goals in the first half of extra time ended Liverpool's attempt to reach a third final in four years.
