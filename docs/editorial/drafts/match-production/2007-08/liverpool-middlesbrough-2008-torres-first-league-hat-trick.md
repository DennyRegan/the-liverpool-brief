Fernando Torres scored his first Premier League hat-trick as Liverpool beat Middlesbrough 3–2 at Anfield on 23 February 2008. The treble brought his total in all competitions for the season to 21, although a late Middlesbrough goal ensured Liverpool still had to protect a narrow lead.

Tuncay Şanlı had put the visitors ahead in the ninth minute, meeting Stewart Downing's free-kick. Liverpool's response came through two Torres finishes in quick succession, each exploiting a different opportunity.

For the equaliser, Torres intercepted Julio Arca's weak headed backpass, went around Mark Schwarzer and scored. Little more than a minute later, he received a pass from Fábio Aurélio and struck from outside the penalty area to give Liverpool a 2–1 advantage.

Middlesbrough thought they had answered before half-time, but another Tuncay finish was disallowed for handball. Liverpool took their lead into the interval, with Torres having changed the match through the interception and long-range shot.

The third goal arrived after the break. A long ball from Dirk Kuyt exposed hesitation between Schwarzer and David Wheater. Torres reached it and lifted the ball into the unguarded goal, completing his hat-trick and giving Liverpool a two-goal cushion.

Downing reduced the deficit late on, bringing the visitors back to 3–2. Jérémie Aliadière was then sent off after raising his hand to Javier Mascherano, and Liverpool held on for the points.

The victory moved Liverpool into fourth place in the immediate league table. Torres had already scored a League Cup hat-trick at Reading earlier in the campaign, but this was his first in the Premier League. His three goals converted a difficult opening into a win and pushed his first-season Liverpool total beyond twenty.
