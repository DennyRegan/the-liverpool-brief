Liverpool's Champions League recovery was completed in Marseille on 11 December 2007. A 4–0 victory at the Stade Vélodrome carried Rafael Benítez's side into the last sixteen, turning a group campaign that had begun with one point from three matches into qualification as runners-up.

The position remained demanding before the final game. Liverpool had beaten Beşiktaş 8–0 and Porto 4–1, but Marseille could still finish above them. With Porto defeating Beşiktaş in the other match, a draw would have suited the French side. Liverpool won emphatically instead.

Steven Gerrard opened the scoring in the fourth minute. Gaël Givet fouled him inside the penalty area, and although Steve Mandanda saved the resulting spot-kick, Gerrard followed in to score from the rebound.

Fernando Torres doubled the lead seven minutes later. Harry Kewell found him on the left and the striker carried the ball past defenders before finishing across Mandanda. Liverpool had established a two-goal advantage before the home side could make the occasion a prolonged contest over qualification.

Marseille tried to change the game, introducing Samir Nasri before half-time and Djibril Cissé at the interval. Liverpool nevertheless scored again soon after the restart. Kewell intercepted a poor clearance from Mandanda and supplied Dirk Kuyt, whose finish made it 3–0.

That goal left Marseille needing an extraordinary response. Cissé headed a chance wide, but Liverpool retained their lead and added a fourth in stoppage time when substitute Ryan Babel went around Mandanda to score.

The final Group A table placed Porto first and Liverpool second. Three successive victories had supplied nine points and sixteen goals, enough to reverse the damage of the opening three fixtures. The decisive step was taken away from Anfield: Liverpool arrived in Marseille with qualification still unsettled and left with a place in the knockout draw.
