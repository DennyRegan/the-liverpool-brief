Championship side Wolverhampton Wanderers knocked Liverpool out of the FA Cup with a 2–1 fourth-round win at Anfield on 28 January 2017. Richard Stearman and Andreas Weimann scored before half-time, and Divock Origi’s late reply could not prevent another cup exit.

Jürgen Klopp made nine changes from the team beaten by Southampton three days earlier, retaining only Loris Karius and Roberto Firmino. Wolves took the lead within a minute when Stearman headed Hélder Costa’s free-kick across goal and past Karius.

Costa threatened again with a solo run and shot wide before helping create the visitors’ second goal. His pass on a counter-attack found Weimann, who stepped around Karius and scored into an empty net four minutes before the interval.

Liverpool sent on Philippe Coutinho at half-time and later introduced Daniel Sturridge, but Wolves protected their lead into the closing stages. Sturridge kept a corner alive for Origi to score with four minutes remaining. Origi almost equalised from a similar situation soon afterwards, only for Harry Burgoyne to block on the line.

Liverpool had lost three successive home matches in eight days, against Swansea City, Southampton and Wolves. Both domestic cup campaigns were now over, with the pursuit of league points remaining after a January containing only one competitive victory.
