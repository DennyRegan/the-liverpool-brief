Liverpool secured fourth place in the Premier League by beating Middlesbrough 3–0 at Anfield on 21 May 2017. Georginio Wijnaldum’s goal in first-half added time opened the way to a victory that earned a Champions League play-off berth.

Liverpool knew a win would guarantee a top-four finish, but Middlesbrough kept the game goalless through most of the opening half. The visitors appealed for a penalty when Patrick Bamford went down under pressure from Dejan Lovren, but Martin Atkinson did not award one. Wijnaldum broke the deadlock after Nathaniel Clyne found Roberto Firmino, whose pass sent the midfielder through to finish inside the near post.

Coutinho doubled the lead six minutes after the restart with a low free-kick past Brad Guzan. Five minutes later, Middlesbrough were caught on the counter-attack and Lallana finished after the ball was worked back to him inside the area. Liverpool held the three-goal advantage to complete their league programme.

They finished with 76 points, one ahead of fifth-placed Arsenal, after winning their final two games against West Ham and Middlesbrough. The season record was twenty-two wins, ten draws and six defeats.

Fourth place provided a route back to the Champions League, but Liverpool still had to win a two-legged play-off in August to reach the group stage.
