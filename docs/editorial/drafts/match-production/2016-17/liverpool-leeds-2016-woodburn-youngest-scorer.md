Ben Woodburn became Liverpool’s youngest goalscorer in a 2–0 League Cup quarter-final victory over Leeds United at Anfield on 29 November 2016. At seventeen years and forty-five days, he surpassed Michael Owen’s record on only his second competitive first-team appearance.

Liverpool had made eight changes from the league win over Sunderland, with Woodburn initially on the bench. Simon Mignolet denied Hadi Sacko early on, while Marco Silvestri saved Georginio Wijnaldum’s effort at the other end. The teams reached half-time without a goal.

Leeds threatened after the break when Kemar Roofe curled a shot against the post. Woodburn replaced Kevin Stewart after sixty-seven minutes, and Wijnaldum soon struck the post for Liverpool.

Divock Origi finally put Liverpool ahead with fourteen minutes remaining, sliding in to meet Trent Alexander-Arnold’s cross. Five minutes later, Woodburn received Wijnaldum’s lay-off and drove the ball into the roof of the net from close range.

The two late goals sent Liverpool into the competition’s semi-finals for a third successive season. That run ended against Southampton in January, but the Leeds tie had already produced a new individual landmark in the club’s history.
