Liverpool earned their first Premier League win of 2017 by beating Tottenham Hotspur 2–0 at Anfield on 11 February. Sadio Mané scored both goals in a two-minute spell during the first half.

The opening goal began when Adam Lallana regained possession from Victor Wanyama and passed to Georginio Wijnaldum. Wijnaldum released Mané through the defence, and the forward lifted his finish over Hugo Lloris. Mané then won the ball from Eric Dier and supplied Lallana before converting the rebound after Lloris had saved from Lallana and Roberto Firmino.

Lloris denied Mané another goal, while Simon Mignolet saved from Son Heung-min as Tottenham threatened at the other end. Liverpool carried their two-goal lead into the break and held it through a goalless second half.

The victory ended a sequence of five league matches without a win, including defeats against Swansea City and Hull City. That run had overlapped Liverpool’s exits from both domestic cups, though they had won their FA Cup replay at Plymouth in January.

Liverpool moved into fourth place on goal difference with 49 points, one behind second-placed Tottenham. The result restored a place in the top four on the night after the difficult opening weeks of the year.
