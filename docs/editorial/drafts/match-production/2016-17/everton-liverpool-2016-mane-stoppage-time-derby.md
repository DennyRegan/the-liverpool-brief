Sadio Mané’s stoppage-time goal gave Liverpool a 1–0 victory over Everton at Goodison Park on 19 December 2016. The forward reacted to Daniel Sturridge’s shot against the post to settle a derby that had remained goalless into added time.

Jürgen Klopp retained the starting eleven from the previous week’s 3–0 win at Middlesbrough. Divock Origi went close before half-time, sending Nathaniel Clyne’s cross over the bar, while Ramiro Funes Mori headed wide for Everton. The teams reached the interval level.

Roberto Firmino threatened after the break, but Maarten Stekelenburg stopped his attempt to finish from a long ball. Joel Robles replaced the Everton goalkeeper midway through the half and later saved Firmino’s volley from James Milner’s corner.

Liverpool introduced Sturridge and Emre Can with eight minutes of normal time remaining. Sturridge’s low shot beat Robles but struck the post, leaving Mané to finish from close range in the fourth minute of stoppage time.

A second successive away league win returned Liverpool to second place. They had 37 points after seventeen games, six behind Chelsea, and ended December with further wins over Stoke City and Manchester City.
