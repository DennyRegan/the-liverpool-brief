Liverpool suffered their second league defeat of the season when Bournemouth overturned two separate two-goal deficits to win 4–3 at the Vitality Stadium on 4 December 2016. Nathan Aké scored the winner in stoppage time after Liverpool had led 3–1 midway through the second half.

The visitors had gone ahead through Sadio Mané, who reached Emre Can’s pass before Aké and finished underneath the advancing Artur Boruc. Within two minutes, Divock Origi doubled the lead by rounding Boruc and curling in from a tight angle after Jordan Henderson’s ball forward. Liverpool carried that 2–0 advantage into half-time.

Bournemouth substitute Ryan Fraser won a penalty after James Milner fouled him, and Callum Wilson converted to reduce the deficit. Can restored Liverpool’s two-goal cushion with a curling finish from Mané’s pass.

Fraser then scored himself before supplying the cross that Steve Cook controlled and turned into Bournemouth’s equaliser. Loris Karius saved from Benik Afobe, but could not hold another shot in added time and Aké converted the rebound.

The defeat left Liverpool third, four points behind leaders Chelsea. Their next match brought a draw with West Ham before away wins at Middlesbrough and Everton restored league momentum.
