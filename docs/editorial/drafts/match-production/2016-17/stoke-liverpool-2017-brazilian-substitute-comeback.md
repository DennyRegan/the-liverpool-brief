Liverpool came from behind to beat Stoke City 2–1 at the bet365 Stadium on 8 April 2017, with half-time substitutes Philippe Coutinho and Roberto Firmino scoring both goals. Simon Mignolet’s saves from Charlie Adam and Saido Berahino also proved important in securing the three points.

Trent Alexander-Arnold and Ben Woodburn started, with Woodburn making his first Premier League start. Stoke took the lead just before half-time when Jonathan Walters headed Xherdan Shaqiri’s cross past Mignolet at the near post. Coutinho and Firmino replaced the two young starters at the interval.

Mignolet kept Liverpool in contention by saving Adam’s close-range effort after a corner early in the second half. The visitors threatened through their substitutes, and Dejan Lovren headed against the crossbar.

Coutinho equalised when Emre Can’s ball into the area eventually reached him for a low finish. Within two minutes, Firmino met Georginio Wijnaldum’s ball over the defence with a first-time half-volley to put Liverpool ahead. Mignolet then denied Berahino at the back post to preserve the lead.

Klopp explained afterwards that he had been unsure whether Coutinho and Firmino would be ready for forty-five minutes. Their contributions helped Liverpool remain third with 63 points after thirty-two league games.
