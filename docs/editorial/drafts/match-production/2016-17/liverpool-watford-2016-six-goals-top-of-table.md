Liverpool moved to the top of the Premier League with a 6–1 win over Watford at Anfield on 6 November 2016. Sadio Mané scored twice as Jürgen Klopp’s side extended their unbeaten league run to nine matches.

Mané opened the scoring with a header from a short-corner routine involving Philippe Coutinho and James Milner. Coutinho quickly added a second with a shot from outside the area, and Emre Can headed in Adam Lallana’s cross before half-time.

Lallana supplied Liverpool’s fourth after the interval, breaking into the left channel and crossing for Roberto Firmino to finish from close range. Firmino then turned provider for Mané’s second, leaving Liverpool five goals ahead by the hour.

Loris Karius made saves from Étienne Capoue and Miguel Britos, but Daryl Janmaat denied Liverpool a clean sheet with a curling finish after exchanging passes with Nordin Amrabat. Substitute Daniel Sturridge twice struck the crossbar before Georginio Wijnaldum completed the scoring from a rebound in added time. It was Wijnaldum’s first competitive Liverpool goal.

Liverpool entered the international break with 26 points from eleven league games, one ahead of Chelsea. The victory established the autumn high point of a campaign that would ultimately bring a fourth-place finish.
