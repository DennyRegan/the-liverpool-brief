Liverpool opened the 2016–17 Premier League season with a 4–3 victory over Arsenal at the Emirates Stadium on 14 August 2016. Sadio Mané scored on his competitive debut, while Philippe Coutinho struck twice in a match that Liverpool led 4–1 before Arsenal recovered two goals.

Simon Mignolet saved Theo Walcott’s penalty, but Walcott put Arsenal ahead soon afterwards with a low finish into the far corner. Liverpool reached half-time level after Coutinho curled a free-kick into the top corner in added time.

The visitors took the lead early in the second half when Georginio Wijnaldum found Adam Lallana, who controlled the ball and finished past Petr Čech. Coutinho added his second from Nathaniel Clyne’s low cross before Mané drove in from the right, beat two defenders and scored with his left foot.

Alex Oxlade-Chamberlain cut in from the left to score almost immediately after Mané’s goal. Calum Chambers then headed in a free-kick delivery to reduce Arsenal’s deficit to one with fifteen minutes of normal time remaining. Liverpool held that advantage to take the three points.

Mané, Ragnar Klavan and Wijnaldum had all made their first competitive Liverpool appearances that afternoon. The season began with an away win against Arsenal and would finish with Liverpool securing fourth place.
