Liverpool’s first home league match of 2016–17 marked the opening of the expanded Main Stand with a 4–1 victory over champions Leicester City on 10 September 2016. Roberto Firmino scored twice, with Sadio Mané and Adam Lallana also finding the net at Anfield.

Firmino put Liverpool ahead after thirteen minutes, receiving James Milner’s pass before stepping past Robert Huth and finishing low beyond Kasper Schmeichel. Mané doubled the lead when Daniel Sturridge’s backheel released him to lift the ball over the goalkeeper.

Leicester were handed a way back into the match when Lucas Leiva’s misplaced pass across his own goal reached Jamie Vardy, who scored into an empty net. Huth then clipped the crossbar with a header, but Liverpool remained 2–1 ahead at the interval.

Lallana restored the two-goal advantage eleven minutes into the second half, striking into the top corner from Georginio Wijnaldum’s lay-off. Simon Mignolet denied Vardy as Leicester sought a response. With a minute of normal time remaining, Mané beat Schmeichel to a ball forward and squared for Firmino to complete the scoring.

The victory followed the opening win at Arsenal, defeat at Burnley and draw at Tottenham. Liverpool’s enlarged home ground began its competitive life with a win over the previous season’s title holders.
