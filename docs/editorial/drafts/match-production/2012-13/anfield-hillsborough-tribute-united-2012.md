Liverpool’s first Anfield match after the Hillsborough Independent Panel’s report ended in a 2–1 defeat by Manchester United on 23 September 2012. Steven Gerrard put the hosts ahead after Jonjo Shelvey’s dismissal, but Rafael and Robin van Persie turned the match in United’s favour.

The Panel had released its report on 12 September, confirming that supporters neither caused nor contributed to the disaster. Before kick-off, mosaics at Anfield displayed Truth, Justice and the number 96. The tributes marked the findings before the football began; they did not represent the end of the families’ pursuit of justice.

Liverpool reached half-time without conceding but had lost Shelvey, who was sent off following a challenge with Jonny Evans. Gerrard gave the ten men a lead just after the restart, controlling the ball on his chest and volleying past Anders Lindegaard. Rafael equalised five minutes later with a curling shot into the far top corner.

The deciding goal came from the penalty spot in the 81st minute. Referee Mark Halsey awarded United a penalty after Antonio Valencia went down during Glen Johnson’s challenge in the area. Van Persie beat Pepe Reina to leave Liverpool without a point from a match they had led with ten men.

Rodgers’ side remained without a league win after five matches, with two points from draws against Manchester City and Sunderland. Their first league victory came six days later, away at Norwich City.
