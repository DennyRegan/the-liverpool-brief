Liverpool beat Zenit Saint Petersburg 3–1 at Anfield on 21 February 2013 but went out of the Europa League on away goals. Two Luis Suárez free-kicks and a Joe Allen goal brought the round-of-32 tie level at 3–3 on aggregate, without overturning the visitors’ decisive away goal.

Liverpool had lost the first leg 2–0 in Russia. Their task became harder when Hulk collected Jamie Carragher’s mis-hit back-pass and scored after 19 minutes. Liverpool now needed four goals to go through.

Suárez began the recovery in the 28th minute after Daniel Agger was fouled by Nicolas Lombaerts on the edge of the area. His free-kick passed through a gap in the wall and beat Vyacheslav Malafeev. Allen made it 2–1 before half-time, scoring the rebound after Malafeev stopped his header from José Enrique’s cross. Enrique had worked the crossing position through an exchange with Jordan Henderson.

Just before the hour, Suárez curled in his second free-kick after a foul by Luís Neto. Malafeev later saved from Steven Gerrard and substitute Jonjo Shelvey shot into the side netting, while Reina also denied Hulk.

There was no fourth Liverpool goal. The elimination also closed Carragher’s European career: this was his 150th and final appearance in continental competition.
