Jamie Carragher captained Liverpool in his 737th and final appearance as they beat Queens Park Rangers 1–0 at Anfield on 19 May 2013. Philippe Coutinho scored the only goal in the final fixture of Brendan Rodgers’ first season.

Jordon Ibe made his senior Liverpool debut at the age of 17. He supplied the winning goal in the 23rd minute, cutting in from the left and finding Coutinho, whose low shot from distance beat Robert Green.

Coutinho had earlier met a Downing corner with a diving header which Michael Harriman cleared, with no goal awarded. Loïc Rémy had opportunities for QPR, shooting wide and mis-kicking when trying to meet Armand Traoré’s cross.

Carragher came close to adding a goal shortly after the hour. His first-time shot from long range struck the post and came back into play. He was replaced by Sebastián Coates late on, ending his final match with Liverpool still ahead and without conceding.

Liverpool finished seventh with 61 points, two behind Everton. They had ended the league season with eight unbeaten matches, including this final victory.
