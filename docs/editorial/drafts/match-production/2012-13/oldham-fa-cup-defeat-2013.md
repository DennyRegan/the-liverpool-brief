League One Oldham Athletic knocked Liverpool out of the FA Cup with a 3–2 fourth-round victory at Boundary Park on 27 January 2013. Matt Smith scored twice before half-time and Reece Wabara added a third, leaving goals from Luis Suárez and Joe Allen insufficient to keep Liverpool in the competition.

Oldham entered the tie one point above their league’s relegation zone, having lost seven of their previous eight league matches. Under Paul Dickov, they took an early lead when Smith headed in Youssouf M’Changama’s cross. Suárez, captaining Liverpool for the first time, equalised after an attempted pass was intercepted and the ball came back to him.

Smith restored Oldham’s advantage shortly before the interval, scoring after Brad Jones failed to hold a cross. Liverpool then conceded again soon after the restart, Wabara heading in Carl Winchester’s delivery.

Rodgers brought on Gerrard and Stewart Downing as Liverpool tried to recover from 3–1 down. Allen’s deflected volley reduced the deficit in the 79th minute. Gerrard hit the crossbar late on, but Liverpool could not find another equaliser.

Oldham went through to a fifth-round meeting with Everton. For Liverpool, the cup campaign ended after two ties: a win at Mansfield followed by this defeat.
