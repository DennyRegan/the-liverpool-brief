Luis Suárez scored a hat-trick as Liverpool beat Norwich City 5–2 at Carrow Road on 29 September 2012. It was Brendan Rodgers’ first Premier League win as Liverpool manager, following five league matches without a victory.

Suárez had also scored three times on Liverpool’s previous visit to Carrow Road, five months earlier. This time he opened the scoring in the second minute with a shot from the edge of the area. He added a second before half-time after taking the ball from Michael Turner and curling his finish beyond John Ruddy.

Liverpool had started Andre Wisdom on his Premier League debut, with Suso also in the line-up. They led 2–0 at the interval and extended that advantage through Nuri Şahin shortly after the restart. Suárez supplied Şahin’s close-range finish, then completed his own hat-trick from Şahin’s pass in the 57th minute.

Steve Morison replied after Pepe Reina could not hold an initial shot. Liverpool’s fifth came from Gerrard’s shot following Raheem Sterling’s work, with the ball deflecting off Leon Barnett. Grant Holt added Norwich’s second late on, but the hosts could not recover the four-goal deficit Liverpool had established.
