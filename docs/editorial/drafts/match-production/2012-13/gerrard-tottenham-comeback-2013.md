Steven Gerrard’s late penalty gave Liverpool a 3–2 victory over Tottenham Hotspur at Anfield on 10 March 2013. The hosts had led through Luis Suárez, then fallen behind to two Jan Vertonghen goals before recovering in the second half.

Jamie Carragher made his 500th league appearance for Liverpool, becoming the second player to reach that total after Ian Callaghan. Brad Jones started in goal in place of the injured Pepe Reina.

Suárez opened the scoring in the 21st minute, finishing from José Enrique’s pass. Vertonghen equalised shortly before the break with a header from Gareth Bale’s cross. The defender then put Spurs ahead after Liverpool failed to clear Bale’s free-kick early in the second half.

A mistake at the other end allowed Stewart Downing to level in the 66th minute. Kyle Walker’s back-pass drew Hugo Lloris from his goal, but the goalkeeper’s attempted clearance reached Downing, who scored.

With eight minutes of normal time remaining, Benoît Assou-Ekotto fouled Suárez in the area. Gerrard converted the penalty to complete Liverpool’s recovery.

The result ended Tottenham’s twelve-match unbeaten run in the Premier League. It was Liverpool’s third consecutive league win, following a 5–0 victory over Swansea City and a 4–0 win at Wigan Athletic.
