Liverpool beat Newcastle United 6–0 at St James’ Park on 27 April 2013, in their first match after Luis Suárez’s ten-game suspension was imposed. Daniel Sturridge and Jordan Henderson scored twice each, with Daniel Agger and substitute Fabio Borini completing the scoring.

Agger opened the scoring in the third minute, heading in Stewart Downing’s cross. The second goal followed a Philippe Coutinho pass through to Sturridge, who squared the ball for Henderson to finish. Liverpool led 2–0 at half-time.

Coutinho supplied Sturridge for Liverpool’s third nine minutes after the restart. Sturridge scored again in the 60th minute, this time from Henderson’s pass.

Borini marked his return from a shoulder injury by scoring shortly after coming on for Gerrard. It was his first Premier League goal for Liverpool. Mathieu Debuchy was then sent off for a second yellow card after a challenge on Coutinho. Henderson’s free-kick went into the net without another touch to complete the six-goal win.

The result equalled Liverpool’s biggest away winning margin in the Premier League at the time.
