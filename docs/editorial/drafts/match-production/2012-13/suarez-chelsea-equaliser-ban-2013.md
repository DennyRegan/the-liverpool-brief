Luis Suárez headed an equaliser deep in added time as Liverpool drew 2–2 with Chelsea at Anfield on 21 April 2013. Rafael Benítez returned as Chelsea manager for a match in which both Liverpool goals involved another former Chelsea player, Daniel Sturridge.

Oscar put Chelsea ahead in the 26th minute with a header. Rodgers replaced Philippe Coutinho with Sturridge at half-time, and the substitute hit a post before bringing Liverpool level. Suárez provided the delivery for Sturridge’s close-range finish in the 52nd minute. Chelsea regained the lead shortly afterwards through Eden Hazard’s penalty, awarded for Suárez’s handball.

In the 65th minute, Suárez bit Branislav Ivanović on the arm. The match officials did not see the incident and took no action against him during the game. He remained on the pitch and eventually headed in Sturridge’s cross to deny Chelsea victory.

On 24 April, the FA imposed an immediate ten-match suspension. Suárez had accepted the charge of violent conduct. The sanction comprised the standard three-match suspension plus a further seven matches.
