Jonjo Shelvey came off the bench to score twice as Liverpool beat Young Boys 5–3 at the Stade de Suisse on 20 September 2012. Brendan Rodgers’ side had fallen 3–2 behind before recovering to win their opening Europa League Group A match.

Liverpool had reached the group stage after qualifying ties against Gomel and Hearts. Rodgers gave senior debuts to Andre Wisdom, Suso and Oussama Assaidi in the starting line-up in Switzerland.

An own goal from Juhani Ojala put Liverpool ahead in the fourth minute, after Stewart Downing’s cross and Christoph Spycher’s attempted headed clearance. Raphael Nuzzolo equalised late in the first half, but Wisdom restored Liverpool’s lead by heading in Nuri Şahin’s corner.

Ojala headed Young Boys level after the interval, this time from Raúl Bobadilla’s cross. Bobadilla then supplied Gonzalo Zárate, whose chip over Brad Jones put the hosts in front. Sebastián Coates answered with another header from a corner, meeting Downing’s delivery to make it 3–3.

Shelvey’s introduction gave Liverpool the two goals that decided the contest. Jordan Henderson laid the ball off for his first finish, before Shelvey added a second with a drive in the 88th minute. Liverpool had conceded three times but left their first group fixture with three points.
