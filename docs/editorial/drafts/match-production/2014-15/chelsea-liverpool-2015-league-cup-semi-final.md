Liverpool’s League Cup run ended at Stamford Bridge on 27 January 2015, when Chelsea won the semi-final second leg 1–0 after extra time. Branislav Ivanović’s header settled a tie Chelsea won 2–1 on aggregate.

The first leg at Anfield had ended 1–1, and the return remained goalless through ninety minutes. Away goals counted only after extra time under the League Cup rules, so Chelsea’s first-leg goal had not yet decided the tie.

Thibaut Courtois had kept out Alberto Moreno and Philippe Coutinho as Liverpool created openings in the first half. Simon Mignolet also denied Diego Costa, and neither side could find the goal that would settle the contest in normal time.

Four minutes into extra time, Ivanović met Willian’s free-kick with a header to put Chelsea ahead. Jordan Henderson then had an opportunity to reply when he reached Raheem Sterling’s cross, but his header went wide.

Liverpool had reached the last four by overcoming Middlesbrough, Swansea City and Bournemouth. Their second semi-final appearance of the season would come in the FA Cup against Aston Villa, but this defeat ended their first chance of reaching a domestic final.
