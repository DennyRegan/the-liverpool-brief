Mario Balotelli’s first Premier League goal for Liverpool gave them a 3–2 win over Tottenham Hotspur at Anfield on 10 February 2015. The substitute settled a match in which Spurs had twice recovered after Liverpool took the lead.

Daniel Sturridge made his first start since returning from injury and helped create the opening goal for Lazar Marković in the 15th minute. Harry Kane replied before half-time, finishing after Érik Lamela’s pass to make it 1–1.

Liverpool went ahead again when Steven Gerrard converted a penalty awarded for Danny Rose’s foul on Sturridge. Moussa Dembélé restored parity after Simon Mignolet saved Christian Eriksen’s free-kick and Kane delivered the loose ball across goal.

Brendan Rodgers replaced Sturridge with Balotelli and later sent on Adam Lallana for Marković. Both substitutes contributed to the winner: Jordon Ibe found Lallana, whose low cross was turned in by Balotelli in the 83rd minute.

Liverpool remained seventh, one point behind Spurs in sixth, while their unbeaten league run reached nine games. The victory came between a goalless Merseyside derby and the FA Cup fifth-round visit to Crystal Palace.
