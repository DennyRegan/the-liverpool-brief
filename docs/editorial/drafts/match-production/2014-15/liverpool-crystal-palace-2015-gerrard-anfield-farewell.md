Steven Gerrard’s final Anfield appearance ended in a 3–1 Liverpool defeat to Crystal Palace on 16 May 2015. Before kick-off, both teams formed a guard of honour for the captain and his daughters, while supporters displayed a mosaic in the stands. It was his 709th Liverpool appearance, with one more fixture remaining at Stoke City.

Adam Lallana put Liverpool ahead in the 26th minute after Martin Kelly’s misplaced pass left Scott Dann under pressure. Lallana held off Dann, ran through and finished past Wayne Hennessey.

Jason Puncheon equalised before half-time with a free-kick from the edge of the area. Palace then took the lead around the hour when substitute Wilfried Zaha scored with his first touch, turning in Yannick Bolasie’s low cross.

Bolasie later hit the crossbar as Liverpool sought a response. In stoppage time, Simon Mignolet saved Glenn Murray’s penalty, but Murray scored from the rebound to complete the 3–1 result.

Liverpool remained fifth after the match, but the subsequent defeat at Stoke left them sixth in the final league standings.
