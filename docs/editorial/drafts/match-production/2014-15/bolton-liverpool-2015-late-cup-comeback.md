Steven Gerrard’s 700th Liverpool appearance ended in a 2–1 FA Cup fourth-round replay win at Bolton Wanderers on 4 February 2015. Liverpool overturned a one-goal deficit with two late strikes at the Macron Stadium after the first meeting at Anfield had finished goalless.

Raheem Sterling hit the post in the first half, but neither side scored before the interval. Bolton went ahead just before the hour when Eidur Gudjohnsen converted a penalty after Martin Škrtel’s challenge on Zach Clough.

Neil Danns was then sent off for a second yellow card, leaving the home side to defend their lead with ten men. Liverpool brought on Daniel Sturridge and continued to threaten, with Jordan Henderson hitting the post and Emre Can striking the bar.

Can supplied the pass that finally broke through, lifting the ball into Sterling’s path for the equaliser in the closing minutes. Coutinho then struck in added time, curling a shot into the top corner to win the replay.

There was no need for extra time: Liverpool had recovered before the end to earn a fifth-round visit to Crystal Palace. They would win that tie and a replay against Blackburn Rovers before their FA Cup run ended in the semi-final against Aston Villa.
