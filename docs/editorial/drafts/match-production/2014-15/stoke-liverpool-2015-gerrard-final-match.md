Steven Gerrard scored on his 710th and final Liverpool appearance, but his farewell match ended in a 6–1 defeat at Stoke City on 24 May 2015. Stoke led 5–0 by half-time at the Britannia Stadium.

Mame Diouf opened the scoring in the 22nd minute after Simon Mignolet pushed out Charlie Adam’s shot. Diouf added a second four minutes later with a drive from outside the area. Jonathan Walters made it three after Emre Can’s short header back to Mignolet, scoring from the rebound when the goalkeeper saved his first attempt.

Adam intercepted a Mamadou Sakho pass and struck Stoke’s fourth before Steven N’Zonzi curled in the fifth just before the interval. Liverpool replaced Alberto Moreno and Can with Jordon Ibe and Kolo Touré for the second half.

Substitute Rickie Lambert supplied a flick-on for Gerrard, who finished past Asmir Begović in the 70th minute. Peter Crouch then headed Stoke’s sixth in the 86th minute, completing the score against his former club.

Liverpool finished sixth with 62 points, eight behind Manchester United in the final Champions League qualification place. The season had brought two domestic semi-final exits, and its final fixture added a heavy defeat to Gerrard’s departure.
