Liverpool were eliminated from the Europa League at the Atatürk Stadium on 26 February 2015, losing a shoot-out 5–4 after Beşiktaş won the second leg 1–0. The teams finished level at 1–1 on aggregate after Liverpool’s victory at Anfield a week earlier. This time, the stadium where Liverpool had won the 2005 Champions League final brought a penalty defeat.

The return leg was goalless at half-time before substitute Tolgay Arslan struck in the 72nd minute. Gökhan Töre’s pass found Demba Ba, whose lay-off set up Arslan’s first-time shot from outside the area. Ba then hit the crossbar from a corner near the end of normal time.

Neither side scored during the additional half-hour, leaving the tie to penalties. The first nine kicks were all successful, including Beşiktaş’s fifth from Arslan. Dejan Lovren took Liverpool’s fifth and sent it over the bar, ending the contest.

Liverpool’s European season had begun in the Champions League before December’s group-stage exit. Their next fixture was at Anfield against Manchester City three days later, as attention returned to the league campaign.
