Liverpool’s thirteen-match unbeaten league run ended with a 2–1 defeat to Manchester United at Anfield on 22 March 2015. Steven Gerrard was sent off within a minute of coming on at half-time, while Juan Mata scored both United goals.

The teams began the game two points apart, with United occupying fourth place immediately above Liverpool. Mata put the visitors ahead in the 14th minute, collecting Ander Herrera’s pass and finishing across Simon Mignolet.

Brendan Rodgers replaced Adam Lallana with Gerrard at the interval. The captain was dismissed after stamping on Herrera, leaving Liverpool with ten men for almost the whole second half.

Mata made it 2–0 in the 59th minute with a scissor-kick volley from substitute Ángel Di María’s pass. Daniel Sturridge reduced the deficit ten minutes later, taking Philippe Coutinho’s pass and beating David de Gea at the near post.

Mignolet saved Wayne Rooney’s penalty in stoppage time, but Liverpool could not find an equaliser. The defeat left them five points behind United with eight league games remaining; Liverpool would finish the season sixth.
