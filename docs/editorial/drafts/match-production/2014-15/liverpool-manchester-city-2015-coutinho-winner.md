Liverpool beat Manchester City 2–1 at Anfield on 1 March 2015, three days after their Europa League elimination in Istanbul. Jordan Henderson and Philippe Coutinho scored from outside the area as Liverpool extended their unbeaten league run to eleven matches.

Henderson opened the scoring in the 11th minute after Coutinho won possession from Vincent Kompany and Raheem Sterling laid the ball into his path. The captain curled his shot beyond Joe Hart to give Liverpool the lead.

Sergio Agüero struck the post before helping City equalise. His pass found Edin Džeko, who finished first time in the 25th minute to leave the sides level at the interval.

Coutinho restored Liverpool’s lead with fifteen minutes remaining, curling a shot around Samir Nasri and into the far corner. The 2–1 win took Liverpool to fifth on 48 points, two behind Manchester United in fourth.

Further victories against Burnley and Swansea City followed before United’s visit ended the unbeaten league sequence later in March. The City result had kept Liverpool in contention during that recovery, even though they would eventually finish sixth.
