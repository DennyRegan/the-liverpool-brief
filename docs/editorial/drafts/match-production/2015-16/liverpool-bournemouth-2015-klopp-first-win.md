Jürgen Klopp secured his first competitive victory as Liverpool manager with a 1–0 League Cup win over Bournemouth at Anfield on 28 October 2015. Nathaniel Clyne scored his first goal for the club as Liverpool reached the quarter-finals.

Klopp gave Connor Randall his senior debut and selected Joao Carlos Teixeira and Cameron Brannagan for their first senior starts. Adam Bogdan was in goal and made an early save from Junior Stanislas before Liverpool took the lead in the 17th minute.

Roberto Firmino combined with Jordon Ibe and found Teixeira, whose backheel was cleared before it could cross the line. Clyne followed in to convert the loose ball. Bournemouth continued to threaten, with Bogdan denying a Stanislas header and free-kick before the interval.

Kolo Toure’s injury forced a first-half defensive change, bringing Martin Skrtel into the match. At the other end, Firmino had efforts off target and Teixeira tested Adam Federici from a free-kick. Shaun MacDonald also headed wide for Bournemouth after half-time.

Liverpool could not add a second goal, with substitute Divock Origi missing a late opportunity after Lucas Leiva’s pass. Bournemouth’s final attempt came through Matt Ritchie in stoppage time, but Bogdan made the save that preserved the lead. After four draws, Klopp had a win and a place in the last eight.
