Liverpool lost the League Cup final to Manchester City on penalties at Wembley on 28 February 2016. Philippe Coutinho’s late goal had cancelled out Fernandinho’s opener for a 1–1 draw, but City won the shoot-out 3–1 after Willy Caballero saved three Liverpool kicks.

Simon Mignolet pushed Sergio Aguero’s first-half shot onto the post. Liverpool also lost Mamadou Sakho, who was replaced by Kolo Toure after an accidental clash of heads with Emre Can. Neither side scored before the interval.

City went ahead in the 49th minute when Aguero supplied Fernandinho inside the area. His angled first-time shot went underneath Mignolet’s attempted save. Raheem Sterling missed opportunities to extend the lead before Liverpool equalised in the 83rd minute: Daniel Sturridge’s ball reached Adam Lallana, whose shot hit the post, and Coutinho finished the rebound.

Mignolet denied Fernando and Yaya Toure late in normal time, then kept out Aguero in extra time. Caballero responded with a save from Divock Origi’s close-range header. The additional half-hour brought no further goal.

Emre Can converted Liverpool’s opening penalty and Fernandinho struck the post with City’s first attempt. Caballero then saved from Lucas Leiva, Coutinho and Lallana, while Jesus Navas, Aguero and Yaya Toure scored. The decisive conversion left Liverpool without the trophy in Klopp’s first final as Liverpool manager.
