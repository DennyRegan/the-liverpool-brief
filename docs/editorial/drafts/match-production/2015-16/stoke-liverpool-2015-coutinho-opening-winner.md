Philippe Coutinho’s late strike gave Liverpool a 1–0 victory at Stoke City in their Premier League opener on 9 August 2015. Brendan Rodgers’s side returned to the ground where they had lost 6–1 on the final day of the previous season and began the new campaign with three points.

Four summer signings made their competitive Liverpool debuts in the starting line-up: Christian Benteke, Nathaniel Clyne, Joe Gomez and James Milner. Roberto Firmino became a fifth debutant when he replaced Jordon Ibe in the second half. The new arrivals were part of a contest that remained goalless well into its closing stages.

Stoke had a first-half opportunity through former Liverpool defender Glen Johnson, who shot over after the ball reached him inside the area. Simon Mignolet later saved from Marco van Ginkel and Charlie Adam as the home side looked for a breakthrough after the interval.

Liverpool’s decisive moment came in the 86th minute. Coutinho took Gomez’s pass, turned away from Steve Sidwell and drove forward before striking from outside the penalty area. His dipping shot beat Jack Butland and gave Liverpool the lead.

Afterwards, Rodgers said he had considered replacing Coutinho with Danny Ings, but had kept the Brazilian on. The player he had thought of withdrawing supplied the only goal of the match.
