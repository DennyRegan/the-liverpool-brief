Liverpool beat Everton 4–0 at Anfield on 20 April 2016 in Jürgen Klopp’s first home Merseyside derby as manager. Divock Origi and Mamadou Sakho headed Liverpool ahead before half-time, with Daniel Sturridge and Philippe Coutinho adding goals after Ramiro Funes Mori’s dismissal.

Adam Lallana had an early chance saved by Joel Robles and later headed narrowly wide from Nathaniel Clyne’s cross. Everton threatened through Kevin Mirallas, while Sakho intervened to stop Romelu Lukaku. Roberto Firmino also had an opportunity kept out by Robles and John Stones.

The breakthrough came in the 43rd minute when James Milner crossed from the right and Origi headed in at the back post. Milner supplied a second delivery before the interval, this time from the left after a corner, for Sakho to head Liverpool into a 2–0 lead.

Funes Mori was sent off early in the second half following his challenge on Origi. The injured striker was replaced by Sturridge, who soon scored his 50th Liverpool goal. Lucas Leiva intercepted a loose ball and found him with a diagonal pass into the area for a low finish.

Coutinho completed the scoring in the 76th minute, taking Joe Allen’s pass and driving the ball in from the edge of the area. Liverpool followed wins over Stoke, Dortmund and Bournemouth with a fourth consecutive victory in all competitions.
