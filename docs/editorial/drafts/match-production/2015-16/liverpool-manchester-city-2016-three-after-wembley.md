Liverpool beat Manchester City 3–0 at Anfield on 2 March 2016, three days after losing to the same opponents on penalties in the League Cup final. Adam Lallana, James Milner and Roberto Firmino scored as Liverpool completed a Premier League double over City.

Lallana opened the scoring in the 34th minute with a low left-footed shot from outside the area. He was also involved in the second goal before half-time, backheeling to Firmino, whose pass allowed Milner to take a touch into the penalty area and finish on his left foot.

City had chances to respond before the interval. Jon Flanagan’s sliding tackle prevented Raheem Sterling from getting a clear shot, and Milner blocked Sergio Aguero’s header on the line. Liverpool nevertheless went in with a two-goal lead.

The third goal came in the 57th minute. After a heavy Divock Origi touch, Lallana recovered the ball and played Firmino into the area. The Brazilian opened his body and finished past Joe Hart, extending the lead to 3–0.

Hart then kept out Milner’s powerful drive, while Kolo Toure intervened to stop Aguero at the other end. Liverpool’s November victory at the Etihad and this home win gave them six league points against City, despite the different outcome in the Wembley final.
