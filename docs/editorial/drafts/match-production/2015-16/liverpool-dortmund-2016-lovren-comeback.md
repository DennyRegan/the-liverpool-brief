Dejan Lovren headed Liverpool into the Europa League semi-finals with an added-time winner against Borussia Dortmund at Anfield on 14 April 2016. Liverpool won the second leg 4–3 and the tie 5–4 on aggregate after trailing by two goals both at half-time and midway through the second half.

The first leg had ended 1–1, but Dortmund quickly changed the balance of the tie. Henrikh Mkhitaryan scored in the fifth minute after Simon Mignolet saved Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang’s effort. Aubameyang added a second four minutes later from Marco Reus’s pass, leaving Liverpool needing three goals to progress.

Divock Origi pulled one back early in the second half after Emre Can advanced and played him through. Dortmund restored their two-goal advantage when Mats Hummels found Reus, who finished around Mignolet in the 57th minute. Liverpool again needed three further goals.

Joe Allen and Daniel Sturridge entered before Philippe Coutinho exchanged passes with James Milner and struck into the bottom corner. Coutinho then delivered the corner which Mamadou Sakho headed in for 3–3 on the night and 4–4 on aggregate. Dortmund still held the advantage on away goals.

Liverpool found the final goal in the first added minute. Milner made room near the byline and crossed for Lovren, who rose to head into the corner. The defender’s finish overturned Dortmund’s away-goal advantage and completed Liverpool’s route into the last four.
