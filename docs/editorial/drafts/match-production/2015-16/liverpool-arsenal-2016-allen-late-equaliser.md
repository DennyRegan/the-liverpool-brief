Joe Allen’s 90th-minute equaliser earned Liverpool a 3–3 draw with Arsenal at Anfield on 13 January 2016. Roberto Firmino scored twice as Liverpool led on two occasions, before Olivier Giroud’s second goal put the visitors ahead after half-time.

Firmino opened the scoring after Petr Cech parried Emre Can’s drive into his path. Arsenal replied through Aaron Ramsey, who reached Joel Campbell’s pass and finished past Simon Mignolet. Liverpool then regained the lead when Firmino took James Milner’s lay-off and curled a shot into the top corner from outside the area.

Giroud made it 2–2 in the 25th minute, meeting Ramsey’s low corner at the near post. Firmino came close to a first-half hat-trick in added time when his turn and shot from a partly cleared Jordon Ibe cross skimmed the top of the bar.

Arsenal took the lead ten minutes after the interval. Giroud collected Campbell’s deflected pass, turned Kolo Toure and finished low. Liverpool responded with changes: Christian Benteke replaced Milner, while Allen and new loan signing Steven Caulker entered later, with Caulker used as an auxiliary striker.

Benteke’s header across goal created the final opening. Allen swept the ball in to make it 3–3, completing the third equaliser of the evening. Liverpool had recovered from the only Arsenal lead after the visitors had twice answered Firmino’s first-half goals.
