Liverpool reached the Europa League final by beating Villarreal 3–0 at Anfield on 5 May 2016. A Bruno Soriano own goal and second-half strikes from Daniel Sturridge and Adam Lallana overturned the first-leg deficit for a 3–1 aggregate victory.

Emre Can returned to the side and was involved in the seventh-minute opener, feeding Nathaniel Clyne on the right. Alphonse Areola could not hold the full-back’s cross, and Roberto Firmino sent the loose ball back across goal before Bruno turned it into his own net.

Liverpool could have extended the lead through Lallana, who could not make enough contact with James Milner’s low delivery. Villarreal also created chances, with Simon Mignolet saving from Mario Gaspar and Cedric Bakambu. Dejan Lovren intervened to stop Bakambu early in the second half.

Sturridge put Liverpool ahead on aggregate in the 63rd minute, finishing low after Firmino’s effort was deflected into his path. Villarreal’s task became harder when Victor Ruiz received a second yellow card following a challenge on Firmino.

Lallana added the third in the 81st minute. Firmino reached the byline and cut the ball back; Sturridge could not finish it, but Lallana converted. Liverpool would face Sevilla in Basel after the Spanish holders won the other semi-final.
