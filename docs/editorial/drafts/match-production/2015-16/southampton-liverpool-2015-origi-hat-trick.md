Divock Origi scored his first Liverpool goals in a hat-trick as Jürgen Klopp’s side beat Southampton 6–1 in the League Cup quarter-final at St Mary’s on 2 December 2015. Daniel Sturridge scored twice on his return to the starting line-up, while Jordon Ibe also found the net.

Southampton had led inside the opening minute. Ryan Bertrand’s cross reached Sadio Mane, whose header beat Adam Bogdan. Liverpool reversed the advantage through two Sturridge goals: he first took Joe Allen’s pass, worked past Steven Caulker and finished, then met Emre Can’s outside-of-the-foot delivery for the second.

Origi made it 3–1 before half-time. Adam Lallana’s corner reached Alberto Moreno, whose shot was diverted in by the Belgian striker. Liverpool’s response to conceding early had produced three goals before the interval.

Origi added another in the second half, taking Ibe’s pass and driving his shot into the roof of the net. Ibe then scored himself, controlling Moreno’s delivery on his chest and volleying home. The two goals extended Liverpool’s lead to 5–1.

The sixth came from substitute Brad Smith’s cross, which Origi headed in to complete his hat-trick. Sturridge had made his first start since the October derby and contributed the goals which first changed the lead; Origi finished the evening with three as Liverpool moved into the semi-finals.
