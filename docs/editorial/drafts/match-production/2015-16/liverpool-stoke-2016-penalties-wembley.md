Liverpool reached the League Cup final by beating Stoke City 6–5 on penalties at Anfield on 26 January 2016. Stoke won the second leg 1–0 through Marko Arnautovic, leaving the semi-final tied 1–1 on aggregate after extra time before Simon Mignolet’s saves and Joe Allen’s kick settled it.

Liverpool had won the first leg through Jordon Ibe’s goal. Jon Flanagan made his first start since May 2014 in the return match, replacing the injured Nathaniel Clyne. Jonathan Walters threatened twice for Stoke before Arnautovic finished Bojan Krkic’s cross in first-half added time to erase the aggregate lead.

Jack Butland denied Roberto Firmino early in the second half, while Mamadou Sakho blocked another Walters effort. Liverpool introduced Christian Benteke but could not find a goal. The tie remained level through extra time, when Marco van Ginkel struck the post and later had an angled attempt held by Mignolet.

The shoot-out also stayed close. Mignolet saved Peter Crouch’s kick and Emre Can missed for Liverpool, leaving both sides on four successful penalties after five attempts apiece. Further conversions from Van Ginkel and Lucas Leiva took the scores to 5–5.

Mignolet then stopped Marc Muniesa’s attempt, giving Allen the opportunity to end the contest. The substitute converted Liverpool’s seventh kick to secure the place at Wembley. Liverpool had lost the match on the night but progressed from the two-legged tie.
