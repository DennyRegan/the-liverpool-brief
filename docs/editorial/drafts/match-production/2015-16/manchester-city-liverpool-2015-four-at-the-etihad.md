Liverpool scored three times inside the opening 32 minutes on their way to a 4–1 Premier League victory at Manchester City on 21 November 2015. Roberto Firmino’s first Liverpool goal was part of a first-half sequence which also brought an Eliaquim Mangala own goal and a Philippe Coutinho finish.

The breakthrough came in the seventh minute when Firmino crossed from the right and Mangala diverted the ball past Joe Hart. Firmino then supplied Coutinho, who guided his shot through Hart’s legs to double Liverpool’s lead in the 23rd minute.

Liverpool’s third followed an exchange involving Emre Can’s backheel and Coutinho’s square pass, leaving Firmino to finish from close range. Hart denied further efforts from the Brazilian before Sergio Aguero pulled a goal back with a shot from outside the area shortly before half-time.

City sought another reply after the interval, with Simon Mignolet saving from Aguero. Coutinho had an effort ruled out for offside, and Hart continued to keep Firmino out. Coutinho later left the match with a knock, while substitute Christian Benteke also tested the home goalkeeper.

Hart’s save from Benteke gave Liverpool a late corner. City could not clear it fully and Martin Skrtel struck the loose ball firmly into the net, restoring the three-goal margin. His finish completed Liverpool’s fourth goal of an away win in which both Coutinho and Firmino had scored and created goals.
