West Ham United ended their long wait for a win at Anfield by beating Liverpool 3–0 on 29 August 2015. Manuel Lanzini, Mark Noble and Diafra Sakho scored as Brendan Rodgers’s side suffered their first defeat of the Premier League campaign.

Liverpool had taken seven points from their opening three matches without conceding a goal. That defensive sequence ended in the third minute when Aaron Cresswell crossed from the left and Lanzini finished inside the area. Roberto Firmino threatened a reply with a shot that clipped the outside of the post.

The visitors doubled their advantage before the half-hour. Lanzini took possession from Dejan Lovren in Liverpool territory and sent in a cross which reached Noble. West Ham’s captain placed his finish into the bottom corner, leaving Liverpool two goals behind at the interval.

Rodgers introduced Alberto Moreno at half-time, but the attempt to recover became harder when Philippe Coutinho received a second yellow card for a challenge on Dimitri Payet. Danny Ings then made his competitive Liverpool debut from the bench and set up a James Milner effort that went wide.

Noble was also sent off later in the half following a challenge on Ings. Liverpool still could not find a goal, and Sakho added West Ham’s third in stoppage time. The visitors departed with their first Anfield victory since September 1963.
