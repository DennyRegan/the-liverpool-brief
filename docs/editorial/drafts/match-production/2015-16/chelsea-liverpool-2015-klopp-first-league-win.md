Liverpool came from behind to beat Chelsea 3–1 at Stamford Bridge on 31 October 2015, giving Jürgen Klopp his first Premier League victory. Philippe Coutinho scored twice before substitute Christian Benteke completed the recovery against the reigning champions.

Chelsea took the lead in the fourth minute. Cesar Azpilicueta crossed from the left and Ramires headed past Simon Mignolet. Liverpool pursued an equaliser through attempts from Adam Lallana and Nathaniel Clyne, but remained behind until added time at the end of the first half.

Coutinho then found space on the edge of the area, moved away from Ramires and curled a left-footed shot into the corner. The equaliser made it 1–1 at the interval, with Roberto Firmino leading Liverpool’s attack before Benteke replaced James Milner in the second half.

Mignolet kept the scores level by saving Oscar’s ambitious effort from distance. Shortly afterwards, Benteke’s knockdown reached Coutinho, whose shot took a deflection on its way past Asmir Begovic. Liverpool had turned the match around with a little over a quarter of an hour remaining.

Benteke added the third late on. Lallana’s step-over helped the ball reach the striker, who worked room inside the area and finished low past Begovic. Three days after Klopp’s first cup win against Bournemouth, Liverpool had their first league success under him.
