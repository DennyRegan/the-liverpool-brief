Christian Benteke scored the only goal as Liverpool beat Premier League leaders Leicester City 1–0 at Anfield on 26 December 2015. The substitute’s finish from Roberto Firmino’s delivery inflicted Leicester’s second league defeat of the campaign.

Liverpool threatened before the breakthrough, with Emre Can forcing Kasper Schmeichel into a full-stretch save and Divock Origi holding off Wes Morgan before testing the goalkeeper at his near post. Origi’s afternoon ended before half-time when injury forced him off and Benteke replaced him.

Leicester had a chance to lead before the interval, but Simon Mignolet beat away Riyad Mahrez’s drive. The match remained goalless until the 63rd minute, when Firmino made room on the left and sent the ball across the area. Benteke stretched to strike it first time into the bottom corner.

Mignolet was called on again soon after the goal, denying Nathan Dyer’s close-range effort from Christian Fuchs’s cross. Philippe Coutinho shot just over at the other end and Firmino sent a half-volley across goal as Liverpool sought a second.

Leicester’s final pursuit of an equaliser took Schmeichel upfield for a long throw in stoppage time. Liverpool broke away, but Benteke’s attempt at the exposed goal was blocked by Morgan. The single goal proved sufficient against a Leicester side which would finish the season as champions.
