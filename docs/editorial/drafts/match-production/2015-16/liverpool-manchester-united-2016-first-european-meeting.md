Liverpool beat Manchester United 2–0 at Anfield in the clubs’ first European meeting on 10 March 2016. Daniel Sturridge and Roberto Firmino scored in the Europa League round-of-16 first leg, with David de Gea preventing Liverpool from taking a larger lead.

Sturridge opened the scoring from the penalty spot in the 20th minute after Memphis Depay’s challenge on Nathaniel Clyne. De Gea got a touch to the kick but could not keep it out. He soon denied Philippe Coutinho from close range after Sturridge had sent the ball across goal.

The United goalkeeper continued to resist before half-time. He blocked Sturridge’s shot after Firmino’s diagonal pass and then reacted to stop Adam Lallana’s near-post effort from another Firmino delivery. Liverpool went into the interval one goal ahead.

United introduced Michael Carrick at half-time, but De Gea was again required to save when Coutinho struck a dipping half-volley in the second half. Jordan Henderson also shot wide after Lallana created space for him.

Liverpool doubled the lead in the 73rd minute. Henderson sent the ball into the area, Carrick could not clear it fully and Lallana set up Firmino for the finish. The two-goal advantage would accompany Liverpool to Old Trafford for the return match a week later.
