Jürgen Klopp’s first competitive match as Liverpool manager ended in a 0–0 draw with Tottenham Hotspur at White Hart Lane on 17 October 2015. Divock Origi hit the crossbar early on, while Simon Mignolet and Mamadou Sakho helped keep Spurs out.

Origi started in attack with Christian Benteke, Danny Ings and Daniel Sturridge unavailable. Liverpool created their clearest early opening from a corner in the tenth minute. James Milner’s delivery was flicked on by Emre Can, and Origi’s close-range header struck the bar before Tottenham cleared.

Adam Lallana also tested Hugo Lloris, but Spurs made Mignolet work before half-time. The goalkeeper stretched to turn away Clinton Njié’s first-time effort after Harry Kane’s pass. He then blocked a Kane shot, with Sakho getting in the way of Dele Alli’s follow-up.

The second half brought further attempts without a breakthrough. Kyle Walker’s low shot was held by Mignolet, and Origi shot straight at Lloris from a narrow angle. Later, Christian Eriksen created a chance for Kane, whose strike was kept out by interventions from Sakho and Mignolet.

Can sent a late shot narrowly wide, leaving both sides with a point. Liverpool had created an early opportunity to lead and survived Tottenham’s responses, completing Klopp’s opening afternoon with a clean sheet.
