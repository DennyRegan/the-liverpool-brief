Liverpool left the FA Cup after Angelo Ogbonna’s late extra-time header gave West Ham United a 2–1 victory at Upton Park on 9 February 2016. Philippe Coutinho had cancelled out Michail Antonio’s opener in the fourth-round replay, but the tie was decided before it could reach penalties.

Following the goalless first meeting at Anfield, Coutinho returned for his first start since 5 January. Lucas Leiva captained a Liverpool side that included Tiago Ilori, Brad Smith, Kevin Stewart, Pedro Chirivella and Joao Carlos Teixeira.

Both teams struck the woodwork before half-time. Joey O’Brien’s deflected shot hit the post for West Ham, while Coutinho was denied by the other goal’s near post and Dimitri Payet’s free-kick also hit the frame. Antonio then volleyed in at the back post in the final minute of the half.

Coutinho equalised three minutes after the restart with a low free-kick following a foul on Christian Benteke. Divock Origi and Daniel Sturridge entered later in the half, but no winner came in normal time. In extra time, Benteke shot wide and was denied in a one-on-one by Darren Randolph, before Sturridge narrowly missed from James Milner’s pass.

Payet’s final free-kick supplied the decisive chance. Ogbonna rose to head across Simon Mignolet and into the corner in added time at the end of the extra period, eliminating Liverpool after 120 minutes of the replay.
