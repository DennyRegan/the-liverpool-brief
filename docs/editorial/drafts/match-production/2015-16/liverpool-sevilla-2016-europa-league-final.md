Liverpool lost the Europa League final 3–1 to Sevilla in Basel on 18 May 2016. Daniel Sturridge’s first-half goal gave them the lead, but Kevin Gameiro equalised immediately after the interval and Coke scored twice to secure Sevilla’s third consecutive title in the competition.

Liverpool threatened through Emre Can’s shot and a Sturridge header which Daniel Carrico cleared from the line. Sturridge was also denied by David Soria after Adam Lallana’s reverse pass before producing the opening goal in the 35th minute. From inside the area, he curled a shot into the corner with the outside of his left boot.

Dejan Lovren then headed into the net from a corner, but the goal was ruled out for offside. Liverpool’s one-goal lead survived until half-time and disappeared seconds after the restart, when Mariano went past Alberto Moreno on the right and supplied Gameiro for a close-range finish.

Kolo Toure prevented another Gameiro chance with a sliding challenge, and Mignolet saved from the striker after a long throw. Sevilla nevertheless took the lead midway through the half, with Coke finishing first time from the edge of the area after Vitolo’s approach.

Coke struck again from close range shortly afterwards, completing the turnaround. Liverpool could not recover and ended the campaign with defeats in both cup finals, alongside an eighth-place finish in the Premier League.
