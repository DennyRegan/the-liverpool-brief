Philippe Coutinho’s goal helped Liverpool eliminate Manchester United from the Europa League with a 1–1 draw at Old Trafford on 17 March 2016. Liverpool advanced to the quarter-finals 3–1 on aggregate after following their Anfield victory with an away equaliser.

United threatened first through Jesse Lingard, whose close-range header Simon Mignolet saved. David de Gea then stopped Coutinho’s low effort before Anthony Martial put the home side ahead from a penalty awarded after Nathaniel Clyne’s challenge in the 32nd minute.

Liverpool responded with Daniel Sturridge’s free-kick against the crossbar and a Jordan Henderson effort over the bar. Marcos Rojo also went close for United, but the next goal belonged to Coutinho in first-half added time. He escaped Guillermo Varela down the left and chipped the ball over De Gea.

The away goal left United needing three more to overturn the tie. They did not score again: Marouane Fellaini headed into Mignolet’s arms, while Liverpool created further chances through Coutinho and Sturridge. Divock Origi and Joe Allen entered in place of Sturridge and Henderson.

Coutinho continued to test De Gea, first shooting at him after Emre Can’s backheel and later forcing a fingertip save from outside the area. The second half finished without a goal, leaving Liverpool’s Brazilian scorer with the decisive away contribution to their aggregate win.
