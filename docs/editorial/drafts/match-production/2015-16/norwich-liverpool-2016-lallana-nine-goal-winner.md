Adam Lallana scored in stoppage time to give Liverpool a 5–4 victory at Norwich City on 23 January 2016. Liverpool had recovered from 3–1 down to lead 4–3, only for Sebastien Bassong to equalise before Lallana supplied the ninth and final goal.

Roberto Firmino put Liverpool ahead in the 18th minute, taking James Milner’s reverse pass and finishing via the post. Norwich turned the score around before half-time: Dieumerci Mbokani backheeled in after a corner and Steven Naismith finished from Wes Hoolahan’s pass.

The home side extended their lead early in the second half when Hoolahan converted a penalty awarded after Alberto Moreno’s challenge on Naismith. Jordan Henderson quickly reduced the deficit, and substitute Lallana then delivered the cross which Firmino lifted over Declan Rudd for his second goal and a 3–3 score.

Milner restored Liverpool’s lead after intercepting Robbie Brady’s under-hit back-pass, going around Rudd to finish. Norwich nevertheless found another response in added time, when a free-kick fell for Bassong and his low shot from outside the area made it 4–4.

There was still time for Liverpool to score again. The ball reached Lallana, whose half-volley struck the turf and bounced over Rudd into the net. His finish in the fifth added minute restored Liverpool’s advantage after Bassong’s late equaliser.
