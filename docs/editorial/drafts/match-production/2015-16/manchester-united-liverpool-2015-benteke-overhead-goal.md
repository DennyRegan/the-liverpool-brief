Christian Benteke’s overhead kick gave Liverpool a late route back into their match at Manchester United, but Anthony Martial’s reply completed a 3–1 home victory on 12 September 2015. All four goals came after a goalless first half at Old Trafford.

Danny Ings made his first Liverpool start in place of the suspended Philippe Coutinho. The opening half produced little direct threat to either goalkeeper, with Liverpool’s Nathaniel Clyne sending an effort off target and Marouane Fellaini shooting over after a loose sequence involving Simon Mignolet’s throw.

United broke through early in the second half. Juan Mata directed a free-kick back towards the edge of the area and Daley Blind swept his shot into the top corner. David de Gea then saved Ings’s volley from Dejan Lovren’s header, while Blind cleared a Martin Skrtel header and thwarted Roberto Firmino near the goal line.

Ander Herrera doubled United’s lead from the penalty spot after a challenge by Joe Gomez. Liverpool nevertheless reduced the deficit in the 83rd minute: Jordon Ibe’s cross was flicked into Benteke’s path and the striker produced an overhead finish to beat De Gea.

The chance to pursue an equaliser was short-lived. Substitute Martial moved in from the left, found space through Liverpool’s defence and finished past Mignolet. Liverpool followed their home loss to West Ham with another league defeat, despite Benteke’s striking contribution.
