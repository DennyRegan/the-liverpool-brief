Liverpool lost 3–2 at Southampton on 20 March 2016 after leading by two goals at half-time. Philippe Coutinho and Daniel Sturridge scored early, but Sadio Mane’s second-half double and a Graziano Pelle goal turned the match around at St Mary’s.

Jon Flanagan captained Liverpool with James Milner suspended and Jordan Henderson on the bench. Coutinho opened the scoring in the 17th minute with a low curling shot, and Sturridge added the second five minutes later after Divock Origi carried the ball forward and supplied him.

Joe Allen had a chance to extend the lead, but Fraser Forster saved his effort. Allen also put the ball in the net before half-time, only for it to be ruled out for offside with Mamadou Sakho standing in its path. Liverpool went into the break 2–0 ahead.

Martin Skrtel replaced Dejan Lovren at half-time and was penalised for a challenge on Pelle early in the second half. Mignolet saved Mane’s penalty, but the substitute later reduced the deficit in the 64th minute. Liverpool could have restored the two-goal margin when Christian Benteke shot wide from Adam Lallana’s pass.

Southampton completed the recovery in the closing minutes. Pelle equalised with a curling shot from outside the area in the 83rd minute, then Mane broke behind Liverpool’s defence and finished low past Mignolet. The three second-half goals erased Liverpool’s advantage and left them without a point.
