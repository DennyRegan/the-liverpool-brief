Liverpool scored six goals through six different players in a 6–0 Premier League victory at Aston Villa on 14 February 2016. Daniel Sturridge, James Milner, Emre Can, Divock Origi, Nathaniel Clyne and Kolo Toure all scored, with Toure recording his first Liverpool goal.

Sturridge made his first league start under Jürgen Klopp and headed Liverpool ahead from Philippe Coutinho’s cross in the opening quarter of an hour. Milner’s free-kick then passed through the crowded penalty area without a touch and found the corner, giving Liverpool a two-goal advantage.

Villa avoided a third before half-time when Jores Okore blocked Jordan Henderson’s shot close to the line. Mark Bunn also saved from Alberto Moreno and Sturridge. Simon Mignolet was called on early in the second half to keep out Leandro Bacuna’s drive.

Can extended Liverpool’s lead after winning possession from Bacuna and exchanging passes with Roberto Firmino. Substitute Origi then ran onto Coutinho’s outside-of-the-foot pass and finished with his first touches. Clyne followed by putting a loose ball over the line after a scramble in the six-yard box.

Toure completed the scoring with a header from Henderson’s corner in the 71st minute. Liverpool had added four goals after the interval, and the centre-back’s first club goal completed the set of six scorers.
