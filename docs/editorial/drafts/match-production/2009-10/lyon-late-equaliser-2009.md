For seven minutes at the Stade de Gerland, Ryan Babel’s goal appeared to have restored Liverpool’s Champions League prospects. His strike from distance put them ahead in the 83rd minute on 4 November 2009. Lisandro López then equalised in the 90th, securing Lyon’s qualification and leaving Liverpool five points behind Fiorentina with two Group E matches remaining.

Liverpool had already lost at Fiorentina and at home to Lyon. They needed points from the return meeting, and created enough chances to take more than the one they eventually collected.

Hugo Lloris was responsible for several of those chances passing without a goal. He stopped Fernando Torres’s first-time effort from Emiliano Insúa’s cross, tipped over an attempt from Dirk Kuyt and used his feet again to deny Andriy Voronin after the forward ran through on a long pass.

Lloris continued to obstruct Liverpool after the break. Lucas Leiva found space in the area and directed a shot towards goal, but the goalkeeper reached it with his left hand. Aly Cissokho cleared Kuyt’s overhead follow-up from the line.

Reina also had an important save to make, advancing to stop Lisandro after a weak back-header. When substitute Babel finally beat Lloris with a powerful shot into the top corner, Liverpool were close to converting those interventions into a victory.

Lyon’s equaliser came from a flicked header by Michel Bastos. Lisandro ran into the area and lifted his finish over Reina, turning what had been a Liverpool lead into a 1–1 draw and confirming the home side’s place in the last 16.

Liverpool were still mathematically capable of qualifying, but now depended on results elsewhere. On 24 November, Fiorentina beat Lyon 1–0 while Liverpool won at Debrecen. Those results ended Liverpool’s hopes of reaching the knockout stage: the two points lost in France had left them unable to control their own progress.
