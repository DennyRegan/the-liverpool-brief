Fernando Torres scored twice as Liverpool overturned a one-goal deficit against Lille to reach the Europa League quarter-finals on 18 March 2010. The 3–0 victory at Anfield secured a 3–1 aggregate win, although the tie remained vulnerable to an away goal until Liverpool's late third.

Eden Hazard had given Lille their first-leg advantage a week earlier. Liverpool removed that lead early in the return when Lucas Leiva advanced into the penalty area and was brought down by Adil Rami. Steven Gerrard converted the penalty, bringing the aggregate score level.

Liverpool had further opportunities before half-time. Glen Johnson volleyed at Mickaël Landreau, and a Daniel Agger header from Gerrard's corner led to a scramble near the line. Torres headed a Dirk Kuyt cross wide.

Lille also had a chance that could have changed the tie. Hazard exchanged passes with Ludovic Obraniak and broke through, but Pepe Reina stood in his path and the shot struck the goalkeeper's head before going behind. Liverpool reached the interval 1–0 ahead on the night, with neither team yet holding the aggregate advantage.

Torres put Liverpool in front soon after the restart. Ryan Babel sent a long ball out of defence, Rami misjudged its bounce, and Torres accelerated clear before lifting his finish past Landreau.

At 2–0, Liverpool led 2–1 overall. A Lille goal would still have put the French side ahead on the away-goals rule, and substitute Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang threatened when he met a free-kick at the far post but sent his effort over.

The third goal finally gave Liverpool a wider margin. Landreau stopped Gerrard's attempt, but Torres followed up to score his second of the night. Lille now needed two goals to reverse the outcome; Liverpool held their 3–0 lead and advanced to the last eight.
