Liverpool were close to reaching the FA Cup fourth round when Reading won a penalty in stoppage time at Anfield on 13 January 2010. Gylfi Sigurdsson converted it, and Shane Long's header in extra time completed a 2–1 victory for the Championship side.

A 1–1 draw at Reading had brought the tie to a replay. Rafael Benítez started Fernando Torres and Steven Gerrard, but both left the match injured: Torres was replaced by David N'Gog during the first half, and Gerrard did not return after the interval.

Reading had already created opportunities before Liverpool went ahead. Diego Cavalieri saved from Sigurdsson and Simon Church, while Grzegorz Rasiak missed a close-range chance. Liverpool threatened through a Philipp Degen run that ended with Yossi Benayoun shooting narrowly wide.

The home side nevertheless reached half-time in front. Gerrard sent the ball into the goalmouth, where it struck Ryan Bertrand and beat Adam Federici. The own goal gave Liverpool an advantage they held through most of the second half despite the loss of their captain and leading striker.

Reading continued to find openings. Jobi McAnuff ran through the Liverpool defence but finished wide, and Church headed over. The equaliser finally came when Benayoun was adjudged to have fouled substitute Long in the penalty area. Sigurdsson sent Cavalieri the wrong way from the spot, taking the match into an additional half-hour.

Long then scored the winner. Brynjar Gunnarsson advanced on the right, went past Benayoun and put the ball through Emiliano Insúa's legs before crossing. Long headed in from close range in the first period of extra time.

Liverpool could not recover. Reading advanced to the fourth round. Liverpool's FA Cup campaign was over after two third-round meetings with second-tier opposition.
