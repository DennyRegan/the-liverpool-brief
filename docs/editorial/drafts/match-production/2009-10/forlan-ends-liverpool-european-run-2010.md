Yossi Benayoun’s goal early in extra time put Liverpool ahead in their Europa League semi-final against Atlético Madrid. Within minutes, Diego Forlán had changed the destination of the tie. Liverpool won 2–1 at Anfield on 29 April 2010, but a 2–2 aggregate score sent Atlético through on away goals.

Forlán had scored the only goal of the first leg in Madrid. Liverpool therefore needed to recover a deficit for the third successive round, having already overturned away defeats against Lille and Benfica. This time they would win the home match without reaching the next stage.

Benayoun almost supplied an immediate breakthrough, forcing David de Gea to save from a narrow angle. Dirk Kuyt later went close from Javier Mascherano’s cross, and Daniel Agger headed in Steven Gerrard’s free-kick only for an offside decision to rule it out.

Atlético also tested Liverpool. Pepe Reina turned away Raúl García’s shot, while Sergio Agüero reached a position beyond the goalkeeper but sent a cross across goal without finding Forlán. The first half remained goalless until Alberto Aquilani struck just before the interval.

Mascherano, playing at right-back, threw the ball down the flank to Benayoun. His cross passed Kuyt and reached Aquilani, whose first-time low finish beat De Gea. Liverpool had levelled the aggregate score, with neither side yet holding an away goal.

The second half produced chances without another breakthrough. Glen Johnson moved inside and forced De Gea to turn his shot over the bar. Near the end of normal time, Forlán failed to control when he had the opportunity to break clear. The match went into extra time with Liverpool leading 1–0 on the night.

Lucas Leiva then lifted a pass into the inside-left channel for Benayoun. His left-footed shot beat De Gea, who got a hand to the ball but could not keep it out. Liverpool led 2–0 and, for the first time in the semi-final, were ahead on aggregate.

Atlético’s response came before the extra-time interval. After José Manuel Jurado had shot narrowly wide, José Antonio Reyes got past Johnson on the right and crossed for the unmarked Forlán to score in the 102nd minute.

The away-goals rule also applied during extra time. Liverpool needed one more goal to advance, but none came. Atlético would meet Fulham in Hamburg; Benítez’s side were eliminated despite winning the return leg.

It proved to be Rafael Benítez’s final European match as Liverpool manager. Only the league fixtures against Chelsea and Hull City remained that season, and his departure by mutual consent was announced on 3 June.
