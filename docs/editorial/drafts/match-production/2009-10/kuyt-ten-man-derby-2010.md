Dirk Kuyt scored his fiftieth Liverpool goal to settle the Anfield derby on 6 February 2010. His header brought a 1–0 victory over Everton even though Rafael Benítez's side played most of the match with ten men after Sotirios Kyrgiakos was sent off.

Liverpool had begun to recover ground in the league after a difficult autumn. This result extended their unbeaten Premier League sequence to seven matches, with six clean sheets, and left them fourth in the table at the end of the day's fixtures.

The first half supplied more disciplinary incidents than scoring chances. In the 34th minute Kyrgiakos was dismissed after a challenge with Marouane Fellaini. The Everton midfielder was injured in the incident and had to be replaced by Mikel Arteta, but the visitors retained their numerical advantage.

Liverpool still came close before the interval when Steven Gerrard struck the crossbar with a free-kick. At the other end, Pepe Reina tipped a Leighton Baines free-kick over, and Tim Cahill headed another opportunity above the bar. Neither side had scored by half-time.

The decisive goal arrived ten minutes after the restart. Gerrard delivered a corner, and Kuyt held his position close to goal before heading past Tim Howard. Liverpool now had a lead to defend as well as a missing player to compensate for.

Everton could not find an equaliser. Their own numbers were reduced in stoppage time when Steven Pienaar received a second yellow card after a challenge on Gerrard; he had been cautioned earlier for a challenge on Javier Mascherano.

Both teams therefore finished with ten players, but Liverpool had spent the greater part of the contest a man down. Kuyt's goal ended Everton's nine-match unbeaten league run and completed a Liverpool league double over their neighbours.
