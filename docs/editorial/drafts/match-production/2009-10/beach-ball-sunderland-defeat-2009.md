The only goal at the Stadium of Light on 17 October 2009 came with an intervention from an object that should never have influenced the score. Darren Bent’s shot struck a beach ball in Liverpool’s penalty area, changed direction and went past Pepe Reina. Referee Mike Jones allowed the goal to stand, and Sunderland won 1–0.

Andy Reid and Steed Malbranque had combined to create the fifth-minute opening before Bent shot. Liverpool’s players protested about the deflection, but play resumed with Sunderland ahead. Former referee Graham Poll subsequently explained that the interference should have led to play being stopped and restarted with a dropped ball.

Liverpool were without Steven Gerrard and Fernando Torres, and the remainder of the match did not bring an equaliser. Ryan Babel forced Craig Gordon to block an early effort, while Fábio Aurélio sent a free-kick wide.

Bent also had chances to make the disputed goal less decisive. He headed Reid’s cross at Reina and another delivery wide before the interval. After the break, Reina denied him following Kenwyne Jones’s pass, and a further attempt struck a post.

The defeat was Liverpool’s fourth in their opening nine league matches. Sunderland moved above them into seventh, leaving Rafael Benítez’s side eighth.
