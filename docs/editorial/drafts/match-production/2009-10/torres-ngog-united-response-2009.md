Liverpool entered Manchester United’s visit on 25 October 2009 after four successive defeats. They left Anfield with a 2–0 victory, Fernando Torres supplying the breakthrough and David Ngog finishing the counterattack that finally secured the points.

The preceding losses had come against Fiorentina and Lyon in the Champions League and Chelsea and Sunderland in the Premier League. Liverpool were again without Steven Gerrard, but Torres returned to the side that had lost at home to Lyon five days earlier.

A goalless first half included opportunities at both ends. Edwin van der Sar saved Fábio Aurélio’s free-kick and then stopped Dirk Kuyt’s follow-up. Pepe Reina held Wayne Rooney’s header from Antonio Valencia’s cross, leaving neither side with an advantage at the interval.

Torres changed that midway through the second half. He held off Rio Ferdinand as he moved into the area and drove his finish past Van der Sar, putting Liverpool ahead in front of the Kop.

The closing stages included several contentious challenges. Jamie Carragher was cautioned after bringing down substitute Michael Owen, with United appealing for a dismissal. Nemanja Vidić did receive a second yellow card after hauling down Kuyt, his third successive sending-off against Liverpool. Javier Mascherano then followed him off after a challenge on Van der Sar brought another second caution.

Both teams were down to ten when Liverpool scored again. Lucas Leiva led a counterattack and supplied Ngog, who finished past Van der Sar deep into added time. United no longer had time to turn their pressure into an equaliser.

Liverpool had their first victory since September, ending a run that had brought four defeats in two competitions.
