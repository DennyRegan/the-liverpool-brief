Liverpool won the League Cup at Wembley on 26 February 2012, beating Cardiff City 3–2 on penalties after a 2–2 draw. The club’s record eighth success in the competition was their first trophy since 2006, but it required a recovery in normal time and another deciding phase after an extra-time lead was lost.

Cardiff went ahead through Joe Mason in the first half. Kenny Miller’s pass released him to finish beyond Pepe Reina, giving the Championship side an advantage they retained until the interval. Liverpool had reached the final by eliminating Manchester City, but the last obstacle had established a lead of its own.

Martin Skrtel equalised after the break. Andy Carroll’s header led to Luis Suárez striking the post, and Skrtel finished the rebound. The match was level at 1–1, and neither side found a winner before extra time.

Dirk Kuyt put Liverpool ahead in the 108th minute. His first attempt was blocked, but he followed it to score at the second opportunity. Cardiff then answered near the end of extra time, with Ben Turner converting from a corner in the 118th minute. Liverpool’s lead had lasted only briefly before the final became a penalty contest.

The shoot-out began badly for Liverpool. Steven Gerrard’s penalty was saved and Charlie Adam missed, but Kuyt, Stewart Downing and Glen Johnson converted the next three. Cardiff scored twice; Anthony Gerrard’s final miss left Liverpool the winners at 3–2 in the shoot-out.

The score after extra time remained 2–2: the penalty result decided the trophy rather than being added to the match’s goal total. Liverpool had come from behind, conceded a late equaliser and recovered from two unsuccessful opening penalties. The cup was finally secured through the three successful kicks that followed.
