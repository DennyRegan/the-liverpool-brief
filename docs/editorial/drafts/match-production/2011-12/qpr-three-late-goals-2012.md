Liverpool lost a two-goal lead at Loftus Road on 21 March 2012, conceding three late goals in a 3–2 defeat by Queens Park Rangers. Sebastián Coates and Dirk Kuyt had put the visitors ahead before Shaun Derry, Djibril Cissé and Jamie Mackie changed the result.

The match followed Liverpool’s FA Cup quarter-final victory over Stoke City. Their league form had been interrupted by Gerrard’s derby hat-trick, and for much of this game another league win appeared available. The first half ended goalless, however, leaving the decisive scoring to the second period.

Coates opened the scoring with an acrobatic scissor-kick. Kuyt then added the second after Luis Suárez had struck the post and Paddy Kenny had saved from Stewart Downing. Liverpool led 2–0 with less than twenty minutes of normal time remaining.

Derry began QPR’s recovery with a header from Adel Taarabt’s corner. Cissé, a former Liverpool striker, equalised with another header, this time from Taye Taiwo’s cross. Two deliveries and two finishes had erased the advantage Liverpool had built.

Mackie supplied the winner in added time. A flick from Luke Young and an error by José Enrique left the substitute with the opening to finish beyond Pepe Reina. QPR had gone from two goals behind to a lead that Liverpool could not answer.

The result moved QPR two points clear of the relegation places. For Liverpool, it was a defeat after holding a two-goal lead late in the game. Three separate QPR scorers had turned that position into another setback, with the following home league fixture against Wigan offering the next chance to recover.
