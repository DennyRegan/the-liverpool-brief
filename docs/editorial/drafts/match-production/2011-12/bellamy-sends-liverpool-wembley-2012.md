Craig Bellamy’s equaliser secured Liverpool’s place in the League Cup final on 25 January 2012. A 2–2 draw with Manchester City at Anfield completed a 3–2 aggregate victory, with Liverpool answering both of City’s goals to earn a Wembley meeting with Cardiff City.

Steven Gerrard’s penalty had given Liverpool a 1–0 advantage in the first leg at the Etihad Stadium. That narrow lead left the second game open, and Nigel de Jong cancelled it out with a long-range shot in the first half. City were ahead on the night and the aggregate score was level.

Liverpool responded before the interval. Micah Richards was penalised for handball after the ball had deflected onto his arm, and Gerrard converted the awarded penalty. It restored Liverpool’s aggregate advantage while making the score 1–1 in the second leg.

Joe Hart made a series of saves that kept City in the tie. Their second goal arrived when Aleksandar Kolarov crossed and Edin Džeko finished, again giving the visitors a lead on the night. The match still required Liverpool to answer a City goal rather than simply protect the first-leg result.

Bellamy supplied the response in the 74th minute. He exchanged passes with Glen Johnson and finished low to bring Liverpool level at 2–2. The aggregate score was now 3–2 in Liverpool’s favour, and no further goal changed it.

The semi-final had been decided across two games by Gerrard’s penalties and Bellamy’s finish. City had twice drawn the tie level on aggregate at Anfield, but Liverpool had found a reply each time. Cardiff, from the Championship, awaited in the final on 26 February.
