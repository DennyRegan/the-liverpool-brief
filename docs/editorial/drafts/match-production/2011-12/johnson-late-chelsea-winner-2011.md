Glen Johnson returned to Stamford Bridge to score the late goal that beat his former club on 20 November 2011. Liverpool’s 2–1 victory over Chelsea came after Daniel Sturridge had cancelled out Maxi Rodríguez’s first-half opener, leaving the visitors to win a game that had been level for much of the second half.

Maxi’s goal began with Charlie Adam dispossessing John Obi Mikel. Craig Bellamy and Luis Suárez exchanged passes, and Bellamy supplied the final ball for Maxi to finish. Liverpool had converted a Chelsea loss of possession into a lead they carried to half-time.

Chelsea introduced Sturridge at the interval. He brought the hosts level from Florent Malouda’s delivery, and the match remained in the balance. Pepe Reina then saved a Branislav Ivanović header, preventing Chelsea from turning their recovery into a lead.

Johnson decided the contest in the 87th minute. Adam switched the ball across to the right, Johnson moved past Ashley Cole, and the full-back finished left-footed beyond Petr Čech. A defender who had previously played for Chelsea had supplied Liverpool’s winning goal through an attacking run and finish.

The result put Liverpool level on points with Chelsea. It was a league success at a ground where they would return only nine days later, this time for a League Cup quarter-final. That second visit carried a different prize: progression in the competition rather than three Premier League points.

Here, Johnson’s goal was the difference. Liverpool had led, been pegged back and then found a late winner, with Adam involved in the creation of both goals and Reina making a significant save while the score was level.
