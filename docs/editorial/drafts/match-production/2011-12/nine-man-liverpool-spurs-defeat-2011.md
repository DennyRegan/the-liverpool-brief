Tottenham inflicted Liverpool’s heaviest defeat of the early campaign on 18 September 2011. The 4–0 result at White Hart Lane combined four home goals with two Liverpool dismissals and an injury to Daniel Agger, leaving Kenny Dalglish’s side to finish with nine men.

Liverpool had already beaten Arsenal and Bolton before losing at Stoke. Tottenham made it two successive league defeats, but the manner of this one was more severe: Luka Modrić put the home side ahead with a shot from outside the area in the opening minutes.

Agger’s injury brought Sebastián Coates into the game for his Liverpool debut. Charlie Adam then received a second caution after a challenge on Scott Parker. Liverpool were a goal and a player down before the interval, with the early changes limiting Dalglish’s available options.

A second sending-off followed after the break. Martin Skrtel, playing at right-back, was shown another yellow card following a challenge on Gareth Bale. Tottenham now faced nine men, and Jermain Defoe turned and finished to increase the lead.

Emmanuel Adebayor scored next after Pepe Reina could not hold an initial effort. He added another in stoppage time, completing the four-goal margin. Liverpool’s dismissals were part of the recorded sequence, but Tottenham had established their lead before either player was sent off.

The defeat supplied a sharp early setback after the promise of the Arsenal win. Liverpool had conceded four without reply, lost two players to second yellow cards and seen a new defender introduced through injury rather than a planned change. Their next league fixture, against Wolves at Anfield, offered the first opportunity to respond.
