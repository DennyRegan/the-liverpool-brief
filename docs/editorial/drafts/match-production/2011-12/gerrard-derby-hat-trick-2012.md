Steven Gerrard scored a hat-trick against Everton on 13 March 2012, marking his 400th Premier League appearance with a 3–0 Liverpool win at Anfield. The captain supplied every goal as Liverpool ended a run of three consecutive league defeats.

The losses had come against Manchester United, Arsenal and Sunderland, with the League Cup final falling between them. Everton arrived with an opportunity to move above Liverpool in the table, but Gerrard’s goals kept the league derby firmly with the home side.

The opener came after Tim Howard had saved Martin Kelly’s attempt. Gerrard met the loose ball with a lofted finish, placing it beyond the goalkeeper and the defenders between him and the goal. Liverpool carried the one-goal advantage into half-time.

Luis Suárez helped create the second early in the next half. His work inside Everton’s area provided Gerrard with the opportunity to finish again. Liverpool now had a two-goal lead, and the partnership would produce one more before the game ended.

In added time, Suárez supplied Gerrard for his third. The captain completed the hat-trick and a 3–0 scoreline, giving the evening an individual milestone as well as a clear derby result.

It was the first league hat-trick in this fixture since Ian Rush scored four at Goodison Park in November 1982, and the first at Anfield since Fred Howe’s treble in September 1935. Those records belonged to the league derby; Gerrard’s 400th appearance also referred specifically to the Premier League, rather than his total Liverpool career.

Liverpool had completed a league double over Everton after the October win at Goodison. Gerrard’s treble ended the losing sequence with a result that also protected their position above their neighbours.
