Luis Suárez scored his first Liverpool hat-trick at Norwich City on 28 April 2012, completing a 3–0 victory with a long-range lob. Two goals before half-time established the lead, and the third arrived late in the second half.

The win came one week before the FA Cup final. Liverpool were still playing through the closing league fixtures, and Carrow Road offered Suárez an individual landmark within a campaign whose strongest team achievements had come in the domestic cups.

Steven Gerrard helped create the first goal by dispossessing David Fox and supplying Suárez. The striker finished to put Liverpool ahead in the 24th minute, then added another four minutes later after beating Elliott Ward. Norwich had conceded twice in a short spell, and Liverpool carried the 2–0 advantage into the interval.

Jonjo Shelvey struck the crossbar during the second half. The score remained 2–0 until Suárez produced the third in the 82nd minute, seeing John Ruddy away from his line and lifting a shot over the goalkeeper from long range. The finish completed the hat-trick and the final three-goal margin.

The victory left Liverpool eighth, two points behind Everton. Suárez would finish his first full Liverpool season with seventeen goals in thirty-nine appearances, but this was his first three-goal match for the club. Its final strike supplied the distinctive finish to that milestone.

Liverpool’s next competitive fixture was a home league game against Fulham before the Wembley final against Chelsea. The Norwich result had given them an away win and a clean sheet, with Suárez’s three goals deciding every change to the scoreline.
