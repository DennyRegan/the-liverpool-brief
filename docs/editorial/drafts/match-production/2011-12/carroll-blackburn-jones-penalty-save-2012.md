Andy Carroll’s added-time header gave Liverpool a 3–2 win at Blackburn Rovers on 10 April 2012. The result came after two Maxi Rodríguez goals, Doni’s dismissal, an immediate penalty save by substitute goalkeeper Brad Jones and two replies from Yakubu.

Maxi scored twice early in the match. Bellamy supplied the first after a pass from Martin Skrtel, and Maxi followed Jonjo Shelvey’s saved effort for the second. Liverpool had a two-goal lead before a back-pass from Jon Flanagan put Doni under pressure.

The goalkeeper brought down Junior Hoilett and was sent off. Jones replaced him and saved Yakubu’s penalty, preserving the 2–0 score at his first intervention. That advantage did not survive intact until half-time: Yakubu headed in David Dunn’s free-kick to reduce it to one goal.

Blackburn equalised through a second penalty after the break, this time awarded for Jones’s foul. Yakubu converted to make it 2–2, completing his own double. Liverpool were down to ten men and had lost their lead, but they had not trailed in the match.

The winning goal arrived in added time. Sebastián Coates delivered the ball, Daniel Agger headed it back across and Carroll finished with a header. Liverpool had recovered a win from a game in which the early two-goal margin had disappeared.

The goalkeeper changes also reached beyond Ewood Park. Pepe Reina was already suspended, and Doni’s dismissal ruled him out of the FA Cup semi-final against Everton four days later. Jones would therefore be required at Wembley. His penalty save had helped keep Liverpool ahead at Blackburn before Carroll supplied the late goal that secured the points.
