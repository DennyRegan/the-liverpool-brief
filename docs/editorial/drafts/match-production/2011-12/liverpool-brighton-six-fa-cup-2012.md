Liverpool reached the FA Cup quarter-finals with a 6–1 victory over Brighton & Hove Albion on 19 February 2012. The Anfield tie was level after the opening exchanges, but Liverpool led by half-time and added four more goals after the break, one week before their League Cup final.

Martin Skrtel opened the scoring with a header from Steven Gerrard’s corner. Kazenga LuaLua brought Brighton level with a free-kick, ensuring that the early Liverpool goal did not immediately settle the fifth-round contest.

A Liam Bridcutt own goal restored Liverpool’s lead before the interval. Andy Carroll then made it 3–1 in the second half, finishing from Stewart Downing’s delivery. Brighton had answered the first goal but could not repeat that recovery as the score widened.

The fourth came from Gerrard’s shot, which deflected off Bridcutt. It was initially described as another Bridcutt own goal, but the club subsequently awarded the goal to Gerrard; that credit is also recorded by LFChistory. Lewis Dunk then scored an own goal, making the score 5–1. The initial three-own-goals description therefore differs from the club’s subsequent scoring record.

Luis Suárez had a penalty saved by Peter Brezovan before scoring Liverpool’s sixth. Carroll headed the ball across for him to finish with a header, completing the largest Liverpool victory of the campaign. The saved penalty had delayed his goal rather than denied him a place among the scorers.

Liverpool would face Stoke City in the quarter-finals. Before that tie came the League Cup final against Cardiff City: the six-goal win had extended one cup run while the other was already at its deciding match.
