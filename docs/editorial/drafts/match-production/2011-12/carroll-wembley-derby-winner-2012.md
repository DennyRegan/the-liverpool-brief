Andy Carroll headed Liverpool into the FA Cup final on 14 April 2012, completing a 2–1 recovery against Everton at Wembley. Nikica Jelavić had given Everton a first-half lead before Luis Suárez equalised and Carroll supplied the late winner.

Liverpool had already beaten their neighbours twice in the league, but this meeting decided a place in another domestic final. The League Cup was secured in February; Everton stood between Liverpool and a return to Wembley for the FA Cup’s deciding match.

Everton took the lead when Jamie Carragher’s attempted clearance struck Tim Cahill and reached Jelavić. The striker finished past Brad Jones, leaving Liverpool to enter half-time behind. The earlier league derby results offered no protection against the deficit in this separate cup tie.

Suárez brought Liverpool level in the second half. Sylvain Distin’s short back-pass gave him the opening, and he finished to make it 1–1. The equaliser ensured that Everton’s first-half lead would not be enough on its own to reach the final.

Carroll had missed opportunities before finding the decisive header in the 87th minute. Craig Bellamy delivered a free-kick, and the striker met it to put Liverpool ahead. The winning goal followed his added-time winner at Blackburn four days earlier, giving him two important finishes in the same week.

Liverpool held the lead and advanced to the final against Chelsea. It was their third derby victory of the season, but the first of those wins in which they had needed to come from behind. Suárez’s equaliser and Carroll’s header had turned a half-time deficit into a second domestic cup final place.
