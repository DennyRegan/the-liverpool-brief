Ian Rush became Liverpool's record goalscorer at Old Trafford on 18 October 1992. His 287th goal for the club put Liverpool 2–0 ahead before half-time, surpassing Roger Hunt's total. The personal landmark survived intact; the lead did not. Two late Mark Hughes goals earned Manchester United a 2–2 draw.

Liverpool's first goal came from Don Hutchison midway through the opening half. Jan Mølby supplied the pass and Hutchison's shot took a deflection off Steve Bruce on its way in. With Mølby's passing helping Liverpool build their attacks, the visitors established a position that belied their place in the lower half of the league.

Rush doubled the lead in the 44th minute. Ronny Rosenthal reached the byline and pulled the ball back for Rush to finish. It was his first goal at Old Trafford as well as the one that made him Liverpool's leading scorer. Having already passed Hunt's European total against Apollon Limassol in September, Rush now held the overall record too.

Liverpool retained their two-goal advantage deep into the second half. United's recovery began when Hughes met Clayton Blackmore's delivery and sent the ball over Bruce Grobbelaar. Mølby subsequently left the field injured, removing an important influence on Liverpool's play as they tried to protect the remaining lead.

Hughes struck again near the finish, diving to head in Ryan Giggs' cross. Liverpool's prospect of an away victory had disappeared in the closing stages, leaving a point from a match they had controlled on the scoreboard for most of the afternoon.

The draw left Liverpool sixteenth with 13 points from twelve league fixtures. Rush's achievement was considerable, but the team's position gave the occasion a divided character: a club scoring record secured at Old Trafford, and two points relinquished after establishing a winning lead.
