Dean Saunders' first home appearance for Aston Villa came against the club he had left less than a fortnight earlier. By the end of the match at Villa Park on 19 September 1992 he had scored twice, helping Villa beat Liverpool 4–2 and leaving Graeme Souness' side with two wins from their first nine league matches.

Liverpool might have established an early lead. Ronny Rosenthal went round Nigel Spink but struck the crossbar with the goal open. The opportunity passed, although Liverpool did eventually go ahead when Mark Walters scored shortly before half-time.

That advantage lasted barely a minute. Steve Froggatt crossed and Saunders arrived at the near post to equalise. Liverpool had recovered from Rosenthal's miss to take the lead, then surrendered it before the interval.

Villa's second-half goals drew further contributions from Liverpool's former players. Ray Houghton was involved in the move from which Garry Parker's mis-hit effort reached Dalian Atkinson, who put Villa 2–1 ahead. Houghton then supplied Saunders for his second goal, extending the lead and giving the striker a decisive role against his former team-mates.

Parker added Villa's fourth after an attacking move involving Saunders and Atkinson. Rosenthal eventually found the net with a late header, but his goal only reduced an already substantial deficit. Three days after beating Apollon Limassol 6–1 in Europe, Liverpool had conceded four in the league.

The match also brought a Liverpool debut for Torben Piechnik. It was a difficult introduction to a defence unable to contain Villa's attack after half-time. Saunders' two goals supplied the most immediate story, while the league table recorded the broader damage: Liverpool stood seventeenth after nine fixtures. The departures of Saunders and Houghton had acquired a particularly direct consequence in a match both helped Villa win.
