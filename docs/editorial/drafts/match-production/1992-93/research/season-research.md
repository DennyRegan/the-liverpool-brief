## Whole-season assessment

The complete competitive ledger was reviewed, including all domestic and European cup ties and the Shield. Liverpool finished sixth with 59 points. The first Premier League campaign included early European elimination, an FA Cup third-round replay defeat while holders, and a League Cup fourth-round replay exit. The league-position sequence shows a winter in the lower half followed by a recovery. The selection therefore includes important defeats as well as scoring landmarks and the spring improvement. It does not equate sixth place with a successful campaign or impose a fixed count.

## Source method and limitations

For each selected match, the LFChistory teamsheet and timeline were compared with contemporary newspaper reports preserved in Graeme Riley's archive. Those reports are primary contemporary evidence, although accessed through later transcriptions. Multiple newspapers in one compilation remain distinct reporting voices; their contradictory details are recorded, not silently merged. Official club evidence is preferred for club milestones. No footage has been watched. No quotation is needed in the finished copy.

The current Premier League Tottenham match URL was opened but supplied only navigation in the tool, so it cannot independently verify that match's detailed scoring record. A search extract is not used to assert an adjudication. The LFChistory note explicitly explains the changed Harkness/Nethercott attribution; other independently retrieved evidence will be used where available.
