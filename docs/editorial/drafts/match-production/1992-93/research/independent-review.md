## Corrections requested and verified

1. Chesterfield: change incorrect first names Dave Norris and Jason Lancaster to **Steve Norris and Dave Lancaster**. The actual teamsheet, scoring timeline and contemporary reports agree.
2. Villa: replace the claimed ten-day interval since Saunders' departure. The official Liverpool profile says nine days; Reuters' report dated 11 September records his sale on Thursday, 10 September. The match was 19 September. Contemporary match prose saying ten days is not retained as a more authoritative date calculation.
3. Forest: use **Mark Crossley**, not Steve Crossley. The writer independently spotted this too. Prefer a general half-hour reference over conflicting 28th/29th-minute conventions.
4. Apollon: remove the unsupported description of the dispossessed opponent as a defender. The newspaper accounts differ on identity, and British Soccer Week calls Kenny a midfielder. The draft need only say Stewart won possession.
5. Spartak: after Radchenko's opening goal the aggregate was Liverpool 2–5 Spartak. Liverpool required **three unanswered goals**, producing 5–5 aggregate and a 2–1 away-goal advantage. Four goals were not necessary merely to draw aggregate.
6. Bolton: McGinlay supplied the **cross** for Walker's finish; wording saying he supplied the finish obscures the correctly identified scorer.
7. Small prose changes: remove the implication that the three-defender system was first tried in the Shield (the contemporary account says it was used during pre-season); remove repetitive closing summaries in Everton/Coventry and redundant control-of-tie language in Apollon.
8. Chesterfield sequence: replace wording implying Liverpool had already equalised during their first recovery. They had recovered from 0–3 to 2–3, then from 2–4 to 4–4. The final manuscript now makes that distinction.

All eight correction groups are resolved in the files inspected. Villa uses the cautious and accurate “less than a fortnight” interval. Existing manuscript source lists are supplemented by the independently retrieved evidence recorded here; no original manuscript was replaced.
