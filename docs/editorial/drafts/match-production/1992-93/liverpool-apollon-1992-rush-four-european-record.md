Ian Rush's four goals against Apollon Limassol took him beyond Roger Hunt as Liverpool's leading scorer in European competition. The 6–1 victory at Anfield on 16 September 1992 also gave Liverpool a commanding advantage in the first round of the European Cup Winners' Cup, a competition they had entered as FA Cup holders.

Paul Stewart began the scoring in the fourth minute after winning possession. He added Liverpool's second with a header from Jan Mølby's free-kick, before Rush opened his own account from Mike Marsh's pass shortly before the interval. Liverpool led 3–0 at half-time, with Apollon also reduced to ten men after Charalambous received a second booking for a foul on Stewart.

Rush then scored twice in quick succession after the restart. Mark Walters supplied the first of those goals; a header completed the hat-trick. That third goal of the night carried Rush past Hunt's European total of 17. A fourth followed later in the half, taking Rush's tally in European competition to 19 and Liverpool's lead to six.

Apollon's late consolation came from a Spoljaric penalty after Marsh fouled Pittas. It changed the score but did little to alter the balance of a tie Liverpool now controlled.

The return in Cyprus ended in another Liverpool victory, 2–1, completing an 8–2 aggregate success. Rush's evening at Anfield remained the defining performance of the round: four goals in one match and a European club record secured. His pursuit of Liverpool's overall scoring record would reach its own conclusion later that autumn.
