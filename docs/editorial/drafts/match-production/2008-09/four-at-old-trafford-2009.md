Four days after putting four goals past Real Madrid, Liverpool scored four more at Old Trafford. The 4–1 victory over Manchester United on 14 March 2009 completed a league double over the champions and reduced their lead at the top to four points.

United still had a game in hand, making a Liverpool victory valuable without making it decisive. Defeat would have widened the gap considerably, and Liverpool were behind midway through the first half when Pepe Reina brought down Park Ji-sung. Cristiano Ronaldo converted the penalty.

The lead lasted only five minutes. Nemanja Vidić failed to deal with a long ball, Fernando Torres took possession and ran clear to finish past Edwin van der Sar. Liverpool had recovered before United could consolidate their advantage.

Torres then helped create the move that put Liverpool ahead. His pass sent Steven Gerrard into the penalty area, where Patrice Evra fouled him. Gerrard took the penalty himself and Liverpool reached the interval leading 2–1.

Vidić’s second-half dismissal created the next decisive moment. The defender brought down Gerrard as Liverpool’s captain threatened to break clear and received a red card. Fábio Aurélio took the resulting free-kick, bending it past Van der Sar to give Liverpool a two-goal cushion.

Substitute Andrea Dossena added a fourth late on. Having scored against Madrid in midweek, he ran on to Reina’s long clearance and lobbed Van der Sar to make it 4–1. Liverpool had turned an early deficit into their biggest win at United since 1936.

September’s 2–1 victory at Anfield and this away success gave Liverpool six points from their league meetings with United. The champions retained the lead and control of their remaining fixtures, but Liverpool had reduced the deficit on the one afternoon when they could directly take points from the team above them.
