Four times Liverpool hit the woodwork before half-time at Craven Cottage. They still had no goal as the match entered stoppage time, until substitute Yossi Benayoun finally found a finish that counted and secured a 1–0 victory on 4 April 2009.

Liverpool arrived after league wins over Sunderland, Manchester United and Aston Villa, a sequence in which they had scored 11 goals and conceded one. Another victory would take them above United, who were due to face Villa the following day.

The first half produced repeated chances. Mark Schwarzer saved Andrea Dossena’s shot, then a Martin Škrtel effort glanced off Dossena’s head and struck the bar. Xabi Alonso also hit the bar with a volley from outside the area.

Fernando Torres came close after combining with Steven Gerrard, but his angled shot struck a post. Dossena then headed against the woodwork again from Gerrard’s cross. Schwarzer’s saves, as well as the frame of his goal, kept Fulham level at the interval.

Liverpool continued to seek the breakthrough after the restart without finding it. Fulham had already held them to a goalless draw at Anfield in November, and a second such result was close when Benayoun intervened in the 92nd minute, driving in the winning goal.

The three points put Liverpool two clear of United, although the champions had two games in hand. Benayoun’s winner therefore placed a demand on United’s next result rather than transferring control of the race to Liverpool. After a match of missed opportunities, Liverpool had secured the victory required to make that pressure count.
