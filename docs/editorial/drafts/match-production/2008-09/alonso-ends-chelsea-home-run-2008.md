Chelsea had not lost a home league match since February 2004. Liverpool ended that run on 26 October 2008, winning 1–0 at Stamford Bridge to move three points clear at the top of the Premier League.

The decisive moment arrived in the tenth minute. Xabi Alonso struck from the edge of the penalty area and the ball deflected off José Bosingwa, wrong-footing Petr Čech before entering the net. Liverpool had an early lead to defend against opponents who had gone 86 home league games unbeaten.

Luiz Felipe Scolari’s side pushed for an equaliser, but Liverpool’s defence, with Jamie Carragher and Daniel Agger at its centre, protected the advantage. The visitors also threatened to extend it. In the second half Alonso’s free-kick struck the base of a post, coming close to putting the result beyond Chelsea.

A late opening fell to Ashley Cole after Franco Di Santo headed the ball down, but Cole failed to connect properly. Chelsea could not find the goal that would preserve a sequence stretching back to Arsenal’s 2–1 victory at the ground on 21 February 2004.

The result also ended Chelsea’s unbeaten start to the current league season. Liverpool were left as the division’s only unbeaten side, with Chelsea and Hull City three points behind them.
