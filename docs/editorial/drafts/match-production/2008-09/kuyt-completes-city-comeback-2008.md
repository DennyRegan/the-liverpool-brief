Dirk Kuyt’s stoppage-time finish completed a recovery that had looked a considerable distance away at half-time. Liverpool trailed Manchester City 2–0 on 5 October 2008, yet left the City of Manchester Stadium with a 3–2 win and their unbeaten league start intact.

City’s advantage came through Stephen Ireland and Javier Garrido. Ireland finished after Shaun Wright-Phillips’ work on the right, and Garrido’s free-kick gave the home side a second goal before the interval. Liverpool had created chances, including an opening Kuyt sent over, but had nothing to show for them.

Fernando Torres changed the match after the break. Steven Gerrard released Álvaro Arbeloa down the right and the full-back’s delivery gave Torres a close-range finish. The deficit was halved, although City still had an opportunity to restore it when Robinho failed to convert Wright-Phillips’ pass.

Pablo Zabaleta’s dismissal for a challenge on Xabi Alonso then left City with ten men. Liverpool pressed their advantage and Torres scored again, meeting Gerrard’s corner with a header to make it 2–2. Having struck twice at Everton the previous weekend, he had produced another double away from Anfield.

The equaliser did not end the complications. Martin Škrtel was injured late on, and Liverpool had already used their substitutes. They too had to finish with ten players, while Torres had passed up an opportunity to complete a hat-trick.

Kuyt supplied the decisive finish in added time, reacting to a loose ball in the area and driving it into the net. Liverpool had scored three times without reply in the second half.

Three weeks after coming from behind against Manchester United, Liverpool had recovered from an even larger deficit against City. Torres had provided the way back and Kuyt had secured the additional two points.
