Yossi Benayoun’s stoppage-time equaliser preserved Liverpool’s unbeaten home league record, but it could not turn four goals into the victory they wanted. Arsenal left Anfield with a 4–4 draw on 21 April 2009, all four of their goals scored by Andrey Arshavin.

Liverpool’s first-half pressure had brought saves from Łukasz Fabiański, including one from Fernando Torres, but Arsenal went ahead before the break. Cesc Fàbregas supplied the cut-back and Arshavin’s finish went in off the underside of the crossbar.

Two Dirk Kuyt crosses changed the score early in the second half. Torres headed the first into the net, and Benayoun met the second to put Liverpool 2–1 ahead. Fabiański attempted to claw Benayoun’s header away, but the goal was awarded.

Arshavin then scored twice in three minutes. He took advantage of Álvaro Arbeloa’s hesitation to drive in the equaliser from outside the area, before punishing a poor clearance by Fábio Aurélio to complete his hat-trick. Liverpool’s lead had become another deficit.

Torres answered with his second goal to make it 3–3. Neither side could protect the advantage it had gained, and the closing minutes brought another exchange. Theo Walcott led an Arsenal counterattack from which Arshavin scored his fourth, apparently securing an away victory as the match moved into added time.

Benayoun still had a response, converting from close range to draw Liverpool level again. He and Torres had both scored twice, while Kuyt’s deliveries had created the earlier recovery from Arsenal’s first-half lead.

The point put Liverpool top on goal difference, but Manchester United had two games in hand. The position in the table therefore concealed the cost of the draw: Liverpool had avoided defeat without securing the three points that would have increased the pressure on United.

Five league matches remained. Liverpool went on to win all five, finishing with 86 points, four behind the champions. This draw was not the moment the title became mathematically unreachable, but it was the last time Liverpool dropped points in a challenge that ultimately fell short.
