Shay Given kept Liverpool waiting for half an hour at St James’ Park. Once Steven Gerrard beat him, the league leaders went on to score five, closing their 2008 fixtures with a victory that left them three points clear at the top by the end of the day.

The 5–1 result on 28 December reflected Liverpool’s repeated opportunities in front of goal. Given saved from Dirk Kuyt, Gerrard and Lucas Leiva before the breakthrough, preventing Newcastle from falling behind considerably earlier.

Gerrard opened the scoring in the 31st minute, finishing Yossi Benayoun’s cut-back with a shot that went in off a post. Five minutes later the captain supplied the corner from which Sami Hyypiä headed Liverpool’s second.

Newcastle recovered a foothold just before the break. David Edgar headed in a corner to make it 2–1, giving the home side an interval scoreline that left the match open despite Liverpool’s chances.

Ryan Babel removed much of that uncertainty soon after the restart. Newcastle failed to clear another corner and Babel converted from close range, restoring the two-goal advantage. Gerrard then ran on to Lucas’s through-ball and lifted his finish over Given for his second goal and Liverpool’s fourth.

The final goal came from the penalty spot. Edgar brought down substitute David Ngog, and Xabi Alonso converted to complete a scoring sequence shared by four Liverpool players. Given’s earlier interventions had delayed the visitors, but could not contain them over the whole match.

Chelsea subsequently drew 2–2 at Fulham, with Clint Dempsey’s late equaliser preventing Liverpool’s nearest challengers from matching their victory. Liverpool therefore ended the day, and the calendar year, three points clear.
