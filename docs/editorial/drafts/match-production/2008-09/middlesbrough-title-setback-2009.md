Three days after winning at Real Madrid, Liverpool lost 2–0 to a Middlesbrough side that had gone 14 league matches without a victory. The defeat at the Riverside on 28 February 2009 left them seven points behind Manchester United, having played a game more than the leaders.

Liverpool had opportunities to make the afternoon develop differently. Brad Jones stopped early efforts from Nabil El Zhar and Dirk Kuyt, while Middlesbrough threatened through Gary O’Neil, whose attempt required a save from Pepe Reina.

The opening goal came from a corner. Stewart Downing’s delivery reached Xabi Alonso, whose attempted intervention diverted the ball into his own net. Middlesbrough had ended a long league scoring drought as well as taken the lead against one of the title contenders.

Liverpool still had time to repair the damage. El Zhar had an important opportunity just after the interval but failed to convert it. Instead of an equaliser, the next goal belonged to the home side: Jérémie Aliadière delivered from the right and Tuncay finished to make it 2–0.

That second goal gave Middlesbrough the margin they needed to end their winless run. For Liverpool, an away match that offered the chance to maintain pressure on United had produced neither a goal nor a point.

The setback did not end the title challenge. Liverpool would lose no further league game that season and eventually finished four points behind United. It did, however, increase the work required of them at the start of March. Their second and final league defeat of the campaign came against opponents fighting at the other end of the table, immediately after one of their most valuable European away victories.
