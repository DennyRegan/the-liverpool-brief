Rafael Benítez’s first eight league meetings with Manchester United had yielded one draw and seven defeats. On 13 September 2008, Liverpool finally ended that sequence, recovering from an early setback to win 2–1 at Anfield.

The defending champions went ahead after only three minutes. Dimitar Berbatov, making his United debut, reached the byline and found Carlos Tevez, whose finish gave Liverpool another deficit to overcome against these opponents.

Liverpool’s equaliser owed much to an error by Edwin van der Sar. Xabi Alonso’s effort took a deflection off Patrice Evra; the goalkeeper came forward but pushed the ball against Wes Brown, and it rolled into the unguarded net. Brown’s own goal restored parity before the interval.

The second half brought opportunities for Liverpool to take the lead. Robbie Keane narrowly failed to connect with Javier Mascherano’s ball across goal, while United retained a threat of their own: Pepe Reina had to save a dipping volley from Ryan Giggs.

Benítez’s changes included the introduction of Steven Gerrard and Ryan Babel. It was Babel who settled the match. Mascherano kept the attack alive near the byline, Dirk Kuyt supplied the pass and the substitute finished in front of the Kop.

United’s attempt to recover ended with another setback when Nemanja Vidić received a second yellow card after a challenge on Alonso. Liverpool held their 2–1 advantage, turning a match that had begun with a United goal into the first league victory of Benítez’s tenure against the champions.
