Robbie Fowler scored in each half as Liverpool beat Manchester United 2–0 at Anfield on 17 December 1995. A free-kick just before the interval broke the deadlock; his late second goal settled a match in which Peter Schmeichel had repeatedly kept Liverpool out.

Stan Collymore had several opportunities before Fowler opened the scoring. Schmeichel denied him after passes from John Barnes and Rob Jones, ensuring that Liverpool's pressure did not immediately produce a goal.

The breakthrough followed Lee Sharpe's foul on Barnes outside the penalty area. Fowler curled the resulting free-kick into the top corner, giving Roy Evans's side a 1–0 half-time lead. It was the first of two finishes that would account for all the afternoon's scoring.

Alex Ferguson replaced Andy Cole with Paul Scholes early in the second half. Liverpool continued to threaten, and Collymore struck the angle of post and crossbar. United also created chances: David James saved Eric Cantona's volley, and Ryan Giggs missed the target from a promising position.

The score remained 1–0 until the closing minutes. Steve McManaman carried the ball forward and found Fowler, who moved inside David Beckham before lifting his finish past Schmeichel. Liverpool now had the second goal their earlier chances had failed to produce.

The win took them to 31 points from 18 league games, four behind United. Fowler had also scored both Liverpool goals in October's 2–2 draw at Old Trafford. Across the season's two league meetings he had therefore scored four times, helping Liverpool take four points from their rivals.
