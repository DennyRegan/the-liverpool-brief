## Conflicts, exclusions and copy audit

11v11 credits Udo Onwere at 60 minutes; LFChistory credits substitute Sean Farrell at 62. The British Soccer Week narrative explicitly describes Farrell scoring shortly after entering. **Low confidence, corroboration only** — a contemporary 23 September 1993 Morgunblaðið results listing returned in retrieved search text also names Farrell, at 63 minutes: [archived page](https://timarit.is/page/1792568). Scope is that brief indexed results text only, not the original page image, which was not inspected. Farrell is retained on the fuller British Soccer Week and LFChistory evidence; his exact minute is omitted.

Rush’s opener is 19 minutes in the statistical records and 17 in British Soccer Week. Its minute is omitted. Clough is the credited scorer despite the reported Pike touch; do not reclassify it as an own goal. No official assist statistic is asserted: the article describes Fowler’s involvement. The late finish is independently corroborated by the club. No footage was watched and no quotation, tactical formation, weather claim or invented reaction is used. The conclusion distinguishes this debut from the later five-goal match without making it a second report of that event.
