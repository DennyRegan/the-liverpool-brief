## Whole-season baseline checked

Both full result sequences were read. The 60 fixtures comprise 42 First Division, eight FA Cup, nine European Cup and one Charity Shield match; no League Cup game. Liverpool finished seventh on 44 points: 17 wins, ten draws and 15 defeats, goals 67–73. Home: 12 wins, five draws, four defeats, 42–33; away: five wins, five draws, 11 defeats, 25–40. Manchester United and Leeds both finished on 61 points, United ahead on goal average. Chelsea were third on 56. These are final standings, not positions carried backwards to a cup match.

The Shield against West Ham at Anfield on 15 August ended 2–2. European competition began away at Reykjavík on 17 August, 5–0. The league opener was Arsenal at Anfield on 22 August, 3–2. The existing 14 September Reykjavík report covers the 6–1 return and 11–1 aggregate; it must not be duplicated. A 0–4 home derby defeat on 19 September left Liverpool twenty-first in the recorded match-day table; the next game was a 5–1 home win over Aston Villa. Later wins at Burnley on 5 December (5–1), Sunderland on Boxing Day (3–2) and Blackpool on 2 January (3–2) are genuine distinct fixtures, not the prior season's high-scoring games.

FA Cup route: West Brom away 2–1 on 9 January; Stockport home 1–1 on 30 January and away replay 2–0 on 3 February; Bolton away 1–0 on 20 February; Leicester away 0–0 on 6 March and home replay 1–0 on 10 March; Chelsea at Villa Park 2–0 on 27 March; Leeds at Wembley 2–1 on 1 May. The match-level extra-time/scorer/action claims remain to be independently checked before final draft review.

European route after Reykjavík: Anderlecht home 3–0 on 25 November and away 1–0 on 16 December; Cologne away 0–0 on 10 February and home 0–0 on 17 March, followed by the 2–2 Rotterdam play-off on 24 March; Inter home 3–1 on 4 May and away 0–3 on 12 May, elimination 3–4 on aggregate. The drawn Cologne result must not be described as an ordinary match victory; qualification mechanism requires its own source. The league programme ended at Wolves on 26 April, before the FA Cup final and both Inter legs.

Hunt's season total is 37, not his league-only 25. St John's 11 comprise four league, two FA Cup and five Europe. Callaghan scored eight; Milne and Thompson six each; Wallace, Stevenson and Graham five each; Smith four; Strong three; Lawler, Chisnall and Byrne two each; Arrowsmith, Ferns, Sealey and Yeats one each. Smith's 25 league, eight cup and seven European appearances establish a regular role this season but are not his career debut. Strong's 16 comprise 13 league, one cup and two Europe. No appearance/goal milestones are inferred from a signing date.
