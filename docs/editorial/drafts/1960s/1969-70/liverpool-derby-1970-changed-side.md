Liverpool made four changes from the side beaten at Watford, but Derby County won 2–0 at Anfield on 28 February 1970. Terry Hennessey and John O’Hare scored as Derby completed a league double over Liverpool.

Ray Clemence, Tommy Smith, Doug Livermore and Peter Thompson came into the starting eleven. Tommy Lawrence, Ron Yeats, Ian Ross and Ian St John were the four players from the Watford line-up who did not start. The comparison shows a substantial change in personnel within the space of a week.

Clemence was making his second league appearance, following his debut at Nottingham Forest on 31 January. Hennessey put Derby ahead after 14 minutes, and the visitors carried that 1–0 lead into half-time.

O’Hare added Derby’s second after 71 minutes. Liverpool did not score, leaving them with a second successive defeat after the FA Cup exit. The result also followed Derby’s 4–0 victory at the Baseball Ground in November.

Peter Wall started for Liverpool in what proved his final competitive appearance for the club. His first-team record ended at 42 matches. Alongside Clemence’s early league involvement, Wall’s final outing gives the team sheet another place in the chronology of changing personnel.

Liverpool were ninth after the defeat, with 36 points from 30 matches and games in hand on several clubs above them. A changed side had not brought an immediate result: Derby left Anfield with two points and two unanswered goals.
