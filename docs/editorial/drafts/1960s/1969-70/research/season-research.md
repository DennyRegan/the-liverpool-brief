## Whole season before selection

Liverpool played 54 competitive matches: 42 First Division, six FA Cup, two League Cup and four Inter-Cities Fairs Cup. They finished fifth with 51 points, from 20 wins, 11 draws and 11 defeats, scoring 65 and conceding 42. Everton won the league with 66, followed by Leeds (57), Chelsea (55) and Derby (53). Liverpool's previous runner-up season had yielded 61 points and 24 conceded. Graham and Lawler appeared in all 54 competitive matches and every league game. The club's independently retrieved Graham obituary confirms 21 goals, his most productive season; the full season goalscorer page has repeatedly failed retrieval and its competition splits remain unverified for prose.

Four opening league wins brought twelve goals, including victories over City both home and away. Liverpool were briefly first in September before their first defeat at Old Trafford. The ten-goal European home win over Dundalk, on Lindsay's competitive debut, was followed by a 4–0 away victory. City then eliminated Liverpool in the League Cup third round. A late equaliser at West Brom accompanied Lloyd's competitive debut. League form became uneven, including Derby's 4–0 November win. A 3–2 home victory over Vitória Setúbal could not repair a first-leg 1–0 defeat: 3–3 on aggregate meant elimination on away goals.

A three-goal derby win at Goodison in December was followed immediately by United winning 4–1 at Anfield. Hunt made his final Liverpool appearance as a substitute in the latter and departed for Bolton in December. The FA Cup route began with a draw at Coventry and a 3–0 home replay win, followed by 3–1 against Wrexham. A goalless home fifth-round match against Leicester was settled by substitute Evans's two goals in the away replay. Second Division Watford then won the quarter-final 1–0, Liverpool's final cup exit.

The changing team is important but requires precise chronology. Clemence's FIRST LEAGUE appearance was at Forest on 31 January, NOT the Derby home match after Watford. Lawrence returned for the February FA Cup ties. Clemence resumed league duty against Derby on 28 February and was the goalkeeper for the remaining league programme. League appearance totals are Lawrence 28, Clemence 14; competitive totals Lawrence 37, Clemence 17. Lloyd played nine competitive matches and Lindsay seven. Neither had played competitively in their 1968–69 arrival season. Some older players continued thereafter: Watford was not Lawrence's final appearance (April 1971), and Hunt had already left before that cup defeat. Official retrospective accounts identify Watford as Shankly's turning point; any such conclusion in prose must be attributed rather than made to erase the earlier recruitment and appearances.

Everton won the return derby 2–0 at Anfield in March. Liverpool's final sequence included wins over Ipswich, Wolves, Palace and Sunderland but ended with defeat at Chelsea. Fifth place reflects a season of uneven league form and early cup disappointment, rather than a late championship decision.
