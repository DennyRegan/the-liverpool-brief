Liverpool beat Vitória Setúbal 3–2 at Anfield on 26 November 1969 but were eliminated from the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup on away goals. Roger Hunt’s late winner brought the aggregate score level at 3–3, leaving the Portuguese club’s two goals at Anfield decisive.

Liverpool had lost the first leg 1–0. Vagner’s goal after 23 minutes in the return therefore increased their aggregate deficit to two, and Vitória led 1–0 at half-time.

Bill Shankly made two changes at the interval, sending on Alun Evans for Bobby Graham and Hunt for Steve Peplow. Liverpool then fell further behind when Geoff Strong’s own goal after 56 minutes made it 2–0 on the night and 3–0 across the tie.

Tommy Smith began the recovery with a penalty after an hour. Evans scored after 88 minutes and Hunt added Liverpool’s third after 90, completing a turnaround from a two-goal deficit to a one-goal victory in the match.

The recovery could not undo the away-goals disadvantage. Liverpool had scored none in Portugal, while Vitória had scored twice in England. Three goals apiece across the two matches consequently sent Vitória through and ended Liverpool’s European campaign.

The final goal was also Hunt’s last for Liverpool. Peplow’s first-half appearance proved his final competitive outing for the club, ending a first-team record of three matches. Both milestones sit within an unusual result: an Anfield win that still meant elimination.
