Ray Clemence made his first league appearance for Liverpool on 31 January 1970, when Nottingham Forest won 1–0 at the City Ground. Jimmy Richardson scored the only goal in the first half, leaving Liverpool behind at both half-time and full-time.

The match was a league debut rather than Clemence’s first competitive outing. He had already played in the League Cup in 1968, both Fairs Cup matches against Dundalk in September 1969 and the FA Cup win over Wrexham on 24 January 1970.

Liverpool stood fourth in the table after the defeat, with 35 points from 28 league matches.

Lawrence also played in the February FA Cup matches against Leicester City and Watford. Clemence’s second league appearance followed against Derby County at Anfield on 28 February. The January debut therefore did not immediately end Lawrence’s involvement.

Liverpool’s official Lawrence profile places his final appearance in April 1971. The Forest match marks Clemence’s first league game, while the subsequent records show the change of goalkeeper unfolding across more than one fixture.
