Liverpool suffered their heaviest away league defeat of 1969–70 when Derby County won 4–0 at the Baseball Ground on 1 November. Two goals in successive early minutes established a lead that the newly promoted side extended after the interval.

John McGovern scored after 13 minutes and Kevin Hector added another after 14. Those goals put Liverpool two behind before a quarter of an hour had elapsed. There was no further scoring before half-time, leaving Derby with a 2–0 advantage.

Hector scored his second after 54 minutes, taking Derby’s lead to three. John O’Hare supplied the fourth after 69. Liverpool did not score, and the final margin exceeded every other away league defeat they sustained that season.

Derby had reached the First Division as the previous season’s Second Division champions. The result therefore brought a substantial win for a promoted side against the club that had finished runners-up in the top flight in 1968–69.

Liverpool remained third in the table after the match, with 24 points from 18 games. Everton had 32 points from the same number of fixtures, leaving an eight-point gap under the two-points-for-a-win system. Derby were fourth, a point behind Liverpool.

The clubs’ final positions would reverse that immediate order. Derby finished fourth with 53 points, while Liverpool ended fifth on 51. The November meeting was the first of Derby’s two league victories over Liverpool; they later won 2–0 at Anfield in February.
