Alun Evans scored twice as Liverpool beat Leicester City 2–0 at Filbert Street in an FA Cup fifth-round replay on 11 February 1970. He had begun the match as a substitute, entering after Peter Thompson was injured.

The original tie at Anfield four days earlier had finished goalless. Leicester were playing in the Second Division, and the replay offered both clubs another chance to reach the quarter-finals after neither had scored in the first meeting.

Evans replaced Thompson after 21 minutes. Liverpool again reached half-time without a goal, with Tommy Lawrence in their starting side as goalkeeper. The replay remained level until Evans put Liverpool ahead after 64 minutes.

His second goal came after 90 minutes, completing the 2–0 victory. In the account accompanying his later LFChistory interview, the opening goal is described as coming from a near-post pass by Ian Callaghan. The same article records Evans’s recovery after an assault in December, placing the replay double within his return to the side.

Liverpool’s FA Cup route had already required one replay, against Coventry City in the third round. They then beat Wrexham before the two Leicester matches. Evans’s goals resolved another drawn tie and took Liverpool into the last eight.

The next opponent was Watford, away at Vicarage Road. After the replay win over Leicester, the quarter-final would bring a fresh single-match test against another club from the Second Division.
