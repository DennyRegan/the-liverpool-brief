Roger Hunt scored all three Liverpool goals in a 3–1 victory at Tottenham Hotspur on Good Friday, 27 March 1964. His hat-trick gave Liverpool the first of three Easter wins that would strengthen their position in the championship race.

White Hart Lane had been the scene of a 7–2 Liverpool defeat the previous Easter. This time Liverpool kept the scoring under their control. Hunt opened the scoring before the half-hour and the visitors preserved the lead until the interval.

The decisive burst came after the hour. Hunt scored his second with Ian St John involved, then completed the hat-trick three minutes later after a contribution from Ian Callaghan. Tottenham were three goals behind with twenty minutes remaining.

Maurice Norman replied late for Spurs, but the goal did not threaten the outcome. Liverpool had won away against a direct rival and moved to 46 points from 34 matches. Everton stood one point ahead, having played two games more; Tottenham had 44 points from 35.

There was little time to pause. Liverpool won 2–0 at Leicester the next day, then beat Tottenham 3–1 again at Anfield on Easter Monday. The following Saturday, Manchester United were also beaten as the winning run continued.

The sequence would reach seven successive league victories and finish with the title secured against Arsenal. At White Hart Lane, Hunt’s three goals had settled the first major away test of that Easter programme and put the previous year’s defeat firmly behind Liverpool.
