Tony Hateley scored his first three Liverpool goals in a 6–0 victory over Newcastle United at Anfield on 26 August 1967. In his third league appearance since arriving from Chelsea, the club’s record signing shared five goals with Roger Hunt, while Emlyn Hughes scored his first for Liverpool.

Bill Shankly had paid £96,000 for Hateley during the summer. The opening fixtures had brought a goalless draw at Manchester City and a 2–0 home win against Arsenal, both goals scored by Hunt. Newcastle therefore met a Liverpool attack in which the new centre-forward was still waiting to score.

That wait lasted eight minutes. Hateley put Liverpool ahead, Hughes doubled the advantage on the half-hour and Hunt added a third before the interval. Liverpool had established a 3–0 lead with three different scorers, and the second half brought the same number of goals.

Hateley scored again just after the restart. His third came with fifteen minutes remaining, completing the hat-trick, before Hunt supplied the sixth near the end. The two forwards had each scored on both sides of half-time; Hughes’s contribution completed an afternoon of personal landmarks.

Liverpool ended their first three league matches with five points and eight goals without reply. The six-goal win against Newcastle would remain a prominent early success in a season in which Hateley scored 27 times across all competitions. For Hughes, who had joined from Blackpool during the previous campaign, this was the first entry in a Liverpool scoring record that would eventually reach 49.
