George Best scored twice as Manchester United beat Liverpool 2–1 at Anfield on 11 November 1967 and moved above them at the top of the First Division. Roger Hunt’s late goal reduced the deficit but could not retrieve a point for Bill Shankly’s side.

Four days earlier Liverpool had scored eight against TSV 1860 Munich in the Fairs Cup. They began this league match with the same eleven players, but United established a very different scoreline. Best put the visitors ahead in the eighteenth minute and scored again five minutes before the interval.

Liverpool therefore faced a two-goal deficit at half-time. The score remained 0–2 through most of the second half, until Hunt struck in the 83rd minute. His goal left seven minutes to find an equaliser, but United retained their lead and took both points.

The result also has a place in Hunt’s scoring history. LFChistory’s corrected register identifies this as his 242nd Liverpool goal, taking him beyond Gordon Hodgson’s overall club record. The archive’s later research removed a goal wrongly credited to Hunt against Tottenham in 1966, changing the timing of the milestone. The late strike at Anfield consequently carries a significance that the defeat could not erase.

United finished the afternoon first with 22 points from sixteen games. Liverpool were second on 21, with Manchester City one point behind them. Shankly’s team had suffered their first home league defeat of the campaign: their previous seven Anfield league games had all been won.

The week had brought Liverpool a record European victory followed by the loss of the league lead. They remained close to the top, but this time Best’s first-half goals had decided the contest before Hunt could supply a response.
