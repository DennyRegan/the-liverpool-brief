## Conflicts and review cautions

- Newcastle, 26 August: season list ranks Liverpool second, while the match page table ranks them sixth. Exact early ranking is omitted unless the conflicting methods are explicitly explained; unbeaten opening three fixtures and 8–0 goal total are secure.
- United, 11 November: the corrected LFChistory register places Hunt's 242nd overall goal here. [The club profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/roger-hunt) still dates the milestone to the Munich double. Do not represent the correction as the undisputed contemporary celebration.
- [LFChistory correction research](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/3453), High for its explicitly documented reattribution of the March 1966 Spurs goal and consequential league milestone; not an independent second source to the same site's register.
- [Daily Express report, 8 November 1967, reproduced by LFChistory](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/3541), Medium: retrieved textual contemporary description of the Munich goal construction. Its minutes disagree with the ledger; do not silently combine clock times. Its statement that the tie was already settled is newspaper rhetoric, not the first-leg competition result. No long quotation or close paraphrase.
- [Official Tony Hateley profile](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/info/tony-hateley), High: third-game hat-trick, Walsall four, 16 league/eight FA Cup/three European goals in 1967–68. Career total differs from season total and must not be confused.
- Tottenham replay: Smith's initial penalty saved, retaken because Spurs had twelve players during substitution; Jones scored late. The ledger supports this incident but must not be embellished with an invented handball/foul.
- Old Trafford: Best 3, Yeats 9, Hunt 17; 1–2 half-time and final. Attendance conflict known from earlier evidence should be omitted.
- West Brom second replay: neutral Maine Road, not Anfield. Nine FA Cup games across four ties.
- Ferencváros: ledger 19th-minute goal, 46,892 crowd. Earlier Puskás evidence records 20 and 54,000. Minute and crowd are safely omitted pending source-specific treatment.
