## Season development and complete cup routes

The first three league games brought a draw at Manchester City and wins over Arsenal and Newcastle, eight goals without conceding. Liverpool spent much of autumn first in the season sequence, with the home derby victory maintaining that position after defeat at Southampton. The 11 November home defeat by Manchester United transferred the lead to the visitors, four days after the European eight. Liverpool did not return to first in the remaining recorded sequence.

The Fairs Cup began with Malmö beaten 2–0 away and 2–1 at home. The second round brought 8–0 at home to TSV 1860 Munich and a 1–2 away defeat,9–2 on aggregate. Ferencváros then won 1–0 in Budapest on 28 November and 1–0 at Anfield on 9 January, eliminating Liverpool 2–0. RSSSF records the Hungarian side’s eventual run to the final, where Leeds prevailed. The second-leg match report will distinguish contemporary action from later memories and will not invent a watched-film account.

The League Cup route was a 1–1 home draw with Bolton and a 3–2 replay defeat at Burnden Park. The FA Cup required a replay in every round: Bournemouth 0–0 away/4–1 home; Walsall 0–0 away/5–2 home; Tottenham 1–1 away/2–1 home; West Brom 0–0 away/1–1 home/1–2 at neutral Maine Road. Hateley scored four in the Walsall replay. Nine games took Liverpool only as far as the quarter-finals.

The April win at Old Trafford left Liverpool fourth on 45 points from 33 matches, two points behind Leeds and United, who had played 34 and 35 respectively. They remained in the contest, but the Easter home defeat by Sheffield United and subsequent loss at West Ham cost points. The 6–1 final home win against Forest moved them to third on 55 with one match left; City had finished on 58 and United on 56. Liverpool could still finish second, but lost 2–1 at Stoke four days later and remained third. The final-home report can convey the season outcome without automatically creating a separate final-day article.
