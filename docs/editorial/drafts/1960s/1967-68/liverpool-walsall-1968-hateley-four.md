Tony Hateley scored four times as Liverpool beat Walsall 5–2 in their FA Cup fourth-round replay at Anfield on 19 February 1968. Geoff Strong supplied the other Liverpool goal, and two late replies from Tommy Watson came after the home side had established a five-goal lead.

The first match at Fellows Park two days earlier had ended goalless. Liverpool had already needed a replay to beat Bournemouth and Boscombe Athletic in the third round, drawing 0–0 away before winning 4–1 at Anfield. Once again, a tie that had produced no goals on the opposition’s ground brought a substantial Liverpool total at home.

Bill Shankly retained the same eleven who had played at Walsall on the Saturday. Hateley broke the deadlock after 24 minutes and added his second nine minutes later. Strong scored almost immediately afterwards, taking Liverpool to a 3–0 half-time lead.

Hateley completed his hat-trick in the 64th minute and scored a fourth in the 71st. Liverpool now led 5–0, with all their goals coming from two players. Watson reduced the margin five minutes later and scored again near the end, making the final result 5–2 without changing which side would progress.

The four-goal performance was a major contribution to Hateley’s cup total. He finished the campaign with eight FA Cup goals, half of them scored in this replay, as part of a 27-goal first season at Liverpool.

Liverpool advanced to face Tottenham Hotspur in the fifth round. Their first two ties had both required second matches, but each replay had been won at Anfield; against Walsall, Hateley’s scoring had settled the issue well before the final whistle.
