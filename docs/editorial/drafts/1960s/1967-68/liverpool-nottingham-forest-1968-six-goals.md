Tony Hateley scored a hat-trick as Liverpool beat Nottingham Forest 6–1 at Anfield on 11 May 1968. Roger Hunt added two and Ian St John the other in the final home match of the season, after Forest had taken an early lead through Joe Baker.

Baker scored in the fourteenth minute, and Liverpool remained behind until St John equalised in the 34th. Hateley put the home side ahead two minutes later and scored again before the interval. In seven minutes Liverpool had changed a 0–1 deficit into a 3–1 lead.

Hunt extended the advantage ten minutes into the second half. His second came in the 71st minute, taking Liverpool to five, before Hateley completed his treble six minutes from the end. Five of Liverpool’s six goals had again come from Hunt and Hateley, as in the 6–0 win over Newcastle near the beginning of the campaign.

The result took Liverpool into third place with 55 points from 41 matches. Manchester City had completed their programme on 58 and Manchester United on 56. Liverpool could still finish second by winning their remaining match, away to Stoke City four days later.

That last game ended in a 2–1 defeat, leaving Liverpool third. The Forest victory had closed their Anfield league programme with seventeen wins, two draws and two defeats, and 51 goals scored against seventeen conceded. Their away record produced only five wins, nine draws and seven defeats.

Hateley finished his first Liverpool season with 27 goals in all competitions, while Hunt scored thirty. The final home afternoon had brought another hat-trick for the summer signing and a substantial winning margin. It also left a clear distinction in the season’s results: Liverpool had won far more often at Anfield than away, and their six goals against Forest could improve their placing without bringing the championship back within reach.
