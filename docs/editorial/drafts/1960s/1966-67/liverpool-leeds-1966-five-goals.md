Liverpool turned a narrow half-time lead into a 5–0 victory over Leeds United at Anfield on 19 November 1966. Geoff Strong scored twice in the closing quarter-hour as the champions completed a fourth successive league win and moved within a point of leaders Chelsea.

The run had begun with a 4–0 home victory over Nottingham Forest, followed by 2–0 wins against Burnley and Newcastle. Leeds arrived to face a Liverpool side whose November league results had begun to repair the inconsistency of the opening months.

For most of the first half the game remained goalless. Chris Lawler broke through shortly before the interval, giving Liverpool a 1–0 advantage. Peter Thompson added the second after the restart, and the score remained 2–0 until the final fifteen minutes.

Then the margin widened sharply. Strong made it three, Ian St John scored the fourth and Strong added his second just before the end. Liverpool had put four past Gary Sprake in the second half, with four different scorers contributing to the afternoon’s total.

Those four November victories had yielded thirteen goals without reply. Liverpool stood second on 23 points from seventeen games, one behind Chelsea; Leeds were eleventh. A difficult start to the title defence had become a sustained run of wins, and Liverpool were again close enough to challenge the leaders directly.
