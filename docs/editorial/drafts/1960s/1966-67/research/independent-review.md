## Independently checked season outline

Liverpool played 42 league, five European Cup, four FA Cup and one Charity Shield match. There was no League Cup participation. The league finish was fifth on 51 points, nine behind Manchester United; Nottingham Forest and Tottenham both had 56, separated by goal average, then Leeds 55. Liverpool's home record was 12W/7D/2L, 36–17, away 7W/6D/8L, 28–30. This is goal-average-era football; do not silently substitute goal difference as the ranking rule.

The Shield was won 1–0 at Goodison on 13 August. FA Cup: Watford away 0–0 on 28 January, Anfield replay 3–1 on 1 February, Aston Villa home 1–0 on 18 February, Everton away 0–1 on 11 March (fifth round). Europe: Petrolul home 2–0 on 28 September, away 1–3 on 12 October, neutral Heysel replay 2–0 on 19 October; Ajax away 1–5 on 7 December, home 2–2 on 14 December. Do not call the Petrolul play-off a deciding away leg or apply a modern away-goals result to its tied aggregate.
