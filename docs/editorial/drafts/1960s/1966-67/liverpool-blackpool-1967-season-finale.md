Liverpool’s league season ended with a 3–1 defeat by bottom-placed Blackpool at Anfield on 13 May 1967. All four goals came before half-time, and a late substitution marked both Gordon Milne’s final Liverpool appearance and Dave Wilson’s only one.

Blackpool were already bound for the Second Division, while Liverpool’s championship defence had subsided into a fifth-place finish. Shankly’s side had led the table after beating Stoke in early March, but had won only twice in the ten league matches since. There would be no third victory to close the campaign.

Ray Charnley put Blackpool ahead almost immediately. Peter Thompson equalised midway through the first half, but Liverpool’s recovery lasted only until Graham Oates restored the visitors’ lead. Jimmy Robson added a third soon afterwards, leaving the home side 3–1 down at the interval.

The second half brought no change to the score. With about ten minutes remaining, Wilson replaced Milne. Signed from Preston North End in February, the winger finally entered the first team on the last day of the league programme; he would never make another senior appearance for Liverpool.

Milne was also leaving, joining Blackpool that month after 282 Liverpool appearances. His career at Anfield had encompassed promotion and two league championships. He went off here against his next club, with another recent Blackpool arrival, Emlyn Hughes, already in Liverpool’s side.

The defeat left Liverpool on 51 points, nine behind champions Manchester United. From the final eleven league fixtures they had taken only eight points. Blackpool’s three first-half goals provided the last result in a title defence that had remained promising into March and then fallen away.
