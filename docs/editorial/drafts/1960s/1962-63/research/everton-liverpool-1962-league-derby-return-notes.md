## Claim-by-claim manuscript audit

1. Opening paragraph: correct date and 2–2 result follow the core match record and contemporary report. Official Liverpool retrospective corroborates eleven years since the previous league derby and Hunt’s late equaliser, but its headline date is rejected.

2. Paragraph 2: promotion and Morrissey’s August transfer follow season/transfer records. St John’s failed morning fitness test and Lewis at centre-forward are explicitly in Michael Charters’s contemporary report, attributed in the article.

3. Paragraph 3: Vernon penalty following a handball decision against Byrne, A’Court cross to Callaghan, return delivery and Lewis finish are in Charters. The article does not adjudicate the disputed handball or use conflicting minutes.

4. Paragraph 4: Vernon blocked by Yeats, Morrissey’s resulting shot, An attempted clearance and Howley’s goal award are in Charters. The defender is not identified because Yates gives a different account. No unsupported claim about exactly where the ball crossed is made.

5. Paragraph 5: A’Court cross, Lewis downward header and Hunt finish are supported by contemporary transcriptions, including the Daily Post. “Last minute” is sufficient precision.

6. Paragraph 6: Everton’s previous home winning start is established by the contemporary report. RSSSF confirms their subsequent unbeaten home league season and championship. This is league-specific, not all competitions.

## Conflicts, omissions and evidence limits

The official Liverpool retrospective headline and URL incorrectly say 16 September; contemporary newspaper dates and match record establish 22 September. LFChistory and contemporary transcription differ on Vernon/Lewis first-half minutes; neither is printed. Attendance variants are omitted. Charters identifies Byrne attempting the clearance from the line; Horace Yates’s 24 September account names Moran and describes a header. The manuscript therefore omits defender identity and clearance technique. The eleven-year gap is for league derbies only: an FA Cup meeting intervened in 1955.

Unreported tactical instructions, dressing-room dialogue, weather, crowd atmosphere, emotions, shot counts and visual details are UNVERIFIED unless specifically supported above; none has been invented. No video footage or newspaper-image scan was claimed as viewed. Source-derived paraphrase is kept within each retrieved source's word allowance, with no long direct quotations.
