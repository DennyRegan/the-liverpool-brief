## Claim-by-claim manuscript audit

1. Opening paragraph: defeat, date, opponent and Kevan as scorer have independent opposition corroboration. Melia’s missed penalty is recorded by LFChistory.

2. Paragraph 2: official Liverpool Lawrence profile supplies signing in 1957, five-year wait and Furnell injury. Appearance totals and full sequence confirm Furnell started the first 13 league matches; 11v11 confirms both Lawrence and Wallace debuts.

3. Paragraph 3: 17-minute goal and 19-minute saved penalty follow the LFChistory incident record. Tony is the familiar form of goalkeeper Anthony Millington. Neither cause of penalty nor saving technique is known from retrieved evidence.

4. Paragraph 4: core score progression establishes 1–0 at half-time and full-time. Full match sequence shows the immediately preceding game was Leicester away, 0–3, on 13 October. This was the second consecutive league blank, not the third.

5. Paragraph 5: 11v11 identifies Leishman’s final appearance. Transfer register confirms Stevenson’s October arrival and Leishman’s January move. Stevenson’s debut was the following week at Burnley, not at The Hawthorns.

6. Paragraph 6: official Lawrence profile says he kept his place; appearances register and match sequence corroborate 29 league and six cup games. The contrast with the immediate defeat is editorial interpretation of these facts.
