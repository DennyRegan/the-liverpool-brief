Liverpool went into Christmas with seven successive league victories after beating Blackburn Rovers 3–1 at Anfield on 22 December 1962. Kevin Lewis’s first-half goal was answered by Fred Pickering, but Ian St John and Ronnie Moran scored after the interval to complete another win.

The run had changed the shape of Liverpool’s return to the First Division. They had been twentieth after losing at home to Burnley in early November. A draw at Manchester United followed, then victories over Arsenal, Leyton Orient, Birmingham City, Fulham, Sheffield Wednesday and Blackpool. Blackburn were the seventh opponents to be beaten in succession.

Lewis opened the scoring after 14 minutes, with Ron Yeats involved in the goal. Pickering equalised before the half-hour, leaving the teams level at the interval. Blackburn had already won the first meeting at Ewood Park in August; Liverpool still had work to do to reverse that result.

St John restored the lead just after the hour, with Jimmy Melia contributing to the opening. Moran then made it 3–1 in the closing stages, after Willie Stevenson’s involvement. The goals came from three different players, and Liverpool finished the afternoon fifth with 26 points from 23 games.

There would be a long wait for the next league match. The severe winter disrupted football across the country, and Liverpool did not resume their league programme until 13 February. They did, however, play FA Cup ties at Wrexham and Burnley during January.

When league football returned to Anfield, the winning sequence continued: Aston Villa were beaten 4–0 and Wolves 4–1. Nine successive league victories had spanned the interruption before Leicester City brought the run to an end in March.
