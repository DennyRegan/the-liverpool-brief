Jimmy Greaves scored four goals as Tottenham Hotspur beat Liverpool 7–2 at White Hart Lane on Easter Monday, 15 April 1963. Only three days earlier Liverpool had defeated the same opponents 5–2 at Anfield; the return brought a much heavier result in Tottenham’s favour.

There had been another match between the two meetings. Liverpool beat Manchester United 1–0 on Saturday, while Tottenham drew 1–1 with Fulham. Monday completed a crowded holiday programme for both sides.

Greaves opened the scoring early, but Roger Hunt equalised midway through the first half. Liverpool stayed level until the closing stages before the break, when Cliff Jones restored Tottenham’s lead and Greaves converted a penalty. The home side went in 3–1 ahead.

Jones scored again soon after the restart to make it four. Hunt’s second goal reduced the deficit to 4–2 with twenty minutes left, but this time Liverpool could not mount the recovery they had produced at Anfield.

The final ten minutes made the defeat much worse. Frank Saul scored Tottenham’s fifth, then Greaves added two more to complete his four-goal afternoon. Hunt had scored twice and Liverpool still finished five goals behind.

Across the two Easter meetings the teams had shared 16 goals, with each winning emphatically at home. Tottenham eventually finished second in the league. Liverpool ended eighth, and their away record showed a large part of the gap: four wins, seven draws and ten defeats, compared with thirteen league victories at Anfield. White Hart Lane was the most severe of those away defeats.
