Peter Thompson scored the winner as Liverpool came from behind to beat reigning champions Manchester City 2–1 at Anfield on 10 August 1968. Bobby Graham’s first-half equaliser kept the opening match level until Thompson settled it with seventeen minutes remaining.

City had won the previous season’s championship, while Liverpool finished third, three points behind them. The new campaign therefore began with a direct meeting between the champions and one of the sides that had pursued them.

Neil Young put City ahead after seven minutes. Liverpool were still behind until the 24th minute, when Graham equalised. The teams reached half-time at 1–1, with the contest still undecided.

Thompson supplied the decisive third goal of the afternoon after 73 minutes. Young’s early advantage had become a home victory, and Liverpool began with the two points available for a league win.

The result did not immediately become a sustained run. Liverpool lost their next match at Southampton and later in August at Leeds. Their stronger sequence came in September and early October, when five successive league victories took them to the top. Against City, though, the first requirement of the season had been met: Liverpool recovered from an early deficit and beat the defending champions.
