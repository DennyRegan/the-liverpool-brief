Alun Evans scored twice against his former club as Liverpool beat Wolverhampton Wanderers 6–0 at Molineux on 28 September 1968. Roger Hunt and Peter Thompson also scored twice, with each of the three contributing a goal in each half.

Evans had left Wolves for Liverpool earlier in September and scored on his debut against Leicester a week before this return. His next league appearance brought him back to the ground he had just left, now as part of a Liverpool attack extending a run of victories.

Hunt opened the scoring after fifteen minutes. Thompson added the second after 25 and Evans made it three after half an hour. Liverpool went into the interval 3–0 ahead, with all three eventual double scorers already on the scoresheet.

Evans struck again after 63 minutes, Hunt after 74 and Thompson after 80. Six goals without reply made this Liverpool’s largest competitive win of the campaign and took them to second place.

Evans later recalled Liverpool supporters thanking Wolves for selling him, a memory that captured the immediate impact of his move. Across the Leicester and Wolves matches he had scored three times while Liverpool scored ten without conceding. A further 4–0 league win at Burnley would complete five successive victories and take Liverpool to the top.
