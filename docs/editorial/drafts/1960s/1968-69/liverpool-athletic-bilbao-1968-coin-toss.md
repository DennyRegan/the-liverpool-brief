Liverpool won 2–1 against Athletic Bilbao at Anfield on 2 October 1968 but were eliminated from the Inter-Cities Fairs Cup after losing a coin toss. Chris Lawler and Emlyn Hughes recovered a two-goal aggregate deficit late in normal time; extra time produced no further goal.

Athletic had won the first leg in Bilbao 2–1. Roger Hunt’s goal in that match had reduced Liverpool’s deficit, leaving a single goal between the teams before the return at Anfield.

The task became harder after 32 minutes, when Argoitia put Athletic ahead on the night. Liverpool now trailed 3–1 across the two matches. They remained behind at half-time and well into the second half, with the visiting lead still intact as the closing quarter-hour approached.

Lawler scored after 78 minutes to make it 1–1 on the night. Hughes added Liverpool’s second after 87, bringing the aggregate score to 3–3. Both sides had won their home leg 2–1, and another thirty minutes at Anfield failed to separate them.

The tie was decided by a coin toss, which Athletic won. The result removed Liverpool from Europe at the first round despite their recovery and match victory. Within days Liverpool would take the domestic league lead at Burnley, but their continental campaign had ended through the deciding procedure used for this tie.
