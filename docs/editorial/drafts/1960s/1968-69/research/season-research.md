## Season understood before selecting

Liverpool played 51 competitive matches: 42 league, four FA Cup, three League Cup and two Fairs Cup games. They finished second with 61 points from 25 wins, 11 draws and six defeats, scoring 63 and conceding a division-best 24. Leeds finished with 67 points. Liverpool lost only one league game at Anfield, to Nottingham Forest on 15 February. Lawrence, Lawler and Smith played all 42 league matches. Hunt led the all-competition scoring with 17 (13 league, one FA Cup, two League Cup, one Fairs Cup), followed by Callaghan on ten and Thompson on nine.

The opening win against reigning champions Manchester City was followed by an uneven August. Five successive league victories from 7 September to 5 October produced 18 unanswered goals and first place. Evans joined from Wolves for £100,000 in September, scored on debut against Leicester and twice in the 6–0 win at Molineux. European progress ended at the first hurdle when Athletic won a coin toss after both home sides won 2–1 and extra time at Anfield stayed goalless. Arsenal eliminated Liverpool in the League Cup fourth round on 15 October.

Liverpool repeatedly regained first place in October/November, then remained there from 9 November through their last January/first February fixtures. Forest's February win was followed by a draw at West Ham and an FA Cup replay defeat by Leicester after a 0–0 away match. Liverpool remained unbeaten in the league from that Forest defeat until the Leeds match: a 0–0 draw on 28 April secured the title for Leeds. Liverpool finished with a defeat at Manchester City and a draw at Newcastle, still second.

Changes were beginning without a wholesale new side: Evans played 39 competitive matches; Clemence made one competitive appearance, his debut in September. Lindsay arrived in March and Lloyd in April but neither appeared competitively this campaign. Hateley left for Coventry in September and Arrowsmith for Bury in December. These moves inform the assessment but do not themselves commission extra match reports.

## Conflicts and deliberate omissions

- A tentative selection label called Smith's October derby equaliser a penalty. The retrieved match timeline identifies a direct free kick; the label was corrected before manuscript drafting. Use the verified free kick, not the tentative label.
- A tentative Forest label called the goals late. Lyons scored after five minutes and again in the second half; it was corrected before drafting. LFChistory gives the second at 62 and Forest specialist at 67. Omit its exact minute.
- Athletic's official account accidentally calls the first-leg side Zurich and gives the wrong manager. It gives Lawler 77 rather than LFChistory/RSSSF 78. Omit those errors and peripheral toss mechanics; describe only corroborated outcomes, with any memory clearly attributed.
- The debut retrospective gives Smith's penalty a minute after Yeats, whereas core gives minutes two and four. Use broad early chronology and preserve all four goals inside twelve minutes. It also calls Leicester 16th (apparently pre-match) versus 21st post-match; unnecessary position omitted.
- Liverpool's Evans profile calls him 19 on arrival; precise age is omitted, retaining teenager and £100,000 only where corroborated. The article's account of every subsequent league appearance is corroborated by the appearance table.
- LCFC's Shilton career article says Lochhead scored late after the penalty; this conflicts with its detailed replay history and LFChistory. The detailed history and core agree goal 34, penalty 40. Follow the corroborated sequence and record the discarded account, without importing its chronology.
- Hunt's record was believed at the time to have fallen against Chelsea on 18 January because a prior goal was misattributed. Current club account and LFChistory corrected record agree 1 February. Distinguish this league record from his November 1967 overall club record; do not recreate an unverified reaction on the day.
- Crowd description, tactical intent, goal mechanics, direct quotes and saves are supplied only when an actually retrieved scoped source establishes them. No linked video has been watched and no footage claim is made.
