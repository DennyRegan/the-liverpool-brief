Roger Hunt scored twice as Liverpool beat Burnley 4–0 at Turf Moor on 5 October 1968 and moved to the top of the First Division. Peter Thompson and substitute Geoff Strong added second-half goals to complete a fifth successive league victory without conceding.

The run had begun with 2–0 wins over Queens Park Rangers and Ipswich Town. Liverpool then scored four against Leicester and six at Wolves. Burnley became the fifth opponent in a sequence that produced eighteen unanswered league goals.

Hunt gave Liverpool the lead after twenty minutes and scored again after 35. Liverpool reached half-time 2–0 ahead. Thompson, who had also scored twice at Molineux, added the third after 62 minutes.

Strong replaced Ian St John after 82 minutes and scored five minutes later. His contribution completed the 4–0 scoreline and extended a week in which Liverpool had scored freely in the league while suffering a very different outcome in Europe.

Three days earlier they had beaten Athletic Bilbao at Anfield but left the Fairs Cup after a drawn aggregate score, goalless extra time and a lost coin toss. The Burnley victory restored the focus to the championship. Liverpool, Leeds and Arsenal each had eighteen points, with Liverpool occupying first place. The next fixture was the Anfield derby, where a draw would end the winning sequence.
