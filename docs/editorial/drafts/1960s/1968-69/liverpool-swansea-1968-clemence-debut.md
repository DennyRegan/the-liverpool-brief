Ray Clemence began his competitive Liverpool career with a clean sheet in a 2–0 League Cup victory over Swansea Town at Anfield on 25 September 1968. Chris Lawler and Roger Hunt scored in the second half to send Liverpool through the third round.

Clemence had joined from Scunthorpe United in 1967, but Tommy Lawrence remained the regular first-team goalkeeper. The cup tie gave the younger keeper his first competitive appearance after that wait.

The opening half produced no goals. Lawler broke the deadlock after 52 minutes, and Hunt added Liverpool’s second after 85. Swansea were unable to score, so Clemence’s first appearance ended with both a victory and a clean sheet.

His own recollection makes clear that the evening was hardly an easy introduction. In a later interview, Clemence remembered heavy rain and a gale, and recalled struggling with his kicking in the conditions. That account adds a participant’s memory to the result without turning the clean sheet into a claim that every part of his debut went smoothly.

It remained his only competitive appearance of the season. Lawrence played all 42 league matches, and Clemence’s first league game did not come until January 1970. Liverpool’s immediate cup reward was a fourth-round tie at Arsenal, where their run ended the following month.
