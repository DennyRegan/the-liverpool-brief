## Season established before selection

Shankly managed the complete competitive campaign. Liverpool contested 42 Second Division games, two FA Cup games and three League Cup games. No European competition appears in the competitive register. The separate friendly/regional/testimonial list was not mixed with national competition.

The opening eight league fixtures brought two wins and four defeats. Recovery from September led to second place in November; Liverpool beat leaders Sheffield United at Anfield. The holiday period brought consecutive defeats at Rotherham and home to Middlesbrough, followed by defeat at Brighton in January. Four February league wins revived the challenge. March and April were less secure, including the Easter draw at Bramall Lane and defeat at Eastville. The last game was a 3–1 defeat at Stoke on 3 May.

RSSSF and LFChistory independently give third place: 52 points, behind Ipswich (59) and Sheffield United (58). Home league record: 14 wins, five draws, two defeats; away: seven wins, five draws, nine defeats. League goals: 87 for, 58 against. Do not assign modern three-point arithmetic.

Kevin Lewis led competitive scoring (22), followed by Roger Hunt (19) and Dave Hickson (17). Lewis’s league component was 19; Liverpool FC independently confirms both his seasonal totals and June 1960 arrival. Bert Slater and Dick White appeared in all 47 national competitive matches. Liddell played once; Milne 17 times; these were not yet the settled later championship teams.

The transfer ledger establishes August arrivals Milne and Arrowsmith and in-season exits Arnell and Harrower. St John’s 2 May 1961 signing precedes the last fixture but belongs to the next campaign editorially: he made no competitive appearance in this one. No transfer fee is needed in these reports. Liddell’s final competitive match and Milne’s debut coincide on 31 August.

Cup progression: Coventry beaten 3–2 before Sunderland won 2–0 at Anfield in the FA Cup fourth round. In the inaugural League Cup, Liverpool drew with Luton, won the replay 5–2, then lost 2–1 at home to Southampton. Neither cup run is omitted from season assessment.

## Selection and exclusions

Five matches are selected for distinct reasons, fully stated in `selections.json`: the Liddell/Milne transition, first League Cup tie, Harrower against the leaders, FA Cup elimination, and Lewis’s hat-trick in a damaging Easter defeat. This is an editorial selection after reviewing the whole season, not a quota.

The 20 August Leeds opener (Lewis’s scoring debut) was considered; Lewis is better served by the extraordinary Eastville performance and its consequences. The 24 October Luton replay is covered within the first-tie report rather than expanded into another article with overlapping significance. The Southampton League Cup exit is retained as consequential context. The Coventry third-round victory does not displace the more consequential elimination. The 5–0 Leyton Orient win and Hickson hat-trick were considered against the rival-specific importance of Harrower’s performance. Bramall Lane on 1 April is integral to the Easter account; without fuller retrieved action, it does not warrant a separate manuscript. The Stoke finale supplies the final outcome rather than a forced closing fixture article. Regional matches and Liddell’s testimonial remain distinct from this national-match selection.
