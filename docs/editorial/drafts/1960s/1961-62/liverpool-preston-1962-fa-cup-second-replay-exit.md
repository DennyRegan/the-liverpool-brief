Peter Thompson finally separated Liverpool and Preston North End at Old Trafford on 26 February 1962. His second-half goal won the FA Cup fifth-round second replay 1–0, ending a three-match contest in which Liverpool never scored.

The first meeting at Anfield on 17 February had finished goalless. The replay at Deepdale three days later remained 0–0 after extra time, taking the tie to a neutral ground. Before the teams began in Manchester, they had already played three and a half hours without a goal.

Liverpool kept the same eleven who had contested both earlier meetings. Bert Slater was in goal, Ron Yeats captained the side, and the attack again included Roger Hunt, Ian St John and Ian Callaghan. League scoring form had provided little comfort against this opposition: between the two replays, Liverpool had beaten Middlesbrough 5–1 at Anfield.

Old Trafford produced another goalless first half. The breakthrough came after the interval from Thompson, the Preston winger who would later join Liverpool. A Lancashire Evening Post retrospective describes Jim Smith sending the ball forward, Yeats heading it clear and Thompson driving the loose ball back past Slater.

Liverpool could not answer. Preston advanced to face Manchester United in the quarter-finals, while Shankly’s side returned to their pursuit of promotion. The league meetings told a different story: Liverpool won 3–1 at Deepdale and would beat Preston 4–1 at Anfield in March. Across the three cup ties, however, one Preston goal was enough.

Thompson’s performance had a longer consequence. Shankly brought him to Anfield in August 1963, and he became part of the team that won the league title in his first Liverpool season. On this snowy February evening, his contribution had ended their cup campaign.
