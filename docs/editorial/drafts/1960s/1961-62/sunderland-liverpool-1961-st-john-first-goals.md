Ian St John scored his first Liverpool league goals at Roker Park on 30 August 1961, sharing four with Roger Hunt in a 4–1 victory over Sunderland. Brian Clough’s reply before half-time briefly narrowed the gap, but Liverpool won their fourth successive match of the new season.

Sunderland had already lost 3–0 at Anfield a week earlier. Liverpool followed that with five goals against Leeds United, so the return fixture offered another examination of a side which had yet to concede. The forward line again included Kevin Lewis, Hunt, St John, Jimmy Melia and Alan A’Court.

Hunt opened the scoring midway through the first half. St John then added his first, with Melia involved, to put Liverpool two ahead. Clough scored on the stroke of half-time, leaving Sunderland only a goal behind at the interval and ending Liverpool’s run of clean sheets.

The next goal restored the visitors’ control of the score. Melia was again involved as Hunt made it 3–1 after the hour. St John completed the scoring at the end, this time from Lewis’s supply. Both central forwards had scored twice; their new partnership had produced all four Liverpool goals.

Four wins now brought eight points, with 14 goals scored and only Clough’s conceded. The two Sunderland matches accounted for seven of those goals. Liverpool would stretch their unbeaten opening to eleven league games before losing at Middlesbrough in October.

Sunderland eventually recovered to finish third, a point short of promotion. Liverpool finished champions, with Hunt contributing 41 league goals and St John 18. Their first shared double at Roker Park was an early demonstration of the scoring combination that carried the campaign.
