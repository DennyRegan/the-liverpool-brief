## Conflicts, omissions and limits

League debuts is deliberate: St John had played in a May 1961 Liverpool Senior Cup tie, so an unqualified first-ever Liverpool appearance would mislead. Yeats was not yet captain; White was. The archived newspaper-image thumbnail could not be inspected, so only the readable Yates transcription in the match page was used.
