## Whole campaign, before selection

Bill Shankly remained manager. Liverpool won the Second Division with 62 points from 42 matches: 27 wins, eight draws and seven defeats; 99 goals for, 43 against. Leyton Orient finished second on 54, Sunderland third on 53. Liverpool's home league record was 18 wins, three draws and no defeats (68–19 goals); away it was nine wins, five draws, seven defeats (31–24). LFChistory and RSSSF independently agree on the final table.

The full competitive sequence contains 42 league matches and five FA Cup matches, with no League Cup or European fixtures. The FA Cup route was Chelsea 4–3, Oldham 2–1, then Preston 0–0 at Anfield, 0–0 at Deepdale and 0–1 at Old Trafford in the fifth-round second replay. Liverpool began with ten wins and one draw in eleven league matches before losing 0–2 at Middlesbrough on 7 October. December ended with successive 0–1 defeats at Leeds and Rotherham. The next league match, after the Chelsea cup win, was the 5–4 Norwich match. A strong spring brought promotion against Southampton on 21 April with five league games remaining. The final match was a 2–4 defeat at Swansea; final home game a 2–1 win over Charlton.

Ron Yeats and Ian St John made league debuts at Bristol Rovers on 19 August. St John's first league goals came at Sunderland on 30 August. Dick White captained both of those early matches; do not assume Yeats immediately captained the side on arrival. Hunt scored 41 league goals and one in the FA Cup; St John 18 league and four cup goals. The official club retrospective independently confirms their competitive totals of 42 and 22. A'Court, Byrne, Melia and Milne appeared in all 47 competitive fixtures. The fresh appearance table records 17 players used. Bert Slater played 29 league matches and five cup ties; Jim Furnell, signed from Burnley on 23 February, played the final 13 league games. Tommy Lawrence did not make a competitive appearance this season. Transfer fees are unnecessary to the reports and Furnell's fee is omitted because older contextual research reports competing figures.
