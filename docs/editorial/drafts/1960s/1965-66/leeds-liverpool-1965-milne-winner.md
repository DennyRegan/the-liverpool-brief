There was only a day to wait for Liverpool’s response. Beaten 1–0 by Leeds United at Anfield on 27 December 1965, they won by the same score at Elland Road on the 28th, Gordon Milne scoring early in the second half.

Peter Lorimer’s goal had ended Liverpool’s nine-match unbeaten league run in the first meeting. Bill Shankly sent out the same starting eleven for the return, with Geoff Strong again on the bench. Leeds made a change: Paul Madeley, who had replaced Johnny Giles at Anfield, started in his place.

The first half brought no goal. Liverpool had been behind from the fourteenth minute the previous afternoon; this time the match reached the interval level. Three minutes after the restart, Milne scored, with Roger Hunt involved in creating the opening.

That goal was enough. Liverpool kept a clean sheet through the rest of the half and took both points from the second of the back-to-back meetings. The Christmas fixtures had produced one win apiece, each secured away from home.

The table gave the away victory its immediate value. Liverpool finished the day with 34 points from 24 matches, two ahead of Burnley, who had played one fewer. Leeds had 27 points and four games in hand on Liverpool, so the gap did not remove them from the title race. Milne’s winner had, however, prevented them taking all four points from the two meetings.
