The 53 recorded official fixtures comprise 42 league, nine European Cup Winners' Cup, one FA Cup and one Charity Shield match; no League Cup fixture. Liverpool finished first with 26 wins, nine draws and seven defeats, 79–34 and 61 points. Home: 17 wins, two draws, two defeats, 52–15; away: nine wins, seven draws, five defeats, 27–19. Leeds finished six points behind, ahead of Burnley on goal average. The championship is not to be described as decided by goal difference.

The complete sequences agree. Liverpool began by sharing the Shield at Old Trafford, 2–2 on 14 August, then won the league opener 3–1 at Leicester on 21 August. The 5–1 away win at West Ham on 6 September is distinct from the 1–1 home draw on 15 September. Everton were beaten 5–0 at Anfield on 25 September; the Goodison return on 19 March was goalless. November included 5–0 Northampton and 5–2 Blackburn wins; the recorded first-place position begins with the 17 November Blackburn match. Leeds won at Anfield on 27 December, Liverpool won at Elland Road the following day. The New Year home Manchester United win was 2–1. The final league fixture was 1–1 at Forest on 10 May, after the European final, not before it.

The FA Cup defence lasted one match: a 1–2 Anfield third-round defeat by Chelsea on 22 January 1966. The distinct league home meeting on 30 April ended 2–1 and sealed the championship. European route: Juventus away 0–1 on 29 September, home 2–0 on 13 October; Standard Liège home 3–1 on 1 December, away 2–1 on 15 December; Honvéd away 0–0 on 1 March, home 2–0 on 8 March; Celtic away 0–1 on 14 April, home 2–0 on 19 April; Dortmund final at Hampden on 5 May, 1–2 after extra time. Aggregate wins were 2–1, 5–2, 2–0 and 2–1 respectively. This is the Cup Winners' Cup, distinct from the previous season's European Cup.

Byrne, Callaghan, Lawrence, Smith and Yeats played all 53; St John and Stevenson 52; Lawler and Thompson 50; Hunt 46; Milne 36; Strong 27; Arrowsmith six; Chisnall one European appearance; Graham one league appearance. Consequently the fourteen-player claim is league-specific, not the entire season. No new first-team debut is inferred from Ogston's signing: he does not appear in this season's participation table. The all-competition 94 is not the league total.
