Two goals in five first-half minutes overturned Juventus’s lead at Anfield on 13 October 1965. Chris Lawler and Geoff Strong scored in Liverpool’s 2–0 win, sending Bill Shankly’s side through the opening tie of the European Cup Winners’ Cup, 2–1 on aggregate.

Juventus had won the first leg in Turin through Gianfranco Leoncini. Liverpool therefore began a goal behind, but the players who removed that deficit were Lawler at right-back and Strong, selected in Gordon Milne’s place.

The first goal came from a Willie Stevenson free-kick. Strong rose to turn the delivery across the goalmouth, where Lawler headed past Roberto Anzolin from close range. The aggregate scores were level before twenty minutes had passed.

Strong then scored himself. Dave Horridge’s contemporary Daily Mirror report traced the opening to a mistake by Luis del Sol, followed by Lawler’s header to Ian Callaghan. Callaghan crossed, Sandro Salvadore’s attempted clearance fell to Strong, and his shot from outside the penalty area struck the underside of the bar on its way in.

Liverpool had moved from needing a goal to defending an aggregate advantage. At half-time they led 2–0 on the night, with both goals coming from players whose duties also included protecting Tommy Lawrence.

Juventus still had the second half to respond. Giampaolo Menichelli tested Lawrence soon after the restart, but the goalkeeper held his shot. Liverpool conceded no goal, preserving the margin created by that brief first-half burst.

Their previous European campaign had ended against Internazionale in the semi-final. The new competition had immediately brought another Italian opponent and another away deficit, but this time Liverpool finished the tie at Anfield with a place in the next round.
