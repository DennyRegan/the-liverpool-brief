Liverpool went top of the First Division with a 5–2 win over Blackburn Rovers at Anfield on 17 November 1965. It was their fourth successive league victory, and the lead they took that night would remain theirs for the rest of the season.

The run had begun with a 4–0 home win against Nottingham Forest, followed by 2–0 at Sheffield Wednesday and five without reply against Northampton. Blackburn now faced a side that had scored eleven league goals without conceding in its previous three matches.

Willie Stevenson put Liverpool ahead from the penalty spot after seventeen minutes. Ian St John added the second just after the half-hour, and Roger Hunt scored immediately before the break. At 3–0, Liverpool had again established a substantial lead before their opponents had scored.

Blackburn changed their attack at half-time, Ben Anderson replacing Mike England. George Jones pulled a goal back after the hour, leaving Liverpool two clear with much of the second half still to play.

The home side settled the result in the closing minutes. Tommy Smith made it 4–1 and St John added his second, before Anderson scored Blackburn’s final consolation. Liverpool’s five goals had come from four different players, with St John completing the evening’s only double.

The win took Liverpool to 24 points from seventeen games, one ahead of Burnley, who had played sixteen. Leeds were another point behind, also with a game in hand. The advantage was narrow and the challengers had matches to recover it, but Liverpool’s sequence of four wins had changed the order at the top.
