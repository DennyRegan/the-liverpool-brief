# Barnes and Nicol score twice as Liverpool hit seven at Derby

Liverpool returned to the top of the First Division with a 7–1 win at Derby County on 23 March 1991. John Barnes and Steve Nicol scored twice each, while Jan Mølby, Ian Rush and Ray Houghton also found the net at the Baseball Ground.

The opening exchanges gave little indication of the eventual margin. Mølby put Liverpool ahead from the penalty spot after Mark Wright fouled Rush, but Dean Saunders equalised with another penalty after Mike Hooper brought down Ted McMinn. Derby had restored parity before Liverpool’s attack took control.

Barnes put the visitors back in front, following up after Peter Shilton saved from Rush. Before the interval, a move involving David Burrows, Barnes and Peter Beardsley ended with Rush scoring Liverpool’s third. Ronnie Moran’s side went in 3–1 ahead.

Barnes added another soon after the restart, finishing from Beardsley’s pass. Nicol then scored twice in seven minutes, taking the score to 6–1 by the midway point of the second half. Barnes helped create both of those goals as Liverpool repeatedly opened up the Derby defence.

Houghton completed the scoring at the end of the match after a Barnes backheel. Five different players had scored, and Liverpool had added four goals after half-time without conceding again.

The win had an immediate effect on the title race. Arsenal drew 0–0 at Norwich City, leaving Liverpool a point ahead with both clubs having played 29 matches. The six-goal margin also cut Arsenal’s advantage on goal difference to four.

It was Liverpool’s third consecutive league victory under Moran, following wins over Manchester City and Sunderland. Less than a month after Dalglish’s departure, the caretaker had brought them back to first place. Derby, meanwhile, remained bottom after an afternoon in which their early equaliser had been followed by six unanswered goals.
