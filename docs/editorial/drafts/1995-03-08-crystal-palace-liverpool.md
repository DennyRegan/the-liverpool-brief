Robbie Fowler scored in both legs of Liverpool's League Cup semi-final against Crystal Palace. His goal at Selhurst Park on 8 March 1995 completed a 2-0 aggregate win and booked a Wembley final against Bolton Wanderers.

Liverpool arrived with the narrow advantage Fowler had given them at Anfield three weeks earlier. Palace, missing Chris Armstrong and Iain Dowie, used Chris Coleman alongside Andy Preece in attack. Both tested David James early in the second leg, but the goal that changed the tie came at the other end.

Steve McManaman put Fowler through down the left channel. The striker moved beyond Palace's defensive line, drew Nigel Martyn and slipped his finish across the goalkeeper. Liverpool were ahead after 27 minutes and two clear on aggregate.

Fowler celebrated by taking off his shirt and putting it back on the wrong way round, displaying his name and number across his chest.

Palace could not find a reply. Liverpool kept a clean sheet for the second time in the tie, and Roy Evans made no substitutions as his starting eleven saw out the 1-0 victory.

The final against Bolton was set for 2 April, giving Evans the chance to win his first trophy as Liverpool manager.
