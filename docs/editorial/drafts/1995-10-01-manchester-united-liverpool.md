Robbie Fowler scored twice at Old Trafford on 1 October 1995, turning an early Manchester United lead into a Liverpool advantage. Eric Cantona's second-half penalty then made it 2–2 on his return from suspension.

United went ahead in the second minute. Cantona received Andy Cole's pass on the left and crossed for Nicky Butt, who finished past David James. Liverpool had been behind almost from the start, but Fowler's two goals would change the course of the match.

Roy Evans had selected Ian Rush alongside Fowler, with Stan Collymore on the bench. Michael Thomas and Steve McManaman also started, and both were involved in the goals that took Liverpool into the lead.

Fowler equalised before half-time, coming in from the left and beating Peter Schmeichel with a powerful shot. Early in the second half, Thomas played him through again. Fowler held off Gary Neville and lifted the ball over the advancing goalkeeper. Liverpool now led 2–1.

United's equaliser came after Jamie Redknapp challenged Ryan Giggs in the penalty area. Referee David Elleray awarded a penalty, and Cantona sent James the wrong way to score in the 71st minute. His comeback had produced a goal as well as his part in the opening move.

The draw left Liverpool on 16 points from eight league matches, one behind United. Neither side had held its lead. Cantona's return was the occasion's central story, but Fowler had scored both Liverpool goals and given them the advantage for part of the second half. The visitors left Old Trafford with a point.
