## Fact-check and exclusions

The club and independent specialist agree on the score and scorer distribution; the database independently matches the club’s scoring order and penalty-miss note. The club says the penalty hit the crossbar; the draft uses that detail with its scope recorded. No penalty minute is asserted. Goal minutes retained in the copy come from LFChistory; the club independently confirms the early two goals and broad order. No footage or original newspaper was inspected. No invented descriptions of individual finishes, crowd reactions, quotations or managerial intent.

The 10–0 result is called Liverpool’s biggest domestic winning margin, not its biggest win in all competitions: the 11–0 Strømsgodset result is explicitly distinguished. This is a historical statement, not a claim to have exhaustively audited every result through publication day. No claim that Liverpool won the 1986–87 League Cup is made.
