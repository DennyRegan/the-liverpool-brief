# Liverpool 0–0 Nottingham Forest: the European defence ends

Liverpool surrendered the European Cup without scoring in either leg of their first-round tie against Nottingham Forest. The goalless draw at Anfield on 27 September 1978 left Bob Paisley's side beaten 2–0 on aggregate and ended their pursuit of a third consecutive title.

Garry Birtles and Colin Barrett had established Forest's advantage at the City Ground a fortnight earlier. Liverpool now needed at least two goals, but Peter Shilton and the defence in front of him protected the lead through another 90 minutes.

Paisley kept the starting eleven from the first leg. Kenny Dalglish, Steve Heighway, Jimmy Case and Terry McDermott were again among those asked to break down Forest. Brian Clough's principal change was at left-back, where Frank Clark took Barrett's place; Larry Lloyd and Kenny Burns remained in the defence with Viv Anderson.

Liverpool were still goalless at the interval. In the second half Paisley brought on David Fairclough and David Johnson for Case and McDermott. The additional forwards could not produce the breakthrough, and an attendance of 51,679 saw Forest complete a second clean sheet against the holders.

Liverpool's campaign was over after two matches. Forest advanced to face AEK Athens and eventually won the competition, beating Malmö in the final. The side that had taken Liverpool's English championship in 1978 would succeed them as European champions in 1979.
