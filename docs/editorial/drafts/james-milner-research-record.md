## Conflicts, exclusions and limits

- LFChistory's prose says 42 appearances in 2015–16; its season table says 45 (28 league + 1 FA Cup + 4 League Cup + 12 Europe). The article avoids that season total. Eight table totals sum to 332.
- LFChistory lists eight European assists in 2017–18; UEFA explicitly credits nine in the Champions League proper. The article attributes nine to UEFA, rather than presenting differing providers as identical definitions. No speculative explanation of the difference is supplied.
- Liverpool's overall goals and league goals are explicitly separated. Shoot-out kicks are not added to career goal totals.
- The final source record uses current retirement evidence. Old profiles ending at Brighton's 2025–26 contract are not treated as current playing status.
- Honours are named individually. No ambiguous claim of seven “major trophies” is made; Community Shield counting is therefore immaterial.
- No assertion that Milner scored in the FA Cup final shoot-out is needed. The retrieved FA report directly establishes his open-play contribution.
- Evidence is primarily official, supplemented by an independent specialist ledger and Reuters. Official praise is used only as attributed testimony where relevant. Current records would need rechecking if publication is delayed substantially.
