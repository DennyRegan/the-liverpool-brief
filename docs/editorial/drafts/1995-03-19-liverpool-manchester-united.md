Liverpool stopped a damaging run at Anfield by beating Manchester United 2-0 on 19 March 1995. Defeats by Tottenham Hotspur in the FA Cup and Coventry City in the league had preceded the visit of Alex Ferguson's side; Jamie Redknapp's opening goal helped Roy Evans's team avoid a third home defeat in succession.

Evans had to reorganise his defence because Rob Jones was suspended. Mark Wright came into the side and John Scales moved to the right, where he faced Ryan Giggs.

Redknapp gave Liverpool the lead after 25 minutes. He went past Denis Irwin and shot through Gary Pallister's legs, beating Peter Schmeichel from inside the penalty area. Schmeichel subsequently saved from Steve McManaman to keep United within one goal at half-time.

United sent on Andy Cole at the interval, but Liverpool retained their advantage. Giggs shot narrowly over after Mark Hughes headed the ball back to him, and David James saved another effort from Hughes.

The second goal came late. Michael Thomas crossed from the right and McManaman's shot deflected off Steve Bruce, wrong-footing Schmeichel. Recorded as an own goal, it secured Liverpool's points.

The result also mattered at Ewood Park. Kenny Dalglish's Blackburn Rovers were now six points ahead of United, with both teams having eight league matches left. Liverpool remained fourth, and would meet Blackburn themselves on the final day.
