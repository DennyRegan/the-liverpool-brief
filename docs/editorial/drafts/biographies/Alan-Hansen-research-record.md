- S3 describes Hansen scoring the 1981 League Cup final winner; this is not used. No claim that he played every winning League Cup final.

- S1's 1977–1990 heading denotes playing appearances, not formal retirement. Draft distinguishes them.
- No direct quotation is necessary. No current-health statement, unsupported superlative, invented scene or supporter reaction is included.
