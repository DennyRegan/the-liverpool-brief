## Decisions before drafting

- Career centre: a local defender connecting Shankly’s first FA Cup team, his rebuilt side and Paisley’s European champions. Explain football ability through the 1974 passing contribution as well as the 1977 header.
- Separate joining the club in 1960 from first-team debut in 1963. Four league titles are 1966, 1973, 1976, 1977; do not award him the 1964 title, in which he made no first-team appearance.
- T1/T11 and T3 credit the 1978 European Cup and 1977 Super Cup; T2/T5 use an older nine-major-trophy summary. Avoid a single medal total. Describe 1978 contribution and absence from final distinctly.
- T1 says ‘By the end of 1977’ immediately before presenting the May 1977 final as still to come. Correct chronological order independently, never reproduce that sentence.
- T4 calls Dalglish a team-mate in the 1977 final. False: the actual XI and Dalglish’s own account show he arrived afterwards. Exclude.
- T6 gives an age inconsistent with the birth/debut dates and places the 1973 walkout at Anfield; T3 places the Arsenal fixture at Highbury. Use no stadium for the dispute and no disputed debut-age claim beyond the arithmetic checked against dates.
- T15 marks Smith’s goal 65 minutes, T2 says 64. Use ‘after the hour’ rather than manufacture a reconciliation. Its photograph caption also mislabels the season; not used.
- Briefly include lost captaincy and 1971 defeat. Do not embellish personal feud, alleged match-fixing conversation, threats or opponents’ motives. No quote folklore, weight/height or dismissal total is needed.
- England limited to one cap; no speculative explanation for selection. Later illness stated only as reported. No medical causal claim about heading or tackling.
- Source-use allocation: public draft synthesises records across distinct pages, ordinarily below 150 words attributable to any one 200-word source; Guardian pieces used for checking, not extended paraphrase. No direct quotations intended.

The separate factual audit is to be written only after reading the finished manuscript afresh.

Audit follow-up: another worker flagged Hughes’s captaincy chronology; this worker independently opened T17. Public prose now separates the 1973 succession from the Arsenal disagreement, with no claim that one caused the other. Exact timing remains unresolved and is excluded.
