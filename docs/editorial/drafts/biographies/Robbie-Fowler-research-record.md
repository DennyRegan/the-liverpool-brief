## Conflicts, exclusions and statistical scope

- Hat-trick: S02 says 4:35; Premier League S06 and the newer club account S03 agree on 4:33. The competition’s record takes precedence.
- Final 1996–97 seasonal total must not be described as achieved before Fowler’s 22nd birthday. The draft uses the completed three-season span instead.
- The dockers shirt is described as displayed against Brann in 1997; no unsupported exact fine or display date is added.
- The 1999 sanctions are separated by incident. No allegation of actual drug use is made.
- S11 contains intermediate career statistics; these are not used as lifetime totals.
- S15’s historical all-time scoring rank is not presented as a current ranking.
- Liverpool totals include competitive first-team games, excluding friendlies and testimonials. League goals are separately labelled.
- No second-spell FA Cup winner’s medal or Champions League win is attributed to Fowler. The piece discusses his demonstrated participation in the 2001 treble rather than assigning every team honour during his employment to him.
- Research only corroborated essential later playing clubs; current coaching and ambassadorial roles were intentionally left outside this playing-career piece.
- No unresolved low-confidence claim remains in the manuscript.
