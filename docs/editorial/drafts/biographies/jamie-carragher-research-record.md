## Conflicts and exclusions identified before drafting

- C3 erroneously dates the Villa first start 18 January 1998; C4 and the official profile place it ten days after the 8 January 1997 debut. Use 18 January 1997 after separate match-record confirmation.
- C3 places Euro 2004 in Holland and Belgium. This is an editorial error; exclude that location and do not use this page alone for tournament details.
- C3 says six months lost to the 2003 injury whereas C6 says four. Avoid a precise duration pending a return-match cross-check.
- Five Liverpool goals are confirmed by the official profile and the current LFChistory career table. Use that verified total.
- No invented dialogue, private motives, or recalled quotations. Direct quotations, if used, must remain within the source's 25-word allowance.
