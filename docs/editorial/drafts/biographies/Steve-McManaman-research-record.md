### Conflicts and exclusions

- **1991–92 goals:** S2’s prose says ten; its table lists 5 league + 3 FA Cup + 3 League Cup = 11. S9 independently states eleven. Draft uses eleven. Confidence: high.
- **Appearance scope:** 364 is all competitive Liverpool games; 272 is league games, including the pre-Premier League First Division. Neither is a friendlies-inclusive total.
- **Additional inspected source:** [Liverpool FC, Three things we learned from Steve McManaman on We are Liverpool podcast, 22 March 2023](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/three-things-we-learned-steve-mcmanaman-we-are-liverpool-podcast). Confidence: high for directly attributed recollections; low for its unqualified description of 272 as first-team appearances. That total is the league figure. Source not used in final copy.
- **Madrid totals:** S2’s prose differs from the official Madrid totals. The biography does not need those numbers, so they are omitted; S14 would take precedence for Madrid competitive statistics.
- **Celtic report provenance:** S11 is labelled Guardian Service. It is one report, not independent evidence from two newspapers. The separate match record corroborates the event details.
- **Celtic timing:** Narrative says late rather than reproducing the report’s slightly inconsistent minute descriptions; S12 records 89 minutes.
- **Assists:** Historical providers may differ in definition. The 1995–96 figure is expressly credited to Liverpool’s retrospective; no claim of a uniform cross-era assist measure.
- No direct quotations appear in the biography. No private thoughts, atmosphere or invented supporter reactions are supplied. The departure reaction is supported by S1.
- No unresolved low-confidence material appears as fact in the final prose. Current employment and other ongoing activities are outside this playing-career biography.
