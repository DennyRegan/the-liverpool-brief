- F1/F6 associate Jim Beglin with Fagan's purchases without a reliable distinction between coaching influence and formal managerial signing. Beglin purchase attribution omitted.

Manuscript uses the uncontested 1958 arrival/reserve role, Shankly/Paisley continuity and F2's explicit 1979 assistant promotion. No exact first-team-coach promotion date asserted.
