## Conflicts resolved and material omitted

- UEFA's departure article gives 14 league goals, while LFChistory gives 15. The official LFC and archive agree on **19 total goals**. The draft uses the verified total and omits the disputed league split.
- Older LFC text gives 66 FA Cup final minutes and LFChistory prose 67. The final-minute claim is omitted.
- Newcastle long-shot yardage varies between sources. “From inside his own half” is supported and used; exact distances are omitted.
- Madrid fee was officially undisclosed. Estimated €35m/approximately £30m figures are unnecessary and omitted.
- No “best midfield in the world” objective ranking; no speculative psychological account of leaving; no quotation taken from an unviewed video/podcast; no claims about live managerial status in October 2026.
- The Fulham article's misleading anniversary wording was not adopted as match date. The independent Sky Chelsea page is truncated later by the publisher; only fully visible paragraphs are relied on.
