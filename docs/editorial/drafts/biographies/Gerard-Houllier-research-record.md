## Conflicts and exclusions

- Trophy totals vary by convention. The UEFA obituary says eight Liverpool trophies, which is not supported by the itemised club record. This number is excluded. The biography names the 2001 treble, the two August 2001 honours and the 2003 League Cup separately.
- Managerial totals: LFC lists 325 including 18 jointly with Evans; LFCHistory lists 307 for the sole-manager tenure. These are compatible definitions. No ambiguous career match total is inserted into the biography.
- The sole-tenure statistics include the period when Thompson deputised. They must not be described as matches in which Houllier personally occupied the dugout.
- S09’s page header shows 14 October 2014, while its text identifies the captaincy announcement as 15 October 2003. The biography uses only the month and year.
- S02 contains an unrelated assertion about foreign-player restrictions during the Celta Vigo tie that was not independently verified, plus speculation about the effect of illness on subsequent management. Neither is used.
- S14’s reproduced report refers to a Wednesday afternoon despite its index identifying the match as 19 March 2002. The weekday is excluded; the biography uses March 2002 only.
- No transfer fees, medical diagnosis, verbatim quotations, imagined thoughts or unsupported supporter reactions are included.
- Club publications and LFC.tv articles reproduced by LFCHistory are not counted as independent evidence from one another. The research also uses LFCHistory’s own records and UEFA’s competition reporting.
- No unresolved claim is retained as fact in the publishable text. Excluded uncertainties are not obstacles to the narrower wording used.
