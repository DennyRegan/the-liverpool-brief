## Discrepancies and exclusions

- Transfer fee: S2 gives £180,000; S3 and Guardian give £200,000. Exact fee UNVERIFIED; omitted.
- Diagnosis date: S2 narrative gives November 1986, while S11 and Guardian give 1984. Exact date UNVERIFIED; omitted. No retrospective medical explanation of individual performances is asserted.
- Liverpool’s profile heading ends his years in 1981, while its own text confirms January 1982 departure. Use 1974–1982 for tenure; last appearance was in 1981.
- S2 includes the 1982 League Cup in its honours list; S1 lists 1981 only. Article states the documented 1981 success and does not claim a second League Cup medal.
- S2’s later-club narrative contains internally inconsistent dates. No precise later-club years used.
- Medal-sale dates conflict. Omitted, as are unsupported assumptions about the cause of death.
- S7 refers to three points for a 1976 win. That incidental error is not repeated; the game was played under two points for a win.
- Historical assist totals are unnecessary to the argument and not used. The debut pass is identified in S4’s match record.
- No quotation from an autobiography or historical newspaper has been represented as directly inspected. No low-confidence material appears in the biography.
