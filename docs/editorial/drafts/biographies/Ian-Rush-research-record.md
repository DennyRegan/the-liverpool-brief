## Discrepancies and exclusions

- LFCHistory’s prose calls Rush 19 when bought in April 1980. Its own birth and joining dates make him 18. Use 18.
- The return fee appears as £2.7m in S11 and £2.8m in S2/S8. UNVERIFIED exact amount: omitted.
- Juventus totals vary with competition classification, including the qualification play-off. S2 gives 14 overall/eight league; S11 quotes Rush describing 13 league-and-cup goals. No Italian total is used.
- S8 incorrectly states 49 goals in 1983–84. S1/S2/S7 agree on 47; use 47. Its 1985–86 total also differs from S2 and is not used.
- S8 dates Hillsborough to 16 April. That erroneous detail is not used. The biography gives only the verified relationship between the disaster and the subsequent final.
- S8 repeats the supposed “foreign country” quotation. Not independently verified and not used.
- S8 and S2 differ on time out after the knee operation. State the operation without a duration.
- The historical record-breaking goal is described as Rush’s 287th in Liverpool’s 18 October 2023 article, [On this day: Ian Rush becomes Liverpool’s record goalscorer](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/day-ian-rush-becomes-liverpools-record-goalscorer), also retrieved on 25 September 2026. S3 gives Hunt’s recognised total as 285. Confidence: high for date and opponent; unresolved historical counting convention. Do not state a threshold goal number; October 1992 is corroborated across the inspected sources.
- European Cup honours lists include 1981, but a squad honour must not be presented as a final appearance. Biography centres on verified 1984 final participation and does not imply he played in the 1981 final.
- UEFA’s 2016 claim that he remained Wales’s record scorer is superseded by the FAW account. The final piece explicitly dates Bale’s change to 2018.
- S8’s wider errors reduce its confidence. Its retained claims were checked against other retrieved sources, not accepted because of the site’s reputation.
- No direct quotations appear in the finished piece. No motivations, dressing-room scenes, crowd reactions or personal thoughts have been invented.
