## Conflicts and exclusions resolved before drafting

- F05 prose says first league start at Huddersfield on 19 October. F09 contemporary official report explicitly calls it a substitute appearance (the Saturday was 20 October 2018). Do not repeat archive prose's first-start/date claim. Competitive debut and first **overall** start separately verified F07/F08.
- F05 biography loosely gives São Paulo birth while its personal table and F01 give **Campinas**. Use Campinas. No conflation of city and state.
- Honour accounting: F03 names five major honours (Premier League, Champions League, FA Cup, League Cup, UEFA Super Cup). No Club World Cup credited to Fabinho simply because Liverpool won it while he was employed; he was injured then. Community Shield is excluded from the **major** five rather than denying it existed. FA Cup winner in 2022 does not mean FA Cup final player: F27 confirms absence; F28 proves return to start the European final.
- Announcement 28 May 2018 versus registration/join 1 July 2018 distinguished; no claim he played for Liverpool in the 2018 final.
- Transfer fees vary (£39m/£40m/base+add-ons/£43.7m) and official announcements do not disclose a precise sum. Omit fees in both directions; no money-driven/private motivation asserted.
- No current club, total international caps, health explanation for decline, irreversible physical decline or intention to exit asserted. F30 records his May 2023 expectation of another Liverpool pre-season; later transfer is a subsequent event, not proof he lied.
- **UNVERIFIED/omitted:** single-player solution to 2020–21 collapse, best defensive midfielder objective global ranking, guessed tackle chart values, hidden medical causes for 2022–23 form, contract/private-board motives, universal supporter sentiment, unviewed videos, imagined dialogue.
- Reopened Super Cup and 2019 final reports as team-role checks; the Super Cup report shows Fabinho's lofted involvement in the equaliser but this scene is not necessary to manuscript. G19/G20 shared Wijnaldum title/final-day reports are not needed for Fabinho prose, avoiding unnecessary shared derivation.
