## Decisions and conflicts

- Liverpool Senior Cup debut and conventional competitive/league debut are distinguished. The local hat-trick is not added to recognised career totals.
- League goals are explicitly distinguished from all-competition totals. No false contradiction between the current club biography and its 2026 retrospective.
- J2's narrative says Motherwell lasted four years before Portsmouth. J3/J9, the employer's own histories, put the spell at little more than a year. Use the club histories; no 1977 departure assertion.
- Sources differ over Motherwell signing/first-team chronology. No exact recruitment year asserted; childhood attendance, league record and international emergence suffice.
- No precise duration of the Hibernian hat-trick, reconstructed transfer-room dialogue, Salvation Army poster legend, claim of permanent personal estrangement from Shankly, or psychological motive.
- J4 is an oral-history interview, used as attributed self-description rather than as unquestionable evidence of every remembered incident. It supplies no sensational altercation reconstruction.
- No historical assist aggregate: early assists are retrospectively reconstructed and not necessary to establish the creative partnership.
- Publication date differs from death date; the announcement is not treated as the death occurring on 2 March.
- No claim that the FA Cup final was the whole career, or that all success was caused by the two Scottish recruits. Other players' contribution retained.
