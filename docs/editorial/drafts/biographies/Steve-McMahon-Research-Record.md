## Conflicts and excluded material

- S1’s “23 games” in 1985–86 means league appearances, not the full competitive total. S2 separates 23 league from 36 total; S3 independently gives 36 total and ten goals. Draft uses the distinction.
- S1’s “15 games” before the 1991 departure likewise describes league appearances. S2 records 24 competitive matches that season. Draft does not repeat 15 as the total.
- S2 narrative says November 1991 departure; its own structured record and S1 give 24 December 1991. Use 24 December.
- Everton-to-Villa fees differ: S1 £175,000; S2 £300,000; S4 £250,000. UNVERIFIED — omitted. Liverpool’s £350,000 fee is separately corroborated.
- S2’s Arsenal anecdote implies a direct pass to Aldridge. S9 and contemporary S10 include Beardsley; S9 specifies his saved shot. Draft follows that sequence without awarding McMahon the final assist. The recovery itself is supported across accounts. No video viewing is claimed.
- S2 and S4 disagree about the season of an Everton supporters’ award. UNVERIFIED — omitted.
- S16 initially discusses a suspected broken leg. S2’s retrospective account identifies a knee injury and season-ending absence. Draft uses the retrospective outcome, not the preliminary suspicion.
- [Liverpool FC’s England legacy-cap article, 18 March 2026](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/former-reds-receive-england-legacy-caps-special-anfield-presentation) was inspected (confidence: medium). Its 1982 start date conflicts with S2’s 1988 senior debut and S4’s 1988–90 senior span; 1982 may conflate youth and senior representation. It is not a source for the final article.
- [LFChistory: McMahon once a Blue, always a Red](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/1126) was inspected (confidence: medium). Retrospective quotations lack sufficiently clear original publication details for this draft; no quotation used.
- [John Martin, LFChistory: Four LFC Macs](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/2096) was inspected (confidence: medium). Its Arsenal description repeats the simplified direct-to-Aldridge account; not treated as independent corroboration or cited in final copy.
- The former members.liverpoolfc.com profile redirected to the homepage. It is not cited; the working /info/steve-mcmahon page was inspected instead.
- No invented dialogue, personal motivation, dressing-room claim, crowd reaction, or present-day employment claim used.
