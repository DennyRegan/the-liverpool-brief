## Conflicts and decisions

- **84 goals, not inherited 83:** the current official June 2026 profile and LFCHistory's indexed table agree on 178 appearances and 84 goals. Several older articles use 83, sometimes with 180 appearances. The final manuscript follows the current official/indexed record, not the older prose.
- **1947–48 league scoring:** the current table and June 2026 official article support 25 league goals. The evergreen official biography and some older retellings repeat 24. The manuscript uses 25 and distinguishes league from all-competition appearances and goals throughout.
- **Transfer fee:** evergreen Liverpool gives £12,500, while the updated official profile and the contemporary Daily Mail give £13,000. The manuscript uses the latter as the club-record fee. It does not call this a British transfer record: contemporary coverage identifies a higher previous transfer elsewhere.
- **Cinema and coin:** the Daily Mail describes efforts to locate him and a message received after reaching home, while later narratives simplify the episode into an on-screen announcement. The manuscript says messages were sent to cinemas and he was at the cinema while sought. The first-person coin account determines which club he heard first; it is not represented as a binding random choice of employer.
- **Contract:** Stubbins's later first-person explanation concerns McConnell's writing arrangement. An obituary offers a different account involving residence. The draft attributes the explanation and the amicable characterisation to Stubbins, and does not assert a full independently reconstructed contractual history. Injury-related absence is retained separately.
- **Title timing:** victory at Wolves put Liverpool in position to win the title; Stoke's defeat on 14 June confirmed it. The manuscript distinguishes these events and checks the final seven **league** games separately from Cup ties.
- **Last match and departure:** the indexed match is 3 January 1953; 1952 in old obituary wording is not followed. Joining Ashington later in 1953 is distinct from his final Liverpool appearance.

## Unverified or deliberately omitted issues

- **UNVERIFIED:** exact birth date. Retrieved sources disagree on day and, in the Guardian, year. Wallsend is consistent and sufficient for this Liverpool-first story.
- **UNVERIFIED:** one definitive wartime goal total; several totals cover unclear or different competition scopes. The manuscript includes reputation and experience without merging unofficial and Football League records.
- **UNVERIFIED:** exact year of the early Newcastle registration; retrospective and indexed accounts differ. The necessary pre-war background is retained without that year.
- **UNVERIFIED:** a United States national-team coaching post. Retrieved accounts also identify club coaching in New York. The manuscript uses the supported location and coaching work, without naming a national appointment or asserting a date.
- **UNVERIFIED:** who specifically chose the photograph for the Beatles cover. Only the image's appearance and Stubbins's reported permission are used. No claim about McCartney's or Lennon's motive is included.
- Anonymous threatening-message anecdotes before Huddersfield, exact knee-wound counts, disputed birth-age calculations and counterfactual claims that he alone kept Paisley at Liverpool were omitted.
