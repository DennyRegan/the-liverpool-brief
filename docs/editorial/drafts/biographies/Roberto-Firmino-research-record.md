## Resolved discrepancies and exclusions

- LFChistory's narrative says 26 goals in 2017–18; Liverpool's official retrospective and profile give 27. Use 27. UEFA gives ten in the Champions League main competition; Liverpool's eleven European goals includes qualifying. Do not call all eleven main-competition goals.
- LFChistory's narrative calls the August 2022 Bournemouth performance a hat-trick. Liverpool's official account identifies a brace. Use two goals.
- LFChistory's narrative calls the 36 league appearances in 2020–21 starts. Its table is an appearance table. That starts claim is excluded.
- June 2015 announcement and July completion are different milestones. The biography says an agreement was announced in June; it does not claim registration was completed then.
- Six major honours excludes the Community Shield. The biography names particular honours and makes no claim that he played in every winning final.
- Al-Ahli goal totals differ between LFChistory and Al Sadd. UNVERIFIED: definitive all-competition Al-Ahli goal total. Omitted as unnecessary to this Liverpool biography.
- The Premier League player-statistics URL redirected to a page whose retrieved body lacked its statistics. It was not used to substantiate a claim. LFChistory's explicit statistical tables and Liverpool's totals were used instead.
- No quotations, private thoughts, invented crowd reactions or speculative motivations appear in the biography.
