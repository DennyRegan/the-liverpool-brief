## Conflicts, exclusions and evidence limits

- **European Cup 1981:** S1’s honours panel includes 1981, but its appearance table shows no European appearances that season; S2 lists 1984 only. The copy states his 1984 final participation and does not assign him two European Cup victories. No inference about medal possession.
- **FA Cup 1992:** A Liverpool honour during his career is not proof he played in the final. The copy identifies his semi-final contribution and final absence.
- **Southend:** Some retrospective profiles collapse joining and becoming manager into 1994. S10’s first-person chronology and S14’s contemporary report distinguish 1994 player arrival from 1995 managerial appointment. Corrected in copy.
- **Hansen in the 1989 final:** [Liverpool FC, Kop 10 cup lifting captains](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/116643-kop-10-cup-lifting-captains), retrieved 25 September 2026, misleadingly refers to Hansen’s absence. S13’s team sheet confirms he played. Confidence: high for Whelan lifting the cup; low for the absence claim. Rejected that detail.
- **Hillsborough captaincy:** S10’s narrative calls Whelan captain on the day. This was not needed for the biography and was not carried over. His own testimony about the aftermath is retained and attributed. Exact match captaincy left outside the copy rather than relying on inconsistent summaries.
- **This Is Anfield totals:** S11 says 61 appearances in his final four seasons. S1’s season totals add to 71 (17 + 13 + 18 + 23). Neither combined figure is needed in the copy; use the season table if subsequently adding one.
- S11’s Southend timing is also compressed. The article is not treated as uniformly reliable or as independent confirmation of copied errors.
- The older official player profile and title-timeline pages redirected to the Liverpool homepage on opening, so they are not cited in the biography. An attempted 1992 match URL failed; no content from that failed retrieval is claimed as inspected.
- No direct quotations appear in the finished biography. Attributed recollections are paraphrased. No invented motives, scenes, crowd reactions or interior thoughts.
- No unresolved or low-confidence factual material is retained as fact in the finished copy. Detailed contract negotiations remain a personal recollection, explicitly framed as such.
