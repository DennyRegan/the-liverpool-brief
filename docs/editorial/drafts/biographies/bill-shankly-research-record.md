## Conflicts and omissions

- Do not quote a total of managerial matches: current LFC/LFChistory show 784; a retrieved [LFC 2019 overview](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/features/374756-60-years-ago-today-bill-shankly-s-liverpool-revolution-begins) gives 783. Appointment, retirement and ceremonial Shield participation are distinguished.
- LFC profile says seven Scotland caps; Scottish museum and sportscotland give five. Exact cap total omitted.
- S8 compresses the departures into a post-Watford account although Hunt left in December 1969. Manuscript explicitly corrects that sequence.
- No assertion that the 1974 final was his final competitive match: S24 records Spurs four days later. No unqualified attribution of the August Shield trophy to Shankly’s managerial tenure.
- Refereeing corruption allegations in the specialist profile are not established as fact and are omitted. Likewise precise medical claims about training preventing illness/injury.
- All-red first-night sock detail is disputed in an identified press lead; rather than resolve it by assumption, the article makes only the securely supported change-of-strip claim. No exact first date asserted.
- A source calls Byrne’s 1965 injury a dislocated shoulder. Injury not needed here and omitted.
- BBC article retrieval failed (robots); legacy LFC timeline URLs redirected to the homepage. Neither is counted as retrieved evidence for this manuscript.
- No invented dialogue; no direct quotations in manuscript. Retirement motives are restricted to recorded public/family explanations, not speculative psychological diagnosis.
