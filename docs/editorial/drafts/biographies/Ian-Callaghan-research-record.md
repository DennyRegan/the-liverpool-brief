### Conflicts and exclusions

- S2 calls Callaghan 17 on his April 1960 debut. His birth date, match date and S3’s corrected account establish that he was 18. Final prose uses 18.
- S2 associates overtaking Liddell’s “492” appearances with 1973–74, mixing the league record with an unqualified appearance total. S1 describes the all-competition milestone in August 1972 against Liddell’s 534. The biography omits the record-breaking date rather than importing the ambiguity.
- S11 gives Liverpool service as starting in 1959 and misorders the Canberra spell in its introductory summary. Use S1/S4 for chronology. Canberra is omitted because other-club coverage is deliberately brief.
- S11 uses “unused substitute”/“bench” descriptions for the 1966 World Cup, when substitutions were not permitted. The article simply states his appearance and non-participation in knockout matches; no bench claim is used.
- The claim that the appearance record can never be broken is a prediction and is omitted.
- No direct quotation appears in the biography. The private contract offer is appropriately attributed to the 1979 recollection.
- No unresolved low-confidence claim is included as fact in the article. No transfer fee is asserted.
