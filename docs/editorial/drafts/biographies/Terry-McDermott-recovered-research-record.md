Corrections and exclusions:

- S1's narrative reverses the two 20-goal seasons. Its itemised lists establish 22 in 1980–81 and 20 in 1981–82.
- S1 incorrectly places the Everton chip in the replay. Excluded from the draft rather than adding another match digression.
- S2 lists five championship seasons but its prose calls 1981–82 the fourth title. Draft avoids a personal medal total, explaining the limited 1975–76 participation instead. Exact individual medal eligibility remains UNVERIFIED here.
- Avoid “final three seasons”: 1982–83 contained appearances but no goals. The three full campaigns ending in 1982 are the relevant scoring period.
- No invented quotation, crowd reaction, scene, motivation or injury explanation. No assertions about present health.
- No low-confidence material retained in publishable prose. Secondary recollections remain explicitly attributed.
