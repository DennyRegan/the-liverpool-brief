## Resolved conflicts and excluded claims

- Official profile header ends in 1995 (last playing year); prose and Swansea record show departure February 1996. Historical period will be 1984–1996; explain final competitive game March 1995 separately.
- £200,000 (LFCHistory), £225,000 (archived club search text) and £575,000 (contemporary Independent) cannot be resolved from retrieved full source record. Fee UNVERIFIED and excluded, not averaged or asserted.
- Club lists FA Cups 1986, 1989, 1992, while LFCHistory player honours list 1986 and 1992. Avoid definitive individual-medal tally; identify three league-winning campaigns and his two victorious final appearances, and distinguish team's 1989 success from personal playing role. Do not claim six personal major winners' medals.
- Official retrospective says footage surfaced three decades later; precise account says 2009, 24 years. Use 2009.
- Prison: sentence three months, time served about six weeks. Do not equate sentence to time served. Exact conviction/release dates and disputed detailed driving narrative UNVERIFIED, excluded. Do not diagnose alcohol dependency or excuse offence.
