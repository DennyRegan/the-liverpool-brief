## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Preston’s division in 1977:** S1 and the prose in S2 say Second Division; S5 describes Third Division. The repeated profile wording is not independent confirmation. Division omitted; unnecessary to this biography.
- **Brighton transfer fee:** £100,000 appears in Liverpool/LFCHistory narratives; S3 gives more than £110,000. Precise amount **UNVERIFIED** in this research, excluded. Liverpool’s later £900,000 fee is separately supported.
- **Chelsea 1986:** S5 says Lawrenson watched from the sidelines. S12 lists him in the starting XI, consistent with S2. The match record is the stronger evidence; the biography correctly places him on the pitch.
- **Gillespie’s FA Cup final absence:** injury in S1/S2, stomach illness in S5. Precise cause **UNVERIFIED**, omitted rather than combined into a misleading explanation.
- **1984 all-competition participation:** S2 shows 66 appearances; no claim of complete all-competition ever-presence. All 42 league matches is supported.
- **Retirement:** Liverpool’s short profile can read as complete retirement from playing. S2 records subsequent American and non-league football. Biography distinguishes the end of his highest-level career from later appearances.
- **Oxford departure:** retrieved secondary accounts disagree over resignation/dismissal. The reason, mechanism and exact appointment/departure dates are omitted. No unverified job-exit story is included.
- **Quotation provenance:** the underlying print source for Paisley’s reproduced account was not established. No verbatim quote is used, and the reproduction is explicitly identified in prose. Similarly, late-career recollections are identified as later accounts recorded by LFCHistory.
- No active playing or managerial career is asserted. Research date is supplied for this record; current broadcasting employment is not claimed.
