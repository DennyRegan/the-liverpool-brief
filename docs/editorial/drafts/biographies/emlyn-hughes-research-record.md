## Historical problems handled

- Captaincy succession: H1 says summer 1973 and H5 before next campaign; H18 says November after the Highbury dispute. The draft says only **1973**, and does not make the specific dispute the proven immediate cause. Smith’s resentment and Paisley’s acknowledgement are attributed to their later accounts reproduced in H2; no unverified accusation of misconduct is included. Coordinator and Smith worker informed.
- Aggregate career totals: H15/H17 use 657 appearances/48 goals; H1/H2/H5 and the later UEFA H11 support 665/49. H2 includes four Charity Shields and four European Super Cup matches, explaining an eight-match difference in scope; do not call either number an unqualified identical measure. Draft uses the club’s current comprehensive competitive total. No league-only total is presented as an overall figure.
- H17 wrongly assigns a 1979–80 Liverpool championship and says ten games in 1967–68. These are rejected against the modern seasonal tables. H2 prose refers to an approaching thirtieth birthday after 1978; birth date makes that impossible. Age at departure is not copied.
- First honours were in 1973; first major trophy lifted **as captain** was the 1974 FA Cup. The draft never credits Hughes as captain of the 1973 double.
- 1978–79: 16 is league appearances, **28 all competitions**. Draft distinguishes both.
- FWA award is 1977, explicitly identified in H2 and corroborated H3/H5; no PFA award conflation.
- Jimmy Armfield transcript is used as an attributed early interview, without claiming its header’s 1964 dating is independently authenticated. No childhood motive or private thought is inferred.
- No transfer-out fee, exact Rotherham dismissal date, disputed nickname-origin incident, television start year or medical causation is asserted.
