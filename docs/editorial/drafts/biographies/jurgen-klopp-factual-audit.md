## Corrections and source precision

- **Concurrent 2019 competitions:** an independent agent review relayed by `/root` identified that the transition from the completed league account could imply Liverpool reached the European final after losing the title race. Barcelona's second leg was on 7 May while the league remained live; its final day was 12 May. Replaced the transition with explicit concurrent framing, consistent with K12/K13. Read together with the following Barcelona paragraph, the sequence is now unambiguous.
- **Finals sequence:** the first saved wording followed UEFA's shorthand about six successive club final defeats. An independent agent review relayed by `/root` identified the missing qualification: Dortmund won the 2013 and 2014 Supercups. I opened and inspected the [Bundesliga's official Supercup history](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/history-of-supercup-records-goals-all-matches-bayern-dortmund-leipzig-20635), confirming Dortmund 4–2 Bayern on 27 July 2013 and 2–0 on 13 August 2014 under Klopp. Final wording is restricted to his **three preceding Liverpool finals**. This resolves the issue without introducing a disputed definition of “major final”.
- **Title timing:** 25 June is the event date; 26 June is the Premier League page date. Seven games remaining is the relevant measure, not an early calendar date in the pandemic-delayed season. Reopened K17/K34 in this pass to confirm scope.
- **Honours reconciliation:** Champions League 2019 + UEFA Super Cup 2019 + Club World Cup 2019 + Premier League 2020 + League Cup 2022 + FA Cup 2022 + League Cup 2024 = **seven major trophies in six competitions**. Community Shield 2022 is additional and explicitly separate. No credit given for trophies won under subsequent managers.
- **Source errors/limits:** K9's 2019–20 goals-against table is incomplete; only its 2018–19 total is retained. Playing-career shorthand in K2 and competing civic-honour rankings in K33/K34 are avoided. Win percentages and 305-win convention are omitted rather than silently mixing regulation and shoot-out outcomes.
- **Sensitive history:** Paris 2022 is described through UEFA's own apology with the correction of unjust supporter blame. No speculative emotion, victim figure, legal attribution or Hillsborough conflation. The historical Liverpool career scope does not require an added Hillsborough claim.
- **Time boundary:** researched as of 1 October 2026, with historical endpoint May 2024. No unsupported claim about Klopp's current job, permanent retirement, subsequent Liverpool results or future plans.
