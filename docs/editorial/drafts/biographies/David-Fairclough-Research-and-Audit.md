## Conflicts and exclusions

- An older official profile found in search gives 61 substitute appearances with 92 starts and 154 overall. The current official page and LFChistory both give 62; use the consistent current figures.
- S6 remembers a debut against Dresden. S3 records the Middlesbrough debut; S4 establishes the first goal against Real Sociedad. Use match records.
- Honours lists differ between S1 and S2. **UNVERIFIED: a complete personal medal tally.** Omitted rather than equating membership of the squad with individual participation or medal entitlement.
- Substitution timing against Saint-Étienne is variously described as 73 minutes or the 74th minute. Exact entry time omitted; goal minute agrees across inspected records.
- A contemporary Liverpool Echo clipping landing page, “Crossroads for young Fairclough”, was opened, but the clipping text was not retrievable in the page output. Not used as evidence or cited in the biography.
- Some date-route match URLs failed. None is included in the Sources list; successful canonical match records are used instead.
- No contemporary book was represented as having been read. S6 is a retrieved interview; material it quotes from other publications is not treated as separately inspected evidence.
- No low-confidence claim retained. No fabricated quote, motivation, scene or supporter reaction.
