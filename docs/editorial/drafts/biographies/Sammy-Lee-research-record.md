## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Honours versus participation:** S3’s honours panel includes the 1977 European Cup, despite its appearance table recording no senior matches that season. The biography counts only his two victorious final starts, in 1981 and 1984. It does not reproduce the panel’s aggregate trophy number.
- **League titles:** Liverpool’s current profile lists five championship seasons involving Lee. His central playing period included the three successive titles of 1981–82 to 1983–84. The biography describes this precisely and does not assert five personally awarded contemporary winner’s medals. Exact original medal eligibility/receipt across earlier seasons is **UNVERIFIED** here and omitted.
- **66 versus 67 in 1983–84:** S9 says 66; S1 explicitly says 67 starts. S3’s competition breakdown totals 67: 42 league + 2 FA Cup + 13 League Cup + 9 European Cup + 1 Charity Shield. The draft includes the Charity Shield explicitly. Excluding it produces 66.
- **Coaching return date:** S3 says 1992; S11 and S12 say 1993. Exact appointment date remains **UNVERIFIED**. Draft states only that he returned under Souness.
- **Bolton assistant appointment month:** S3/S12 say June 2005; S11 says September. Exact month **UNVERIFIED**; omitted.
- **Reason for playing decline:** Sources offer differing emphasis, including form and injuries. Draft states reduced appearances without asserting an unverified medical or personal cause.
- **Unused interview:** Sid Lowe’s Guardian interview of 17 November 2025 was retrieved, but no material from it is used in the biography. It is not included as a supporting citation.
- No direct quotations, invented scenes, imagined motivations or unsupported crowd reactions appear in the draft. Medium-confidence retrospective evidence is attributed and used only for the limited matters identified above. No low-confidence claim is presented as fact.
