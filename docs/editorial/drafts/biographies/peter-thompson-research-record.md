1. **Playing end versus departure:** Official Liverpool profile uses 1963–72 for first-team football; contract record continues until 5 January 1974, after December 1973 loan. Metadata and narrative distinguish both. No false permanent exit in 1972.
2. **Bolton 126 versus 132:** Official Bolton obituary says 126 all-competition games, LFChistory prose says 132. Exact total excluded without a complete Bolton ledger.
3. **Arrival year:** PlayUpLiverpool transcription labels 1964. Archive's 14 August 1963, actual debut on 24 August 1963, official first-season championship and age twenty establish 1963. Mislabelled reference excluded.
4. **Death day:** Archive says 30 December, Englandstats 31 December; announcements on 31 December do not establish the day of death. Draft says December 2018.
5. **Every match:** Modern club wording is narrowed to **all 42 league games**. Archive shows 47 total appearances, including five FA Cup games. Six goals stated specifically as league goals.
6. No 1973 league / UEFA Cup or 1974 FA Cup assigned to Thompson merely because he was still contracted. No senior appearances in those seasons; official personal honours list 1964 and 1966 leagues plus 1965 Cup.
7. Exact schoolboy suitor count, signing-on dialogue, verbatim medical prognosis, injection details, unverified assists and international counterfactuals excluded.
8. Finney advice anecdote contains uncertain retrospective timing (“still playing” versus senior debut after retirement). Only comparison and pressure retained.
