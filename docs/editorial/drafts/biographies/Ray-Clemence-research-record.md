## Conflicts, limitations and exclusions

- S1’s narrative calls the May 1982 return “a few weeks” after joining Spurs. S11 dates the move to August 1981; S8 supplies the correct return date. The inaccurate interval is not repeated.
- S3 places the permanent takeover at the start of 1970–71. S1’s season and match chronology establishes regular selection during 1969–70. The article follows that chronology.
- S3 appears to associate the Dresden penalty save with 1972–73. S1 distinguishes it as 1976. This incident is unnecessary to the finished article and omitted.
- S12 calls the 1984 UEFA Cup medal his second, despite listing Liverpool’s two wins; it also says “six years” before listing managers across a longer coaching tenure. Neither error is repeated.
- Early Scunthorpe appearance counts vary across biographies. No figure included.
- No cause of departure asserted. No claim of being driven out by Bruce Grobbelaar. No unsourced motivations, dressing-room scenes or superlative rankings.
- No direct quotations in final article. No unresolved claim presented as fact. Sources are retrospective records and testimony; no claim that contemporary newspaper archives were inspected.
