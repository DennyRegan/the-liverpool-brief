## Conflicts resolved before drafting or in the separate audit

- The 2024 LFC anniversary says Evans succeeded Souness in March 1994. The player profile, detailed manager record, appointment comments and **first match on 5 February** establish **31 January 1994**. March excluded.
- The same anniversary appears to attach Tottenham's 3–1 win to 12 November. Contemporary match report and archive establish **10 November 1998**, followed by departure **12 November**. These events are separately dated.
- Club manager totals include the joint phase: **244 = 226 sole + 18 joint**. No 277-game or 243-game third-party total retained. Separate 172 and 12 league counts not confused with all-competition totals.
- The 2024 anniversary places the apprentice start on 16 January 1964; LFC's America piece says 1963 and some retrospective service totals imply it. Manuscript says youth system/1960s and uses secure October 1965 professional forms. Precise apprentice date **UNVERIFIED and excluded**.
- The August 1974 prediction is John Smith's in the contemporary announcement. LFC's 2024 text attributes its wording to Paisley. Manuscript uses Smith; Paisley's separate remarks concern coaching ability.
- Retrospective age “26” conflicts with October 1948 birth and August 1974. Manuscript uses **25**.
- British Soccer Week's archived appointment article wrongly implies injury-ending career in 1973, Shankly appointed the coach, nine reserve titles in eleven years, and a Paisley FA Cup. These **excluded**; contemporaneous August 1974 and official player record take priority. Primary Evans explicitly denied the injury myth.
- No global “40 trophies” or “13 league titles” assigned to Evans. Coaching staff participation and managerial honours are explicitly distinguished. Atoms honour is an overseas playing achievement, not an Anfield first-team winner's medal.
