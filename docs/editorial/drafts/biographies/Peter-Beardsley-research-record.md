## Conflicts, exclusions and corrections

- Birthplace: LFCHistory and Everton Encyclopedia say Newcastle; Newcastle’s own Hall of Fame page says Hexham. UNVERIFIED — omitted rather than choosing one.
- The LFCHistory profile labels the Hunter interview as January 1999. The article itself says 18 years after 1991, gives Beardsley’s age as 48 and discusses Benítez’s title challenge: it belongs to the 2009 context. The biography says “later” and does not repeat the erroneous date. Exact publication date was not independently retrieved.
- LFCHistory’s narrative places the Arsenal Charity Shield winner in 1990–91, while its goals table assigns the sole Shield goal to 1989–90. That detail is unnecessary and omitted.
- Newcastle’s abbreviated early-career account omits the first Vancouver spell. The biography uses only the broad club sequence supported by the fuller LFCHistory account.
- Everton Encyclopedia incorrectly describes Newcastle’s 1984 promotion as a Second Division title. The biography says only “promoted”, corroborated by Newcastle’s official profile.
- Everton transfer-day accounts differ. The biography uses summer 1991 and the corroborated £1m fee.
- Several sources use different Newcastle appearance totals. No Newcastle aggregate appears in the biography.
- Sources’ claims about a Souness falling-out or private motives are not adopted.
- FA statement and written-reasons links were located but could not be retrieved in full. They are not presented as inspected primary evidence. The disciplinary account uses the retrieved Reuters report quoting the FA, with independent reporting as corroboration.
- No precise feelings, supporter reactions, dressing-room scenes or motives have been supplied.
