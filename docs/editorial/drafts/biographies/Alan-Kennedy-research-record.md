## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Championship accounting:** S1, S2 and S9 include 1985–86. S1 records eight league games followed by a September 1985 departure. The draft states five championships before departure, then explicitly describes the early contribution to 1985–86. It does not claim six medals or participation in the 1986 FA Cup win. Physical receipt/eligibility of a sixth championship medal: **UNVERIFIED; omitted**.
- **Fee:** S1 gives £330,000; S12 has £333,000. Use the dedicated statistical record. Do not present the interview as independent agreement on the fee.
- **2015 interview errors:** S6 is dated 26 May but says “On this day”; S7 fixes the final at 27 May. S6 also prints a claim that the European Cup came in his first Liverpool season, inconsistent with the 1978 arrival. Neither error is reproduced.
- **AS errors:** interview wording gives 15 Liverpool goals without the league qualifier, and the question dates departure to 1986. S1 distinguishes 15 league goals from 20 overall and establishes 1985 departure. Those AS details are unused.
- **Paris goal timing:** nine minutes remaining follows UEFA’s 81st minute. Alternative rounded timings elsewhere are not imported.
- **Rome terminology:** decisive shoot-out penalty, not a second official European Cup final goal. No shoot-out goal added to career statistics.
- Source independence is limited for club retrospectives: several are from the same organisation. They are used for discrete records, not treated as separate independent witnesses. UEFA supplies competition authority; LFChistory supplies detailed career tables; AS supplies an identified interview.
- No low-confidence material retained. No claim of having watched archival footage. No active-career or present-day employment claims included.
