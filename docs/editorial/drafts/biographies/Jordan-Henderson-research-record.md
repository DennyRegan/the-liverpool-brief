### Reconciliations and exclusions

- **Honours:** seven major trophies, plus the 2022 Community Shield. Of the major trophies, six came during his captaincy; the 2012 League Cup preceded it. Avoid the ambiguous claim “seven major trophies as captain”.
- **Appearances:** 492 competitive Liverpool appearances; 360 in the league. **Goals:** 33 overall; 29 league. No friendlies or international appearances included.
- **Transfer fee:** LFCHistory gives £16m; Sky’s retrospective gives £20m. Exact basis not resolved. **UNVERIFIED as a single definitive fee; omitted.**
- **Captaincy:** use Liverpool’s contemporary 10 July 2015 announcement. Chelsea’s later biography incorrectly says June. For vice-captaincy, use September 2014, as stated in S05 and consistent with Liverpool’s season chronology; Liverpool’s short past-player profile compresses this misleadingly around the 2014 World Cup.
- **Brentford signing day:** displayed club-page date and wider reporting differ. Exact day **UNVERIFIED; omitted**. July 2025 is consistently supported.
- **Injury detail:** omit LFCHistory’s stated 28 February 2021 derby injury date, which was not independently established. Only centre-back deployment is used.
- **2014 title:** the proposition that Henderson’s availability would have won the league is **UNVERIFIABLE**; not claimed.
- **Saudi move:** no salary, tax, personal motivation or legal penalty claims included. Criticism is attributed, not presented as unanimous fan opinion.
- **2026 status:** checked rather than assuming his 2025 Brentford move remains current. Chelsea’s own announcement and Sky report were both inspected. Later club coverage is deliberately brief.
- **Quotations:** no direct quotations in the biography. Recollections and managerial judgements are paraphrased and attributed.
- **Unavailable sources:** failed attempts to open AP, Reuters and one Sky transfer-story route were not used as evidentiary support. Successful alternate sources are listed above.
