## Reconciliation and exclusions

- S1's “1975–1981” heading covers first-team appearances. Joining in May 1973 is supported by S2 and S3. These are different milestones.
- The source material is inconsistent about the duration of Case's semi-professional arrangement. The biography retains the arrangement and reason he recalled, without attempting an exact apprenticeship chronology.
- S2 reproduces a recollection saying he left aged 28. His recorded birth date and summer 1981 transfer establish 27; the incorrect age is not repeated.
- 269 appearances and 46 goals are Liverpool competitive totals, including Charity Shield and European Super Cup matches. League-only figures are 186 and 23. No friendlies are included.
- The honours list contains ten trophies using the club's convention, excluding Charity Shields. It does not imply Case started every final. His 1981 substitute role is explicit.
- S14 has an anomalous headline starting year and its league appearance breakdown differs from other search results. Southampton totals and transfer fees are omitted; the narrative March 1985 arrival is used.
- Exact transfer terms, the mechanism of any connection to Mark Lawrenson's transfer, and a single definitive cause for the departure: UNVERIFIED in this research; omitted.
- No direct quotations are used. Sources containing reproduced material or Case's same interview are not treated as independent corroboration of that interview.
