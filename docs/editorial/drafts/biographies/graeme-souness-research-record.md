## Conflicts, exclusions and writing decisions

- Liverpool player total is **359 appearances/55 goals**, independently matching S1/S3/S8. Museum gives 358/56; excluded. League-only ledger 247/38 is distinct. Shootout conversions are not added as match goals.
- Incoming fee appears as £350,000 (rounded club/museum) and £352,000 (S3 exact). Use ‘about £350,000’, a record **between English clubs**, not British record. No outgoing fee needed.
- Captaincy: January/early 1982 supported S1/S8/S3. Museum says start of 1981–82; reject that precision.
- Five titles, three European Cups and **four** League Cups. S10’s three is a mistake. Major playing honours total twelve, but enumerate rather than headline a trophy total including/excluding Shields inconsistently.
- Souness arrived in April 1991 with five league games left, not mid-season; manager departure January 1994, not April as in an erroneous retrospective line in S4.
- Sun treatment must distinguish the interview from its **publication on 15 April 1992**. The interview concerned recovery after heart surgery. Explain the boycott with restrained reference to false Hillsborough coverage; do not repeat allegations about victims. No unsupported inference that Souness chose the anniversary, and no claim that all supporters hold an identical view. His timing explanation, payment and destination of proceeds are unnecessary and omitted.
- Health is historical event context, not a diagnosis or causal explanation of every failure. Moran’s contribution is explicit. Do not suggest Souness ran the final unaided.
- Dinamo violence should be named, with no celebration or invented excuse. Other details of that semi-final can be omitted to keep source use narrow.
- Avoid folk stories about tearing down the Boot Room, unverified wages, betting/transfer allegations, invented dressing-room reactions or easy causation that makes one manager solely responsible for a decades-long decline.
- S19 first-leg score and career totals conflict with established records; no first-leg score or career total for Fowler will enter this biography.
- Wider career remains proportionate: Tottenham/Middlesbrough, Scotland, Sampdoria/Rangers and short later-management context. No claim about current employment in 2026.
- No direct quotation planned. The brief Guardian context and Independent violence sentence remain within the stricter 25-word source allowances. Other narrative is distributed over match records, primary recollections and distinct histories, within 200-word per-source budgets.
