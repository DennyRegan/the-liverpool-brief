## Discrepancies and exclusions

- Honours: use an explicit list. The 13-trophy conventional grouping is 6 league titles + 3 European Cups + 1 UEFA Cup + 3 League Cups. The European Super Cup is additional. Do not confuse this with 20 trophies including Charity Shields. No complete all-trophy total is claimed in the prose.
- Managerial match total: the current club profile and LFChistory give 534; an older club article gives 535. Omitted. UNVERIFIED: precise accounting explanation for this difference, including treatment of the 1974 Charity Shield.
- First season: use “finished second”, not “trophyless”, to avoid ambiguity over the 1974 Charity Shield and managerial attribution.
- LFChistory’s manager narrative says Souness arrived two months after Dalglish. The January 1978 date is supported by UEFA’s specific Souness interview and used instead.
- LFChistory’s closing narrative muddles Fagan/Dalglish succession and the order of board/consultancy service. The club profile and UEFA account provide the stronger chronology used here: Fagan succeeded in 1983, Dalglish became player-manager in 1985.
- The 2018 Wolves article incorrectly describes three points being available in 1976. That detail is excluded. Its precise “only a point required” formulation is also unnecessary and omitted.
- Neal’s UEFA recollection misidentifies the goalkeeper beaten by the 1977 penalty. No goalkeeper is named in the biography. The tactical recollection is presented as Neal’s later account.
- The 1996 Independent report wrongly says Paisley joined after the war. The 1939 date is established by club, LFChistory and UEFA records.
- Avoid present-day “only manager” European Cup claims. Use the historically bounded, verified “first manager to win three”.
- No invented quotes, motivations, crowd reactions or scenes. No low-confidence claim retained. Wider all-time manager ranking remains judgement, not a statistical fact.
