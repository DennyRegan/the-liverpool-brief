### Discrepancies and exclusions

- LFCHistory’s retrieved profile honours box includes 1989–90, while Liverpool’s official profile credits the 1987–88 title and 1989 FA Cup. Aldridge made only two league appearances in 1989–90 and left in September. Do not turn club participation into an unsupported personal medal claim. The biography names the two established major honours only. Whether he later received a 1989–90 medal is UNVERIFIED and omitted.
- LFCHistory gives whole-career totals of 476/889; Liverpool’s profile gives 474/882. Scope not resolved; whole-career aggregates omitted.
- LFCHistory’s 1988–89 total includes two Charity Shield goals. The biography’s 31 across all competitions follows that convention; it does not mean 31 league goals. Competitive Liverpool appearances include the Charity Shield and exclude friendlies.
- The old Liverpool profile says nine goals in the first nine games. The narrower, corroborated wording used is that he scored in each of the first nine league matches.
- A dated 2024 article calls the ten-game run a current club record. The biography reports the historical achievement without making an unchecked September 2026 record-holder claim.
- The 1989 semi-final newspaper file is headed 7 May but its text says “yesterday”. The biography avoids assigning the file date to the match.
- The Sky page uses the then-established toll of 96. The draft uses the later official figure of 97 and does not repeat historically superseded wording.
- Real Sociedad’s “first non-Basque player” label was not investigated sufficiently; omitted. Alleged retirement intentions after Hillsborough and funeral counts were also omitted rather than relying on snippets.
- Liverpool’s legacy Hillsborough timeline redirected to its homepage; the Irish Independent item returned an error. Neither is cited as evidence. No source is taken from another AI-written Liverpool Brief article.
