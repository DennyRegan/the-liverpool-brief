## Conflicts resolved and exclusions

- Signing versus debut: 1958 signing in current LFC obituary, LFChistory and Sky; 1959 is senior debut. Some summaries conflate them. No inaccurate age-at-signing from FA/family-feature lead is repeated.
- Current official club figures and LFChistory’s explicit competition table agree on **285 competitive goals, 244 league goals, 492 competitive appearances**. Guardian and museum give 245 league goals; older 286-total material also exists. Current reconciled figures govern. No speculative account of which individual goal was reallocated is supplied.
- H1 gives 30 in 1966; H3’s season table and the 2025 club league review give **29 league goals**, used here. Cup goals are not mixed into league figures.
- Narrow birthplace differs: family feature/Guardian say Glazebury, LFChistory/Sky Golborne. “Near Warrington” follows Bolton’s geographical description and avoids false precision while retaining the undisputed date.
- ‘Sir Roger’ is supporter usage, not a knighthood. MBE is supported by FA and museum.
- Testimonial attendances are rounded differently (over 55,000/56,000). “More than 55,000” preserves the shared scale without an invented exact count.
- No reconstruction of disputed 1966 final goal-line events; no claim Hunt displaced Greaves single-handedly or made the referee award Hurst’s goal. National career kept proportionate to Liverpool subject.
- Brief Hellenic loan retained, without an unverified count or competition total. Family/private matters not necessary to the career are omitted. No diagnosis or causal medical claim.
- England Football Online France record timed out and is not used. No claim that an unopened or failed page was checked.
