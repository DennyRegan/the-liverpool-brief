## Discrepancies and exclusions

- League totals: LFCHistory currently displays 342 appearances/15 goals, while a search result for a secondary overview gave 343/16. Resolution UNVERIFIED. League career totals omitted. The independently agreeing club and LFCHistory all-competition figures, 379/17, are used.
- Regular-player chronology differs between the 2026 club feature and older profiles. The biography uses the broader, supported Second Division period without selecting a disputed first season.
- Coaching invitation: sources use 1966 and the 1966–67 pre-season. These are compatible; final copy says 1966. Last senior match in 1965 is not treated as retirement from all playing activity.
- Service length: some sources say 46 years, counting from professional terms in 1952. Final copy counts from joining in 1949 to retirement in 1998: 49 years.
- S4 describes a 1957 win as earning three points. That is an anachronism; the anecdote is not used.
- S9’s opening narrative places Dalglish’s resignation the day after 20 February, while its appointment field gives 22 February. The biography uses February only.
- Avoided blanket trophy totals that mix competitions and roles, and any claim that Moran played in the winning 1965 FA Cup final or managed the 1992 final.
- No claims about private motives, unrecorded conversations, crowd reactions or invented atmosphere. No unresolved claim retained as fact in final copy.
