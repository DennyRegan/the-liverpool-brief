## Conflict resolution update

2. Sporting Heroes and This Is Anfield both explicitly explain the missing 1976 championship medal. Public narrative can describe the frustration without claiming five physical medals. Liverpool's five title-winning seasons during membership are established by all tabulated honours lists.
3. LFChistory narrative incorrectly places the 1977 chip in the replay. The actual original game's timeline and two independent features place it on 23 April in the 2–2 draw; reject replay designation.
4. LFChistory narrative swaps the last two full-season goals totals (20/22). Its detailed table and Sporting Heroes agree: 22 in 1980–81; 20 in 1981–82. Total 58 = 16+22+20. Use table / independent biography.
5. Sporting Heroes gives total four goals in 1976–77 only implicitly elsewhere; one passage suggests two vital goals as if total. Do not use that as season total. Draft uses appearances and specific goals.
