## Discrepancies and exclusions

- The older official profile's honours box omits 1992. S1, S2 and the July 2026 official article include it. The draft explains his contribution and final absence instead of silently equating an honour with playing in the final.
- S1 retains an outdated Hillsborough death toll. The draft uses the eventual total in S8.
- S3's claim of uniformly high performance across nine subsequent years is too broad to reproduce given injury and form difficulties documented elsewhere.
- Competitive total includes the Charity Shield and Football League Centenary Trophy. S1 separately lists 314 league, 51 FA Cup, 26 League Cup, 12 European and four other appearances: 407. Friendlies are excluded. The 403 figure elsewhere can denote starts, not total appearances.
- Recollections have been attributed. No direct quotations, imagined feelings, invented scenes, or unsupported claims about how all supporters reacted appear in the biography.
- No unresolved low-confidence claim has been retained in the article. Exact publication dates not established above remain unverified bibliographical details, not factual assertions in the biography.
- Career is retired; the research date records this editorial check, not a claim to cover every later personal or public development.
