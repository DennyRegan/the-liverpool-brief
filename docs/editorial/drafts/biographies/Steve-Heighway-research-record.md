## Conflicts and exclusions

- S2 distinguishes an amateur agreement on 28 February 1970 from professional signing on 19 May. The text preserves the distinction. Transfer fee omitted.
- S11 contains background figures inconsistent with specialist records: 329 league appearances and retirement in 1980. Those details were rejected in favour of S2 and the official club profile.
- S11 describes 19 years of youth work; S12 explicitly records 18. Use the club’s later formal record alongside S2’s 1989 appointment date.
- S9 says the 1977 league title was secured in April. That exact timing was not used. Its extravagant atmospheric descriptions were also excluded.
- No claim of a third European Cup winners’ medal in 1981. Club seasonal success is not treated as proof of personal final involvement.
- University name, international cap total, transfer fee and detailed youth-coaching philosophy omitted: unnecessary to this account and not separately resolved here.
- Search results and failed page opens were not treated as inspected sources. The failed old club timeline page on the 1996 Youth Cup was replaced with the successfully retrieved current honours page.
