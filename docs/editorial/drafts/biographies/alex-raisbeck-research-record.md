## Reconciled conflicts and UNVERIFIED omissions

- **Selling club:** Hibernian after a Stoke loan. R1’s older claim that Liverpool paid Stoke is rejected in favour of R2, R3 and Raisbeck’s account. Fee £350 independently agrees.
- **Arrival day:** LFCHistory gives 30 April 1898; Play Up index gives a 6 May transaction. Current club account gives April. Month retained, exact day **UNVERIFIED** and omitted.
- **Captaincy:** nine principal-captain seasons from 1900–01, supported by R4, R2 and R3. Memoir’s “first day” claim and historical accounts giving 1899 are not used. Match records show Storer captain in 1898 and February 1899.
- **Birth:** modern indexed record gives 26 December 1878; memoir/programme say 1879. Exact birthday/year omitted rather than inserting a needless disputed datum.
- **1899 Villa:** 5–0 in records, not 5–1 in memoir.
- **1901 final day:** point required, not a win. Walker scored; exact minute omitted because the versions differ.
- **1904 Doig:** signed for promotion season 1904–05, not relegation season 1903–04 as recalled in R14.
- **1905–06 opening:** first three losses, then Sunderland win; no first-four-losses assertion.
- **Scotland:** eight official caps, with 1902 void match excluded. First-match captain claim is contradicted by official match-search information identifying John T. Robertson. Exact captaincy dates **UNVERIFIED** here and omitted.
- **Later injury:** no specific diagnosis or assertion that injury alone forced departure. Four-month absence is supported but expressed simply as lengthy absence.
- **Exit fee/registration day:** conflicting £75/£500 and June/August dates; **UNVERIFIED**, omitted. Partick move in 1909 secure.
- **Scouting:** connection secure; claimed start in 1939 versus later accounts’ post-war start **UNVERIFIED**, no year used.
- **1902 Ibrox tragedy:** witness memoir retrieved but separate authoritative cross-check incomplete; omitted, including fatality estimates. This omission does not conceal any central Liverpool result or honour.
- **Private motives:** staying after relegation is reported as Raisbeck’s later explanation. No invented inner thoughts or inferred family reasons.
- **Honours:** two First Division titles and one Second Division title are team achievements with Raisbeck as captain; semi-final defeats are setbacks. No FA Cup win attributed to him. No invented personal award.
