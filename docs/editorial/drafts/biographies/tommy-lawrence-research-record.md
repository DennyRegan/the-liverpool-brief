## Conflicts, exclusions and confidence decisions

- **1957 versus 1962:** amateur arrival in April, professional forms in October; competitive debut in October 1962. Official profile years describe senior service; PFA's October signing refers to the professional stage. No 1962 recruitment claim or 1962 promotion medal assigned.
- **Sweeper-keeper:** official profiles use pioneer/first language. The manuscript describes the demonstrated Liverpool role and modern analogy, without asserting worldwide invention or precedence. Lawrence's Melwood recollection is retrospective testimony; Evans corroborates the practical relationship with pressing.
- **Durability:** the Independent says five missed games in eight seasons, inconsistent with indexed records and official wording. Use only the cross-checked four league absences in six seasons, 1963–64 to 1968–69; no all-competition equivalence.
- **Ending:** Watford ended the regular spell, not all Liverpool appearances. Final start 26 April 1971; departure November 1971. A substitute squad listing is not an appearance. Initial Tranmere loan/registration mechanics omitted because not independently corroborated.
- **Death:** LFCHistory has 9 January; official reports say Wednesday morning, 10 January. Manuscript gives January 2018 only. Sensitive illness/private-life claims omitted.
- **UNVERIFIED / omitted:** categorical first-ever sweeper-keeper; exact height/weight; worldwide imitation; retirement family consequences; lost Cup-lid anecdote; precise post-football employment dates; BBC viral-view numbers. No invented quotation or detailed save mechanics.
- Attempts to open two contemporary souvenir PDFs failed (1964–65 `intoeurope`, 1965–66 `review`), so their snippets were not used and they are not listed as retrieved substantive sources. Wrong initial LFCHistory player ID opened Roger Hunt; it was corrected to 353 and is not manuscript evidence.
