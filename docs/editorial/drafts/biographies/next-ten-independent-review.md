## Method and limits

Read each completed manuscript together with its research record and separate author factual audit. Independently retrieved the principal records and selected high-risk supporting sources listed below. Checked statistical scope, honours, chronology, major events, attributed recollection, sensitive material and whether the biography gives its subject more than a list of trophies. This is targeted independent checking, **not exhaustive re-research of every sentence or every source in all ten bibliographies**. An author's claim-to-source table is evidence of their work, not proof that this reviewer independently repeated every check. Technical metadata, inventory, build and route validation belong to the coordinator.

Confidence labels apply only to the stated scope: **High** for concordant club/competition ledgers, direct statements and clearly attributed testimony; **Medium** for retrospective narrative, transcription or interpretive synthesis; **Low** for unresolved particulars, which must be omitted or qualified. Source pages were opened and relevant article bodies/tables inspected, with targeted find operations where navigation obscured them. Search snippets alone supply no finding. Images, match footage, original manuscript books and original archival clippings were not independently examined.
