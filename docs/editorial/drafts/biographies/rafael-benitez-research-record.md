## Conflicts and exclusions before drafting

- Official manager profile lists Champions League, FA Cup and Super Cup; anniversary also includes Community Shield. Count **three major trophies plus the 2006 Community Shield**, not four major honours.
- Departure archive headline uses “sacking”; the reproduced official statement and UEFA say **by mutual consent**, dated 3 June 2010. Use official formulation while explaining disappointing season.
- LFC profile says Liverpool could easily have won in 2009 without injuries: this is speculative. Do not convert it to fact. Draws, United's points and squad decisions require separate treatment.
- **UNVERIFIED/excluded:** exact childhood episodes, internal transfer-veto motives, exact net-spend arithmetic, invented dressing-room dialogue, proof that 2009 press conference caused loss of title.
- FA's 2020 final report failed retrieval. Not a manuscript source unless successfully reopened; UEFA full report supplies final evidence.
- No detailed Hillsborough disaster narrative is required: mention charitable support accurately and respectfully without importing the historical 96 death count as today's total.
