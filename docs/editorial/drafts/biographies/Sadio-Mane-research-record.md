### Conflicts and exclusions

- LFCHistory’s narrative dates the Southampton record hat-trick to 18 May 2015. The Premier League gives 16 May; the manuscript follows the competition organiser.
- LFCHistory’s narrative incorrectly credits Mané with a goal against Barcelona during the 2019 knockout rounds. Its own opponent goal table contains no Barcelona entry. Krawietz explicitly states that Mané did not score in the 4–0. The erroneous claim is excluded.
- Transfer fees vary across accounts (initial sum versus reported packages may differ). No fee is required to explain this career, so it is omitted rather than treated as settled.
- Liverpool’s 2022 departure account and its 2026 retrospective differ on 2019–20 assists. Assists are omitted throughout.
- Liverpool’s departure announcement is the authority for the completed Bayern move. An earlier agreement date is not substituted for completion.
- No personal motivations, private thoughts, crowd reactions, philanthropy claims or unverified quotations have been invented or added.
- A Sky Sports search result on the difficult 2020–21 season could not be opened successfully. It is not cited, and its purported interview quotation is not used.
- No unresolved claim is retained as fact. The omitted transfer-fee reconciliation remains UNVERIFIED in this record.
