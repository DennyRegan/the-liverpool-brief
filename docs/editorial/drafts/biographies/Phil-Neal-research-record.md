## Conflicts and exclusions

- **Signing month:** S2’s narrative says November 1974, while its transfer table gives 9 October; S4 is an explicit 9 October anniversary. October used.
- **Consecutive league appearances:** sources differ between 365 and 366. Exact league streak **UNVERIFIED** here and omitted. The independently corroborated 417 figure is explicitly all competitions, not league matches.
- **Most decorated ranking:** club records list Hansen and Neal jointly on 18 major honours, while other club pages describe Neal as uniquely first and give different all-silverware totals. No unique ranking or aggregate trophy total used. Named competitions and years are sufficiently clear.
- **Northampton youth chronology:** S2 and S10 differ over starting year; neither version included. Exact Northampton appearance totals also omitted.
- **Interview imprecision:** S3’s question calls Northampton a third-division club; S1/S10 support Fourth Division at the transfer. The interview is used only for Neal’s attributed recollection of his early role.
- **Injury stories:** recalled durations differ between accounts; omitted. The cheekbone incident cited in S3 predates the 417-match run and must not be described as occurring within it.
- **Liverpool managerial appointment:** Neal’s claimed expectation of succeeding Fagan is retrospective and disputed territory; not needed for this biography and omitted.
- No low-confidence material, invented quotation, private motivation, scene or supporter reaction retained.
