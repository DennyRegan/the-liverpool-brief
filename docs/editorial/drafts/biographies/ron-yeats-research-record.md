## Reconciliation and exclusions

- Transfer fee: Y2 says £22,000; Y3 says £30,000. Exact fee and exact July day omitted. These are not silently harmonised.
- Captaincy: popular summaries sometimes imply appointment on arrival. Y1 and Y13 identify a change during his first season. Draft uses that supported formulation; no exact captaincy-match tally or unsupported ten-full-season tenure.
- Y2 lists final appearance as 28 April 1971 in metadata but says 26 April in narrative. Exact final-match date omitted.
- Cup Winners’ Cup final: Y1/Y12 call the winner an own goal; DFB credits Libuda. Draft describes the post/deflection from Y2 and records DFB attribution, without calling Yeats the official scorer. Exact minute omitted because sources disagree.
- Kit: the two Anderlecht dates are deliberately distinguished. No claim that fully red socks were worn on 25 November.
- Cologne: Yeats’s coin memory is attributed; object and mechanics are not asserted beyond the source’s acknowledged uncertainty.
- Tranmere: no unverified explanation of managerial departure or allegations about the chairman. No later American wage allegations or precise itineraries required for this career narrative.
- No medical causal theory about heading or death. Illness is limited to the club’s public statement.
