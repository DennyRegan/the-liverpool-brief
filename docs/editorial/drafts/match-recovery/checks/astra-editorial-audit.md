## Issues raised and disposition

1. **Tottenham, November 1998 — opener's provider. Resolved.** The draft named an Anderton free-kick; the Independent named a Campbell cross. The writer removed the disputed provider/set-piece description. The retained Iversen header is supported by both accounts. Final wording was rechecked.
2. **Benfica, March 2006 — remaining trophy prospects. Resolved.** The closing line implied Liverpool were mathematically out of the league. The writer removed that inference and ends with the European title defence. Final wording was rechecked.
3. **FA Cup final 2006 — substitutions and shoot-out sequence. Resolved by writer's own final record check.** The writer corrected the mapping to Kewell–Morientes, Alonso–Kromkamp and Crouch–Hamann, and placed Gerrard before Riise in the shoot-out. The corrected text and evidence note were reread. An awkward opening phrase was also simplified.
4. **Crouch FA Cup report 2006 — source breadth and reuse restriction. Resolved.** The reviewer flagged the short allowance on the Guardian/Observer page. The writer added retrieved Daily Post and UEFA evidence; the Observer is retained only for a narrow independent injury check. Detailed action is not dependent on that restricted source alone.
5. **Chelsea semi-final 2005 — source scope. Resolved in notes.** UEFA's “Red army holds firm” concerns the previous Juventus tie. The notes explicitly restrict its Chelsea use to prior domestic results and distinguish it from the Anfield match reports.
6. **Wednesday 1998 and Celtic 1997 — time/access differences. Documented.** Wednesday's Echo prose/score-strip inconsistency does not displace the 73/78-minute goals corroborated by the record and Independent. Celtic clock conventions and transient PA access are documented. No unsupported precise detail was added.

The reviewer also assessed the writers' broader conflict registers. These include corrupt or inaccurate team lists, contemporary reports disagreeing on minute conventions, wrong assist names in retrospective/archived reports, the 2006 Luton penalty-order error in an FA retrospective, São Paulo's distinct disallowed-goal reasons, and bad chronology in older Istanbul and FA-final material.
