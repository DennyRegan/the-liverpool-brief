Celtic: Gould; Boyd, Mahé, McNamara, Stubbs, Larsson, Burley, Blinker, Donnelly, Wieghorst, Hannah. O'Donnell replaced Blinker at half-time; Liverpool made no changes. Goals: Owen 6, McNamara 53, Donnelly penalty 74, McManaman 89. Evans/Jansen; referee Graziano Cesari; attendance 48,526. Return 30 September: 0–0 at Anfield, aggregate 2–2, Liverpool advanced on away goals. Records: LFChistory games 2126 and 2130, independently checked against Celtic Wiki team/Scotsman record.

Audit: paragraphs 1–2 and 7: late run, foot and outcome corroborated by PA, club retrospective and Scotsman; 3–6: early goal, first-half control, second-half reversal and penalty in PA; 8: return-leg record. PA inaccurately calls Owen eighteen and Larsson Dutch: neither error carried into the draft. Celtic Wiki differs on rounded minutes; public 6/89 follows LFChistory and club record.

Wednesday: Pressman; Nolan, Hinchcliffe, Atherton, Newsome, Walker, Rudi, Hyde, Di Canio, Carbone, Pembridge. Liverpool used Berger and Riedle, withdrawing Jones/Kvarme at 71; Wednesday used Magilton for Hyde, Whittingham for Pembridge. Goals: Carbone 7; Owen 27,73,78; Di Canio 63; Hinchcliffe 69. Evans/Atkinson; Mike Reed; 35,405. Liverpool second, 46 points/26 games; United 50/25.

Audit: opening/record age from Guinness and Independent; chances and finishes from Echo and Independent; table from LFChistory. Echo is internally inconsistent about the second and third Owen minutes and which substitute replaced which defender. Public prose uses 73/78, corroborated by Independent's Sunday record (retrieved) and Sky result; it says two defenders withdrawn without pairing them to replacements. This was first **league** hat-trick, not first senior hat-trick: Independent explicitly notes the previous League Cup treble. No claim about an England debut on 9 February was used; article says same week.
