**Status:** 12 finished articles, researched and checked on 27 September 2026. Not published or scheduled.

## Selection

This is a selective season batch, following the supplied longlist’s principle of including outstanding performances, dramatic matches, defining defeats and significant occasions. It is not a complete season history. The supplied longlist ends in 1989-90; the 1996-97 selection was researched afresh.

The 12 pieces cover eight victories, one draw and three defeats. PSG is a second-leg victory followed by aggregate elimination.

## Evidence and limitations

Match records were checked against contemporary reporting and, where available, separate club or competition accounts. Press Association reports hosted on LFChistory are identified by their original agency. Multiple PA articles are not treated as independent corroboration merely because they have different URLs.

No footage was watched. Descriptions of play come from the cited written reports. Confidence labels apply to the facts used from a source, rather than everything on its website.

No unresolved assertion is deliberately retained as fact. The following details were qualified or omitted:

- **Middlesbrough, 14 December:** original reports credit Jan Åge Fjørtoft with the visitors’ goal. Liverpool FC’s retrospective, LFChistory’s match record and Sky Sports record a Michael Thomas own goal, while other statistical sites retain Fjørtoft. The copy simply says Middlesbrough pulled a goal back; it does not require a disputed attribution. Sources: [Liverpool FC — High confidence](https://www.liverpoolfc.com/news/first-team/120389-most-exciting-pl-games-64), [LFChistory — High confidence](https://www.lfchistory.net/games/2092), [Sky Sports — High confidence](https://www.skysports.com/football/liverpool-vs-middlesbrough/teams/277132), [contemporary PA report — High confidence for the observed match, limited for the eventual goal credit](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/1219).
- **Fowler’s century:** 100 goals in 165 appearances is confirmed independently by Liverpool FC. No claim that he was Liverpool’s fastest-ever scorer to 100 is made.
- **Brann:** accounts disagree about exact second-half goal minutes. The narrative uses sequence rather than spurious precision. The match was in the Cup Winners’ Cup; an older Liverpool FC player profile incorrectly calls it a UEFA Cup tie and was not relied on for the competition.
- **Fowler at Highbury:** the distinction concerning his penalty is checked against his reported explanation. The article does not embellish the gesture into a deliberate failed kick.
- **Manchester United:** the April defeat seriously damaged Liverpool’s title prospects but did not mathematically end them. The Wimbledon article correctly distinguishes that later elimination.
- **Owen:** the contemporary PA report calls him 18 in one passage. The copy uses the independently confirmed age of 17 years and 144 days. His youngest-scorer record is described as it stood then.
- **PSG:** a third unanswered Liverpool goal would have forced extra time, not automatically secured a place in the final.
- **Chelsea:** the club’s retrospective includes wider assertions about Barnes’s subsequent position and a historical comeback record. These were not needed and have been omitted.
- Minor one-minute discrepancies between match databases and newspaper descriptions are handled by avoiding unnecessary exact timing or following the match record where the chronology is secure.
