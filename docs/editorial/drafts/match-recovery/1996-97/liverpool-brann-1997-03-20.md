# Fowler’s goals and dockers message as Liverpool reach the last four

**Liverpool 3–0 Brann | 20 March 1997**  
European Cup Winners’ Cup, quarter-final, second leg · Anfield · Season 1996-97

Robbie Fowler scored twice to help Liverpool into the Cup Winners’ Cup semi-finals. His celebration also carried a message beyond the match: support for Liverpool’s sacked dockers.

Brann had held Liverpool 1–1 in Norway. The return at Anfield ended comfortably enough, but the visitors remained in the tie well into the second half.

Fowler gave Liverpool the lead from the penalty spot after Claus Eftevaag brought him down. The goal followed a combination between John Barnes and Steve McManaman, and put Liverpool 2–1 ahead on aggregate.

That margin left little room for error. Brann had threatened early through Tore André Flo, and Eirik Skjælaaen also had an opportunity before half-time. Liverpool were ahead without having removed the possibility of an away goal changing the tie.

Stan Collymore supplied the second soon after replacing Patrik Berger. Receiving Barnes’s pass, he drove at the defence. A challenge sent the ball against him and into the corner. It was an untidy finish to a forceful intervention.

Fowler completed the scoring from Stig Inge Bjørnebye’s delivery, making it 3–0 on the night and 4–1 on aggregate.

His display of a T-shirt supporting the dockers became a lasting part of the occasion. The wording was direct: “Support the sacked 500 dockers.” During a European quarter-final, Liverpool’s leading scorer had used his celebration to draw attention to a dispute involving workers from the city.

The football had brought Liverpool a place in the last four. Fowler’s gesture gave the evening a second significance, connecting a successful night at Anfield with a cause outside the ground.
