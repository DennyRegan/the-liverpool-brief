# Pallister’s headers turn the title race against Liverpool

**Liverpool 1–3 Manchester United | 19 April 1997**  
Premier League · Anfield · Season 1996-97

Liverpool could have gone top by beating Manchester United. Instead, they conceded three headers and left Anfield five points behind the leaders.

Gary Pallister scored twice before half-time. Andy Cole added the third after the break. John Barnes’s equaliser briefly restored Liverpool’s position in the match, but their difficulty defending balls into the penalty area decided it.

Pallister opened the scoring from a corner in the 13th minute. Six minutes later, Barnes met Jason McAteer’s delivery to bring Liverpool level.

The response mattered. This was still an opportunity to change the direction of the championship, with Liverpool close enough to pass United if they could win. Barnes’s goal gave them a chance to recover from the early setback.

Before half-time, another corner produced another Pallister header. Liverpool went into the interval behind for the second time.

Roy Evans introduced Stan Collymore for McAteer early in the second half, adding a forward as Liverpool chased the game. The next goal, however, came at the other end. David James misjudged Gary Neville’s cross and Cole headed in.

Liverpool had scored enough to remain involved but repeatedly failed to deal with United’s deliveries. Pallister’s two goals had exposed them from set pieces; Cole’s extended the damage from open play.

The defeat left Liverpool with three league matches remaining. United had four and a five-point lead. The championship was not mathematically settled, but Liverpool had lost the direct meeting that offered their clearest opportunity to take control.

An afternoon that could have put Evans’s side first instead gave United a substantial advantage for the run-in.
