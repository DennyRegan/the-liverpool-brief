Liverpool secured second place in the Premiership by beating Ipswich Town 5–0 at Anfield on 11 May 2002. John Arne Riise scored twice before half-time, and Michael Owen, Vladimír Šmicer and Nicolas Anelka completed a victory which left Manchester United third and sent Ipswich down.

The two clubs approached the final afternoon with very different requirements. Liverpool had moved into second by beating Blackburn Rovers 4–3 in their previous match. Another win would secure direct entry to the Champions League. Ipswich needed to win and hope Sunderland lost to Derby County to have any chance of surviving.

Riise opened the scoring early in the first half, finishing an attack involving Abel Xavier and Owen. Ipswich still created chances: Darren Bent's close-range header brought a save from Jerzy Dudek, and Martijn Reuser later struck a post.

Liverpool also suffered an interruption when Steven Gerrard went off after 33 minutes. His replacement made an immediate contribution. Šmicer drove forward before the ball reached Riise, who scored his second with another left-footed finish. The 2–0 interval lead left Ipswich facing a task beyond the one they had brought to Anfield.

Owen removed any realistic doubt just after the restart. Titus Bramble's headed attempt to return the ball to Andy Marshall fell short, and Owen reached it to lift his finish over the goalkeeper. Šmicer then added Liverpool's fourth, having already contributed to the second goal.

Gérard Houllier introduced Gary McAllister and Anelka in the closing stages. Anelka scored the fifth soon after coming on, finishing from Owen's pass. Liverpool had followed their 6–0 victory at Portman Road earlier in the season with another five against the same opponents.

Ipswich finished 18th; Sunderland's draw meant even a victory would not have saved them. Liverpool's outcome was the one they had set out to secure. The season ended with a home win, second place and direct entry to the next Champions League.
