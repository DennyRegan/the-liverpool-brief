For the second successive season, Danny Murphy scored the only goal of Liverpool's league visit to Old Trafford. His late finish on 22 January 2002 brought a 1–0 win, another double over Manchester United and Liverpool's fifth consecutive victory in the fixture, including the Charity Shield.

The effect on the table was immediate. United remained top on 45 points, but Liverpool climbed to third on 43. A match which could have widened the distance between the clubs instead left them separated by two points.

Phil Thompson's side had to work for the opportunity. Steven Gerrard was booked in the opening minute, and the first half remained goalless despite chances at both ends. Michael Owen shot wide after a United mistake had let John Arne Riise break through the middle. Ryan Giggs also missed the target as Liverpool's central defenders, Sami Hyypiä and Stéphane Henchoz, resisted the home attack.

With the game still level, Nicolas Anelka replaced Owen to join Emile Heskey in attack.

Anelka quickly forced a save from Fabien Barthez, who also kept out a Riise volley. Liverpool were beginning to produce opportunities without surrendering the protection in front of Jerzy Dudek.

The breakthrough came from Gerrard. His lofted pass went beyond United's defence and found Murphy running into the gap. Murphy lifted his finish over Barthez at the Stretford End, repeating his decisive contribution on Liverpool's previous league visit.

There was still defending to do. Dudek saved from Giggs as United tried to recover, and Patrik Berger came on for the goalscorer. The final whistle confirmed both the clean sheet and the double. Liverpool had beaten United with three goals at Anfield in November; at Old Trafford, one carefully made opening was enough.
