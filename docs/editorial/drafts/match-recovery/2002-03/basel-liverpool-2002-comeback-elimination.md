Liverpool came back from 3–0 down in Basel on 12 November 2002 and were still eliminated from the Champions League. They needed to win their final first-group-stage match. A late Michael Owen equaliser made it 3–3, but the point took Basel through and sent Liverpool into the UEFA Cup.

The damage was done before half-time. Julio Rossi scored in the second minute, Christian Giménez added another after 22 minutes and Timothée Atouba made it three before the half-hour. Hakan Yakin was central to Basel's attacks: he supplied the first two scorers and struck the free-kick from which Atouba scored the rebound.

Gérard Houllier replaced Steven Gerrard with Salif Diao at half-time. The first goal of the recovery came on the hour, when Vladimír Šmicer helped the ball to Danny Murphy and he finished from the edge of the area.

Milan Baroš then replaced Emile Heskey and quickly contributed to Liverpool's second. His flick reached Šmicer, who converted to reduce the deficit to one. The match had changed, although Basel still threatened to score again and Jerzy Dudek remained involved.

Houllier made a further attacking change by introducing El Hadji Diouf for Jamie Carragher. With five minutes left, a handball by Murat Yakin gave Liverpool a penalty. Pascal Zuberbühler saved Owen's kick, but Owen followed in to score from the rebound. It was an equaliser from open play after a missed penalty, rather than a successful spot-kick.

Liverpool could not find the fourth goal. They had recovered the entire half-time deficit and avoided defeat, but the qualification requirement was unchanged. Basel finished above them; Liverpool's European season would continue in another competition.
