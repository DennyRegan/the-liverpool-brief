Liverpool’s hold on the European Cup ended without a goal in either leg against Benfica. A 2–0 defeat at Anfield, following the 1–0 loss in Lisbon, eliminated the holders in the first knockout round on 8 March 2006.

Benítez paired Peter Crouch with Fernando Morientes and Liverpool quickly created the opportunities that might have changed the tie. Crouch struck the post after Luis García slipped him through. García then shot over, and another Crouch opening ended with goalkeeper Moretto blocking the attempt.

There were changes at the other end of the team. Sami Hyypiä was on the bench, Djimi Traoré partnered Jamie Carragher and Stephen Warnock played at left-back. Benfica’s attacking threat made Liverpool’s failure to score more serious. Geovanni hit the bar before Simão found the opening that transformed the arithmetic.

In the 36th minute Simão struck from outside the penalty area, beating Reina and giving Benfica an away goal. Liverpool now needed three. Carragher headed against a post before the interval, another opportunity lost at a time when the home side could still create genuine danger.

The second half brought pressure without the necessary breakthrough. Benítez introduced Cissé, Robbie Fowler and Hamann, but Benfica kept their aggregate lead intact. Even when Fowler put the ball in, the preceding corner was judged to have travelled out of play.

Fabrizio Miccoli, himself a substitute, volleyed in Benfica’s second near the end. The visitors had won both matches and could look forward to the quarter-finals. Liverpool’s early openings had offered a different possible evening; once those chances went and Simão scored, the task became progressively less manageable. The European title defence was over.
