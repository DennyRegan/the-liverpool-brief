Liverpool were eleven minutes from losing the European Super Cup when Rafael Benítez sent on Djibril Cissé in Monaco. By the end of extra time, the substitute had scored twice and made the third in a 3–1 victory over CSKA Moscow.

The European champions began without Steven Gerrard, whose calf injury left Jamie Carragher to captain the side. Fernando Morientes started at centre-forward, with Luis García among those trying to supply a breakthrough. Liverpool created openings but failed to finish them; CSKA were more effective when their chance arrived.

In the 28th minute Vágner Love released Daniel Carvalho through the defence. Carvalho advanced and beat Pepe Reina to give the UEFA Cup holders the lead. The scorer continued to threaten after half-time, while Liverpool’s pressure produced little reward.

Benítez had already introduced Florent Sinama-Pongolle and Mohamed Sissoko before replacing John Arne Riise with Cissé in the 79th minute. Three minutes later the Frenchman followed Reina’s clearance and García’s flick, benefited from the ricochet as Sergei Ignashevich challenged and put Liverpool level. A match that had been slipping away would require another half-hour.

Cissé’s second came in the 103rd minute. Dietmar Hamann’s pass sent him forward, Igor Akinfeev stopped the initial attempt and Cissé converted the rebound. The striker then delivered the cross from which García headed Liverpool’s third, completing the turnaround.

The sequence made this an unusually concentrated match-winning appearance: an equaliser, the lead and the pass which secured it, all after starting on the bench. Liverpool had won the Super Cup twice before, in 1977 and 2001. Their third was another European trophy earned by recovering from behind, with Cissé providing the finishing that had eluded them for most of the evening.
