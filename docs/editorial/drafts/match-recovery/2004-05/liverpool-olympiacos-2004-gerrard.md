Steven Gerrard's 86th-minute strike sent Liverpool into the Champions League knockout rounds on a night when even a narrow victory would have been insufficient. Trailing Olympiacos at half-time, Liverpool scored three times after the interval to advance on the head-to-head record between the clubs.

The qualification problem had become more severe in the 27th minute. Rivaldo, fouled by Sami Hyypiä after carrying the ball forward, scored from the resulting free-kick. With Monaco winning at Deportivo La Coruña, Liverpool now needed three unanswered goals. Gerrard had already hit a post, but the home side went in at the break 1–0 down.

Rafael Benítez replaced Djimi Traoré with Florent Sinama-Pongolle for the second half, adding a forward as Liverpool chased the game. Almost immediately Harry Kewell reached the byline and crossed for the substitute to finish from close range. Liverpool were level on the night, with most of the half still available, but still needed two more goals.

The second did not come quickly. Gerrard had an effort disallowed and picked up a booking which would rule him out of the next European match. Benítez made another attacking change, introducing Neil Mellor for Milan Baroš.

Mellor scored in the 80th minute after goalkeeper Antonis Nikopolidis failed to hold Antonio Núñez's header. That put Liverpool ahead, but Olympiacos were still on course to qualify: their 1–0 home victory and away goal at Anfield would decide the head-to-head comparison if the score stayed 2–1.

Six minutes later, Jamie Carragher sent the ball forward and Mellor headed it down. Gerrard met it outside the area and drove his shot beyond Nikopolidis. Liverpool finally held the two-goal margin they required.

The three goals had come from the captain and two substitutes, with Mellor also providing the final touch before Gerrard's winner. Benítez's changes were central to the recovery. Olympiacos dropped into the UEFA Cup; Liverpool had secured their place among the last sixteen and kept their first European campaign under the new manager alive.
