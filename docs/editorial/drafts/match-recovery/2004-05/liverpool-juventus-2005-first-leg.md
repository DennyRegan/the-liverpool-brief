Sami Hyypiä and Luis García scored inside the opening 25 minutes as Liverpool built a 2–0 lead against Juventus at Anfield. Fabio Cannavaro's second-half header reduced the margin, leaving Rafael Benítez's team with a 2–1 advantage to defend in Turin.

This was the clubs' first competitive meeting since the 1985 European Cup final at Heysel, where 39 people lost their lives. Phil Neal, Ian Rush and Michel Platini appeared on the pitch before the match, a reminder of the history surrounding the quarter-final.

Liverpool's selection included Scott Carson in goal and Anthony Le Tallec in attack. Carson was making only his third appearance for the club; Le Tallec was starting for Benítez for the first time. Against them stood a Juventus side which had conceded only twice in its eight Champions League matches that season.

Liverpool matched that total in little more than a quarter of this game. In the tenth minute, García flicked on Steven Gerrard's corner and Hyypiä finished at the far side of the area. Fifteen minutes later García supplied the second himself, striking a dipping volley beyond Gianluigi Buffon after Le Tallec's pass.

Juventus responded before the interval. Zlatan Ibrahimović hit a post soon after García's goal, and Carson saved from Alessandro Del Piero. Liverpool had played quickly and aggressively to establish their lead, but the visitors' chances showed that it was not secure.

The Italian side made changes, introducing Gianluca Pessotto at half-time and David Trezeguet shortly after the hour. Cannavaro then headed down towards goal in the 63rd minute. Carson got a hand to the ball but could not prevent it bouncing into the net.

Liverpool preserved the remaining advantage, with Núñez replacing Baroš and Šmicer coming on for Le Tallec. Their opening had provided two goals against an exceptionally difficult defence; Juventus's reply meant a 1–0 win in the return would be enough for the Italians on away goals. Anfield had given Liverpool a lead, but qualification still required a substantial performance away from home.
