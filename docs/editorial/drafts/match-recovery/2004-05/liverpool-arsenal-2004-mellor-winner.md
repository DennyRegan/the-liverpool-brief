Neil Mellor's shot in the second minute of added time gave Liverpool a 2–1 victory over Arsenal at Anfield. Patrick Vieira's equaliser had appeared likely to earn the champions a point, but Mellor struck from outside the penalty area to settle the game almost at its end.

Injuries had given Mellor an opportunity in a side which also included Florent Sinama-Pongolle. Behind them Liverpool could call on Steven Gerrard, Xabi Alonso and Dietmar Hamann, while Harry Kewell supported the attack. Arsenal's starting side contained Thierry Henry, Robert Pires and Vieira, together with the young Cesc Fàbregas.

Liverpool created the greater early threat. Kewell forced Jens Lehmann into a save with a header, and Gerrard had a penalty appeal rejected after a challenge involving Kolo Touré. Four minutes before half-time, the captain played a measured pass to Alonso on the edge of the area. Alonso's right-footed shot gave Liverpool a 1–0 lead.

The advantage lasted until the 57th minute. Arsenal put together a quick passing move and Vieira supplied the finish, bringing the visitors level. Arsène Wenger subsequently introduced Robin van Persie, while Benítez sent on Antonio Núñez for Sinama-Pongolle. It was Núñez's Liverpool debut.

The equaliser did not lead to an Arsenal winner. Liverpool continued to seek a second goal, and Lehmann saved again from Gerrard late in the game. Then a ball sent forward dropped outside the area and Mellor met it with a half-volley which travelled into the bottom corner at the Kop end.

Liverpool remained seventh, now with 23 points from fourteen matches. Arsenal stayed second on 31, five points behind Chelsea and having played a game more than Liverpool. The victory did not place Benítez's side in the title contest, but it was a significant league result against the reigning champions, earned by an attacking player given his chance amid injuries.
