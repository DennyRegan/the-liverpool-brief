Liverpool's first FA Cup tie under Rafael Benítez ended in defeat by Championship side Burnley. Djimi Traoré turned Richard Chaplow's low cross into his own net early in the second half, and Liverpool could not recover before Antonio Núñez's late dismissal reduced them to ten men.

The third-round match at Turf Moor had been postponed from 7 January because of heavy rain. When it was played eleven days later, Benítez selected a side containing David Raven, Zak Whitbread, John Welsh and Darren Potter. There was senior experience in Jerzy Dudek, Sami Hyypiä and Igor Bišćan, but Steven Gerrard was absent and Milan Baroš began on the bench.

Burnley dealt more effectively with the strong wind and heavy pitch. Chaplow and Micah Hyde tested Dudek as the home side established the greater threat. Liverpool reached half-time level, yet had done little to take command of the tie.

Florent Sinama-Pongolle headed over before the decisive moment. In the 51st minute Chaplow sent a low ball into Liverpool's penalty area and Traoré's attempted intervention put it past his own goalkeeper. Burnley had the lead their opponents would spend the remainder of the evening trying to remove.

Benítez introduced Baroš for Traoré in the 65th minute, then replaced Welsh with Neil Mellor ten minutes later. Both changes added forwards, but Liverpool failed to score. Chaplow instead had an opportunity to make Burnley's position more comfortable, shooting wide with Dudek to beat.

Núñez was sent off for violent conduct in the 87th minute. Liverpool finished a goal and a player down, eliminated at their first hurdle. Burnley, managed by Steve Cotterill, had advanced without needing a goal credited to one of their own players.

The defeat belonged to the uneven domestic side of Liverpool's season. Their League Cup semi-final remained in progress and their Champions League campaign would resume in February, but their FA Cup involvement was already over.
