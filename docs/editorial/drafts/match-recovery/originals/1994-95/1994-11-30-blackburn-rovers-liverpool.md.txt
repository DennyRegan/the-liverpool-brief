Ian Rush marked his 600th Liverpool appearance by scoring all three goals in a 3-1 League Cup win at Blackburn Rovers. His 16th hat-trick for the club, on 30 November 1994, would also be his last.

The fourth-round tie brought Liverpool to the home of the Premier League leaders and reunited Rush with Kenny Dalglish, now in charge at Ewood Park. Liverpool had lost 3-2 there in the league six weeks earlier. This time, Rush's finishing and David James's goalkeeping took them into the quarter-finals.

Blackburn threatened first. James saved from Tim Sherwood and blocked Alan Shearer, while Stuart Ripley's header struck the bar. Liverpool then went ahead in the 19th minute: Robbie Fowler moved the ball inside from the left and Rush beat Tim Flowers with a left-footed shot from the edge of the area.

James continued to frustrate Blackburn before Rush added a second after the interval. John Scales headed forward, Rob Jones helped the ball on and Rush ran beyond Henning Berg to finish under Flowers.

The third came from a defensive mistake. Graeme Le Saux headed a Stig Inge Bjørnebye cross up into the air, leaving Rush to score from close range. Chris Sutton's late header reduced the margin, but Blackburn were out.

Rush would score once more in that season's competition, the winner against Arsenal in the next round, as Liverpool continued towards a final they would win against Bolton Wanderers.
