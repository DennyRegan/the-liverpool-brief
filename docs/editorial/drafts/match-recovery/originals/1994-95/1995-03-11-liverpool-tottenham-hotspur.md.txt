Three days after reaching the League Cup final, Liverpool lost their chance of a domestic cup double. Tottenham Hotspur came from behind to win 2-1 at Anfield on 11 March 1995, with Jürgen Klinsmann scoring the decisive goal in the 89th minute.

Liverpool's lead had come from Mark Walters's work on the left. He beat two challenges, drew defenders towards him and crossed for Robbie Fowler, who headed in after 38 minutes. Having scored the winner at Crystal Palace in midweek, Fowler had put Liverpool ahead in another cup tie.

Tottenham equalised before the interval. David Howells found Klinsmann, who laid the ball to Teddy Sheringham. From outside the penalty area, Sheringham curled a right-footed shot past David James and in off the post.

The sides remained level deep into the second half. Roy Evans replaced Walters and John Barnes with Stig Inge Bjørnebye and Michael Thomas in the 72nd minute, but Liverpool could not regain the lead.

With a replay approaching, Darren Anderton returned a John Scales clearance towards the Liverpool defence. Sheringham helped the ball on to Klinsmann, whose finish beat James and put Spurs into the semi-finals.

Liverpool's FA Cup campaign ended at the sixth round, leaving the League Cup final against Bolton Wanderers as their remaining opportunity for a cup that season.
