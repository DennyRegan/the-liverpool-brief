Liverpool's place in the League Cup semi-finals was secured by a free-kick routine practised that morning. John Barnes, Neil Ruddock and Ian Rush combined to produce the only goal against Arsenal at Anfield on 11 January 1995.

The breakthrough came in the 59th minute. John Jensen fouled Jamie Redknapp near the edge of the penalty area; Barnes moved the free-kick to Ruddock, whose pass released Rush behind Arsenal's defence. The captain finished to settle a quarter-final in which Liverpool had repeatedly threatened without scoring.

David Seaman had recovered from an early mis-kick to block Steve McManaman. Later in the first half, Lee Dixon cleared off the line with Rush waiting to score, and the Liverpool striker headed another McManaman delivery straight at the goalkeeper.

Arsenal survived those openings but could not answer Rush's goal. Ian Wright received little useful service, and Liverpool completed a fifth successive clean sheet. Roy Evans's side had now gone 11 matches unbeaten.

For Rush, this followed his hat-trick at Blackburn in the previous round. He had scored four goals across two ties against Premier League opposition.

Crystal Palace would be the semi-final opponents. Liverpool won both legs 1-0 before beating Bolton Wanderers at Wembley, but the January quarter-final had required patience: after Fowler's rapid treble against Arsenal in August, it was Rush who finally found a way past Seaman this time.
