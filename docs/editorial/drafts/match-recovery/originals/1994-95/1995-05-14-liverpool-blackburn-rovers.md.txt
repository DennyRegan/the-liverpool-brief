Jamie Redknapp won the match for Liverpool, and Kenny Dalglish still won the championship. Anfield's final afternoon of the 1994-95 season ended with a 2-1 home victory over Blackburn Rovers and a title celebration for the beaten visitors.

Dalglish's side arrived on 14 May knowing that victory would secure the Premier League. Anything less left them dependent on Manchester United's result at West Ham. Liverpool, meanwhile, had their own game to win, despite the affection for their former manager and the prospect of helping United.

Alan Shearer put Blackburn ahead after 20 minutes. Having helped release Stuart Ripley on the right, he met the winger's return pass and finished past David James. It was his 34th league goal of the season.

Liverpool equalised in the 64th minute. Steve McManaman found Mark Kennedy on the left, Kennedy delivered a low cross and John Barnes steered it in. Blackburn subsequently had a chance to restore their lead when James blocked Shearer and the ball fell to Chris Sutton, but John Scales got back to clear Sutton's effort.

Then Redknapp scored from a free-kick in stoppage time, after Colin Hendry had fouled McManaman. Liverpool had come from behind to beat the leaders. For Blackburn, everything now rested on the score from Upton Park.

United could manage only a 1-1 draw. Blackburn finished with 89 points to United's 88, and the championship belonged to Dalglish's team despite their defeat. Liverpool supporters applauded the new champions as they celebrated at Anfield.

Roy Evans's side finished fourth on 74 points. They had beaten both title contenders at home during the run-in; on the last day, their victory and Dalglish's triumph happened together.
