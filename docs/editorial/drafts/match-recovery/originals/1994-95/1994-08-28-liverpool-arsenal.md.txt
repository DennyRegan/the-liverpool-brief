Arsenal had reached the 25-minute mark at Anfield without conceding. By the 31st, Robbie Fowler had scored three times. The 19-year-old's hat-trick on 28 August 1994 took four minutes and 33 seconds, decided a 3-0 Liverpool victory and set a Premier League record.

Fowler seized on a loose ball in the penalty area to score his first. For the second, Steve McManaman carried possession forward and found him on the left of the box. Fowler's shot went through Lee Dixon's legs and in off the far post.

The third required another attempt. John Barnes sent Fowler through, but his first shot hit David Seaman. Fowler followed up from a difficult angle to complete his treble.

Seaman had Tony Adams and Martin Keown in front of him, with Dixon and Nigel Winterburn completing Arsenal's back four. Fowler had broken into Liverpool's first team the previous season; this was only his second league appearance of 1994-95.

Fowler's record finally fell in May 2015, when Sadio Mané scored three times for Southampton against Aston Villa in two minutes and 56 seconds. For almost 21 years, however, the Premier League's fastest hat-trick belonged to a teenager in Liverpool's No.23 shirt, and to one brief, devastating spell against Arsenal.
