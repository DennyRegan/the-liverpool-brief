Roy Evans's first full season in charge began with six goals at Selhurst Park. Liverpool beat Crystal Palace 6-1 on 20 August 1994, with Ian Rush and Steve McManaman scoring twice each.

Jan Mølby started it from the penalty spot. Rob Jones had been brought down in the area, and the Dane converted after 12 minutes. Two minutes later, Mølby helped create a very different goal, sending a first-time pass down the left for McManaman. The winger came inside and curled his shot in from the edge of the box.

Liverpool had established a two-goal advantage before the quarter-hour. Robbie Fowler ran through to make it 3-0 just before half-time.

The home side did briefly reduce the damage. Chris Armstrong headed past David James four minutes after the restart, making the score 3-1. Liverpool's reply came from Rush, who restored the three-goal margin on the hour. McManaman then added his second before Rush completed the scoring.

The margin put Liverpool top of the table on the opening Saturday. That position carried little weight with almost an entire season still to play, but the next match sustained the scoring: Arsenal were beaten 3-0 at Anfield eight days later. Liverpool had begun with nine goals and two wins.
