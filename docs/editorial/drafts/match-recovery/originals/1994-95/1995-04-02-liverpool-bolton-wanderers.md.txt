Steve McManaman scored both Liverpool goals in the 2-1 victory over Bolton Wanderers at Wembley on 2 April 1995. The win brought the club a fifth League Cup and gave Roy Evans his first trophy as manager, though Bolton made Liverpool work to protect it.

Bruce Rioch's First Division side threatened before McManaman struck. David Lee sent a lob narrowly over, and David James tipped Alan Thompson's volley onto the crossbar. Liverpool took the lead in the 37th minute when McManaman collected John Barnes's pass, ran beyond Alan Stubbs and Scott Green and finished past Keith Branagan.

Bolton continued to create chances after half-time. Mixu Paatelainen volleyed wide after James came out of his area, while Liverpool's Stig Inge Bjørnebye hit the post at the other end.

McManaman scored again after 68 minutes. Jamie Redknapp started the move in Liverpool's half and passed to him on the left. McManaman cut inside, beat the defenders in his path and curled a low shot beyond Branagan. The two-goal lead lasted only two minutes: Thompson turned and hooked in Bolton's reply.

Liverpool still had twenty minutes to negotiate. Bolton's goal ensured there would be no comfortable finish, but Evans's side held their advantage and Ian Rush collected the trophy as captain.

McManaman received the man-of-the-match award. Redknapp, recalling the final years later, credited his team-mate with taking the spotlight on an afternoon when so much pre-match attention had been on Robbie Fowler. Fowler had scored both semi-final goals; at Wembley, McManaman supplied the two that won the cup.
