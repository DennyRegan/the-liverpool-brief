# Three leads, one Ravanelli: Liverpool’s six-goal opening day

**Middlesbrough 3–3 Liverpool | 17 August 1996**  
Premier League · Riverside Stadium · Season 1996-97

Liverpool led three times at the Riverside. Each time, Fabrizio Ravanelli brought Middlesbrough back.

The Italian’s hat-trick on his English league debut made this an opening day remembered principally on Teesside. For Roy Evans, there was another way to read it: Liverpool had scored three away from home and still failed to win.

Ravanelli had arrived from Juventus for £7 million. Middlesbrough also gave a debut to Brazilian midfielder Emerson, adding to a side already containing Juninho. Liverpool nevertheless struck first. In the fourth minute, Stig Inge Bjørnebye drove home his first goal for the club after a free-kick had been delivered into the penalty area.

The lead lasted until Ravanelli converted a penalty awarded after Juninho went down under a challenge. John Barnes restored Liverpool’s advantage almost immediately, controlling the ball on his chest before finishing in off a post.

Barnes was providing more than a goal. His use of possession helped Liverpool work through Middlesbrough, but the home side continued to find answers. Ravanelli met Neil Cox’s delivery to make it 2–2 before the interval.

Bjørnebye supplied Liverpool’s third, crossing for an unmarked Robbie Fowler to finish in the 65th minute. Once again, the visitors had something to protect.

They held it until nine minutes remained. Juninho won possession from Steve McManaman, and a loose ball in the area reached Ravanelli. His finish completed the hat-trick. David James then had to deny him a fourth.

Liverpool’s attacking football had produced enough chances and goals to win. What Evans had not received was the control needed after taking the lead. The first afternoon of the season ended with a point and three separate opportunities to have taken all three.
