Michael Owen scored twice after half-time to give Liverpool a 2–0 lead to take back from Rome. The finishes were different — one following an intercepted pass, the other a glancing header — but both punished the space Roma allowed him after a first half in which Liverpool had conceded very little.

Gérard Houllier started Owen alongside Robbie Fowler in the UEFA Cup fourth-round first leg. Nick Barmby, Dietmar Hamann, Gary McAllister and Christian Ziege provided the midfield personnel, with the familiar defensive partnership of Sami Hyypiä and Stéphane Henchoz in front of Sander Westerveld.

Roma's clearest first-half opening came late, when Marco Delvecchio headed wide from Alessandro Rinaldi's delivery. The interval arrived at 0–0. Liverpool had kept themselves in the game without yet forcing a breakthrough.

Rinaldi's next decisive involvement helped the visitors instead. Just after the restart, Owen intercepted his attempted pass and drove his shot beyond Francesco Antonioli. A match shaped by Liverpool's resistance now required Roma to chase an equaliser.

Westerveld saved from substitute Marcos Assunção, and Fabio Capello introduced Gabriel Batistuta as Roma sought more attacking force. Liverpool nevertheless found the next goal. Ziege regained the opportunity to cross after a free-kick had been cleared, and Owen moved into space to glance the delivery past Antonioli.

Barmby almost added a third, only for the goalkeeper to block his shot. Owen later made way for Jari Litmanen, having supplied the two goals that made Liverpool clear favourites for the return.

The result was not qualification in itself. Roma won 1–0 at Anfield a week later, but Liverpool progressed 2–1 on aggregate. The clean sheet and Owen's two finishes in Italy had provided exactly the margin they needed. On a ground associated with Liverpool's European Cup victories, Houllier's team had produced a European result of its own.
