## Conflicts, exclusions and editorial dispositions

- **Everton 2003:** the PA page’s goal list gives Owen’s second as 51, its prose and LFChistory as 52. Article uses sequence without an exact minute. PA title wrongly says Anfield in browser metadata; page body and independent record establish Goodison Park. No attendance used.
- **Chelsea January 2004:** Luzi substitution is 76/77 across accounts; article says late second half. LFChistory records the second Diouf caution being rescinded later; this does not alter that Liverpool finished with ten on the night. Article confines itself to that event.
- **Marseille 2004:** BBC archive summary/prose and record differ on penalty/red-card minutes; article uses before the interval. BBC suggests the initial contact was outside the area, so the prose reports the referee’s decision without claiming the foul location was indisputable. It does not assert a particular post-dismissal formation.
- **Arsenal April 2004:** Arsenal official retrospective search excerpt was retrieved but full opening returned 403; removed from public list. The contemporary report and LFChistory record independently cover retained claims. No unsupported Henry third-goal mechanism is added.
- **United April 2004:** distinction preserved between Liverpool remaining fourth and having clinched qualification. Newcastle’s games in hand are included. The visiting penalty milestone is explicitly Premier League, not all football.
- **Olympiacos:** qualification requirement is conditional on the opposing away goal and Monaco’s result; not presented as an unconditional pre-kick-off requirement to win by two. An exact first-minute/47th-minute dispute for the equaliser is avoided.
- **Burnley:** RTÉ search excerpt was obtained but page opening returned 403; it is not a public source. The retained action is independently available in UEFA and the specialist record. No inference is made about the manager disrespecting the competition.
- **League Cup final:** Riise timing varies between 43, 45 and 46 seconds. Article uses inside the first minute. UEFA report puts Kežman three minutes after Drogba; Reuters and LFChistory support five minutes/112th minute, which is used. No attendance is given.
- **Juventus second leg:** contemporary formation descriptions vary with phase of play. Prose describes Traoré moving centrally and Riise dropping back, avoiding an invariant formation label. Cissé’s recovery is described without rounding absence length to six months.
- **Chelsea semi-final:** the awarded goal is a settled match fact; whether all of the ball crossed remains disputed. No supposedly conclusive replay judgement, automatic red-card counterfactual or invented referee motive is included. Huth’s attacking role comes from the Echo account, not the player-list position.
- **Arsenal November 2004:** UEFA/LFChistory agree on Alonso 41, Vieira 57, Mellor 90+2. Club report provides action, not a retrospective causal claim about Champions League success. Unavailable legacy members page is excluded.
