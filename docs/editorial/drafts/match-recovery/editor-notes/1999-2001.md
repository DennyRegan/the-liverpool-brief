# Editorial notes: 1999–2000 and 2000–01

Research, writing and first factual audit: configured model **gpt-6-astra**, actual worker **/root/astra_1999_2001**. Retrieval and drafting date: 29 September 2026. No other runtime/run identifier was exposed. Independent package review is being carried out by the parent's Astra audit worker; do not treat its unfinished review as completed here.

Fourteen factual review drafts: five in 1999–00, nine in 2000–01. The selection and compact full-match records are in `research/1999-2001.md`. They include both starting elevens, used substitutes, competitions, dates, venues, results, scoring sequences and conflicts. Public prose deliberately uses only relevant personnel and incidents.

The commission expressly prohibited commit/push/publication. Accordingly these files are outside site content loaders, and neither the shared calendar nor published articles were changed. Calendar ownership was checked in the supplied latest-main reference. This package is not a replacement authoritative calendar. All `editorialMode: factual` fields are proposals. `date` is omitted with the agreed comment; the historical date is never used as a publication date.

## Confidence convention

**High**: reliable for the stated scope after comparison with another record or account. **Medium**: a credible retrospective or an identifiable contemporary report preserved on a secondary host, used selectively and checked against another source. **Low**: rejected/unsafe for the specified field. A label applies to the claim scope, not to every sentence on a page. Multiple URLs reproducing the same report are not independent witnesses.

No direct quotation is used in the public articles. No whole report has been copied into this package. The writing divides its factual support among match ledgers, contemporary reporting and independent club/competition checks; near-verbatim phrasing and decorative detail from the sources have been avoided.

## Conflicts, rejected fields and limitations

- Liverpool–Birmingham ledger names substitute/penalty taker **Michael Hughes**. Contemporary BBC consistently names **Bryan Hughes**. Internal record corrects the identity; public prose does not need his name.
- Fowler's League Cup goal: BBC report describes an initial Henchoz clearance; club accounts describe Westerveld's goal-kick. The public account starts with Heskey's aerial touch, which the sources agree on.
- UEFA's 2018 Roma retrospective incorrectly includes Heskey in the February 2001 XI. BBC and LFChistory both identify Barmby; use those. Do not claim official status makes every field correct.
- [Two down, one to go](https://www.lfchistory.net/articles/1653) was actually retrieved but removed from the FA-final public source list. **Low** for its corrupted Arsenal lineup, which contains later players; **Medium** for general match narrative corroborated elsewhere. No unsupported lineup imported from it.
- The club's 2015 FA-final recap places McAllister's introduction after Arsenal scored. Ledger and BBC clockwatch place it around the hour, before the goal. Draft follows the latter chronology.
- Goal times vary across reports: Wednesday, Bradford, Roma, Barcelona and Alavés. Disputed precision is removed instead of silently presented as settled. Alavés dismissals are described without their conflicting minute stamps.
- Old Trafford report's narrative and footer disagree over Chadwick's red-card time. Charlton report's appended statistics are implausible. Neither field is used.
- The Golden Goal own-goal ended the UEFA Cup final immediately; the League Cup final was 1–1 after extra time and **separately** 5–4 on penalties. No confusion of regulation, aggregate or shootout scores remains.
- No claim to comprehensive film review. No embedded photographs/video, invented visual colour, invented dialogue, medical details or off-field allegations. Direct BBC-domain search was blocked; identified BBC reports and clockwatches were retrieved through LFChistory's public preservation pages.
- The legacy club McAllister(94) video-page URL was initially retrieved with a descriptive text excerpt but redirected to the club homepage on final checking. It was replaced in public sources with the stable, retrieved Gary McAllister profile. No footage from it was watched.
- No relevant public claim remains marked UNVERIFIED. This means the selected factual prose has cited support; it does not imply every statistic on every source page was verified. Remaining editorial decisions concern selection, titles, proposed tags and publication approval.

## First finished-prose review

Checked each piece for chronology, competition, result, scorers, substitute identity, player/manager names, missing aggregate/shootout context, later hindsight and unsupported rhetoric. The larger finals retain the match's decisive stages, including Arsenal's superiority and the unawarded handball, Birmingham's second penalty appeal, and Alavés' repeated recoveries. Defeats in 1999–00 remain defeats rather than being recast as inevitable preparation for later success.

All fourteen use British English and the canonical `YYYY-YY` season. Exact event dates and filename/slug match were checked. Only existing registry IDs were used; missing optional opposition and venue tags remain absent. No publication is authorised or implied.
