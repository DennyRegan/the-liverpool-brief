# Liverpool 4–2 Southampton: Fowler’s first league hat-trick holds off Le Tissier

Robbie Fowler needed three goals to settle a match Matt Le Tissier twice threatened to rescue for Southampton. Liverpool won 4–2 at Anfield, but neither of their two-goal leads had been comfortable for long.

Fowler had scored five against Fulham earlier that month. Against Southampton, he secured his first league hat-trick.

Fowler headed in Rob Jones’s cross after 14 minutes, then controlled Neil Ruddock’s long pass on his chest and beat Flowers for a second. With less than half an hour played, Liverpool led 2–0.

Le Tissier halved the deficit before the interval. Taking Iain Dowie’s knockdown, he manipulated the ball past Mark Wright and finished beyond Bruce Grobbelaar. Southampton were back in the game despite Fowler’s early double.

Flowers resisted Liverpool after half-time, but Ian Rush restored the two-goal advantage when the goalkeeper blocked Paul Stewart’s effort and left a rebound. Again Le Tissier replied, running on to Glenn Cockerill’s pass and finishing to make it 3–2.

Fowler finally removed the uncertainty with a free-kick from the right. Rush made a run towards the delivery, but it continued into the net without his touch. After initial doubt over the scorer, the goal was credited to Fowler.

His hat-trick was complete in the 85th minute. Liverpool’s 18-year-old had supplied the first two goals and the last, with Rush scoring between them. Southampton’s two replies had both come from Le Tissier.

The victory moved Liverpool to seventh. Fowler, still only 18, had followed his five-goal League Cup performance with three more in the league.
