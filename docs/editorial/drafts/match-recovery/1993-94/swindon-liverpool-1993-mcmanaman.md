# Swindon Town 0–5 Liverpool: McManaman scores twice as Liverpool go top at Swindon

Liverpool went top of the league for the first time under Graeme Souness with a five-goal win at Swindon. Three matches into the season, they had nine points and ten goals. Steve McManaman had supplied the best of this latest victory.

The newly promoted hosts were still looking for their first point. Their willingness to pass the ball gave Liverpool opportunities to win it back and attack before the defence could recover. McManaman repeatedly made those openings count.

Neil Ruddock scored his first Liverpool goal after 19 minutes. Stig Inge Bjørnebye’s free-kick reached him inside the penalty area, where he controlled the ball and finished. McManaman doubled the advantage before half-time, meeting Steve Nicol’s cross with a volley.

His second goal showed a different part of his game. Released by Ronnie Whelan after Swindon lost possession, McManaman ran at Ross MacLaren, pushed the ball through the defender’s legs and lifted his finish over Fraser Digby. Liverpool led 3–0, and Swindon had no answer to the speed and precision of the attack.

Whelan drove in the fourth from outside the penalty area. Substitute Mike Marsh completed the scoring after McManaman created the opening, giving Liverpool five goals without a contribution from either starting striker, Ian Rush or Nigel Clough.

The result took them above Everton on goal difference. It was Liverpool’s first appearance at the summit since March 1991, before Souness had succeeded Kenny Dalglish.

Souness resisted the wider conclusions. Afterwards, he said it was too early to talk about a revival and criticised the chances his side had wasted. Against Swindon, Liverpool had been comfortably superior. Whether they could sustain it remained another question.
