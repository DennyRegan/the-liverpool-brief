# Classic Liverpool Games, 1970–1990

## Purpose

This is the Archive Editor’s researched editorial longlist for the 20 complete seasons from 1970–71 through 1989–90. It is a queue of potential articles, not a substitute for the normal match-research workflow.

The selection includes triumphs, defining defeats, dramatic draws, turning points and exceptional occasions. It is intentionally uneven: historic seasons contain more entries than quieter ones. A match is included because it has a credible story, not to meet a quota.

**Longlist total: 199 matches.**

## How to use this file

When asked for a classic match, the Archive Editor may use an entry from this list. The request can name:

- a specific match or ID;
- a season;
- a competition, opponent, manager or theme;
- a priority level; or
- “the next strongest unwritten match”.

Before drafting, establish the full record independently and follow the Archive Editor’s research and fact-checking workflow. The short angle below is an editorial lead, not verified article copy. Do not repeat it as fact without fresh research. Check the repository before choosing an entry so an existing article is not duplicated.

Priority meanings:

- **A — Essential:** central to the club’s history or a particularly powerful standalone story.
- **B — Strong:** clearly warrants an archive article, but sits behind the essential queue.
- **C — Selective:** worthwhile when it suits a date, season, theme or connected-history gap.

## Recommended production order

The IDs are chronological, not a strict writing sequence. Start with Priority A, but vary the queue so the archive does not become a succession of finals. A sensible rotation is:

1. a defining European match;
2. a major league or derby match;
3. a domestic cup match;
4. an exceptional performance, upset or unusual occasion;
5. then move to another season.

The strongest immediate queue includes 001, 004, 006, 013, 023–026, 029, 034, 036, 038, 047, 052–054, 057–058, 062–067, 068, 072, 074, 077–079, 082–084, 086, 092, 097–100, 107, 109–110, 114–120, 123, 128, 132, 135–141, 147, 150–151, 155, 159–161, 164, 168, 170–171, 175–180, 185–191, 193, 197–198.

## Special handling

### Hillsborough

Entry 185 must never be assigned or written as an ordinary “classic game”. Begin with the Hillsborough Independent Panel report and later official findings. Do not reproduce discredited allegations about supporters. Do not use footballing drama, spectacle or anniversary content conventions to frame the disaster.

### Heysel

Entry 150 also requires a separate research plan. The deaths of 39 people and the causes and consequences of the disaster must take precedence over the football. Separate supported fact, official finding, contemporary reporting and later recollection.

### Testimonials and ceremonial matches

Entry 042 is included for its club and supporter history. It should be researched as an occasion—why it was staged, who attended and what it represented—not assessed by competitive standards.
