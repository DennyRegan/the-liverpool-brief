Bruno Cheyrou scored the only goal as Liverpool won a Premier League match at Stamford Bridge for the first time. The result required more than his first-half finish: Gérard Houllier's side lost Jerzy Dudek to injury, gave Patrice Luzi his debut and completed the game with ten men.

Eight months earlier, defeat at Chelsea had cost Liverpool a Champions League place. This visit found them still pursuing a return to the competition, with Michael Owen among the substitutes and Emile Heskey leading the attack. Cheyrou, making only his second start of the season, played behind him.

Chelsea suffered an early disruption when Hernán Crespo went off injured after twelve minutes. Eidur Gudjohnsen replaced him and volleyed narrowly wide, but the home side struggled to turn their attacking personnel into sustained pressure.

Cheyrou supplied the decisive movement in the 33rd minute. After exchanging passes with Heskey, he continued into the area to meet the striker's cross with a first-time finish beyond Carlo Cudicini. It was his first Premier League goal.

Claudio Ranieri altered Chelsea's midfield, moving Joe Cole to the left before eventually replacing him with Jesper Grønkjær. Frank Lampard tested Dudek, while Heskey continued to provide Liverpool with an outlet. The visitors had a lead to protect, but their task became more difficult when Dudek suffered a groin injury.

Luzi came on late in the second half for his first Liverpool appearance. Adrian Mutu threatened the equaliser, heading against the crossbar and being denied by the substitute goalkeeper at close range. Then, in the 87th minute, El Hadji Diouf received a second yellow card following a challenge involving Mutu. Liverpool had to see out the remaining minutes a player short.

They did so to move into fifth place, two points behind fourth-placed Charlton Athletic with a game in hand. Cheyrou's finish supplied the lead; the team's resistance, including an unplanned contribution from Luzi, preserved it.
