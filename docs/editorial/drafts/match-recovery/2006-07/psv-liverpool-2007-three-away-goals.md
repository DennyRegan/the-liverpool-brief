Liverpool made the first leg of their Champions League quarter-final at PSV decisive, winning 3–0 through Gerrard, Riise and Crouch. All three goals had arrived soon after the hour, leaving the return at Anfield with a very different shape from the finely balanced Barcelona tie.

Benítez started Crouch and Kuyt in attack, with Mascherano and Alonso in midfield. Finnan’s contribution from right-back became particularly valuable. PSV, missing central defender Alex, used Timmy Simons in the back line and initially kept the game cautious.

Gomes saved a Carragher header from a Gerrard corner before Liverpool took the lead in the 27th minute. Finnan crossed from the right and Gerrard arrived to head into the corner. PSV had briefly threatened through Mika Väyrynen, but Liverpool had found the more convincing route to goal.

The second came early after the interval. A poor clearance reached Riise outside the penalty area and he struck left-footed past Gomes. Ronald Koeman introduced Patrick Kluivert as PSV tried to improve their attack, but the next goal also belonged to Liverpool.

Again Finnan provided the delivery. Crouch rose to head home, adding to the hat-trick he had scored against Arsenal three days earlier. Liverpool could now manage the rest of the match with three away goals and a clean sheet to protect.

The significant setback was an injury to Aurélio, who had to leave on a stretcher. The club subsequently confirmed a ruptured Achilles tendon which would keep him out for the rest of the season. It was a serious loss on an otherwise successful evening.

Liverpool finished the job by winning the second leg 1–0, taking the tie 4–0 on aggregate. The foundations had been laid in Eindhoven: two accurate Finnan crosses, decisive finishing and enough control to prevent PSV from finding a foothold.
