Liverpool played much of the 2007 European Cup final on competitive terms with Milan, but the decisive finishing belonged to Filippo Inzaghi. His goals either side of half-time gave the Italian side a 2–1 victory in Athens. Dirk Kuyt’s late header offered Liverpool a brief chance of another recovery, without enough time to complete one.

Benítez used Kuyt as his lone striker, with Gerrard supporting from an advanced midfield position. Jermaine Pennant and Bolo Zenden played wide; Alonso and Mascherano provided the central protection. It was a different starting arrangement from the semi-final second leg, with Crouch back among the substitutes and Alonso restored to the team.

Milan’s side contained the experience of Paolo Maldini, Alessandro Nesta and Andrea Pirlo, with Kaká behind Inzaghi. The early stages gave Liverpool reason to believe their plan could work. They competed in midfield, avoided the rapid damage suffered in Istanbul two years earlier and created openings of their own.

Pennant had an early shot saved by Dida after Liverpool exploited a mistake on Milan’s left. He continued to offer an outlet on the right, while Alonso sent an effort narrowly wide. Milan also threatened through Kaká, whose shot Reina saved, but neither team had broken through as half-time approached.

Then Alonso fouled Kaká just outside the penalty area. Pirlo took the free-kick, the ball struck Inzaghi and changed direction, and Reina was beaten. Liverpool had conceded at the moment when an even interval score had looked likely. The deflection gave Milan a 1–0 lead and altered the demands of the second half.

Benítez replaced Zenden with Harry Kewell as Liverpool searched for more penetration. Their clearest opening fell to Gerrard just after the hour. A misplaced Gennaro Gattuso pass allowed the captain to move through the Milan defence, but his finish lacked the force or placement to beat Dida. The goalkeeper saved, and the opportunity to restore parity passed.

Liverpool still had Crouch available. Benítez introduced him for Mascherano with the game entering its final stages, placing another forward alongside Kuyt and reducing the defensive cover in midfield. It was an attempt to create the goal they needed; Milan instead found the next one.

Kaká supplied the pass which released Inzaghi beyond Liverpool’s defence. The striker went past Reina and finished from an angle in the 82nd minute. His first goal had benefited from a deflection; this one depended on the timing of the run, the pass and the finish. Milan now had a two-goal cushion.

Liverpool replied before normal time ended. Agger helped on a corner and Kuyt headed in at close range, cutting the deficit to one. It ensured a tense finish but produced no repeat of Istanbul. Milan held on, winning their seventh European Cup and reversing the result of the final between the clubs two years earlier.

For Liverpool, Athens was a defeat shaped by opportunities missed and moments conceded. Their opening approach had kept Milan within reach, and Gerrard’s chance offered a route back after the first goal. Inzaghi converted the opportunities which fell his way; Liverpool’s only goal arrived when there was scarcely any match left.
