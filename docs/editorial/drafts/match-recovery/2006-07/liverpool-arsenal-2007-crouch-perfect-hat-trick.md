Peter Crouch returned from a nose operation to score a perfect hat-trick as Liverpool beat Arsenal 4–1 and moved above them into third place. On 31 March 2007, the opponents who had won both January cup ties at Anfield were beaten by a Liverpool attack which repeatedly found quality from the flanks.

Crouch began as the main striker, with Gerrard supporting him and Jermaine Pennant and Mark González in the wide positions. Javier Mascherano and Alonso played in midfield. Within four minutes Liverpool’s right side produced the opening goal: Pennant released Arbeloa and the full-back crossed low for Crouch to finish at the near post.

The second came from the other flank before half-time. Aurélio delivered a cross which Crouch met with a forceful header, beating Jens Lehmann and giving Liverpool a 2–0 lead. The striker’s first club hat-trick was now within reach, though Arsenal still created opportunities of their own.

Emmanuel Adebayor hit a post after the break. Liverpool responded with another goal from an Aurélio delivery, this time a free-kick headed in by Agger around the hour. Reina later turned an Adebayor header against the woodwork, keeping the three-goal advantage intact for a little longer.

William Gallas eventually scored from close range after a corner. It made the score 3–1 and gave Arsenal a short-lived hope of a recovery, but Crouch settled the matter. He controlled the ball in the area, turned past Touré and finished left-footed to complete his treble.

The three finishes showed different parts of Crouch’s game: anticipation for the first, aerial strength for the second and control in a crowded penalty area for the third. Liverpool had a decisive league win, while Crouch’s return offered an immediate attacking boost before the Champions League quarter-final at PSV.
