Liverpool conceded six goals at Anfield as Arsenal eliminated them from the League Cup on 9 January 2007. Júlio Baptista scored four and could even afford to have a penalty saved in a 6–3 quarter-final victory. Three days after losing their FA Cup to the same opponents, Liverpool were out of both domestic cup competitions.

Both managers changed their teams. Liverpool’s starting eleven combined established players such as Gerrard, Hyypiä, Fowler and Dudek with Lee Peltier, Gabriel Paletta and Danny Guthrie. Arsenal also fielded a much-changed side, but their attacking partnership of Jérémie Aliadière and Baptista repeatedly found space.

An early injury to Mark González forced Benítez to introduce Luis García. Arsenal went ahead when Aliadière controlled Kolo Touré’s pass and beat Dudek at the second attempt. Fowler replied with a close-range flick after García sent the ball across goal, briefly bringing Liverpool level.

The match then moved decisively away from them. Baptista scored with a free-kick, Alex Song added another and Aliadière provided Baptista with his second before half-time. Arsenal had turned 1–1 into 4–1 within the closing part of the half.

Dudek saved Baptista’s penalty after the interval, following Hyypiä’s foul on Aliadière. It delayed the Brazilian’s hat-trick rather than preventing it: Baptista soon struck from outside the area to make it 5–1.

Liverpool kept attacking. Gerrard volleyed in and Hyypiä headed another, reducing the gap to 5–3. García, however, had also left injured, and the prospect of an unlikely recovery ended when Aliadière supplied Baptista’s fourth goal.

The score was exceptional: Liverpool had not conceded six at home since Sunderland’s 6–0 win in 1930. The immediate damage was equally plain. Arsenal had won twice at Anfield in four days, and Liverpool’s cup season at home had collapsed before the middle of January.
