Craig Bellamy and John Arne Riise scored the goals which gave Liverpool a 2–1 victory over Barcelona at Camp Nou. The holders led through Deco, but Liverpool recovered before half-time and found a winner in the second half of this Champions League last-16 first leg.

Benítez started Álvaro Arbeloa in a defence containing Finnan, Carragher and Agger, with Riise further forward. Alonso and Sissoko provided the central midfield base, while Bellamy partnered Kuyt in attack. The task was to withstand a Barcelona side which included Ronaldinho, Messi and Deco without giving up Liverpool’s own attacking possibilities.

Barcelona went ahead after fourteen minutes. Ronaldinho helped release Gianluca Zambrotta down the left and the full-back crossed to the far post, where Deco arrived unmarked to head past Reina. Further openings followed for the home side, but they failed to increase their lead.

Bellamy missed one headed opportunity before converting another just before the interval. Finnan supplied the cross, Bellamy directed it towards goal and Víctor Valdés failed to keep the ball from crossing the line. Kuyt followed it in, but the goal belonged to Bellamy.

Liverpool became more threatening after the break. A close-range Kuyt header went over before the winning move in the 74th minute. Valdés denied the Dutchman, Bellamy gathered the loose ball and passed across to Riise. The Norwegian finished with his right foot, sending the ball high into the net.

Barcelona still had a chance to level when Deco struck the frame of the goal. Liverpool held their lead and took two away goals back to Anfield. Bellamy and Riise, whose reported dispute at a training camp had dominated the build-up, had combined in the decisive attack.

The value of that second goal became clear in the return. Barcelona won 1–0 at Anfield, but the tie finished 2–2 and Liverpool advanced on away goals. The recovery at Camp Nou had supplied the narrow margin they needed to remove the holders.
