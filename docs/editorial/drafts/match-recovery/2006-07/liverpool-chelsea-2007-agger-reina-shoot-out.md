Daniel Agger’s finish brought Liverpool level in the semi-final; Pepe Reina’s penalty saves carried them into the final. A 1–0 victory over Chelsea at Anfield left the aggregate score at 1–1 after extra time, before Liverpool converted all four of their shoot-out kicks to reach Athens.

Benítez started Gerrard and Mascherano in central midfield, with Pennant and Zenden wide and Crouch alongside Kuyt up front. Alonso began on the bench. Chelsea’s one-goal advantage from Stamford Bridge meant Liverpool needed to attack while guarding against an away goal which would sharply increase the task.

The breakthrough came after 22 minutes through a well-worked free-kick. Gerrard was on the left, with Chelsea anticipating a delivery towards Liverpool’s tall forwards. Instead he sent the ball low to Agger, who finished first time past Petr Čech. A defender had supplied the goal which brought the teams level across the two legs.

Reina was needed before half-time, blocking Drogba’s shot after the striker broke through. Liverpool had further chances after the interval. Čech stopped Crouch’s downward header from Pennant’s cross with his feet, then Kuyt headed against the bar from Riise’s delivery.

Chelsea also threatened. Carragher turned Ashley Cole’s low cross over his own bar under pressure from Drogba. Neither side could find another goal in normal time, and Liverpool’s introduction of Alonso for Pennant preceded an extra half-hour.

Alonso’s shooting almost settled the tie. Čech parried his long-range effort and Kuyt put the rebound in, but the offside flag ruled it out. The Dutchman was denied again near the end, while Drogba narrowly failed to reach a cross at the other end. The semi-final went to penalties.

Zenden scored first. Reina saved from Robben, Alonso converted and Lampard replied for Chelsea. Gerrard made it three successful Liverpool kicks before Reina stopped Geremi. Kuyt then scored the decisive fourth, ending a shoot-out in which Liverpool had allowed Chelsea only one goal.

Kuyt had hit the woodwork, had a finish disallowed and been kept out by Čech, but his last attempt counted. Liverpool were through to their second European Cup final in three seasons, having again eliminated Chelsea at Anfield. This time there was no uncertainty about the decisive method: Agger’s goal earned the extra time, and Reina’s two saves gave Liverpool the clear advantage from the spot.
