Six days after scoring twice against Manchester United, Robbie Fowler scored all three Liverpool goals in a 3–1 win over Arsenal at Anfield. Stan Collymore supplied each of them as Liverpool overturned an early deficit on 23 December 1995.

Arsenal took the lead from the penalty spot. Mark Wright was penalised for his challenge on Ian Wright, who beat David James to put the visitors ahead. Liverpool had started the match with the same eleven who had beaten United, but now had to recover from a goal down.

David Seaman saved from Collymore and Michael Thomas as Liverpool sought an equaliser. It arrived five minutes before half-time. Steve McManaman found Collymore, who laid the ball off for Fowler to shoot into the top corner. The teams went into the interval level at 1–1.

Collymore was involved again when Liverpool took the lead in the second half. He flicked on Mark Wright's free-kick and Fowler ran through to finish. Fowler subsequently shot over after exchanging passes with McManaman, but his third goal followed later in the half.

This time Collymore crossed for his strike partner to head towards goal. Seaman got to the ball but could not keep hold of it, and it went into the net. Fowler had completed his hat-trick and Liverpool had a two-goal advantage.

The result put Liverpool third in the league, with Arsenal seventh. Fowler had scored five times in the two Anfield victories over United and Arsenal, while Collymore's contribution against Arsenal was recorded in all three goals. Liverpool had followed a clean-sheet win over one rival by coming from behind to beat another.
