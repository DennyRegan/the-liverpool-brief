Mohamed Salah scored twice as Liverpool beat Salzburg 4–3 at Anfield on 2 October 2019. The European champions had led by three goals before Salzburg drew level; Salah's second, in the 69th minute, finally separated the teams.

Liverpool had opened Group E with a 2–0 defeat at Napoli. Their first home game of the campaign initially offered a convincing response. Sadio Mané scored against his former club after nine minutes, exchanging passes with Roberto Firmino before finishing past Cican Stanković.

Andy Robertson made it 2–0 in the 25th minute. Having started the move on the left, he continued into the penalty area while Liverpool transferred possession across the pitch. Trent Alexander-Arnold's low delivery from the right found the left-back near goal, and Robertson supplied the finish.

Salah added the third after 36 minutes. Mané crossed, Firmino's header was saved, and Salah converted the rebound. All three forwards had contributed to the goal, but Liverpool's commanding advantage did not last until half-time. Hwang Hee-chan cut inside Virgil van Dijk and beat Adrián three minutes later.

Salzburg completed their recovery in the first quarter of an hour after the interval. Hwang crossed for Takumi Minamino to volley in their second goal after 56 minutes. Minamino then provided the low cross from which substitute Erling Haaland equalised on the hour.

At 3–3, Liverpool had lost the entire lead established by Mané, Robertson and Salah. Jürgen Klopp introduced James Milner and Divock Origi for Jordan Henderson and Georginio Wijnaldum as his side sought a fourth goal.

Firmino helped provide it. Liverpool returned a cleared corner towards the penalty area, the Brazilian headed the ball on, and Salah finished beyond Stanković. Unlike their earlier advantage, this one survived to the final whistle.

The victory brought Liverpool their first three points of the group and moved them above Salzburg into second place. It was a win achieved after two very different passages of play: three Liverpool goals in the opening 36 minutes, followed by three Salzburg replies. Salah's second finish ensured that the visitors' recovery did not also cost Liverpool the result.
