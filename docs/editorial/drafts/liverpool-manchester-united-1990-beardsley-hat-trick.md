# Beardsley’s hat-trick keeps Liverpool’s perfect start intact

Peter Beardsley returned to Liverpool’s starting line-up with a hat-trick against Manchester United on 16 September 1990. The 4–0 victory at Anfield gave Kenny Dalglish’s side five wins from their opening five league matches and a four-point lead at the top of the First Division.

United created chances of their own before Liverpool established control. Neil Webb headed against the crossbar, while Mark Robins and Mark Hughes were unable to turn promising openings into goals. At the other end, Beardsley took advantage of the opportunities made for him.

His first came after Steve McMahon and John Barnes combined on the left. Les Sealey blocked the ball following McMahon’s advance, but Beardsley followed in to score. McMahon supplied him again for the second, arriving just after the half-hour to help turn Liverpool’s narrow advantage into a two-goal lead.

Barnes made it 3–0 before the interval. Ray Houghton crossed from the right, Steve Nicol helped the ball on and Barnes headed beyond Sealey. Three different midfield contributors had been involved in the scoring moves, with Barnes supplying a threat as both creator and finisher.

Beardsley completed his hat-trick late in the second half. Houghton took a free-kick quickly, releasing him to lift the ball over Sealey. Ian Rush had chances without joining the scorers, while Liverpool kept United out to secure their second clean sheet of the league campaign.

The champions had already beaten Sheffield United, Nottingham Forest, Aston Villa and Wimbledon. United became the fifth side in that sequence, and Liverpool’s most emphatic league win of the new season left them with 15 points from a possible 15. Six days later, Beardsley would score twice more in the derby at Goodison Park.
