### Check notes
1. Hillsborough: the 9 April semi-final was played at Hillsborough a year before the disaster. The piece doesn't name the venue, so it doesn't touch it. Say if you'd rather name it.
2. Barnes and the PFA award: the Daily Post says he "celebrated his nomination as PFA player of the year". He won the award that season, but the announcement date couldn't be confirmed, so the piece now says only that he would be player of the year that season.
3. Fresh-agent check (10 Nov): fixed the Finney attribution (LFChistory only; the club's version is softer), cut the unsourced "title took longer than expected" and "beyond realistic doubt" lines, changed "12 days" to 11, softened the Barnes PFA line (date of the award not confirmable), and cut four stock transitions. Conflict noted: This Is Anfield says the win put them "within one point"; the Daily Post says two, which matches Dalglish's "six games... two points". The Daily Star gives Aldridge 27 goals after the match, consistent with the Daily Post's 26th for his first.
4. Deliberately left out: why Liverpool were not in Europe (Roworth's full quote alludes to it), the Times "stoking embers" line, Alan Green's BBC commentary (single later source), transfer fees.
