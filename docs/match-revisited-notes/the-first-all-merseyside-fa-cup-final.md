### Check notes
0. Fresh-agent check (10 Nov): no serious errors. Fixes: Rush/Juventus reworded (agreed July 1986, loaned back 1986–87); "only one in the squad" → "on the team sheet" (Paul Walsh was in the wider squad); Man Utd lead "ten points clear in November" (2 Nov 1985) not "before Christmas"; Molby "had a hand in all three" (Whelan the assist for the third); Lineker goal minute 27' (LFChistory; Express and Star says 28); goal minutes otherwise removed (LFChistory 57, 63, 84 v Express and Star 56, 62, 83); "seven minutes to go" → "six or seven"; "strong case to be the better side" cut; clichés removed. Confirmed: 88 pts v 86; Southall injured with Wales; Sheedy ex-Liverpool; fifth double club; Rush 33; Lineker 40 goals. Archive-only: Hetherington's match details, Kendall/Dalglish quotes, Rush's late chance, man of the match. Note: Lawrenson was born in Preston but played for Ireland; the "no Englishman" line is the Express and Star's and matches nationality.
