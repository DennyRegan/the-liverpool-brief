### Check notes
0. Fresh-agent check (10 Nov): Glanville/Botsford Sunday Times investigation ("The Golden Years of the Fix") stated more precisely — it alleged referees were bribed in Inter's 1964 and 1965 semi-finals; second-leg referee (Ortiz de Mendíbil, per LFChistory) deliberately not named next to the bribery allegation, since it was an allegation in a newspaper investigation rather than a proven finding about him; Mazzola's goal no longer blames a named defender (Scotsman says Yeats, This Is Anfield says Moran); Callaghan's goal now the rehearsed free-kick with Hunt's touch (LFChistory gives Hunt the assist); minutes standardised on LFChistory (34', 75'); Smith quote now verbatim; Intercontinental Cup "that September" (play-off 26 Sept 1964); referee in first leg Karl Kainer (Austria).
1. The Daily Post OCR for this page is very poor, so nothing is quoted from Yates. The noise line comes from Tommy Smith's later recollection to the Liverpool Echo, via This Is Anfield.
2. The Gerry Byrne sling detail is confirmed by the Daily Post photo caption of 5 May 1965; it can go back into the Leeds 1965 piece.
