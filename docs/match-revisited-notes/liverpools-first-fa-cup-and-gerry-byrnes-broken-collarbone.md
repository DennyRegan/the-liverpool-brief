### Check notes
0. Fresh-agent check (10 Nov): fixed the Shankly/Worcester timing (Worcester was January 1959, Shankly arrived December 1959); softened Paisley's diagnosis to match his own account (first instinct a stretcher); Byrne's injury now "early in the game" (sources give 3rd, 5th or 10th minute); the sling at the Inter parade was dropped, then restored after the Liverpool Daily Post's photo caption of 5 May 1965 confirmed it; "got down the left" now backed by the club's timeline (Byrne reached the byline on the left and cut back); cut stock phrases and the unsourced "city had never seen anything like it". "Most one-sided" is Yates's own phrase in the Daily Post OCR ("noisiest most one-sided Cup final"), meaning Liverpool's dominance, so it stays.
1. Minute disagreement on the winner: 111' (LFChistory, club, Nottingham Guardian's "nine minutes remaining") v 117' (Wikipedia). Written as "nine minutes left".
2. Deliberately left out: the Liver birds superstition, Shankly's Chelsea brochure story, the coach police escort, the semi-final referee assault (Leeds v Man Utd).
