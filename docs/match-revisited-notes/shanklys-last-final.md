### Check notes
0. Fresh-agent check (10 Nov): Fixes — Lindsay's disallowed goal moved to shortly after half-time (Wikipedia, nufc.com); Boersma: Hoby's version attributed and Daily Mirror's (taxi to team hotel) added, "before kick-off" dropped; Keegan's first no longer "volleyed" (nufc.com says controlled then shot), Hall's part attributed to Liverpool Daily Post; Heighway goal includes Clemence's long kick (liverpoolfc.com); McFaul named Willie (Iam); Shankly retirement 12 July with Charity Shield note; Macdonald's pre-match boasts pinned up by Shankly (liverpoolfc.com); stock opening and "ought to have stood" verdict removed. Confirmed: Newcastle's five Wembley wins (1924, 1932, 1951, 1952, 1955); Keegan 88' after long move; Wolves 3–0 Blackburn 1960; Thompson 20. Archive-only: Hoby details (fans kissing Shankly's feet — Wikipedia says "bowed"), Barnes details, Birmingham Daily Post verdicts, homecoming crowd estimate.
1. The Sunday Express front page says half a million were expected "today", i.e. Sunday 5 May — the day after the final. Text now says "the next day".
2. Nothing in the piece names Hillsborough (Newcastle's semi-final venue).
